# Loser: JV webinar sold straight to high-ticket, no ladder

The Ryan Magdanz (Leadbase) Rainmaker campaign, July 2026. The first full JV run of ROYA into a partner's audience. It underperformed — and it taught us the JV rung structure the hard way.

## What it was
Sell Dan's ROYA program (~$3,497 PIF / ~$5,600 on 12-pay) into Ryan's Leadbase audience via a joint webinar → Lightning Deal waitlist → self-serve deal link. No sales calls; a DM close as backup.

## What happened (the funnel)
- 200 registered → 100 live (**50% show — good**)
- 100 live → 24 Lightning Deal waitlist (**24% hand-raise — fine, intent existed**)
- 24 waitlist → **0 bought off the deal link** (the hole)
- All sales came from **Ryan personally DMing students who already knew him.** Dan's Skool profile closed **nothing.**
- 24hr auction (Mon–Tue): **top bid $500** on a $3.5k+ program — a ~7x price/value gap.
- Net: ~2–3 ROYA closes + ~2 auction sales, all relationship-driven.

## Who we were actually selling to
1. **Free-group members who'd never bought anything** — newbies/dabblers. Never paid Ryan a dollar, let alone Dan. Coldest possible buyer for a $3.5k ask.
2. **$99/mo paid-group members** — proven buyers, but they bought **Ryan**, at **$99/mo**. We asked them for a **35x jump**, to a **stranger (Dan)**, for a **different model** — and skipped the one bridge that closes that gap: the IAA book.

## Why it died
The top of funnel worked. The close died because **the only thing that converted was pre-existing trust.** Everywhere trust had to be built cold, it went to zero. Four failures stacked:

1. **Borrowed the audience, closed with a stranger.** We used Ryan's list but sold with Dan's face. Trust lives in the host (Ryan), and it doesn't transfer on a link. The only closes were Ryan's own warm contacts.
2. **Skipped the indoctrination rung — the IAA book.** The intended ladder is *book → indoctrinate into Dan's world + mechanism → ROYA warm.* We went webinar → $3.5k. The book converts them into a buyer of **Dan**, pre-kills objections at scale on paper, self-selects the serious ones, and builds desire — none of which happened.
3. **Deleted the conversation Dan's own model depends on.** Dan's native funnel is reply → qualify → send doc → link; objections die in the conversation. Webinar → link removed it (0 sales). The DM closes that worked *recreated* the conversation. The conversation is load-bearing.
4. **Unbuilt desire.** $500 top auction bid vs a $3.5k ask = the market telling us the value wasn't built. Dan's students' numbers don't transfer as proof to Ryan's audience the way Ryan's own students would.

Plus a bait/offer mismatch: the webinar teased "No Ad Spend / Sales Wingman" (free lead angle) but sold the whole ROYA pivot. We didn't ADD to their model — we asked them to bolt on a new one (violates [[jv-additive-framing]]).

## The rule it taught
**In a JV, never sell high-ticket straight off a webinar to a partner's audience. Start at a low price point and ascend.** The cheap front-end (the IAA book) is not optional — it's the load-bearing rung: it turns them into a buyer of *our* person, indoctrinates the mechanism, and pre-kills objections at scale before the high-ticket ask. The winning motion, proven by the only closes we got: **warm host + a real conversation + objections handled.** Formalize that, don't leave it as the fallback.

## Applies next to
Bill Macintosh / Russell (BuildYAI) campaign — **start low, ascend later.** Book/low-ticket front-end first, ROYA at the back, partner as closer. Do not repeat webinar → high-ticket.
