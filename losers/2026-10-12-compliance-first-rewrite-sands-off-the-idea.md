# Loser: rewriting the ad to the compliance rules first

Related: [[Promotion]] [[Workbench vs Memory]] [[spec-ads-raw-first-compliance-later]] [[meta-health-compliance-rubric]]

- What it was: Primal Viking AD 70 (the timeline ad) rewritten to mirror the brand's own safest live ad: third person only, no named conditions, feelings in place of numbers, one hedge, the guarantee as back-stop. `swipes/primal-viking/ads-2026-10-08/full/AD70-MIRROR-v2.md`.
- What happened: it had no compliance flags left. Joey's verdict, 2026-10-10: "kinda boring, let's start with a wilder idea."
- Why it died: the brief was a list of things not to say. Nothing in it gave the reader a picture or a reason to keep reading. The ad that replaced it started from an idea (Vikings still went to war in their 50s, and here is why) and was written raw.
- The rule it taught: find the idea and write it raw first. The compliance judge is a round 2 pass on an ad that already works, never the brief.
