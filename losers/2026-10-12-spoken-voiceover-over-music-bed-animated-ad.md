# Loser: spoken voice-over on a looped music bed, long animated ad

Related: [[Promotion]] [[Workbench vs Memory]] [[2026-10-03-video-ad-rules]] [[2026-10-10-ryze-mummy-song-ad-pacing]]

- What it was: the Primal Viking "52-year-old Viking" ad as a 3:43 animation. An AI narrator read the 650-word script, an unrelated instrumental was looped underneath, and each of 38 shots was cut on a sentence start.
- What happened: never ran. Joey killed it on the first 80 seconds in motion, 2026-10-10: "there's something missing from this, it doesn't hit right in the flow", "sluggish" next to a RYZE song ad.
- Why it died: shots averaged 5.9 seconds (the reference: 2.5), there were 39 pauses between lines (the reference: 5), and voice, music and picture were three separate layers with nothing locking them together. Talking speed was not the problem.
- The rule it taught: for a long animated ad, lock the words to a beat and cut on it. The same script sung as a sea shanty, cut every four beats, got "it looks and feels sooo much better".
