# curiosity-headlines

Command: `/curiosity-headlines`

Use this when a post or email is finished and you want 10 headline or subject line options that make people curious.

It finds the piece's strongest argument, then looks at it through a surprising lens (culture, history, status, enemies, odd comparisons, current events) and gives you 3 bold, 4 playful and 3 slightly wild options.

Origin: the Japanese gift-giving post ("What Japanese gift culture knows about landing WHALES").
