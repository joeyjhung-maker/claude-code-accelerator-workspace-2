See before we touched anything, this client was converting 12.27% of their leads.

Not bad.

But there was deffo money left on the table.

So we got surgical.

First move: we cut the SMS copy down and removed all the emojis.

Sounds dumb.

But at this scale, every character costs money.

We saved about 30% on SMS costs alone and that all went to increasing our margins.

Second: we completely changed the tone.

Went from soft and friendly to direct authority.

"Your application is about to expire. Take action now"

That one line shift lifted response quality.

Third (and this is the move that actually moved the needle): we stopped sending peeps to a naked cart page.

Graham built a short link that pre-populates their info.

Now they just add card details and hit submit. Super easy.

And the conversion bump was instant.

Just small problems fixed fast.
