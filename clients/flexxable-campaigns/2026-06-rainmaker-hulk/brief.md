## The one central idea
**The Hulk Android** = a master conductor that runs ALL the androids at once, deployed with one click.

Before: you could only run one android at a time (they'd clash — a nightmare). Now Hulk deploys 2, 3, 4, 5+ androids simultaneously, working together like an orchestra. It "manages your entire AI agency" for you.

This cracks the two blocks that kill one-man AI agencies before they start:
1. **The sales block** — getting a prospect to say YES (and pick you over everyone).
2. **The tech/delivery block** — actually BUILDING the thing without drowning in GoHighLevel/integration hell.

Hulk removes both. One person does the work of a 10-man agency, and gets paid like one.

## What ROYA means (the frame under the whole campaign)
**Rent Out Your Android.** Not "build an agency." Not "become a service provider."

You're a **landlord**, not a freelancer. We give you the asset (an Android) → it gets placed inside a business → it generates revenue → you take a chunky cut. The "UN-business": no employees, no deliverables/deadlines, no boss.

The mindset flip that powers the offer: most people think *more income = more clients = more work = more business*. ROYA does the opposite — **add Androids, not clients.** Stack 5 androids on ONE client and out-earn an agency owner running five.

Promise: land your first $10k client in 30–60 days, scale to $20k+ by year end.

## The Androids (the product stack Hulk deploys)
- **DBR / Sleeping Beauty** — Database Reactivation. The "foot in the door" play. Turns dead CRM leads into revenue, no ad spend.
- **Speed 2 Lead (S2L)** — instant follow-up the moment someone opts in.
- **48 Hour Follow-Up** — persistent follow-up window.
- **Reputation Android** — asks for reviews post-interaction; routes 4–5★ to Google, 1–3★ to the manager. (Proof: brother's biz TruNinja, 3.7→4.2 in 72h.)
- **Social Media Android** — handles Meta ad comments + FB/IG DMs 24/7, sentiment-checks and routes (buyer → checkout, abuse → flag).
- **Live Chat Android (new)** — 24/7 website chat; catches the 80–90% who never opt in. Pitch: "£25k/yr rep that works 8hrs → 24/7 for £1k/mo."

Standalone sells: Reputation, Social, Live Chat each ~£1–2k/mo.
