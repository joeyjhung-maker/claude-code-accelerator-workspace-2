Public source: [ROYA Results — Scale](https://roya-proof.pages.dev/proof/scale/).

Every result below comes from the page. Preserve its qualifiers exactly: `invoiced` is not
collected, `run rate` is not banked revenue, and a contract value is not current income. The page
also states that big results are real but rare; $2k–$5k monthly retainers are the more common
picture of a working ROYA business.

## Reusable mechanism note

The page’s shared path is: get one client producing with a small test, widen inside the same
trusted client, then earn referrals. Bigger databases raise the ceiling but do not determine
whether someone can get started.
