# Topa free-database click-driver: standing rules

Related: [[client]] [[2026-10-06-flexxable-topa-350m-database-optin-headlines]] [[b2b-leads-lab-project]] [[Promotion]]

## The play (Joey, 2026-10-19)
- A short Skool post pushing the free Topa plan (350M+ contacts, 50 million organisations) to the opt-in page: `https://app.topa.io/lander`.
- Goes weekly to **AAA Ninjas, ROYA and Million Dollar AI Deals. Not B2B Leads Lab.** Job: mop up extra leads.
- Benchmark: the last Topa push, during the LinkedIn campaign, got about **250 opt-ins**. Judge each week against that. Internal number, never in the copy.
- Super short. One link. No keyword comment, no P.S., no `Dan` sign-off.

## What Joey rejected (round 1, same day)
- "These suck. Dial up curiosity more."
- Label lines that pretend to be something: "Serious question." isn't a serious question. Also banned: "Quick one", "Real talk", "Listen".
- The same tagline stapled on every version ("Free plan. Unlimited access.").
- Flat feature lists and "Most people are out here..." openers. A short click-driver is not the place to explain Topa.

## What to do instead
- The headline opens a loop the reader can only close by clicking. Concrete, a little unbelievable. The post teases, the page pays off.
- Different lens per version, not the same post reworded: scale math, a mechanism tease, a Rolodex comparison, "your dream client is in here", name-a-job-title. Method: [[curiosity-headlines]].
- Say "free" once, in the voice of that post.
- Only true facts: 350M+ contacts, 50 million organisations, mobiles/emails/addresses, free plan. No invented Dan experience ("I searched...").
