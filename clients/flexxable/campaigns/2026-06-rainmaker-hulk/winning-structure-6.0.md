# The Winning Structure — ROYA 6.0 ($1.08m), mapped to the Hulk campaign

*ROYA 6.0 (Sep 2025, "Sales Wingman" campaign) is the most successful ROYA launch ever — **$1.08m gross**. We are modelling the June Hulk campaign on this exact skeleton. Full source: `source/roya-6.0-WINNER-SOURCE.md`. This file is the map: what each phase does, why it works, and the Hulk equivalent.*

## The big pattern (why 6.0 worked)
1. **Reveal a NEW hero tool BEFORE opening doors.** 6.0 led with *Sales Wingman* (closes + builds bots for you). It's the "AI does the selling AND the tech" promise that dissolves the two blocks (sales + tech). → **Hulk's hero tool = The Hulk Android** (runs every android in one click).
2. **Webinar = "Reveal Party"** for that hero tool. Registration sequence sells the *reveal*, not the program. Doors to ROYA open the day AFTER, on the replay email.
3. **Then a long daily open-cart nurture** — one email/day, each a single angle, each with its own Messenger keyword CTA (reply-based, no sales calls).
4. **Mid-campaign, reveal ANOTHER new android** to re-energise the list (6.0 dropped the *Reputation Android* on day ~13). → Hulk "Part 5: Reveal New Bonus/Addition."
5. **Payment plan + hard deadline is the close engine.** 6.0 introduced an **11-payment plan ($499/mo)** with a fixed end date, then ramped scarcity over ~7 days with multiple sends on the final day (1pm / 6pm / 11pm).
6. **Testimonials carry the whole thing** — a fresh proof story every day, rotated by avatar (9-5 escapee, agency owner, freelancer, benefits-to-rich).

## The 6.0 sequence (37 sends over ~6 weeks)

### Phase 1 — Pre-launch tease + tool reveal (emails 0–4.3)
Tease "biggest update ever" → reveal Sales Wingman → reveal-party hype → drip ONE killer feature (the "Revenue Rescue Calculator") → testimonial tease (Mal's $10k/mo). ~6 emails, all driving webinar reg.

### Phase 2 — Webinar day (5.1 / 5.2 / 5.3)
Same-day chasers: morning ("today's the day"), 7-hours-out, 1-hour-out.

### Phase 3 — Replay + DOORS OPEN (email 6)
Replay link + the big pivot: open ROYA, and frame the hero tool as a **free bonus** for joining. CTA keyword "DOORS OPEN."

### Phase 4 — Daily open-cart nurture (emails 7–21) — the engine room
One angle per email. The 6.0 rotation, in order:
- **Unfireable** (layoffs/job-loss fear) — Katarrey proof
- **Why-problem email** ("you're selling AI, not *more customers*") — Aaron/Mal proof
- **Ecom demo** (Jamie Shopify "mic drop")
- **Testimonial: Aaron** (benefits → $123k/mo)
- **Fastest way to 10k** (short CTA email)
- **Stacking upsells** (Aaron, Seb, mystery 7-fig gent)
- **Testimonial: Mal** (Tuscany, one coffee date → $10k/mo super-connector)
- **95% of AI projects fail** (MIT stat → "good news for you")
- **ROYA reviews** (Kelvin social proof)
- **Competitor comparison** (CloseBot vs Appointwise vs ROYA — 3 reasons)
- **"Sales Wingman saved my ass"** (Jamie's 7-minute demo rebuild → closed)

### Phase 5 — Reveal a NEW android mid-flight (emails 18–21)
Drop the **Reputation & Customer Feedback Android** as a fresh bonus → 3–4 emails working it (passive 1k/mo, "you can't lose," stickiness, "never lose a client"). Re-energises the list right before the close. → **This is the Hulk "Part 5."**

### Phase 6 — Payment plan + scarcity ramp (emails 22–28)
Tease "this can't stay open" → reveal **11-pay $499/mo** → justify with cost/savings math (Sales Wingman pays for ROYA) → support-stack reassurance → deadline pressure ("11-pay GONE tomorrow"). Path A vs Path B framing.

### Phase 7 — Final close (emails 29–36.3)
Multiple deadline days, each carried by a testimonial with urgency:
- **Don** — one call → potential $500k/yr
- **Neeshea** — joined, 3 coffee dates + 1 yes in 24h
- **Josh** — 2 ecom clients closed in 24h
- **Jay** — $20k, 60h after a cold LinkedIn message
- **Charles** — $1k/week from a cold stranger
- **"If you've been waiting for the right time"** — the emotional fence-sitter close
Final day = **3 sends** (8am / 12pm / 4pm), last one "ONE hour left."
