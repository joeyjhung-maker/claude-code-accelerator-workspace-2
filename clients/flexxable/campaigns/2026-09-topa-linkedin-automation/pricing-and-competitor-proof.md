## Pulled back — do NOT claim

- **Lead database vs Apollo.** Sep 23: "Truthfully, our lead database is just like Apollo or whatever at the moment." Being "better than anyone else" at list-building is his *plan*, not a current fact.
- **Database coverage.** The data is licensed, doesn't cover all of LinkedIn, and "some are better than others." He puts accuracy at "like 90%." (Sep 4) He also said on Aug 28: "I use other database tools that are amazing and better than what I've [got]."
- **Knocking other tools' AI openers.** Sep 25: he had Nat amend a post saying other LinkedIn platforms are crap for their AI icebreakers, because Topa does "exactly" that to start with. His rule: "make sure we're not saying it can do anything that it can't do yet."
- **Hey Reach (~$50M valuation, 10k users) and SalesFlow (20k users).** He used these on Aug 28 to prove to Dan that the category works. It's internal, not a "we beat them" claim.
- **Ocean.io.** On Sep 4 he recommended it over Sales Navigator for heavy list-building. Don't position it as a competitor Topa beats.

## Open items

- **$79 vs $99 slips.** On Aug 28 he said "$99 a month" before correcting to "$79 for one account." He also said "$97" once on Sep 23. Always quote **$79 (1 account) / $99 (2 accounts)**.
- **The Dallas free seat.** He said "just say instead of 99" to describe its value. The accurate figure for 1 account is $79.
