Rewrite of the launch video script after Joey's notes (2026-10-05). Write a VOICE-OVER SCRIPT for a launch video for Topa's LinkedIn automation tool (topa.io). It plays over motion graphics. A narrator reads it. It is a launch film AND a mini VSL.

## Format rules for this job
- SPOKEN words only. 390 to 430 words total. Read aloud at about 165 words a minute. Write for the ear: short sentences, plain words, nothing that only works on a page (no CAPS-only emphasis tricks, no brackets, no emojis).
- One sentence per line.
- The narrator is the Topa brand speaking as "we". NOT Dan, NOT first-person "I", no personal backstory. Casual, direct, a little cheeky. Talking to one business owner as "you".
- Say "Topa". Never "Topa, our new LinkedIn automation tool".
- First, give me 6 alternative LEADS (the opening 4 to 6 lines each), all following Joey's shape: what everyone knows about the big deals on LinkedIn, who the decision makers are, what one deal does for you, then the turn into "yet few know how to get them". The very first line of each must be a statement, max 14 words, not a question. Label them L1 to L6.
- Then write the full script using the lead you think is strongest. Mark the sections with a plain label line in square brackets ([LEAD], [PROBLEM], [TURN], [MECHANISM], [PROOF], [HOW IT WORKS], [DATABASE], [OBJECTION], [PRICE], [CTA]). The label lines are not spoken.

## Who is watching
Agency owners, consultants, coaches and B2B service sellers who sell to OTHER business owners. They already believe LinkedIn is where the big-ticket buyers are. They have tried DMing decision makers and got ignored. They have 500 to a few thousand connections they have never worked. They are wary of "LinkedIn automation" because of spam and bans.

## The one idea
Everyone knows the big deals are on LinkedIn. Everyone is fighting for them in the DMs, and the DMs are where deals go to die. The people who close do three things differently: they start with people who already know their name, they open with a message that has no pitch in it, and they get OUT of the DMs fast, onto the phone, the email and the desk. Topa does all three for you.

## The journey the script must walk (in this order)
1. LEAD: Joey's shape. What everyone knows. $250k to $1m plus deals. Decision makers with real budgets. One deal can change your life. Yet few know how to get them.
2. PROBLEM: everyone is DMing these people and nobody is closing. Paint it from the decision maker's side: an inbox full of pitches from strangers, all saying the same thing, all deleted. And even when a DM does get a reply, the convo dies in the DMs because nobody picks up the phone.
3. TURN: the few who do close are not writing cleverer pitches. They start with the people who already know their name (their existing connections), they send something with no pitch in it, and they move the convo off LinkedIn.
4. MECHANISM, the "hand raiser": that is why we built Topa. Topa connects to your LinkedIn account and sends your existing connections one short message for you. We call it the hand raiser. There is no pitch in it. The exact wording stays locked until you are a member, you get it the moment you join. If they do not reply, Topa follows up with a second and third message.
5. PROOF: Jonathan, who runs Topa, sent the hand raiser to 84 of his own existing connections. 23 replied. That is a 27% reply rate. Use these exact numbers. No other results.
6. HOW IT WORKS, fast: connect your LinkedIn account. Pick who to message: your existing connections, or new people. The AI helper suggests your messages. Set your hours. It runs on autopilot. Every reply lands in one inbox. Click "Get Cell Phone" and for about seven in ten people their mobile number comes back in a few seconds. Now you are ringing someone who just replied to you, so the call is warm.
7. DATABASE: this is how you get out of the DMs. You can search the whole database of 350 million plus contacts. And you can download 10,000 of them a month with full contact info: mobile, email, address and more. So you can follow up by phone, by email, and for the hottest names, with a gift on their desk (lumpy mail). Do not claim Topa ships the gift. Cash this out to coffee dates and big deals.
8. OBJECTION, "will LinkedIn ban me", kept SHORT (3 lines max): LinkedIn is strict and we will not pretend otherwise. Topa keeps it slow on purpose: randomised send times, a residential IP in your own location, limits that start at 15 connection requests and 20 messages a day, and you still write every reply yourself. NEVER say "safe", "guaranteed", "approved" or "compliant" as a promise. No tool can promise what LinkedIn will do.
9. PRICE: $79 a month for one LinkedIn account. $99 for two. The database is built in, so you are not paying about $100 a month for Sales Navigator on top. Frame it against the deal size from the lead: one deal covers it many times over (do not put a multiple or a number on that).
10. CTA: go to topa.io, connect your LinkedIn account, and get the hand raiser. End on a line that calls back to the lead (the big deals are on LinkedIn, now you know how to go and get them).
