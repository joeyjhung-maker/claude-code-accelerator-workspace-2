# PROOF BLOCK BRIEF: Topa launch video

Write the [PROOF] block of a voice-over script. Give me 4 options, labelled P1 to P4. Max 42 words each, 3 to 4 lines, one sentence per line. Spoken, written for the ear. Narrator is the Topa brand ("we"). No other output.

## The line right before your block
"For the connections you already have, Topa sends one short message with no pitch in it… we call it the hand raiser."

## The line right after your block
"Connect your account, set your hours, and it runs."

## The facts (use ONLY these, exactly)
These are two of Jonathan's own campaigns inside Topa. Jonathan runs Topa. On screen the viewer sees the real dashboard numbers while you speak.
- Campaign one: 30 people messaged. 22 replied. That is a 73% reply rate (the dashboard shows 73.3%).
- Campaign two, a new-connections campaign: 12 new connections accepted his request. 11 of those 12 replied. That is a 92% reply rate.

## Rules
- Do NOT say what either campaign's message said. Do NOT say these were sent to existing connections. Do NOT call them hand raiser results. Do not explain why they worked.
- Do not promise the viewer the same result. No "imagine", no "you could".
- Say the counts as well as the percentages, the counts are what make it believable.
- Round 73.3% to "73 percent" in speech.
- No "insane", "crazy", "game-changer", "unreal". Let the numbers do it.
- No em dashes. No fragment stacks.
