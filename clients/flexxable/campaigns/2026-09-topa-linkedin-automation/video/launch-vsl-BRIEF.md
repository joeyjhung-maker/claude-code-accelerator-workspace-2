# BRIEF: Topa launch video script (mini VSL, voice-over read)

Write a VOICE-OVER SCRIPT for a 2-minute launch video for Topa's LinkedIn automation tool (topa.io).
It plays over motion graphics. A narrator reads it. It is a launch film AND a mini VSL, so it must carry every direct-response beat and end on a clear call to action.

## Format rules for this job
- SPOKEN words only. 330 to 380 words total. It gets read aloud at about 155 words a minute, so write for the ear: short sentences, plain words, nothing that only works on a page (no CAPS-only emphasis tricks, no brackets, no emojis, no "…" used as glue inside a clause).
- One sentence per line.
- The narrator is the Topa brand speaking as "we". NOT Dan, NOT first-person "I", no personal backstory. Casual, direct, a little cheeky. Talking to one business owner as "you".
- Say "Topa". Never "Topa, our new LinkedIn automation tool".
- First, give me 8 alternative OPENING LINES (the hook). Each one max 14 words, a statement not a question, no pain-first opener, no testimonial opener. Lead with curiosity, novelty, a promise or a world-level observation. It must read like information or a confession, not like an ad. Label them H1 to H8.
- Then write the full script using the hook you think is strongest. Mark the sections with a plain label line in square brackets ([HOOK], [DESIRE], [PROBLEM], [MECHANISM], [PROOF], [HOW IT WORKS], [OBJECTION], [PRICE], [CTA]) so the editor can time the visuals. The label lines are not spoken.

## Who is watching
Agency owners, consultants, coaches and B2B service sellers. They sell to OTHER business owners. They already believe LinkedIn is where the big-ticket buyers are. They have somewhere between 500 and a few thousand LinkedIn connections they have never worked. They have tried, or are scared of, "LinkedIn automation" because of spam and bans.

## The one idea
The best leads you will get this year are already in your LinkedIn connections. They know your name. You have just never asked them how business is.
Topa asks for you, puts every reply in one inbox, and hands you their mobile number, so the first call is never cold.

## The journey the script must walk (in this order)
1. HOOK: curiosity or promise. World-level. See rules above.
2. DESIRE: the overt benefit is BIG DEALS and coffee dates with real business owners. The business owners with the big budgets are on LinkedIn, not Instagram.
3. PROBLEM (pain is the amplifier here, not the opener): they are sitting on 500+ connections who already know their name and they have never messaged them. Manual outreach is boring so it happens for three days then stops. And even when a DM gets a reply, the convo dies in the inbox because nobody picks up the phone.
4. MECHANISM, the "hand raiser": Topa sends your existing connections a short, non-salesy opener like "How's business?" for you, a few a day, like a person would. Then follows up automatically if they do not reply (a 3-message sequence).
5. PROOF: Jonathan, who runs Topa, sent that message to 84 of his own existing connections. 23 replied. That is a 27% reply rate. Use these exact numbers. No other results.
6. HOW IT WORKS, fast: connect your LinkedIn account. Pick who to message: your existing connections, or new people from the built-in database of 350 million contacts. The AI helper suggests your messages. Set your hours. It runs on autopilot. Every reply lands in one inbox. Click "Get Cell Phone" and their mobile number comes back in a few seconds. Now you ring someone who has just replied to you, so the call is warm.
7. One line that threads direct mail back in: the hottest names on that reply list are the ones worth sending lumpy mail to (a gift on their desk). Do not claim Topa ships the gift. Then cash out to coffee dates.
8. OBJECTION, "will LinkedIn ban me": be honest. Send times are randomised. It logs in from a residential IP in your own location. Daily limits are deliberately low: 15 connection requests and 20 messages a day to start. You still write every reply yourself. NEVER say "safe", "guaranteed", "approved" or "compliant" as a promise. No tool can promise what LinkedIn will do. We play it slower than most on purpose.
9. PRICE: $79 a month for one LinkedIn account. $99 for two. Mobile numbers cost less than 8 cents each. The 350 million contact database is built in, so you do not need to pay about $100 a month for Sales Navigator on top. One client a year pays for it.
10. CTA: go to topa.io, connect your LinkedIn account, and send your first hand raiser today. End on a line that calls back to the hook.

## Hard facts (use ONLY these, verbatim numbers)
- $79/mo for 1 LinkedIn account, $99/mo for 2
- Mobile number lookup: less than 8 cents each
- Database: 350 million contacts
- Proof: 84 existing connections messaged, 23 replies, 27% reply rate (Jonathan, who runs Topa)
- Limits: 15 connection requests + 20 messages a day to start
- Randomised send times, residential IP in your location
- Sales Navigator: about $100 a month
- The opener: "How's business?"

## Do NOT say
- No free leads, free trial, coupon codes or discounts. No fake urgency or deadline.
- No "AI icebreakers better than other tools", no "better than Apollo", no lookalike targeting, no address lookup, no ad-spend checker, no CRM claims.
- No invented results, client names, income claims or reply-rate promises beyond the proof above.
- No "leverage", "solution", "deliverable", "onboarding", "game-changer", "unlock", "supercharge".
- Do not open the script on a question, on pain, or on a testimonial.
