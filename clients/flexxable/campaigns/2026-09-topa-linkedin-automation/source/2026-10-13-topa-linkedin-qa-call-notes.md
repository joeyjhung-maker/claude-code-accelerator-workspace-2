**Signals connection note:** "saw your post about [keyword]". No pitch and no CTA.

**Jonny on connection notes:** sometimes a note gets a *worse* response, "because people know I'm gonna try and pitch them" (00:14:00). He leaves them off unless he's running signals.

## How Jonny runs his own agency (useful for DBR-member content)

- Still leads with DBR, "the offer of the century", because it gets replies. Then he works out what else to sell (00:50:49).
- Sweet spot for AI automation: **10–100 staff** companies (00:57:16).
- **Deal structure** (00:57:37): free trial on 300 old leads, and they keep any sales. Then he offers a retainer or pay-on-performance option, priced so the retainer looks better. Minimum retainer **£595/mo** for 500 leads/mo. About **$2,500/mo** for 2,000 leads/mo, and he covers SMS costs. A client feeding in 2,000 leads a month is probably spending "maybe 50K or more a month" on PPC (no currency given).
- **The B2B close move:** once someone shows intent in the DMs, hit Get Cell Phone and *ring them*. "That's where everyone goes wrong in B2B" (00:41:28).
- Car leasing is a good DBR niche on LinkedIn because it's B2B. Car dealers are thin on LinkedIn (00:36:47).

## Reselling / white label (00:59:33)

- Available now. Agencies charge clients **$200–300/mo per account** and pay TOPA the seat.
- The client doesn't need to know TOPA exists. They just see messages going out from their LinkedIn.
- White-label login: Settings → Users → white label. **Inbox only**, generic domain, your logo. Clients can't build campaigns themselves, by design.
- Jonny: "the easiest business model in the world". A lot of people on the call wanted to resell (01:07:35).
- **Angle:** "Buy a second seat, plug in a client, charge $200–300/mo." Strong post or email for the AI-agency crowd.
