# Brief: Skool objection post — "Is it safe? Will LinkedIn ban my account?"

ONE Skool post from Dan in the Topa LinkedIn campaign. Headline (HL:) + body + "Dan". About 250-320 words.

## The objection (say it in the reader's words, then handle it honestly)
"Is this safe? Will I get my LinkedIn banned? LinkedIn is super strict."
Validate it first: yes, LinkedIn IS strict, and people absolutely do get their accounts restricted. That fear is fair.

## The reframe
It's not "automation" that gets people flagged, it's DODGY automation: tools blasting hundreds of requests a day, at robot speed, logging your UK account in from a server on the other side of the world. That's what trips the alarms.

## What Topa does instead (only these facts, from Johnny on the walkthrough)
- It behaves like a human: send times are randomised, not machine-gunned out.
- Sensible daily limits: a new account starts at just 15 connection requests and 20 messages a day. After a few weeks you can build up, and the max Johnny runs is 25 connection requests and 50 messages a day. Some platforms let you go higher; Topa deliberately doesn't.
- It logs in from a residential IP in YOUR location. Pick London, it's London. Not some random country.
- Campaigns run in the hours you set, e.g. 9 to 5, Monday to Friday, like a normal person working.
- Johnny's own experience: he runs at those max limits and hasn't had any problems. (His words: "we don't have any problems doing that.")
- You still write every reply yourself. Topa doesn't automate the convo and likely never will.

## Honesty rules (hard)
- Never say "100% safe", "guaranteed", "you'll never get banned", "LinkedIn approved" or "LinkedIn compliant". No tool can promise what LinkedIn will do.
- Frame it as: we play it slower and safer than most, on purpose. Slow and steady is the whole point (little every day builds the network).
- No invented stats, ban rates or user counts.

## Tie back (one or two lines, not a lecture)
Slow and steady is what builds the network anyway… 15-20 requests a day into your niche, a warm name, more replies, more peeps you can send lumpy mail to, more coffee dates.
