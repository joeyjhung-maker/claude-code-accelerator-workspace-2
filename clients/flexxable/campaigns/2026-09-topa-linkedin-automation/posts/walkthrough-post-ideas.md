## Tactical / how-to (Adult: clearly doable)
1. **The client list hiding in your phone** (18:00) — Dan's starter tip: go through your phone contacts from the last 5 years, you'd be surprised how many are business owners. LinkedIn is the same move. Assignment post: "Scroll your phone tonight, count the biz owners."
2. **200 roofers in Texas in one search** (12:22–13:39) — AI search, type anything as specific as you want; filter by job function, headline, About; 500+ connections = they actually log in. Show-your-work post.
3. **Don't waste a bottle on someone without their mobile** (36:13–36:44) — Johnny: maybe 25% ring straight off the gift, but you've got to ring them, "don't waste bullets" on leads with no cell number. A rule post.
4. **Where to actually send the bottle** (37:41–38:05) — check the address on their website against where their LinkedIn says they live; if they're hundreds of miles from the office, they're probably not going in. Quick practical tip.
5. **The "I don't like it when booze goes to waste" call** (32:28) — Dan's gift follow-up opener: "Hey, I sent you a bottle of booze in the post, I don't like it when booze goes to waste, did you get it?" → they chuckle → easy transition to the offer/coffee date. Script post.
6. **Run two campaigns, not one** (21:33–21:52) — Johnny sets up one on existing connections (low-hanging fruit) and a second on new connections. 3 messages, no reply after 3 = move on.
7. **The AI message writer tip: lead with a hook** (22:33) — it scans your website; Johnny's advice is to lead with something you can do for them (free audit, case study, free trial).

## Philosophy / stance (Parent: clearly different)
12. ✅ **Worked 20 years ago, will work 20 years from now** (intro) — written as the philosophy post.
13. **Don't try to sell in the DM** (23:24–23:58) — Dan: selling an offer via automated LinkedIn is "a really hard thing to do"; get a hand-raiser reply and move them to the offer manually. 25% of 500 = ~125 convos; "if you're not closing like 5… something's a little wrong." (= angle #8 from the brainstorm.)
14. **Your offer is the message** (27:53–29:29) — "are you interested in more sales, only pay us afterwards?" vs "we've got a great deal on business insurance" ("that would be a hell no"). Would YOU reply to it? Offer strength beats wording.
15. **60% of direct mail is the data** (09:00) — Dan: "a rough number, but 60% of the success comes down to the data." 100 bottles ≈ $5,000, so make sure every one lands in the right hands. ⚠️ Conflicts with the banked "40% list / 40% offer / 20% copy" principle in `project.md`. Use Dan's hedge ("rough number") or reconcile before posting.
16. **It's 2026, almost 2027** (09:30) — why now: list brokers used to be the only option; AI enrichment changed direct mail. (Overlaps #12, better as a line inside it.)

21. **The ladder: LinkedIn → phone → bottle** (34:31–37:28) — Dan's own challenge: 125 convos, 10 look interested… "show me how you'd send them a bottle." Travis's integration, straight from the video.
22. **The 4-step "what to do next"** (39:56) — Dan's own recap: join the free community → grab 200 free leads → get started on LinkedIn → start sending bottles. That's a ready-made 3-step (well, 4) plan post, from Dan's mouth.
