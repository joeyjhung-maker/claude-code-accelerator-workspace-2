# Brief: 10 VICIOUS headline/hook options — "Cancel your Sales Nav" Skool post (Dan)

Related: [[cancel-sales-nav-BRIEF]] [[pricing-and-competitor-proof]] [[hooks]]

Joey rejected the tame hooks ("Cancel your Sales Nav", "Sales Nav gives you a name. Topa gives you a number."). Dial it UP. He wants 10 options.

## What the post will say (the body the hook has to open)
1. Sales Nav finds you the perfect prospect... then leaves you stuck knocking on their LinkedIn inbox like everyone else. No mobile number. It's a phone book with the numbers ripped out.
2. Price flip: Sales Nav Core is $119.99 a month... just to SEARCH. Topa is $79 a month: full search of 350 million+ contacts, automated LinkedIn outreach, and 10,000 downloads a month to enrich with mobiles, emails and addresses.
3. Topa can find a mobile number for about 70% of people. Find them on LinkedIn, chances are you can ring them.
4. Payoff, real Dan quote: people ask "how did you get my number?" Dan says "I'm a very resourceful person. Would you like me working on behalf of your business?" They chuckle. It works really well.

## Audience
Business owners and AI-agency / B2B lead-gen people in Dan's Skool groups. Many pay for Sales Nav right now. Many have sent LinkedIn DMs and InMails into the void.

## Hook rules (vicious = it flinches)
- Go for the throat in line one. Name the thing they already feel: paying $119.99 a month to stare at profiles, InMails ignored, a perfect prospect they can't actually reach, LinkedIn making money off them.
- Charged, blunt, Germanic words. Protect the charged word. Caveman language.
- Specific: $119.99, 70%, 350 million. Specifics read like information, not an ad.
- Must not read like an ad. News, a confession, a callout, a paradox.
- Push to the edge of believable, never past it.
- Mix the shapes: callout, paradox, enemy (LinkedIn/Sales Nav), confession, cost-of-waiting, curiosity.
- Max ~12 words each. Skool headline length.

## Truth rules (hard)
- Never say or imply we give away LinkedIn Sales Navigator, and never say "free". Topa is PAID ($79). No free leads, no coupon code.
- Say "Topa", no brackets.
- Don't say Sales Nav is useless or a scam. Its real gap: no mobile numbers, and you pay just to search.
- Don't claim Topa's data is better or more accurate than Sales Nav or Apollo.
- Only use these numbers: $119.99, $79, 350 million+, about 70%, 10,000 a month. No invented reply rates or results.

## Output
10 hooks, numbered, one line each. No commentary.
