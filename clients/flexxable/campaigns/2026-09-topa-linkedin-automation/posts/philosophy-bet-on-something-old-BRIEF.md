I've been doing my due diligence over the last few months deciding on whether or not I want to invest in a new business.

And I'm happy to say that I've made that decision and we're full steam ahead.

And the reason I'm excited is because the business I'm investing in and becoming part of worked 20 years ago, and it's going to work 20 years from now, okay?

With things moving so fast in the AI space, creating a new SaaS product or whatever that might be, I think it's at real risk of being overtaken or being replaced by something superior. It's just too complicated and too confusing out there when it comes to launching a new business in my opinion.

The business I'm investing in, it's a direct mail company. Some of you might have seen me over the last few months sending bottles or anything of high value to a client with a lock on top, okay? And what happens when they receive this bottle, they have to call with the number given on the letter that we send as part of this package to unlock this in order to get the bottle of booze in this case. It works exceptionally well. We're getting on calls with roughly 25% of the people we send the bottle to.

It works like nothing I've ever seen before and the numbers stack really, really well.

And one of the major reasons this is working so well is because now we're able to use AI and all these incredible AI skills that we have to be able to enrich the data, the database of where we send this high value gift to.

Previously you would have to go and buy that list from a list broker and the data could be somewhat sketchy at the best of times, but now we're able to enrich our own data and the quality that we can get, the level of detail we can go into the individual that we're sending the bottle to is just off the charts, which means that the effectiveness of direct mail has just gone through the roof and I don't think anyone really understands that at the moment.

So I'm pretty excited about where this is going, but I have to say I was still a little bit on the fence because I don't like to jump into a new business idea without being completely sold. But what they showed me blew me away.

There's three parts to this puzzle. The first one is the gift with the lock on top or the cage, the high value gift, which just works so well. Then you've got the enriched database, which blows this whole thing out of the water compared to what might have worked five years ago. But the third thing is being able to build a LinkedIn presence around your niche.

So let's say you were going after the HVAC niche. You could send this bottle to HVAC business owners. You could create incredible database of HVAC business owners and get the details of the individual owners that you wanted to send the bottle to. But what happens after you've sent this? The follow-up's important, but we're also built into the system a way to build a LinkedIn profile and presence around that niche, around that HVAC niche.

Today, I'm jumping on a call with a co-founder of the business, Johnny, and we're going to show you how we build a presence around a niche on LinkedIn using the third piece of the puzzle. Okay, so without further ado, I'm going to jump in. I hope you understand how powerful this is, and I'll see you in a bit.
