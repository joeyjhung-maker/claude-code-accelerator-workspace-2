## The argument (the whole post hinges on this — make it land hard)
LinkedIn shows you lots of POTENTIAL leads... but doesn't help you GET to them.
- They don't help you build your network and grow your connections.
- They don't help you open the conversations. Giving you some InMails means jack if they don't help you SEND them and get them opened and replied to.
- They don't help you find the actual contact info you need. In fact they do everything they can to HIDE it.
So they charge you about $100 a month for the privilege of looking at a bunch of names. They get you salivating at the "perfect lead"... then pull the rug out from under you.

## Beats (in this order)
1. HL: "LinkedIn is GATEKEEPING your best leads" (keep it).
2. The tease: Sales Nav shows you the perfect prospect and gets you salivating.
3. The rug-pull: the three "they don't" beats above. Make each one a concrete little picture, not abstract. Don't stack three abstract benefits.
4. Metaphor, carried through: it's like paying a BOUNCER about $100 a month to let you stand outside the VIP room... nose pressed against the glass, looking at everyone you wanna talk to... and he NEVER lifts the rope.
5. The flip: Topa doesn't get you a better bouncer. Topa gets you the owner's mobile number. Mirror the three "they don't" beats, each one answered:
   - Topa sends connection requests every day, so your network grows while you do anything else.
   - Topa opens the conversations for you with an automated message sequence.
   - Topa can find a mobile number for about 70% of people. With 10,000 downloads a month you can pull their mobiles, emails and addresses.
   - Full search of 350 million+ contacts.
   - $79 a month.
6. Payoff (real proof): Jonathan (the guy who built Topa) messaged 84 of his existing connections and got 23 replies. That's a 27% response rate. Then you grab their mobile and ring them. The phone is where the money's at. You skip the queue.
7. CTA.
