HL: Supercharged Cold Outreach: why it books meetings when normal outreach gets ignored

I call our new outreach system the “Supercharged Cold Outreach” for a reason.

Because we don’t focus on one channel… it's a few of them stacked on the SAME person, so the coffee date becomes inevitable.

FIRST…

We automate LinkedIn.

The system builds your audience, adds new connections, and starts real conversations for y automatically with your ideal ICP.

That by itself is a huge gamechanger.

But the real beauty happens AFTER you're chatting and building a bit of rapport.

That's where you've got two routes:

Route A (the cost-saving one): hit the "GET CELL PHONE" button inside Topa and it finds their PERSONAL mobile.

You ring 'em… "Hey, we've been chatting on LinkedIn, thought it'd be easier to have a quick call"… and then book the coffee date.

It takes balls but if you’re hungry, this is the FASTEST way to book calls.

Route B (the heavy hitter): use Topa to enrich their data… get their physical address… and send 'em a locked gift in the mail.

This is the lumpy mail approach we've been using to book meetings with $10 to $50 million a year businesses, and it's working like gangbusters.

And here's WHY Route B hits so hard…

By the time that gift lands… they already KNOW you from LinkedIn.

They're starting to like you, maybe even trust you.

Your name isn't cold anymore.

THEN a well-thought-out gift shows up that's genuinely valuable to them.

That's reciprocity, straight out of Robert Cialdini's book Influence.

At that stage the prospect is 100x more likely to book a call with you.

Compare that to a cold DM, cold call or cold email… all dead easy to ignore.

Combine the channels on the SAME person and you max out your chances of booking a coffee date with your Dream 100.

This gives us UNLIMITED options to follow up - across every channel… with case studies, testimonials, more info…

Until they book a coffee date or tell us to f off lol

If you're ready to supercharge your cold outreach, comment "SUPERCHARGED" and we'll get the deets to ya.

Dan
