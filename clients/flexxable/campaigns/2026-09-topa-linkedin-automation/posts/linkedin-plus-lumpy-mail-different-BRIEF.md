# Brief: Skool post — why LinkedIn + lumpy mail together is clearly different

ONE Skool post from Dan. Under 250 words. Its only job: make the reader instantly SEE why LinkedIn and a locked gift in the post, working on the SAME prospect, is different from anything they've tried. (Travis: "Hey, I've got a better LinkedIn follow-up process" = seen it. "We send them mail" = the lizard brain gets it's different.)

## The core idea (must land in one read)
Everyone is fighting for the same SCREEN. The decision-maker's LinkedIn inbox and email inbox are full of people pitching them today.
We're the only ones who show up on their screen AND on their desk.
- LinkedIn finds them and warms them first (you connect, you send a casual "how's business?"), so by the time the gift lands your name isn't a stranger's.
- The locked gift is the bit nobody else does: a bottle with a lock on top. To open it, they have to call the number on the letter.
- After that, your DM isn't just another one in the pile. It's "I'm the one who sent you the bottle." They answer it.
- And the phone call is easy: "Hey, did you get the bottle? I don't like it when booze goes to waste."
Each touch makes the next one warmer. That compounding is the point. Most people own ONE channel. This stacks two, on the same person.
