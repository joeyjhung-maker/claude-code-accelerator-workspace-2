## The stance
Everyone's been told to "personalise" every LinkedIn message: research the prospect, reference their latest post, mention their company news. Dan says STOP.
- The "personalised" opener ("Loved your recent post about leadership in Q3…") is now the loudest tell that a bot or a ChatGPT prompt wrote it. Decision-makers smell it from the first line and bin it.
- What actually gets replies: dead-simple, human, "generic" messages that sound like a mate checking in. Johnny's opener is basically just "how's business?"… sent to 84 of his existing connections… 23 replied (27%).
- Dan's own words about the 1-2-3 handraiser: it's "generic", which means it works in ANY niche.
- Johnny doesn't even send a connection note, because they already know you're going to sell to them.
- Keep the scare quotes on "personalised". Distinguish: casual and human = good. Fake-researched = the problem.
