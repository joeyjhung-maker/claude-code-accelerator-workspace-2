HL: I just invested in a business that worked 20 years ago. On purpose.

I've been doing my due diligence for months on this one.

I don't jump into anything unless I'm completely sold.

And honestly… I was still on the fence.

Then what they showed me blew me away.

But let me back up.

Here's my philosophy right now:

AI is moving so fast that any new SaaS you launch today is at real risk of being replaced by something better tomorrow.

It's all just too complicated and too confusing.

So instead of betting on something shiny…

I bet on something that worked 20 years ago and will work 20 years from now.

Direct mail.

Specifically… sending a high-value gift, like a bottle of booze, with a lock on top.

To unlock it, they have to call the number on the letter inside the package.

Roughly 25% of the people we send one to end up on a call with us.

Now, what most people don't realise yet…

Direct mail five years ago meant buying sketchy lists from a list broker and hoping for the best.

Today?

We enrich our OWN data with AI.

The level of detail you can get on the individual you're sending to is off the charts.

Which means direct mail just got dramatically more effective… and almost nobody understands that yet.

The best time to move is when everyone's looking the other way.

But there's a third piece that really tipped me over the edge.

Building a LinkedIn presence around your niche (say, HVAC owners) so the follow-up has somewhere to live after the bottle lands.

If you're ready to build on something that'll still work 20 years from now, comment "OLDSCHOOL" and we'll get the deets to ya.
