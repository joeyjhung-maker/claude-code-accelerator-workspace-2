# Brief: Skool click-driver post for the Dan + Johnny YouTube long video (goes up Tue 29 Sept)

Write ONE short Skool post from Dan whose only job is to get people to click and watch the new YouTube video.

## Style (Joey's ask)
- VERY relaxed. Doesn't need full sentences. Fragments, quick hits, "…" pauses, a little list is fine.
- Short: 60-120 words in the body.
- Headline that makes it obvious it's a video, e.g. ends in "(video)" or "NEW VID:".
- Sell the payoff and curiosity. HIDE the how. Don't reveal the actual opener, the actual comeback line, or the setup steps. Those are why they click.
- End: 👉 Watch it here: [YOUTUBE LINK]  then sign off "Dan".
