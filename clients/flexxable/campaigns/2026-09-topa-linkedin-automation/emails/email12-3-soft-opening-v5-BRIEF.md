# Email 12.3 — softer headline and opening

Rewrite ONLY the headline and opening two lines of Email 12.3.

The current version is too confrontational:

HL: Why you barely use LinkedIn PREMIUM

You pay for LinkedIn Premium every month.

But when did it last help you land a client?

Joey's correction: the reader should feel recognised, not accused of wasting money or challenged about results.

Keep the same argument underneath: LinkedIn Premium sounds useful, but in practice it gives the reader a few passive features and leaves all the active client-getting work to them. That is why it slips out of use. Topa later becomes the tool that does the work.

The next fixed line after the new opening is:

It gives you a gold badge, a few InMail credits and a list of people who viewed your profile.

Write 10 complete sets. Each set needs:

- One plain email subject/headline.
- Two opening lines that flow naturally into the fixed line above.
- Recognition first. No accusation, guilt, challenge or "when did you last…" question.
- Calm Dan voice. Short, conversational lines with varied length.
- No Sales Navigator.
- No new facts or numbers.
- No banned filler: "Fair question", "So yeah", "Quick one", "here's the thing", "Be honest".
- Avoid the words "barely", "waste", "forgot", "lazy", "sucks", "useless", "dust", "cancel" and "homework" in the headline or opening.
- Do not rewrite the rest of the email.
