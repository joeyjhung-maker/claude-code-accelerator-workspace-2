## What Joey changed vs. Version A (the lessons)
| Version A (received) | Joey final | Lesson |
|---|---|---|
| "==> Watch how LinkedIn Signals delivers red hot leads to your inbox daily" | "==> Get red hot leads SENT to your inbox daily" | The click-driver link text IS the outcome, in the reader's hands ("Get…"), echoing the headline. Not "Watch how [feature] does X". |
| "We've run campaigns like this and pulled reply rates of…" | "We're running campaigns like this and pulling REPLY rates of…" + "…in all kinds of niches…" | Proof in the present tense (it's live), CAPS on the metric word, and a breadth line so every niche reads itself in. |
| "People who were actively talking about the exact thing you sell… yesterday." | "I'm talking about peeps actively looking for the exact thing you sell…" | Buyer-intent wording ("looking for" beats "talking about"), said in Dan's first person with "peeps". |
| "recorded a video showing what this looks like inside Topa" | "recorded a 15 min video showing how to do this" | Give the video length and promise the how-to for the reader. A pure click-driver doesn't need the product or feature name at all. |
