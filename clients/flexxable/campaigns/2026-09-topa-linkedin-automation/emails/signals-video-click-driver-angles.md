**2. THEY JUST POSTED ABOUT IT**
*Frame: Curiosity (Hidden Cause) + Pain*
Agitate the core problem: you're cold-messaging people who aren't thinking about your thing. Flip it — what if you only ever reached people who had JUST posted about the exact problem you solve? Tease that this is now possible on autopilot, every 24 hours. No explanation of how.

*Frame: Curiosity (Origin/Proof) + Promise*

**4. 224 LEADS IN 5 MINUTES (LIVE)**
*Frame: Proof + Curiosity (Solution)*

**5. THE PR FIRM TRICK**
*Frame: Curiosity (Solution) + Promise*
Open on a PR firm that never cold pitches. Instead, they let LinkedIn tell them who just launched something — and reach out the same day. Tease that this "signal listening" approach works for any B2B offer and that the video shows exactly how to set it up.

**6. NOBODY KEEPS IT UP**
*Frame: Pain + Constraint Elimination*
Agitate the manual LinkedIn grind: saving profiles, scrolling posts, checking every commenter. Everyone starts it. Nobody keeps it up. Then tease: what if the whole thing ran on autopilot and fed you fresh, warm leads every morning? Set it once. Let it run.

**7. COLD LISTS ARE LYING TO YOU**
*Frame: Pattern Interrupt + Hidden Cause*
Challenge the assumption that a "targeted list" is targeted. Stale data, random timing, people who aren't thinking about your thing. Tease the idea that true targeting isn't about WHO someone is — it's about WHAT they just said. The video shows the difference.

**8. THE 1-2-3 HANDRAISER**
*Frame: Curiosity (Solution) + Promise*
Tease the 3-message sequence that works forever because the audience refreshes itself every 24 hours. New people post every day. The sequence stays the same. The leads never dry up. Don't explain the sequence — just make them desperate to see it.

**9. WHAT THEY SAID ON LINKEDIN YESTERDAY**
*Frame: Curiosity + Emotion (Urgency)*
Open on the idea that right now, today, hundreds of people are posting on LinkedIn about the exact problem you solve — and most sellers will never know. Tease Signals as the thing that captures those people before anyone else does, every single day.

**10. 7.5 CENTS**
*Frame: Proof + Curiosity*

**11. "HEY, SAW YOUR COMMENT ON ALEX'S POST"**
*Frame: Curiosity + Proof*
Open on the single most disarming cold opener possible — one sentence that doesn't feel cold at all, because it's true. Tease that this kind of opener is only possible when you know, in real time, who just engaged with what. The video shows how to build it systematically.

**12. THE SIGNAL NOBODY ELSE IS LISTENING FOR**
*Frame: Pattern Interrupt + Curiosity (Hidden Cause)*
Position the insight: the best leads aren't hiding. They're broadcasting. They're posting, commenting, reacting — telling anyone who'll listen that they have the problem you solve. Tease Signals as the tool that actually listens, 24/7, so you don't have to.

**13. SPRAY AND PRAY IS DEAD**
*Frame: Pain + Promise*
Agitate the exhaustion of cold email and cold DM volume games — even with AI writing the messages. Tease that the problem was never the message. It was the timing. Tease the idea of only ever reaching people who are actively thinking about your thing right now.

**15. SET IT ONCE**

**16. THE COLD OUTREACH TIMING PROBLEM**

**18. THE OUTREACH THAT DOESN'T FEEL COLD**
