# Email 12.3 v8 — fresh close, no repeated deadline script

Rewrite ONLY the close of Email 12.3, starting after Noelle's quote. Give 3 complete close options.

## The problem

The audience has already heard the standard close repeatedly across this campaign:

- “Paid plans start at $79/mo.”
- “After tonight the bonuses disappear…”
- “May I suggest making a move…”
- A long P.S. listing all four bonuses.
- “Lock it in / last chance / before tonight's deadline.”

Joey's correction: “We keep reusing this close, people have heard this 12 times already. Switch it up.”

Do not reuse that sequence, wording or rhythm.

## New closing lane

Build the close from THIS email's own price contrast:

- LinkedIn Premium Business starts at $69.99/mo in the US.
- Topa starts at $79/mo.
- The difference is $9.01/mo.
- Premium's features in this email are passive: gold badge, InMails, viewer data.
- Topa actively finds the prospects, sends connection requests, runs the handraiser and pulls the mobile when somebody replies.
- The reader still handles replies/conversations.
- Frame it as moving the software money from the passive subscription to the tool built to start conversations. Do not claim Topa replaces every Premium feature.

The deadline still matters, but mention it only once and in a fresh way: buying any paid Topa plan tonight is what gets the bonuses and current Signals access.

## P.S. direction

Do NOT list all four bonuses again.

Use a self-aware line such as: “I'm not listing the bonuses AGAIN… you know what's in there by now lol.” Then say how to get them: grab any paid plan tonight and DM the receipt + email address so Dan can unlock the lot.

## Rules

- 55–90 words including P.S.
- Dan voice, relaxed and cheeky.
- One CTA link to https://app.topa.io/signup before -D.
- No new proof, promises, scarcity or numbers.
- No “May I suggest”, “bonuses disappear”, “lock it in”, “last chance”, “before tonight's deadline”, or long bonus recap.
- Avoid “swap” if it implies Topa replaces every LinkedIn Premium feature; make the comparison about the client-getting job only.
- No em dashes or hyphens written by Mariobot.
- No headline.
