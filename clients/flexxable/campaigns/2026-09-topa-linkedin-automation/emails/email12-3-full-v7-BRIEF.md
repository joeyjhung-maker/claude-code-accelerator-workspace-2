# Email 12.3 v7 — complete email from Joey's hook

Write ONE complete close-day email from Dan. Body only. Do not add a headline, subject line or subhead.

## Locked first line — Joey's line, preserve exactly

Linkedin premium is one of those weird offers that doesn't make a lot of sense.

## Locked argument and order

1. LinkedIn Premium promises to help you grow your business and connect with potential clients.
2. In practice, Premium Business gives you a gold badge, up to 15 InMails a month, and data on who viewed your profile.
3. Official US monthly price starts at $69.99/month. Use $69.99/mo because the campaign quotes dollars.
4. Despite that, it does not solve the one thing this audience bought it for: getting clients. Land “Getting clients.” as its own line.
5. Explain why: all the active work is still theirs. They still have to find the right prospects, add them, message them and chase them.
6. Contrast with Topa: it finds ideal prospects in a 350M+ contact database, sends connection requests daily, runs the 3-message handraiser sequence, and pulls the mobile number when somebody replies. The user handles the conversations.
7. Bare proof only, no narration: Noelle has 30,000 LinkedIn connections and was still DMing by hand. Quote: “Literally, I'm going to set this up right now.”
8. Deadline: after tonight the bonuses disappear and LinkedIn Signals gets locked behind higher tier plans. Paid Topa plans start at $79/mo.
9. Soft close using “May I suggest…”. One CTA link to https://app.topa.io/signup.
10. Sign off -D.
11. P.S. Name the four bonuses going tonight: Jack Goddard's AI Profile Optimiser, our personal review of YOUR profile, help writing your 1-2-3 handraiser sequence, and LinkedIn Signals. Tell them to DM receipt + email address. Final link.

## Style and truth rules

- 180–220 words before the P.S.
- The OFFER is weird; never accuse or shame the reader.
- Dan, relaxed, Aussie, short conversational paragraphs, varied sentence length.
- No headline or subject line.
- No Sales Navigator.
- No new numbers, promises or results.
- Just say Topa.
- Never say Topa handles replies. The user handles the replies/conversations.
- No narrated proof.
- No banned filler: “Fair question”, “So yeah”, “Quick one”, “here's the thing”, “Be honest”.
- One sentence per line, but do not manufacture choppy fragments.
- Keep Joey's first line exactly, including its casing and punctuation.
