## Style
- Short lines, one idea per line, no walls of text. Bold = skim spine. CAPS = one punch word per line max. "…" as rhythm.
- No stacked abstract triplets. No "No X. No Y. No Z." fragment stacks. No canned signposts ("here's the thing").
- Don't open with proof or pain. Open with the intro line + the news.
- Never invent a number, result, quote, name, feature, bonus or guarantee. Use only the facts below.
- Never mention a ROYA member upgrade price.
