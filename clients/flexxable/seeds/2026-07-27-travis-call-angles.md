## Delivery-bogged-down mechanism — same mechanism as the Sales Bro angles, new mental pictures (saffron move)

MASTER-CONCEPTS-ROYA.md already has this mechanism as "mountain of shit to deliver" vs "low delivery
hassles" (p154, p157). The 07-23 call adds three concrete, unused images for it.

## S1 [source: research] [segment: burned-out agency owner] [awareness: solution] [gap: mechanism-led hook] [family: new-mental-picture]
Hook (verbatim): "DMs out the wazoo... bugs, ongoing support, clients calling mad."
Idea: open on the exact texture of the delivery grind — not "it's hard," but the specific inbox/DM pile and the angry-client-call image. Fills gap 5 (no ad currently leads with the mechanism's pain in concrete detail).

## S2 [source: research] [segment: 9-5 escapee] [awareness: problem] [gap: unaware/pain-first] [family: new-mental-picture]
Hook (verbatim): "what's your first roofer gonna say when their calendar's suddenly blacked out?"
Idea: the vivid client-side mind-movie — reader imagines the moment a real client's business changes overnight because of what they built. Could open an ad with zero AI-jargon (fills gap 1, unaware) by starting entirely from the roofer's point of view before pivoting to "that was Rob's client."

## S3 [source: research] [segment: opportunity seeker] [awareness: product] [gap: mechanism-led hook] [family: cause-effect-flip]
Hook (verbatim): "we can just demo... the AI does all the work, and we make a ton of money... we don't have to deliver anything."
Idea: the "good thing that's actually the whole point" flip — most agency owners think NOT delivering is the risk; reframe it as the entire unlock. Pairs with existing "low delivery hassle" language but states it as bluntly as Travis does, no softening.

## S4 [source: research] [segment: burned-out agency owner] [awareness: solution] [gap: mechanism-led hook] [family: bad-thing-thats-actually-good]
Hook (verbatim): "constant service delivery pain" — reframed as the ONE thing this model removes, not manages.
Idea: instead of promising "less delivery pain," promise its total absence — positions every competitor's "we'll help you manage clients better" as still selling you the pain, just organized.

## Fit-check, not fake scarcity — untouched angle, fills gap 3 (contrarian)

## S5 [source: research] [segment: proof-chaser] [awareness: solution] [gap: contrarian] [belief: challenges "a good agency pitch takes anyone who'll pay"] [family: identity-archetype]
Hook (verbatim): "we're gonna make sure we go together like peanut butter and jelly, rather than peanut butter and mustard... we just don't make a good sandwich together, so we're not gonna let everybody in."
Idea: contrarian qualification hook — instead of "limited spots" scarcity (which the account doesn't use anyway), the ad itself vets the reader ("this isn't for you if X"), which reads as confidence not urgency. New territory — no current ad does a direct fit-check in the hook.

## S6 [source: research] [segment: opportunity seeker] [awareness: product] [gap: contrarian] [family: identity-archetype]
Idea only (no direct quote to lean on further): the inverse framing of S5 — an ad built entirely around "who this ISN'T for" as the opener, letting the reader self-select in by process of elimination. Untested structure for this account; flag as a gambit.

## Bridge, not value-stack — a structural note more than a hook (flag, don't force into a fake seed)

Not a standalone seed — this is a body-copy STRUCTURE principle, not an entry point: Travis's fix for
Dan's "I hate that" reaction to flat value-stack lists was a journey/bridge narrative ("you're gonna come
on, we're gonna find the best fit, then... here's how, here's the exact scripts... you want to lay out
that bridge, and they almost feel like they've walked across it"). Worth carrying into whichever brief
gets picked — the BODY should walk a journey, not stack bullets — but it isn't a hook to seed. Flagging
so /brief can lock it as a body-structure note rather than losing it.

## S8 [source: research] [segment: 9-5 escapee] [awareness: problem] [gap: mechanism-led hook] [family: new-mental-picture]
Hook (verbatim, adapted from the bridge line): "...they almost feel like they've walked across it."
Idea: a body-copy device more than a hook — describe the reader's exact next 90 days as a walked path (sign up → first demo → first client → first payout) so specifically they can feel themselves already at the end of it. Use as body structure, not opening hook.

## Voice note — flag for Joey, not a seed (tension with existing rules)

Travis's strongest opinion (item 10 in the transcript) was addressed directly to Joey: make copy so
obviously human nobody could mistake it for AI — weather-based personal asides, guessing a real detail

a bad one. **Not turning this into a seed** — it's a voice/craft instruction, not a hook, and it sits in
real tension with [[dan-truth-constraint]] and [[no-fake-punchy-fragments]] (invented color/detail vs.
true-to-Dan claims). Worth a call: do you want this tested as a voice variant on 1-2 ads this week, or
