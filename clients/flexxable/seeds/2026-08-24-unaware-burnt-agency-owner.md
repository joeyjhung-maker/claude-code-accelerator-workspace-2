## Emotional drivers the bot surfaced (for reference — informs body copy too, not just hooks)

- Won the game they were supposed to win and it still feels like losing — external
  success, internal exhaustion, can't complain because "I'm doing well."
- The trap has a mechanism they can name (sell more to make more) but don't believe
  is a structural exit, not a design flaw.
- Their own expertise is the prison — clients want THEM, so they can't delegate out.
- Client-pleasing became a compulsive identity, not a choice — answering at 9pm,
  absorbing scope creep, resentment they can't voice.
- "Fire the bad clients" advice already tried, already failed — a nightmare client
  gets replaced by a new nightmare client because the revenue slot needs filling.
- **The most pressurized, most private one: shameful relief when a client fired
  THEM.** Can't say it out loud — sounds ungrateful or unprofessional.
- Burned out on the delivery model, not the craft itself — still likes the actual
  work, hates the client management around it. Hasn't separated the two yet.
- Normalized a stress baseline their pre-agency self would find unacceptable —
  Sunday dread, checking phone before getting up, vacation guilt.
