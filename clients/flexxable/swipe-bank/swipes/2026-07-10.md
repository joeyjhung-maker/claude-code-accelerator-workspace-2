### Patterns to lift for the biz-opp VSL (the swipe DNA)
- **Cold-open income claim**: "The first X I launched made me $10,000. It took no longer than a week." (Tapa/Dimac template)
- **Objection stack, killed fast**: no experience / no product / no investment / not a tech wizard / AI does the heavy lifting.
- **"Boring but lucrative"** + low competition + one client = thousands/mo (Jimmy Fung — our closest match).
- **Overwhelm → done-simple**: name the tutorial-hell, then "that's why we built this / not another course."
- **"I had my doubts too / sounds too good to be true"** — pre-empt disbelief.
- Register: plain, spoken, first-person, short lines. Not agency jargon.
