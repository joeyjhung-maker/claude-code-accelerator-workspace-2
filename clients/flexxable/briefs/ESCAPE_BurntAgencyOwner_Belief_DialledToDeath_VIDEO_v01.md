# ESCAPE_BurntAgencyOwner_Belief_DialledToDeath_VIDEO_v01

Video branch of the "dialled to death" ad. **The DNA is unchanged** — segment, awareness,
belief, concept, angle, mechanism, problem name, The Image, spokesperson, CTA and the whole
proof table live in the TEXT brief:
[[ESCAPE_BurntAgencyOwner_Belief_DialledToDeath_TEXT_v01]]. Read that first. Only the
format-specific spec is below.

## Format spec

- **Format:** Video script, Dan reading to camera. No conceit (no skit, no customer call, no
  podcast) — Dan tells the story straight.
- **Word target: under 250.** The belief script lands at 249. The FINAL scene script runs 306 —
  Joey kept the length to buy the two-pitch structure and the ecom proof.
- **The scene is the ad.** The four-exchange client conversation survives every cut. Everything
  else is negotiable — the explaining is what gets sacrificed, because on camera the scene does
  the explaining.
- **Spoken, not read.** Contractions throughout, lines Dan can breathe through, no text-ad
  furniture (no "tap the link below", no emoji, no headline field).
- **CTA is verbal** — Dan says where the book is out loud, rather than pointing at a link.
- **No camera directions in the file.** The script is the words Dan says, nothing else. Delivery
  notes live in the copy file's header, above the script.

## What the compression cost

Three passes from 546 words down to 249 (scene) and 525 to 249 (belief). What got cut, in order:
the SMS mechanics, the 2.5-years/30-60% response-rate proof, the "here's why it's the easiest
thing you'll ever pitch" block, and the restated $10k client at the close. The proof that
survived is the client's own dialogue plus the $10k a month — which is the proof that was doing
the persuading anyway.

**Worth watching:** the response-rate stat is gone from both scripts. If either video underperforms
its text sibling, that missing proof is the first thing to test back in.
