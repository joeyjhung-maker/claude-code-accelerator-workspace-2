# Fresh Mariobot write — hard 250-word ceiling

Write a fresh spoken Facebook video ad for The Instant AI Agency book.

This must be a new write from the locked idea, not a compression or paraphrase of a previous script.

Return **primary text only**.

## Length

- Hard maximum: **250 words including the CTA and URL**.
- Aim for **225-240 words**.
- Count the words silently before answering.
- If the draft exceeds 240 words, cut it yourself before returning it.
- Do not print the word count, headlines, notes or commentary.

## Locked audience and idea

Speak to agency owners, freelancers, copywriters, funnel builders, SMMA managers and SEO providers.

They fear AI replacing them, but the immediate wound is already here: AI has made buyers value service output less.

The provider can keep doing the same skilled work and carrying the same responsibility while new briefs arrive with smaller budgets.

The client expects more output while wanting to pay less.

The provider tries to defend the old deal with longer proposals, bigger retainers and upfront fees, but buyers are pushing back.

The escape is to stop fighting over the price of output and change what the client pays for.

The Instant AI Agency model uses AI + SMS to reactivate a business's old leads and create measurable sales.

The provider makes a performance-based offer with no setup fee and gets paid 30-50% of the revenue generated.

The client pays when the client gets paid, removing the upfront gamble.

That is the sense in which the provider becomes “AI-proof”: AI is used to create the result, and the provider is paid from the result.

## Offer

- The Instant AI Agency book.
- $14.40.
- Step-by-step blueprint.
- 30-day money-back guarantee where the buyer keeps the book either way.
- CTA URL: `book.flexxable.com/optin`

## Writing constraints

- Find the hook yourself from the idea.
- Curiosity or a blunt market truth first; pain amplifies after attention is earned.
- The opening must not explain the entire argument.
- Dan speaking slowly to camera: casual, direct and Aussie-inflected.
- One sentence per line.
- Use short paragraphs and natural spoken flow.
- No crime-scene, knife, blood, biting, guts or clearance-rack metaphors.
- No corporate language.
- No tidy `not X, but Y` reversals, stacked abstract triplets or fake-punchy close.
- Do not invent a result, statistic, timeframe, personal story, quote or exact market decline.
- Do not use Joey's private funnel figures.
- Do not claim every buyer behaves this way.
- Do not claim a performance deal can never be negotiated or rejected.
- Do not add student proof.
