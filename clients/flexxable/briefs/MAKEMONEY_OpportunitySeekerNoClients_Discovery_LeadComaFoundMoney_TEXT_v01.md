# MAKEMONEY_OpportunitySeekerNoClients_Discovery_LeadComaFoundMoney_TEXT_v01

Mechanism-LED text ad for the BIZ-OP market. Sibling of
[[ESCAPE_MechanismCurious_Discovery_LeadComa_TEXT_v01]] (same mechanism-hook,
re-aimed at the beginner with nothing). Deliberately NOT a duplicate of
[[MAKEMONEY_OpportunitySeeker9to5_Spread_BizOppAIAgency_VIDEO_v01]] — that one is
opportunity-LED ("easiest AI business", 4 hooks, video); this one leads with the
dead-leads discovery itself, in text.

## DNA
- **Segment:** Segment 4 (Opportunity Seeker) primary + the wider mass-market
  "make money with AI" crowd. NOT the agency owner. The reader has **no clients,
  no experience, no product, no ad budget** — every field is written to that reader.
- **Awareness:** Unaware/opportunity, turn inside the hook (mechanism-intrigue
  converts unaware → opportunity-aware in one move). Fishes UPSTREAM of the
  strategy-map's "Segment 4 = product-aware comparison shoppers" — the mass market
  that hasn't seen a vehicle yet. Beliefs it VALIDATES: (a) "businesses are
  wasteful — there's money lying in what they ignore," (b) the reader's lived
  experience of *being* a dead lead (ghosted a gym/dentist, later got the "still
  interested?" text), (c) "everyone's making money with AI except me, and every
  version needs skills I don't have" — then shows the simple door.
- **Concept (flat):** Most businesses trading 5+ years hold databases of old leads
  who enquired and never bought. A simple SMS campaign can restart those
  conversations and turn a portion into sales at zero upfront cost to the business.
  An outsider with no clients, no product, no experience can be the one who runs it
  and take 50% of the revenue — because the business risks nothing by saying yes.
- **Angle:** Discovery / **found-money-anyone-can-claim**. Curiosity-led,
  observational. NOT pain, NOT proof-led, NOT escape-fantasy. Door in: "there's
  unclaimed money sitting inside almost every business in your town, you've
  personally witnessed the mechanism that collects it, and nobody told you the
  sender gets paid."
- **Mechanism:** OLD/DEAD-lead reactivation over SMS — waking a business's
  already-paid-for database with a conversational text campaign (Sleeping Beauty
  Android + Prince Charming SMS, ROYA language). Dead enquiries become new sales;
  revenue splits 50/50; the business risks nothing upfront. NOT new-lead generation
  — no ads, no cold traffic, no finding anybody. **The database is never "yours" or
  "your client's" — it belongs to businesses everywhere, in a coma, and the reader
  is the outsider who walks in and offers to wake it.** The AI holds thousands of
  revived conversations at once — the reader never writes copy, runs ads, or works
  a single lead. The mechanism IS the vehicle. See [[iaa-mechanism-old-leads]].
- **Problem name:** **The Lead Coma** (kept). Re-pointed: not *your* database — it's
  every business past its fifth birthday, on every high street, sitting on leads
  that aren't dead, just unconscious. Coma rhymes toward WAKEABLE and now also
  toward UNCLAIMED — comatose money is money nobody's collecting, until someone
  does. See [[The Lock]].
  *Cognitive binary (propose, keep or kill):* a business sitting on a comatose
  database it's forgotten it owns vs the outsider holding the phone that wakes it —
  and keeping half of everything that stirs.
- **The Image:** **LOCKED — option B (the split pulse).** A flatlined heart monitor
  labelled "10,000 leads this business gave up on" — one SMS bubble touches the line
  — the flatline jumps into a heartbeat made of dollar signs, **and the heartbeat
  splits toward two pockets: 50% to the business, 50% to YOU (the person who sent
  the text).** Three panels a 10-year-old could draw (flat line → text → split
  pulse). The split IS the biz-op payload — it shows not just the mechanism but the
  reader's cut. (If the split ever clutters a specific static execution, the
  label-change-only version is the fallback — but B is the lock.)
- **Spokesperson:** Dan. Archetype — **the operator who mined his own gold first**
  (locked). Not a guru selling a course about a thing he read about — he ran the
  machine on his OWN agency's database before handing anyone the keys. Irreplaceable
  TRUE first-person claim: his agency pulled an extra **$750k**, then **$1.08m of
  "found money,"** from one database (offer doc p3, line 77). **Guard-rail
  [[dan-truth-constraint]]:** NO invented backstory (no redundancy/desk-job/
  failed-IT). Dan's first person is confined to the $750k/$1.08m claim + teaching;
  every "escape the 9-5 / not technical / no experience" beat is
  second-person/observational.
- **CTA:** Client default — the $14.40 Instant AI Agency book + bonuses, 30-day
  keep-everything guarantee. Top-of-funnel; no VSL, no retargeting.
- **Format:** Text ad (Meta primary). The Image briefs a companion static (option B).
- **Guidelines:**
  1. Mechanism-led but consequence-flavoured (inherited): the mechanism must read
     as *found money the reader has personally witnessed*, never a how-it-works
     explainer. A hook that reads like a product spec has failed the brief.
  2. No pain opener — curiosity/novelty only; pain (stuck job, dead side hustles)
     may amplify mid-body at most. See [[pain-is-amplifier-not-opener]].
  3. One curiosity thread in the hook: the coma and the wake-up. AI, escaping the
     9-5, and the 50/50 split all live in the body, not the hook.
  4. **Objection removal is the body's job** (the biz-op engine — steal the VIDEO
     brief's register, not its structure). Kill in sequence: "I'm not technical"
     (AI holds every convo) → "no experience" (you don't write copy or run ads —
     templates + the Android do it) → "no money to start" (performance-based; the
     business pays nothing upfront and risks nothing; the door is a $14.40 book) →
     "too good to be true" (the proof) → **END on the handshake / easy-yes** (see
     loosest dimension below).
  5. Mass-market biz-op voice — no agency jargon, no burnout-confession register.
     Mirror biz-op swipe STRUCTURE, never their invented income-claim cold-opens.
- **Source route:** Regular path in /produce (hook bot → body → headlines). No
  swipe/organic seed. Prime the body bot on `primers/bizopp-video.md` for REGISTER
  only (mass-market voice), not structure.

## The one-breath Lock test
*"Nearly every business trading five-plus years is sitting on thousands of leads in
a coma — people who asked to buy and never got followed up, which is why you still
get the odd 'hey, still interested?' text from a gym you ghosted years ago. Dan sent
one campaign like that to his own agency's forgotten database and rang up $750k in
found money, then $1.08m — and because the business risks nothing by saying yes, a
total beginner like Harry could walk in with no clients and no experience, get handed
100,000 unbought leads in one handshake, and bank £7,000 in a single week for half of
everything the texts woke up. So the play isn't building a business from nothing; it's
claiming half of the money already lying unconscious inside businesses everywhere,
with an AI holding thousands of those conversations at once so you never work a single
lead."*

**Rhyme:** coma → unclaimed/unconscious money → SMS wake-up → found money → 50/50 to
whoever sends the text. Pain metaphor inverts cleanly (coma → wake); the Image lives
in the same root (flatline → pulse → split pulse); the spokesperson embodies the
mechanism (woke his own gold first); Harry's proof is the next chapter (the beginner
claiming someone else's coma). Reads close to lock.

**Loosest dimension (aim, not gate) — the handshake join:** *why does a business hand
a stranger with no track record its most valuable asset?* In the ESCAPE brief the
reader owned the relationship; here a new step (getting the deal) enters, and the coma
metaphor doesn't cover it. Left unearned, the ad tips from "found money" into "too good
to be true" — fatal in this exact segment. Fix is cheap, lives in the body: the
easy-yes offer (no upfront fee, business risks nothing, the AI demo sells itself —
Harry's client said "when do we start?"). So the objection sequence in Guideline 4
MUST end on the handshake, not just tech/experience/money. Flagged for /produce.
Second-loosest, inherited: deliberately bends hook principle 8 (see Guideline 1).

## Hook directions for /produce (angles, NOT final copy)
1. **Reader-as-dead-lead flip, with the biz-op payload.** Open on the reader's own
   experience of *receiving* the mechanism — the "still interested?" text from a
   business they ghosted three years ago — then reveal the person who sent it may
   have had no clients, no product, no experience, and kept half of whatever the
   reader spent. Second-person by construction, so Dan-truth-safe. Strongest door:
   the found-money claim proven by the reader's own phone.
2. **Unclaimed money on every high street (world-level, edge-of-reality).** Almost
   every business past its fifth birthday is sitting on money in a coma — old leads
   it paid for and forgot — and the owner will hand half to whoever wakes it, because
   waking it costs them nothing. Names The Lead Coma in the hook; "nobody's claiming
   it" does the awareness turn. Backed by Seb's 83k list → ~£1m twice + Harry's week.
3. **Dan's found-money confession (first-person TRUE, then hand over the keys).**
   Dan's best month came without a single new customer — $750k from a list his agency
   had stopped looking at, then $1.08m. The turn (strictly second-person): he owned
   that database, but you don't need to — every business already has one, and Harry's
   deal proves they'll split it with whoever wakes it. Dan's history stops at the
   $750k/$1.08m line; the beginner beats are all "you."
