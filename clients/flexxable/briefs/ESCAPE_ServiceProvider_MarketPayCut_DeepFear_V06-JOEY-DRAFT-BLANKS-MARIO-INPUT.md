## What each blank has to do

**BLANK 1 — "AI doesn't have to replace you to take your money. / It just has to [BLANK 1]"**

This is the second line of the hook. It pays off the "wait… what?" of line one with a concrete picture, and it must hand straight into the next line ("The same work you used to charge $5K for… is now "worth" $2K on a good day.").

The idea underneath: AI only has to change what the CLIENT thinks your work is worth. Their side, their head, their screen. Not yours.

Short. Physical. Something you can see. Plain words a tradie would use. No clever metaphor that needs decoding, no crime, knife, blood or clearance-rack imagery.

**BLANK 2 — "Instead they're doing one thing that allows them to [___], [___] and [___]"**

Three things the reader badly wants that charging for results gives them. This line comes BEFORE the reveal ("This one thing is charging for RESULTS… not output."), so do not give the answer away. No mention of results, performance, commission or revenue share in the fill.

Each of the three must be a concrete, picturable thing, not an abstraction. "Charge more, work less and feel secure" is a FAIL. Think: what happens on the sales call, what happens to the invoice, what the client stops saying.

Do not repeat what blank 3's sentence already covers (lowballing, micromanaging).

No invented numbers.

**BLANK 3 — "They don't micro manage you and they definitely don't [BLANK 3]."**

The third and nastiest thing a client stops doing once you charge for results. It should get a wry nod from anyone who has had a client do it. Strongest if it calls back to the AI comparison earlier in the ad ("can't I get AI to do this for me cheaper?") without repeating that line word for word.
