# PIVOT_ActiveAgencyOwner_Reframe_ScratchOutPencilIn_TEXT_v01

## DNA

- **Segment:** the active lead-gen/marketing agency owner adding a new offer for
  existing clients — the Decoder's data-driven segment (08-24 audit), currently only
  6/75 ads (8%) despite being flagged as the account's more qualified, higher-LTV
  buyer and the #1-ranked gap ("cold-open the two agency-owner personas... untapped
  audience"). Deliberately NOT the burnt-out-agency-owner segment already saturated by
  the Unaware batch and the three Most-Aware briefs — this swipe's tone (opportunity,
  abundance, "look what you're missing") doesn't fit a burnout frame; it fits someone
  actively running a fine agency who just hasn't heard of this specific model yet.
- **Awareness:** Solution-Aware. Reader already believes in the general opportunity
  (they run a lead-gen/marketing agency, they know the category), they just don't know
  THIS specific model exists yet — same awareness level as Segment 1 in the
  strategy-map.
- **Concept (flat):** the reader already has the exact skill this new model needs
  (working leads for a business); they're just pointing it at a scarce, fought-over
  category (new leads via ads) instead of an abundant, ignored one (every business's
  own dead lead database). Concept type: **The "What If" Reframe**
  (creative-coverage's Solution-Aware library).
- **Angle:** **Paradox** — "sounds too good to be true, it isn't" is the swipe's own
  explicit structure, not an invented fit.
- **The pivot device (swipe's core mechanic, carried near-verbatim):** "Scratch out
  [old category] and pencil in [new category]." Mapped as: scratch out "New Leads,"
  pencil in "Old Leads." Same category-swap logic as the source ad (RE → Business
  Buying), same skill, different inventory.
- **Mechanism:** unchanged from the rest of the account — AI + SMS reactivates a
  business's old/dead lead database, performance-only (30-50% rev share), no upfront
  cost to the client. This is the "abundant inventory" side of the pivot.
- **Scarcity side of the pivot:** rising ad costs / CPLs, algorithm volatility,
  fighting other agencies for the same new leads — the account's own established
  "serenity-sucking services" framing (Meta ads, funnels, SEO) already covers this,
  reused here as the "scarce category" half of the binary.
- **Spokesperson:** Dan. Archetype unchanged (the reformed insider).
- **CTA:** $14.40 book + bonuses + 30-day keep-everything guarantee →
  `book.flexxable.com/optin`.
- **Format:** Text ad (primary text), matching the swipe's own format — no video
  conceit locked, this brief covers the on-screen text only.
- **Source route:** **Swipe** — near-verbatim structure and length, specifics swapped
  for Flexxable's segment/mechanism, via `swiping-master-bot`. OG ad carried above.
- **Guidelines:**
  - Keep the "scratch out / pencil in" line as close to verbatim structure as the
    swap allows — it's the swipe's signature device, not just a stylistic touch.
  - Do NOT invent a checklist claim the account can't back — see Proof table below,
    two of the source ad's four checkmarks have no equivalent verified claim and must
    either be dropped or rewritten to something true.
  - Do NOT invent a social-proof number. The source ad's "9400+ of our students" has
    no Flexxable equivalent verified anywhere in the client file (checked
    `offer-doc-ROYA.md` and the testimonial bank — no aggregate count exists,
    verified or otherwise). Leave a `[X]` placeholder, flagged for Joey, rather than
    inventing or reusing an unconfirmed number from an unrelated draft.
  - The source ad's "10-Day Business Buying Launch crash course" is a paid course;
    Flexxable's actual product is the $14.40 book. Don't let the mad-lib accidentally
    imply a course/launch event that doesn't exist.
