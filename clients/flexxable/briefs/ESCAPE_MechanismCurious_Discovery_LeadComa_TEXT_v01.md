# ESCAPE_MechanismCurious_Discovery_LeadComa_TEXT_v01

Mechanism-LED ad — the HOOK leads with the dead-leads/SMS reactivation
mechanism itself, not a result or proof. Fills strategy-map Gap 5.

## DNA
- **Segment:** Segment 2 (Aspiring Entrepreneur / 9-5 Escapee) as primary. A
  mechanism-intrigue hook is a wide-net door; it will also catch Segment 4
  Opportunity Seekers, but the body aims at the escapee (bigger top-funnel pool,
  keeps it genuinely top-of-funnel). *(A biz-op / Segment-4 variant is being
  worked separately — see refinement thread.)*
- **Awareness:** Unaware/problem, with the awareness turn happening INSIDE the
  hook (the Redundancy Randy move — the hook converts unaware → solution-aware).
  Belief this ad works — it VALIDATES two things the reader already carries:
  (a) "businesses are wasteful; there's money lying in what they ignore," and
  (b) their own lived experience of *being* a dead lead (everyone has ghosted a
  gym/dentist/course and later got the random "hey, still interested?" text).
- **Concept (flat):** Most businesses trading 5+ years hold databases of old
  leads who enquired and never bought. A simple SMS campaign can restart those
  conversations and turn a portion into sales, at zero upfront cost to the
  business. A third party who runs it can take 50% of the revenue.
- **Angle:** Discovery / found-money. Curiosity-led, observational. NOT pain,
  NOT proof-led, NOT escape-fantasy. The door in: "here's a money mechanism
  hiding in plain sight that you've personally witnessed and never clocked."
- **Mechanism:** OLD/DEAD-lead reactivation over SMS. The business's
  already-paid-for database gets woken by a conversational text campaign
  (Sleeping Beauty Android + Prince Charming SMS, ROYA language); dead enquiries
  become new sales; revenue splits 50/50; the business risks nothing upfront.
  Explicitly NOT new-lead generation — no ads, no cold traffic, no finding
  anybody. The AI's role, stated mechanically: it holds thousands of those
  revived text conversations at once, so the operator never works a single lead.
  See [[iaa-mechanism-old-leads]].
- **Problem name:** **The Lead Coma.** Every 5-yr-old business sits on a
  database in a coma — leads that aren't dead, just unconscious; nobody's spoken
  to them in years. A coma implies WAKEABLE (graveyard/dead rhymes toward
  hopeless; coma rhymes toward revival — [[The Lock]], "watch the direction the
  rhyme points"). Sounds like a diagnosis, carries the why-now (every year of
  trading deepens the coma), and snaps onto the existing Sleeping Beauty Android
  asset name.
  *Cognitive binary (propose, keep or kill):* leads in a coma vs leads that text
  back — a database gathering dust in a CRM vs the same database lighting a phone
  up with replies.
- **The Image:** a flatlined heart monitor labelled "10,000 old leads" — one SMS
  bubble touches the line — and the flatline jumps into a heartbeat made of
  dollar signs. Three panels a 10-year-old could draw (flat line → text bubble →
  pulse of $s). Shows the whole mechanism in one glance; briefs a companion
  static; same metaphor family as the coma + the Sleeping Beauty Android.
- **Spokesperson:** Dan. Archetype — **the operator who mined his own gold
  first** (not a guru with a theory; a guy who ran this on his own agency's
  database before teaching it). Irreplaceable TRUE first-person claim: his agency
  pulled an extra **$750k** from its own database in one month, then did it again
  for **$1.08m of "found money"** (offer doc p3). Guard-rail
  [[dan-truth-constraint]]: NO redundancy/desk-job/failed-IT backstory; every
  escape-9-5 / "not technical" beat is second-person/observational, never Dan's
  history.
- **CTA:** Client default — the $14.40 Instant AI Agency book + bonuses, 30-day
  keep-everything guarantee. (Top-of-funnel → book, not the ROYA funnel.)
- **Format:** Text ad (Meta primary). The Image briefs a companion static; no
  video conceit locked.
- **Guidelines:**
  1. Mechanism-led but consequence-flavoured. Hook principle 8 says "lead with
     the wound, not the scalpel"; this brief deliberately leads with the scalpel,
     so the mechanism MUST be framed as *found money the reader has personally
     witnessed*, never a how-it-works explainer. A hook that reads like a product
     spec has failed the brief.
  2. No pain opener — curiosity/novelty only; pain amplifies later in the body.
     See [[pain-is-amplifier-not-opener]].
  3. One curiosity thread: the coma and the wake-up. Don't also open loops on AI,
     escaping the 9-5, and the 50/50 split in the hook.
- **Source route:** Regular path in /produce (hook bot → body → headlines).
  No swipe/organic seed.

## The one-breath Lock test
*"Nearly every business trading five-plus years is sitting on thousands of leads
in a coma — people who asked to buy and never got followed up, which is why you
still get the odd 'hey, still interested?' text from a gym you visited once.
Dan's agency sent one campaign like that to their own old database and rang up
$750k in found money, then did it again for $1.08m — and his student Seb woke a
'deader than dead' list of 83,000 contacts and pulled close to £1m for his
client, twice. So the play isn't finding new customers; it's waking the ones
businesses already paid for, with an AI holding thousands of those text
conversations at once, and splitting every sale 50/50."*

**Rhyme:** coma → sleeping database → SMS wake-up → found money → 50/50. Pain
metaphor inverts cleanly into the solution metaphor (coma → wake); the Image
lives in the same root (flatline → pulse); the spokesperson embodies the
mechanism (woke his own database first); proof is another chapter of the story,
not a bolted-on defence. Predictable ending from sentence one = inevitability.

**Loosest dimension (aim, not gate):** the AI/product join — the coma-and-kiss
metaphor works *without* AI (a human could send the texts), so the body must earn
the Android with a "which means" join (the AI is what lets one person hold 10,000
revived conversations at once). Otherwise the $14.40 book reads as a swap-in
product at the end of a great story. Cheap to fix in body; flagged for /produce.
Second-loosest: deliberately bends hook principle 8 (see Guideline 1).

## Hook directions for /produce (angles, NOT final copy)
1. **Reader-as-dead-lead flip.** Open on the reader's own experience of
   *receiving* the mechanism — that "still interested?" text from a business they
   ghosted years ago — then reveal someone got paid for sending it. Reads as
   insight; second-person, so Dan-truth safe by construction.
2. **The insider's found-money confession (Dan, first-person, true).** Dan's best
   revenue month came without a single new lead — $750k rung out of a list his
   agency had stopped looking at, then $1.08m on the second pass. "The money was
   already in the building."
3. **The Lead Coma diagnosis (world-level, edge-of-reality claim).** Every
   business over five years old is sitting on a pile of leads in a coma, and one
   text wakes them — backed in the body by Seb's 83,000-contact list paying £1m,
   twice. Names and owns the problem-name in the hook itself.
