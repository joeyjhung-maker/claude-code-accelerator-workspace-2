# ESCAPE_BurntAgencyOwner_Reframe_TwoTypesRevenue_VIDEO_v01

One of 8 briefs filling Diversity Decoder gap #1. Introduces a revenue taxonomy most
agency owners have never heard named.

## DNA

- **Segment:** Segment 1, the Burned-Out Agency Owner.
- **Awareness:** Unaware. No AI/product/mechanism mention until the taxonomy resolves
  into it.
- **Belief this ad challenges:** "All agency revenue is basically the same, I just need
  more of it."
- **Concept (flat):** There's revenue that needs you to keep showing up, and revenue that
  runs without you — most agency owners have only ever built the first kind.
- **Angle:** Hidden-cause reframe — names a distinction the reader's never heard
  articulated, not a pain confession.
- **Mechanism:** AI + SMS revives a business's dead/old lead database, performance-only —
  presented here as the clearest example of "revenue that runs on a system" Dan's found.
- **Problem name:** **"The Vendor Leash"** (shared, see batch).
  - **Binary:** Vendor vs. **Partner** (shared) — this concept is the most mechanism-
    forward expression of the binary in the batch.
- **The Image:** A leaky bucket someone keeps refilling by hand, next to a bucket that
  fills itself. Note: this competes slightly with the shared leash image — see one-breath
  test below.
- **Spokesperson:** Dan. Archetype: **the reformed insider** — spent years building the
  first kind of revenue and calling it success. Per [[dan-truth-constraint]], past/
  resolved framing.
- **CTA:** $14.40 book + bonuses + 30-day guarantee → `book.flexxable.com/optin`.
- **Format:** Video, in-feed MicroVSL, Dan talking-head, explainer conceit — works well
  with a split-screen or graphic overlay for the two-bucket comparison.
- **Source route:** Regular path (hook bot → body → headlines). No full script drafted
  yet.
- **Guidelines:**
  - This is the most mechanism-adjacent hook in the batch — keep the OPEN on the
    taxonomy/reframe, not the mechanism itself; don't let it drift into a straight
    product-explainer too early.
  - The two-bucket image should carry the explainer visually; don't over-describe it in
    words if the visual's doing the work.
