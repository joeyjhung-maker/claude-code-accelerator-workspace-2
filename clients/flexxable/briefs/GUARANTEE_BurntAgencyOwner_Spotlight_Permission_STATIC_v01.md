## DNA

- **Segment:** Segment 2, the Burned-Out Agency Owner (strategy-map) — same segment
  as the Bonus Pile brief and the account's existing Product-Aware ad.
- **Awareness:** Most-Aware. Reader knows Dan, the book, the offer. The remaining
  friction this ad targets is risk-aversion, not unfamiliarity.
- **Concept (flat):** The 30-day keep-everything guarantee means the only way to
  actually lose is not trying. Concept type: **Guarantee Spotlight**
  (creative-coverage's Most-Aware library).
- **Angle:** **Permission** — gives the reader explicit permission to act by removing
  the downside, rather than arguing the upside again.
- **Belief this ad works with:** "what if it doesn't work for me" — the exact
  hesitation the Vendor-vs-Partner OBJECTION ad didn't cover, since that one argued
  the model, not the risk of trying it personally.
- **Mechanism:** unchanged — AI + SMS reactivation, performance-only. Backdrop only,
  not re-explained.
- **Problem name / binary:** not needed — same reasoning as the Bonus Pile brief.
- **The Image:** a badge/shield icon over the guarantee terms — visually "you're
  covered," not a wall of legal-sounding text. Simple enough to read in under a
  second on a static.
- **Spokesperson:** Dan. No new first-person claims.
- **CTA:** $14.40 book + bonuses + 30-day keep-everything guarantee →
  `book.flexxable.com/optin`.
- **Format:** Static, icon/badge style.
- **Source route:** Regular path (hook bot → body → headlines). Same primer gap as
  the Bonus Pile brief — no dedicated Most-Aware primer exists yet; proceeding on
  direct instruction for short, direct-offer copy.
- **Guidelines:**
  - Guarantee language pulled verbatim from `offer-doc-ROYA.md` — nothing invented
    or reworded into something stronger than what's actually offered.
  - Short. Same discipline as the Bonus Pile brief.
