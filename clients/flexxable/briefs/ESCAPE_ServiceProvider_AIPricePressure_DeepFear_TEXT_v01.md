## DNA

- **Segment:** Existing agency owners, freelancers and online service providers —
  especially copywriters, SMMA managers and SEO providers — whose skill-based service
  is being commoditised by AI. This expands the strategy map's Burned-Out Agency Owner
  segment to adjacent solo service providers living the same pricing pressure.
- **Awareness:** Problem-Aware. They already feel the replacement/price threat, but they
  still assume the answer is to defend their rates, increase output or secure the old
  retainer model. The belief this ad challenges: "If I prove my value by doing more,
  clients will keep paying me what I'm worth." The reframe: output is becoming cheaper;
  measurable sales are not.
- **Concept (flat):** AI is attacking output-based service providers from two sides.
  Some are replaced outright. The rest can keep their clients while AI makes their work
  feel cheaper, so buyers expect more output for less money and resist the old
  upfront-retainer deal. **Concept type: The Metaphor** (Problem-Aware library).
- **Angle:** **Deep Fear** — the threat is not a single future redundancy event. It is a
  market squeeze with two bad outcomes: lose the work, or keep doing it after its price
  has been crushed.
- **Hypothesis:** No account-read opening is attached to this brief. This is an
  operator-directed market bet from Joey: AI replacement fear is already hot, and the
  overlooked extension is that avoiding replacement does not mean the provider escaped
  — many can be squeezed through lower rates and higher output expectations instead.
- **Mechanism:** Stop selling units of output through a retainer/upfront fee. Install an
  AI sales bot that reactivates a business's old leads over SMS, creates measurable
  sales, and structure the deal on a zero-risk performance basis so the provider earns
  a share of results.
- **Problem name:** **The Service Provider Squeeze** — AI pushes from one side by
  replacing and commoditising output; buyers push from the other by demanding more,
  paying less and resisting upfront retainers. The provider is crushed between them.
  - **Binary:** **Output Vendor vs. Revenue Partner.** An Output Vendor is compared on
    volume and price; a Revenue Partner is paid from measurable commercial results.
- **The Image:** A service provider trapped in a bench vice. The left jaw is labelled
  **AI: REPLACE OR CHEAPEN**. The right jaw is labelled **CLIENTS: MORE FOR LESS**. The
  old retainer sits cracking in the middle. A path out leads toward **PAID ON RESULTS**.
- **Spokesperson:** Dan. Archetype: **the reformed agency insider who changed the deal,
  not merely the tool.** First-person claims must stay inside Dan's verified history;
  the market-pressure diagnosis should be second-person or world-level unless sourced.
- **CTA:** The Instant AI Agency book for $14.40, including the bonuses and 30-day
  keep-the-book guarantee, leading to `book.flexxable.com/optin`.
- **Format:** Long-form Facebook text ad.
- **Source route:** Regular, revised — Genesis `new-hook-bot` after the Nerd-Free idea
  gate, then Mariobot for body and headlines after Joey chooses the hook. Do not reuse
  the rejected `ad-hook-bot-1` batch.
- **Primers:** `clients/flexxable/primers/hooks.md` for hooks;
  `clients/flexxable/primers/iaa-cold-body.md` for the Problem-Aware pain-led body.
- **Guidelines:**
  - The opening must be curiosity-, novelty- or world-led. Do not begin with a generic
    "AI will replace you" warning.
  - Keep the opening at the market/category level. Do not shrink the angle into one
    revision, one late night, one ChatGPT cleanup job or generic scope creep.
  - The emotional escalation is replacement fear → the two-sided squeeze revealed →
    retained but commoditised → more work for less → old retainer pitch rejected.
  - The pivot is not "use more AI." It is "change what the client pays you for."
  - Do not frame the model as generic AI lead generation. It reactivates old/dead leads
    over SMS and turns them into measurable sales.
  - Do not claim every client or the entire market rejects retainers. Keep the market
    observation conversational and non-quantified.
  - Preserve the distinction between the risk-free client offer and the book's own
    keep-the-book guarantee.
