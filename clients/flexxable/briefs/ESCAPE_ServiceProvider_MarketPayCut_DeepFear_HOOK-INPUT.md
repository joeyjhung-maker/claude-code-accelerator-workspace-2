# Ad Hook Bot 1 input — The Pay Cut Before the Pitch

## Task

Write exactly **10 hooks** for the first line or two of one long-form Facebook text ad.

Then rank the **three strongest hooks**. The shortlist is a judge recommendation only; Joey will make the final selection.

All 10 hooks must be interchangeable above the **same body copy**. Do not create hooks that require different stories, proof, mechanisms or transitions.

## Product

The Instant AI Agency book, $14.40. It teaches agency owners and service providers to stop selling output on upfront retainers and instead use AI + SMS to reactivate a business's old leads, create measurable sales and get paid from results.

Do not mention the book, price, product or solution in the hook.

## Target reader

Agency owners, freelancers and online service providers — copywriters, SMMA managers, SEO providers and similar operators — who have noticed that new projects are harder to win at the rates the category used to command.

## Locked Nerd-Free idea

**The Pay Cut Before the Pitch.**

AI has lowered the going price buyers expect to pay for many output-based services before a provider ever quotes, even where the provider's skill, responsibility and the commercial value expected from the finished work have not fallen.

This hook batch focuses only on **new pitches and incoming briefs**. It is not an existing-client renewal ad.

## Human consequence

Nobody calls to announce the provider's pay has been cut.

A familiar brief simply lands with the same build, same expected outcome and a much smaller budget already baked in.

The provider walks into the pitch already marked down.

## The image

A complete project brief sits on a desk. Before the provider has entered the room or typed a quote, the old fee has been crossed out and a smaller yellow price sticker slapped over it.

## Open-loop command

Show **A and Z** but hide **B through Y**.

Every hook must leave this one question burning:

> How was my fee cut before the buyer even met me?

If the hook explains that AI made output faster, cheaper, abundant or easier, it has closed the loop and must be discarded.

## Intensity and language

- Desired reaction: **“wait… WHAT?”**
- Grab them by the throat, then grease the slide into the same body.
- Use blunt, bodily, tactile language with physical mouthfeel.
- Prefer short Germanic words and hard verbs.
- Do not use scrubbed marketing nouns such as “commoditisation,” “price anchoring,” “market correction,” “value perception” or “pricing pressure.”
- Avoid neat educational summaries and balanced “AI does X, clients do Y” constructions.
- One curiosity thread only.
- One or two lines, ideally 8–22 words.

## Scope locks

- New work, new pitches, incoming briefs and buyer budgets only.
- Do not drift into renewal calls, thanking clients, fuller calendars, doing five jobs, clearance racks, years of expertise, invisible judgment, sales dashboards or getting paid on results. Those belong to other ads.
- Do not use Joey's private funnel numbers.
- Do not invent statistics, quotes, named clients, timeframes, market studies or universal claims.
- Do not say that every provider or every client behaves this way.
- Do not write body copy, headlines or CTA.

## Brand hook references

Study the shape and confidence of these verified Flexxable winners without copying their ideas:

1. I copied this agency model from what Hedge Fund Managers do…
2. I now make 10X what I earn from clients by installing A.I. into their business… plus… A.I. does all the work!
3. Rob Brown earned $341,000 dollars in just 90 days from a single client using my AI system.
4. I've been in the game for a while, and I've never seen ANYTHING with the easy revenue potential of an “Instant A.I. Agency”
5. What Does It Take To Be #1? 🥇

## Required output

1. Brief emotional excavation.
2. Exactly 10 numbered hooks.
3. For each hook: dominant vehicle and the exact question it leaves open.
4. Rank a top three, with one sentence on why each should pull the reader into the shared body.
