# BURNOUT_BurntAgencyOwner_Recognition_BoggedDownByDelivery_TEXT_v01

The delivery-bog recognition ad. Sibling to Ad 2 in [[winning-ads]] (the
"serenity-sucking services" list) — same underlying mechanism, already
approved in [[MASTER-CONCEPTS-ROYA]] (p154/p157: "mountain of shit to
deliver" vs "low delivery hassle"), extended here with sharper, more
physical imagery from the 07-23 Travis call.

## DNA
- **Segment:** Burned-Out Agency Owner (Segment 1, [[strategy-map]]) —
  already running an agency, too many clients/services, grinding for not
  enough.
- **Awareness:** Solution-aware. They know there's a better way, haven't
  found it yet.
- **Concept (flat):** Traditional agency/AI-build delivery means constant
  DMs, bugs, ongoing support, and clients calling mad after the sale. This
  model has none of that — you don't build or maintain anything for the
  client, so there's nothing to deliver.
- **Angle:** Recognition, not contrarian. Mirrors the reader's exact daily
  hell back at them in specific, physical terms, then removes it entirely.
  Not a belief-callout — a "you know this feeling" opener.
- **Mechanism:** Client default. AI + SMS revives a business's dead/old
  leads, performance-based, 30-50% of profit split. No build, no ongoing
  service.
- **Problem name:** **Bogged Down By Delivery.** (Joey: kept the plain
  phrase, killed "Delivery Bog" as a coined shorthand — too cute, doesn't
  sound like something a real agency owner would say about their own week.)
- **The Image:** A phone screen flooding with red notification bubbles
  (old way) vs. one text sent, then silence (new way). Literal, drawable,
  single glance.
- **Spokesperson:** Dan, second-person/observational. Describing the
  READER's pain ("you know the feeling"), not an invented Dan anecdote
  about his own DMs or bugs — see [[dan-truth-constraint]].
- **CTA:** Client default. The Instant AI Agency book, $14.40, 30-day
  money-back guarantee (keep the book + training either way).
- **Format:** Text ad (Meta primary text + headline).
- **Guidelines:**
  1. ⚠️ Do not use Travis's exact private wording verbatim ("DMs out the
     wazoo," "clients calling mad") in the finished copy — that transcript
     is internal strategy talk, not cleared copy. Paraphrase the SHAPE in
     Dan's own voice. Same rule already applied to the sibling
     [[CONTRARIAN_SalesBroObsolete_Solution_DemoNotSell_TEXT_v01]] brief.
  2. Physical/specific over abstract — the whole point of this angle vs.
     the existing "serenity-sucking" list is sharper, more visceral images
     of the exact moment-to-moment pain, not another bullet list.
  3. No stacked abstract triplet ([[no-stacked-abstract-triplet]]) — don't
     let the recognition beats collapse into three vague benefit lines in a
     row.
- **Source route:** research (mined from a real internal call transcript,
  not a swipe or organic post) — hook bot → body → headlines, regular path.
- **Proof:** None needed/used. Mechanism-level claim only, no personal
  result cited. If a proof point gets added later, it must be verified
  against [[offer-doc-ROYA]] with a line reference, not invented.

## One-breath check
Burned-out agency owner, drowning in DMs and angry clients from the old
delivery model → this model has no delivery at all, just a text and a
split → same book, same guarantee. Rhymes clean.
