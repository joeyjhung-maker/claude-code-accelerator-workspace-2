# OBJECTION_BurntAgencyOwner_HeadToHead_VendorVsPartner_STATIC_v01

First ad targeting gap #2 (Product-Aware closer). Sibling to the Unaware batch
(U2/U6/U9/V3/V4/V6/V8/V10) — same segment, same Vendor/Partner binary, opposite end
of the funnel. This one assumes the reader already knows the book exists.

## Locked hook

Graded 20-hook batch against [[hook-rubric]] (all 9 gates, Rob Brown surname fixed to
"Rob," an invented detail cut from a Jacques hook, Gate 9 vehicle variety passed at
6/7). Joey picked:

> I put the retainer model and the performance model side by side on five
> dimensions... and the retainer column reads like a list of reasons your spouse
> never sees you.

Promises FIVE named dimensions in the comparison — the body must actually deliver
five, not gesture at "several." The Image (two-column Vendor vs Partner table) IS
those five dimensions rendered visually — body and image should carry the same five,
not diverge.

## DNA

- **Segment:** Segment 2, the Burned-Out Agency Owner (strategy-map).
- **Awareness:** Product-Aware. Reader knows the book, hasn't bought. Comparison/
  offer primer downstream — not the pain-led Unaware primer the rest of the batch
  used.
- **Concept (flat):** A side-by-side of the retainer model vs. the performance model,
  on the exact dimensions this segment worries about — who takes the risk, who
  re-earns trust every month, what the income ceiling is. Concept type: **Objection
  Crusher** ([[creative-coverage]]'s Product-Aware library — take the top objections
  head-on).
- **Angle:** **Head-to-Head** ([[creative-coverage]]'s 24-angle list) — direct
  comparison against the known alternative (their current retainer model), not an
  abstract claim about a better way.
- **Belief this ad challenges:** "The retainer model is the only reliable way to run
  an agency" + "Performance deals are risky because clients can lowball results" —
  both pulled verbatim from [[2026-08-24-segment-surgeon]]'s constraint read on this
  exact segment (Segment 2's Beliefs field).
- **Mechanism:** AI + SMS revives a business's dead/old lead database, performance-only
  (30-50% rev share, no upfront cost) — unchanged from the rest of the batch.
- **Problem name / binary:** **The Vendor Leash** (shared batch handle) — this concept
  is effectively that binary rendered as a literal comparison table. Carries forward
  for anyone who saw the Unaware ads; stands alone for anyone who didn't.
  - **Binary:** Vendor (retainer — re-earned monthly, reachable anytime, revenue needs
    your presence) vs. **Partner** (performance-only, revenue runs without you, they're
    rooting for you, not auditing you).
- **The Image:** A two-column comparison table, Vendor vs. Partner, down specific line
  items: upfront cost, who re-earns trust monthly, who takes the risk, income ceiling.
  Direct visual rendering of the Head-to-Head angle — the picture IS the argument.
- **Spokesperson:** Dan. Archetype: the reformed insider (unchanged). Per
  [[dan-truth-constraint]], any first-person framing of his own past retainer years
  stays past/resolved.
- **CTA:** $14.40 book + bonuses + 30-day keep-everything guarantee →
  `book.flexxable.com/optin` (the account's top, most-proven funnel page per the
  Decoder audit).
- **Format:** Static, comparison-table graphic. Genuine format gap too — the account
  has zero comparison-style content at any awareness level. A text-ad companion using
  the same table as body copy is a natural follow-up, not required for this brief.
- **Source route:** Regular path (hook bot → body → headlines). Concept+Angle are
  locked directly (not from a mined seed) so hook-bot's instruction is this brief
  itself — the 20-hook pass should all be Head-to-Head deliveries of the Objection
  Crusher concept, graded against [[hook-rubric]] including Gate 9 (vehicle variety
  across the batch still applies — a Head-to-Head concept can still be delivered
  through Contrarian, Proof, Authority, etc. vehicles, don't let all 20 collapse into
  one flavor of comparison).
- **Guidelines:**
  - The comparison table is the star — copy should point at it, not re-explain every
    line item in prose. Show, don't summarize applies doubly here.
  - Lead with the comparison, not a pain re-statement — this reader already knows the
    pain (Product-Aware), re-litigating it wastes the ad's short attention window.
  - Jacques's proof (below) is the "here's what the Partner column actually looks
    like in practice" beat — use it to make the table's Partner column feel real, not
    theoretical.
