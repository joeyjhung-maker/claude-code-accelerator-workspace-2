# New Hook Bot input — The Service Provider Squeeze, vicious open-loop pass

## Locked strategic idea

Audience: agency owners, freelancers and online service providers — copywriters, SMMA managers, SEO providers and similar operators.

They fear AI will replace them. The darker threat is that even if clients keep them, AI can still destroy what the work is worth: more output expected, less money offered, and growing resistance to paid-upfront retainers.

Problem label: **The Service Provider Squeeze.** It is a two-sided vice: replacement on one side, commoditisation and lowballing on the other.

The eventual escape is to stop defending an upfront retainer for output and change the game: make a risk-free offer, solve a sales problem and get paid on results. **Do not reveal that answer in the hook.**

## Joey's correction — this controls the pass

The previous hooks were strategically accurate but too safe. They closed the loop by explaining the two-sided threat inside the opening.

This pass must **grab them by the throat, then grease the slide.** The desired reaction is:

> Wait… WHAT?

Show the shocking contradiction and the dangerous consequence — **A and Z** — while hiding the explanation — **B through Y**.

Every hook must create one urgent unanswered question that can only be resolved by continuing to read or watch. After each hook, state that exact burning question on a separate line for judging. If the hook itself answers its own “why?” or “how?”, discard it and write another.

## Intensity and direction

Go to the vicious ceiling before checking compliance. The hook should feel ominous, counterintuitive and personally threatening — not tidy, educational or merely insightful.

Useful paradox territories:

- Keeping the client may be the dangerous outcome.
- The “survivors” of AI may lose something worse than the people replaced.
- A full client roster can conceal an AI casualty.
- “We still need you” can be more dangerous than being fired.
- A client can replace the economics of a provider without replacing the provider.

These are territories, not final lines. Create fresh executions.

## Hard constraints

- Do not explain the mechanism in the hook.
- Do not say, in the hook, that AI made output cheap, clients expect more for less, rates are being crushed, or retainers are harder to sell. Those are body answers.
- Avoid explanatory constructions such as “because”, “which means” and “the reason is”.
- Do not neatly state both jaws of the vice in the same hook.
- Use the label “The Service Provider Squeeze” in no more than three hooks.
- Stay at the macro business-model level. No revision rounds, scope creep, cleanup work, ChatGPT drafts or TikTok examples.
- No invented statistics, client stories, quotes, timing, results or universal factual claims.
- One or two short sentences. Prefer 8–24 words.
- Do not solve the problem or mention risk-free offers, paid-on-results pricing or Instant AI Agency.
- The hook must still clearly implicate agency owners/freelancers/service providers, either explicitly or through unmistakable context.

## Output format

For each of 20 hooks, provide:

1. Hook
2. Archetype/pattern
3. Exact burning question it leaves open
4. Viciousness score /10
5. Open-loop score /10

Do not include any hook below 8/10 for viciousness or 9/10 for open-loop tension.
