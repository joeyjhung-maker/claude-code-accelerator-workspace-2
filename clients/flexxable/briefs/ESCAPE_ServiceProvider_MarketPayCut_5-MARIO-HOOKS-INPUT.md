# Mariobot hook task — five replacements for one fixed ad

Write exactly **5 new hooks** for the existing Instant AI Agency ad below.

These are five replacement openings for the **same fixed body**, not five separate ad concepts.

Return only a numbered list of five hooks.

## Replacement boundary

The current control hook is:

> Your clients aren't paying you less because your work got worse.

Do not reuse or lightly paraphrase that line.

Each new hook will replace the control and flow directly into this locked second line:

> They're paying you less because AI made the OUTPUT feel cheaper to them… even when you're still the one doing the thinking.

Because the next line begins with `They're`, every hook must clearly name or establish **clients/buyers** as the antecedent.

Do not alter or repeat the locked second line.

## Core wound

AI does not need to replace the service provider to cut their income.

Clients can keep hiring the provider while valuing the same skilled work less.

The hook should leave this question open for the body:

> How can AI cut what my clients pay me without replacing me?

## Hook requirements

- Exactly five hooks.
- One or two short lines each, ideally 8-20 words.
- Every hook must attach naturally to the exact locked second line.
- Every hook must work above the same unchanged body.
- Find five genuinely different entrances, not five synonyms.
- Use a mix of curiosity, contrarian belief, direct call-out, a lived pitch moment and emotional threat.
- State the wound without explaining that AI made output faster or cheaper. The locked body handles that reveal.
- Aim for `wait… WHAT?`, but keep the wording natural and spoken.
- Blunt, simple language.
- No crime-scene, knife, blood, biting, guts, robbery or clearance-rack metaphors.
- No statistics, results, timeframes, client quotes or personal Dan history.
- No universal claim that every client behaves this way.
- Do not mention the book, mechanism, SMS, old leads, price or solution.
- Do not write body copy, labels, commentary or a shortlist.
