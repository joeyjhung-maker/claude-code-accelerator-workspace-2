# Primary text + headlines to sit around a finished video ad

The video script at the bottom is finished and locked. It runs with three different hooks on the same body.

Write the two things that sit around the video in the Facebook ad:

1. **Primary text** — the copy above the video. **50 words.** Between 45 and 55, counting the URL as one word. Count before you answer.

## Primary text — what it has to do

- It appears above ALL THREE hooks, so it must not repeat any of the three hook lines, and it must make sense whichever hook plays underneath.
- Its job is to make a service provider stop and press play, or tap straight through. It is not a summary of the video.
- Same idea as the video: AI hasn't taken their job, it has cut their price, and the way out is getting paid on results instead of output.
- Lead with curiosity or a blunt true line. Pain is the amplifier, not the opener. Do not open on a question. Do not open on Lachie or any student proof.
- One sentence per line. Short lines. Sounds like Dan texting a mate who owns a business. Casual, blunt, Aussie.
- End on the link: book.flexxable.com/optin

## Headlines — what they have to do

- One flowing line each. Aim for 4 to 9 words so they don't get cut off on a phone.
- Plain beats clever. A headline someone understands in one glance.
- Ten genuinely different ways in, not ten rewordings. Spread them across: the price drop, the "results not output" switch, the old-leads mechanism, the no-upfront-payment deal, the book and its price, and curiosity.
- At least two should name the book or the offer directly, in the style of past winners for this account:
  - "[Download] Instant A.I. Agency"
  - "Get Access For Only $14.40"
- No headline may promise a result or an income.

## Verified facts you may use (nothing else)

- The model: find a business with old leads, use AI and SMS to turn those old leads into sales, get paid a cut. No upfront payment from the business.
- Dan has taught over 1,000 everyday people how to do this.
- Lachie was a finance guy with zero business, sales or tech experience. He tried this, hit $20k/mo, then quit his job. (Allowed in a headline as a "How a finance guy…" line. Not in the primary text opener.)

- The $5K and $2K in the script are the operator's own illustration. You may echo them once, exactly, or leave them out. No other numbers.

No invented results, statistics, timeframes, quotes or Dan backstory. Do not say AI can't touch you. Banned words: leverage, solution, deliverable, onboarding. No dashes. No emoji except one 👇 before the link if you want it.
