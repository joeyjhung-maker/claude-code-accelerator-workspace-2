# Mariobot hook revision — first set missed the ad

Rewrite all five hooks from scratch.

Return only a numbered list of exactly five hooks.

## Why your first set failed

1. `Your work didn't get worse...` lightly paraphrased the existing control.
2. `Some of your best clients...` drifted into existing-client renewal territory and made an unsupported future prediction.
3. `same results you delivered last year...` invented a timeframe and again implied an existing client.
4. `client who loved your last project...` invented an existing-client scene and turned the ad into a renewal/discount ad.
5. `Your clients still need you...` restated the conclusion instead of opening curiosity.

Do not reuse any of those frames.

## Exact job

Write five replacement hooks for a fixed ad about **new briefs, prospective buyers and new pitches**.

The provider has not worked with the buyer before.

The market wound is that AI can cut what a new buyer expects to pay before the provider ever quotes, without replacing the provider.

Each hook must flow directly into this locked second line:

> They're paying you less because AI made the OUTPUT feel cheaper to them… even when you're still the one doing the thinking.

Because the locked line starts with `They're`, each hook must clearly establish `clients`, `buyers` or `prospects` as the antecedent.

## Requirements

- Exactly five hooks.
- One or two short lines each, 8-20 words.
- All five attach to the same exact body.
- New-work context only: next brief, new buyer, new pitch or prospect.
- Five distinct entrances: paradox, incoming-brief moment, contrarian belief, direct call-out and emotional threat.
- Leave this question open: `How can AI cut what new clients pay me before I quote?`
- Do not explain the cause in the hook.
- Do not repeat `your work didn't get worse`, `clients still need you`, `same results`, `last year`, `best clients`, discounts or renewals.
- Do not use crime-scene, knife, blood, biting, guts, robbery or clearance-rack metaphors.
- No statistics, proof, timeframes, quotes, results or personal stories.
- Do not claim every buyer behaves this way.
- No labels, analysis, body copy or shortlist.
