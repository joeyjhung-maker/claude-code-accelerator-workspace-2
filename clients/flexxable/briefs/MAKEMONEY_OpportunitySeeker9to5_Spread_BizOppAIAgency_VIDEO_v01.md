# MAKEMONEY_OpportunitySeeker9to5_Spread_BizOppAIAgency_VIDEO_v01

## DNA
- **Segment:** SPREAD — Segment 4 (Opportunity Seeker) + Segment 2 (9-5 Escapee).
  Wider than agency owners. One modular body, hooks aimed at both doors.
- **Awareness:** Opens unaware/opportunity (mass-market "make money with AI"),
  not solution-aware-about-agencies. No agency framing in the open.
- **Concept (flat fact):** There's a simple AI business where the AI does the
  work, one client can pay thousands a month, and you don't need experience, a
  product, or money to start.
- **Angle (the door in):** Opportunity + objection-removal. Lead with the
  upside/ease, then kill the "I'm not technical / it's too good to be true"
  objections. NOT pain-first burnout (that's the other segment's ad).
- **Mechanism:** The Instant A.I. Agency — you take a business's OLD/DEAD leads
  (already sitting in their database, given up on) and AI wakes them up over SMS
  and turns them into NEW sales. NOT new-lead generation. Database reactivation,
  the AI does the heavy lifting, performance-based split, no upfront cost to the
  client, one client = thousands/mo. (Source of truth: offer-doc-ROYA.md — old
  leads → new sales via the "Sleeping Beauty" reactivation + "Prince Charming" SMS.)
- **CTA:** The Instant AI Agency book, $14.40 + bonuses, 30-day keep-everything guarantee.
- **Format:** VIDEO — **one modular body + 4 opening hooks** (per Gav). The body
  is the shared pitch that plays AFTER the hook; it must NOT hard-open on one
  scenario, so any of the 4 hooks can slot in front. Spoken in-feed VO register.
- **Guidelines:** Mass-market biz-opp voice. Prime on the biz-opp swipes, NOT
  the agency winning-ads. Ban the agency-owner burnout/confession register and
  agency jargon.
- **Source route:** Swipe-informed regular path. Prime both bots on
  `primers/bizopp-video.md`. Body → mariobot (video-script instruction);
  hooks → ad-hook-bot-1, curate 4 across the angles below.

## The 4 hook angles (one body underneath)
1. **Opportunity/upside (S4)** — "the easiest AI business nobody's talking about."
2. **"You already use ChatGPT" (S4 / Dabbler Dave)** — monetize what you already do.
3. **Escape-the-9-5 (S2 / Redundancy Randy)** — job-under-review / out of the job.
4. **"Not technical" objection (S2 / Cautious Carl)** — beginner, no coding, proof it works.

## Proof
Keep proof light and VERIFIED only. The only bankable numbers are in
`clients/flexxable/winning-ads.md` (e.g. Rob's $341,042). **Do NOT invent student
income numbers** to match the swipes' "$10k in a week" cold opens — if the body
wants a proof beat, use a verified number or keep it aspirational without a
fabricated figure. (Hard rule: never invent a result.)

**Note for /produce:** the swipes' income-claim cold opens ("the first one I
launched made me $10,000") are VOICE inspiration, not claims we can copy verbatim
with a number we haven't earned. Mirror the structure, not the unverified figure.
