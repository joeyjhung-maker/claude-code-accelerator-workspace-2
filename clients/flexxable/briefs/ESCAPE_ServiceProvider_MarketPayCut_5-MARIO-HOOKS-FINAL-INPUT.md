# Mariobot final hook pass — corrected replacement boundary

Write exactly **5 hook blocks** for one fixed Instant AI Agency ad.

Return only a numbered list of five hooks.

## Correct replacement boundary

Each hook replaces the ad's entire current two-sentence opening:

> Your clients aren't paying you less because your work got worse.
>
> They're paying you less because AI made the OUTPUT feel cheaper to them… even when you're still the one doing the thinking.

After each new hook, the unchanged shared body begins:

> Budgets shrinking, scope creeping, and more briefs landing with that little sting of "can't AI just do most of this now?"

The hook therefore does **not** need to supply an antecedent for `They're`.

## Core idea

Agency owners, freelancers and online service providers fear AI will replace them.

The more immediate threat is that AI can cut what new buyers expect to pay before the provider ever quotes, even while the buyer still needs a skilled human to do and own the work.

Leave one question open:

> How did AI cut the going price of my work without replacing me?

## Requirements

- Exactly five numbered hook blocks.
- Each can be one or two short spoken lines, 8-24 words total.
- Every hook must flow naturally into the locked `Budgets shrinking...` body line.
- All five hooks must fit the same body without requiring different proof or stories.
- Five distinct entrances: paradox, future threat, new-pitch moment, contrarian belief and direct service-provider call-out.
- One promising territory from the previous pass was: `Most freelancers think AI is coming for their JOBS… but it's already come for their price tags.` You may sharpen and broaden that idea, but do not narrow the whole ad to freelancers.
- Use agency owners, service providers or a broad second-person frame where targeting is needed.
- Show the consequence and withhold the explanation.
- Natural, blunt and spoken. No strained gore or clever copywriter metaphor.
- No crime-scene, knife, blood, biting, guts, robbery or clearance-rack language.
- No existing-client renewals, discounts or past-client scenes.
- No statistics, results, exact declines, timeframes, quotes or Dan backstory.
- Do not claim every client behaves this way.
- Do not mention the book, mechanism, old leads, SMS, price or solution.
- Do not add vehicle labels, explanations, body copy or a shortlist.
