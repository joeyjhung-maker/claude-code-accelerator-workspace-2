# Targeted hook pass — The Pay Cut Before the Pitch

The first `ad-hook-bot-1` pass correctly found the wound but failed batch variety: most hooks were the same curiosity accusation restated.

Write **6 new candidate hooks only** to fill the missing vehicles:

- 2 direct **Call-Out** hooks
- 2 **Contrarian** hooks that turn a familiar pitch belief against the reader
- 2 **Urgency/clock** hooks tied to the next incoming brief or next pitch

These are replacement candidates, not a new campaign. Every line must still attach to the same shared long-form Facebook body described below.

## Locked idea

**The Pay Cut Before the Pitch.**

Agency owners, freelancers and online service providers are meeting new briefs whose budgets have already been cut before the provider ever quotes. The provider's skill and the outcome expected from the work have not shrunk, but they walk into the pitch already marked down.

## Required image and itch

Make the reader feel a hand has already **crossed out, hacked, carved, peeled or torn money from their fee** before they enter the room.

Each hook must leave only this question burning:

> How was my fee cut before the buyer even met me?

Show the wound. Hide the explanation.

## Hard constraints

- One or two lines, ideally 8–22 words.
- Blunt Germanic words. Physical mouthfeel. Make it sting.
- Do not explain AI, speed, cheap output, abundance, price anchors or commoditisation.
- Do not mention the book, product, price or solution.
- Do not invent stats, quotes, named people, results or universal claims.
- New work only: next brief, new pitch, buyer budget.
- No renewals, retainers, fuller calendars, clearance racks, years of experience, dashboards or paid-on-results language.
- Do not write body copy or rank a top three.
- Avoid repeating these safe sentence frames: “Your fee got crossed out before…”, “Someone slashed your rate before…”, “You haven't pitched yet and…”. Find fresher physical scenes.

## Output

Exactly 6 numbered hooks. For each, label the dominant vehicle and state the exact open question.
