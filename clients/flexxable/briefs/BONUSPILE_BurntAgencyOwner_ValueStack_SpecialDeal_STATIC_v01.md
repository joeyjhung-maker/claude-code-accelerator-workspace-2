# BONUSPILE_BurntAgencyOwner_ValueStack_SpecialDeal_STATIC_v01

First of three Most-Aware briefs opening Phase 2 (harvest) of the awareness-cycle
framework. This one is built to receive Joey's product stack directly — The Image
IS the stack, rendered as icons.

## DNA

- **Segment:** Segment 2, the Burned-Out Agency Owner (strategy-map) — same segment
  as the account's one Product-Aware ad (OBJECTION_..._VendorVsPartner). This is one
  step further down that same funnel: reader already knows the book, has likely seen
  the Vendor-vs-Partner comparison too.
- **Awareness:** Most-Aware. Reader knows Dan, knows the book, knows the offer. No
  mechanism re-explanation, no pain re-statement — per the framework, most-aware
  copy is short and direct, not a re-sell.
- **Concept (flat):** Everything bundled into the $14.40 book adds up to real,
  nameable value most people never see itemized. Concept type: **Bonus Pile**
  (creative-coverage's Most-Aware library).
- **Angle:** **Special Deal** — the offer's total value vs. its actual price is the
  entire argument, not a story or a comparison.
- **Belief this ad works with:** reader already believes the mechanism works (seen it
  in prior ads) — the only remaining friction is "is $14.40 actually worth grabbing
  right now," which a value stack answers visually.
- **Mechanism:** unchanged from the rest of the account — AI + SMS reactivates a
  business's old/dead lead database, performance-only (30-50% rev share, no upfront
  cost). Not re-explained in this ad, just the backdrop the offer sits on.
- **Problem name / binary:** not needed here — Most-Aware doesn't need a new enemy
  name, the reader is past that stage.
- **The Image:** the literal product stack — the book plus every bonus as its own
  icon, laid out with a price/strikethrough treatment (e.g. "$XXX value" struck
  through, landing on "$14.40 today"). Waiting on Joey's actual stack list to
  populate the icons — DNA locks the shape, not the line items.
- **Spokesperson:** Dan. Archetype: the reformed insider (unchanged). No new
  first-person claims needed — this ad is about the offer, not Dan's story.
- **CTA:** $14.40 book + bonuses + 30-day keep-everything guarantee →
  `book.flexxable.com/optin` (account's top, most-proven funnel page per the
  Decoder audit — no dedicated urgency page exists yet).
- **Format:** Static, icon-set/product-stack graphic. Genuine format gap — the
  account has zero icon-set content at any awareness level.
- **Source route:** Regular path (hook bot → body → headlines). No exact Most-Aware
  primer exists yet in `clients/flexxable/primers/` — flagging that; closest
  available is `iaa-cold-body.md`, which is COLD-audience and long-form, the
  opposite shape of what this ad needs. Proceeding on direct instruction (short,
  direct-offer copy) rather than mad-libbing a cold primer into a warm ad.
- **Guidelines:**
  - Short. This is the framework's whole point — most-aware copy is short-form,
    not a re-sell. If a draft runs long, that's a brief-match FAIL at the judge
    stage, not a style choice.
  - Lead with the stack/value, not a hook that re-opens curiosity about the
    mechanism — the reader already has that.
  - No new proof claims beyond what's already verified for this account.

## One-breath test

*A burnt-out agency owner who's already seen Dan's ads and the book pitch gets shown
everything that's actually inside the $14.40 offer, stacked up next to what it's
worth, so the only question left is why he hasn't tapped the button yet.*

Segment → prior exposure → stack → price gap → decision. Tight for a Most-Aware ad;
the DNA that's still loose is The Image's actual contents, which waits on the
product stack.
