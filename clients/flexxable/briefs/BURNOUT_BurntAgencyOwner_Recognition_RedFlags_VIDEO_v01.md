# BURNOUT_BurntAgencyOwner_Recognition_RedFlags_VIDEO_v01

Red Flag structure for the book offer. Four burnout symptoms acted out, then the one
cause behind all four, then the model that removes the cause.

Locked 2026-10-05. Joey's four calls: condition = Agency burnout, image = the wrong
column, symptom run animated with Dan from beat 3, Rob's line in (winning-ad version).

## DNA

- **Segment:** Segment 1, the Burned-Out Agency Owner.
- **Awareness:** Problem-aware. They feel all four symptoms. They haven't linked them
  to one cause.
- **Belief this ad works:** VALIDATES "it's not me, I'm doing everything right and it
  still feels like this." CHALLENGES "more clients will fix it."
- **Concept (flat):** The four things that wear an agency owner down all come from one
  cause: being paid a retainer as a vendor. Getting paid on performance from a
  business's old leads removes the cause.
  - **Concept type: The Real Cause** (Problem-Aware list, the test-first concept at
    this level).
  - Note: the opening symptom run is a Warning List, which sits on the Unaware list.
    It's the front door of the format, not the concept. The concept is the cause.
- **Angle: Warning Sign.**
- **Hypothesis:** Only 5 of 75 live ads cast the burnt-out agency owner (2026-08-24
  audit, gap #3), and none use a symptom-checklist structure. This structure has run
  on RYZE's page for ~918 days with 10K reactions / 9.2K comments / 13K shares. Bet:
  self-diagnosis gets the agency owner to qualify themselves in the first 9 seconds,
  where our talking-head opens ask them to listen first.
- **Mechanism:** AI + SMS works a business's old, dead lead database. No upfront fee.
  Split the profit.
- **Problem name:** two slots, both locked (Joey, 2026-10-05).
  - Condition on the hook card (the RYZE "high blood sugar" slot): **Agency burnout.**
    A condition they already know. Frame 0 reads "Agency burnout RED flags." with
    three flags.
  - Culprit named at the cause beat (the RYZE "insulin resistance" slot): **The Vendor
    Leash**, the existing Flexxable handle for the retainer trap.
  - **Binary:** Vendor vs Partner (shared with the 2026-08-24 batch).
- **The Image:** **LOCKED, the wrong column** (Joey, 2026-10-05). A client's books
  with two columns, COSTS and SALES. The agency is a line in COSTS, under Rent and
  Software, with a red flag stuck in it. After: the same line has moved to SALES,
  marked as a split.
  - It appears twice: BEFORE at the cause beat (replaces RYZE's organ animation),
    AFTER at the mechanism beat.
  - Frame 0 object: a phone lighting up with a client message at 9pm
    (from [[BURNOUT_BurntAgencyOwner_Recognition_9pmMessage_VIDEO_v01]]).
- **Spokesperson:** Dan. Archetype: **the reformed insider.** He ran lead gen for
  clients, changed the model, and makes more from one than he did from ten. Per
  [[dan-truth-constraint]], the symptoms are put to the viewer as red flags, not
  claimed as Dan's diary. First-person lines stay inside what the winning ads already
  say.
- **CTA:** Client default. $14.40 book + bonuses + 30-day keep-everything guarantee →
  `book.flexxable.com/optin`.
- **Format:** Video, 9:16, about 1:45 (the written script runs longer at Dan's pace, see the runtime table in the copy file). Red Flag structure, 11 beats (beat map in the
  swipe note). Captions on every word.
  - **Symptom run and the wrong-column diagram are animated, built in-house** (Joey,
    2026-10-05). Dan is on camera as talking head from beat 3. So the script has two
    parts: animation captions for beats 1, 2, 4 and 7, and Dan's lines to camera for

- **Source route:** `swipe` → Mad-Lib in /produce. Near-same length, same beat order,
  swap the specifics.
  OG: https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/985013383630734
- **Guidelines:**
  - Pain-led opener is an opt-in for this ad (Joey asked for the template). The usual
    curiosity-first rule is parked here on purpose.
  - Symptoms are shown, not said. Four of them, about 2 seconds each, new setting each

  - The result beat answers the four symptoms line for line.
  - Cut the cause beat to about 12 seconds. RYZE's runs 17 and sags.
  - No income promise at the stakes beat. The stakes are what the model does to you,

  - Video rules apply ([[2026-10-03-video-ad-rules]]): frame 0 already shows the hook

## One-breath test

*A burnt-out agency owner counts off four agency-burnout red flags and finds they all
come from one cause, the Vendor Leash: on a retainer you're a line in the client's
COSTS column, so ten clients is ten bosses. Dan was on that leash until he copied how
hedge fund managers get paid and moved himself into the SALES column, working a
business's dead leads with AI and SMS for a split. That's the $14.40 book, and you
keep it even if you ask for your money back.*

Close to a full lock. The flags, the cause and the fix all live on the one ledger
picture, and the hedge fund discovery explains the move between columns.

Loosest point: three metaphors are in play (red flags, a leash, a ledger). The flag
stuck in the COSTS line ties the first and third together. The leash is a name only
and never gets drawn. Flagged, not fixed. If it reads crowded in the script, the
culprit can be named plainly at beat 4 and the Vendor Leash handle dropped.
