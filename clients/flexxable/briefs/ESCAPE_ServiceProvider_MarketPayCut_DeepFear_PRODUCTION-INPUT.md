# Production instruction — AI does not have to replace you to take your money

Write one complete long-form Facebook text ad and 5 Facebook headline options for The Instant AI Agency book.

This replaces the earlier forced hook batch. Do not reuse the crime-scene, knife, blood, biting, guts or clearance-rack metaphors. They made the hook feel written.

## Opening territory

Build the ad around this plain, alarming paradox:

> AI doesn't have to replace you to take your money.

You may sharpen that line, but keep its simplicity and meaning. It must sound like a real market observation, not a copywriter performing violence.

The hook should open one question: **How can AI take my income without replacing me?**

Do not answer that in the first line.

## Reader

Existing agency owners, freelancers and online service providers, especially copywriters, funnel builders, SMMA managers and SEO providers.

They still have useful skills and may still be winning work, but they feel that new projects are harder to win at the old rates.

## Argument spine

1. AI does not need to replace the provider to hurt their income.
2. The same kind of brief can now arrive with a much thinner budget, even though the buyer still expects the finished work to perform.
3. The buyer sees AI make output quickly and assumes the output should now cost much less.
4. The provider still carries the judgement, revisions, responsibility and blame when the work does not perform.
5. That creates the real squeeze: more expected, less paid.
6. Most providers respond by explaining their value harder, packing more into the retainer or trying to lock clients into upfront fees.
7. The market is pushing back on that deal. Do not fight the buyer over what a piece of output is worth. Change what the client pays for.
8. Stop selling output and sell measurable sales.
9. The Instant AI Agency model: offer to reactivate a business's old leads using AI + SMS. The business has already paid for those leads and largely written them off.
10. Charge on performance, taking 30-50% of the sales generated. The client pays when the client gets paid, so there is no upfront retainer to defend.
11. A buyer can haggle over what a funnel, campaign or batch of copy is worth. It is much harder to dismiss a sale that has actually landed in the till.
12. This is how the service provider becomes AI-proof: not by trying to beat AI at making more output, but by using AI to create money and taking a cut of the result.
13. Transition into The Instant AI Agency book as the step-by-step blueprint.
14. Offer: $14.40, bonuses, and a 30-day money-back guarantee where the buyer keeps the book either way.
15. CTA to `book.flexxable.com/optin`.

## Truth and proof constraints

- Do not use Joey's private funnel-build numbers.
- Do not invent any percentage decline, market statistic, client quote, personal Dan story or universal claim.
- You may use 30-50% only for the verified performance split.
- Do not use student proof unless it is necessary. This ad should sell through recognition and the offer contrast, not bolt on a proof stack.
- Do not claim nobody can lowball a performance deal. Say the provider no longer has to defend an upfront output fee, or that the client pays from sales created.
- Keep Dan's first-person history out unless it is verified in the supplied primer. The market diagnosis can be world-level and second-person.

## Voice and shape

- Dan texting a mate who owns a business: casual, direct, Aussie-inflected.
- Short paragraphs, one sentence per line.
- Use ellipses only where the thought genuinely trails.
- No tidy AI antithesis stacks, no fake three-beat cadence and no corporate nouns.
- Do not over-metaphorise. The concrete images are an incoming brief with a thinner budget, a retainer being haggled over and a sale landing in the till.
- Pain amplifies after the hook; the opening itself is a novel market truth.
- Make the pivot feel like relief: **stop fighting over the price of output and change what the client pays for.**
- Length target: roughly 500-750 words, but stop when the argument is sold.

## Required output

1. Primary text, starting with the hook.
2. Exactly 5 headline options after the ad.
