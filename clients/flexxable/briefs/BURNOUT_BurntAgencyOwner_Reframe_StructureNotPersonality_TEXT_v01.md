# BURNOUT_BurntAgencyOwner_Reframe_StructureNotPersonality_TEXT_v01

One of 8 briefs filling Diversity Decoder gap #1. **Revised 2026-08-24 (post-amplification
pass):** the original single U6 seed hook is superseded by 4 locked hook variants, all
built off the same "retainer is the secret villain" reframe (amplified against the
Genesis Hook Amplification doc's sunscreen-batch templates — trust token, crime verb,
clock, stop-at-the-punch). One shared body, four interchangeable openers — standard
hook-test structure, Joey's explicit call.

## Locked hooks (4, all bolt onto the same body)

1. "The retainer that's SUPPOSED to protect your income... is the exact thing keeping you on call after midnight."
2. "That 'stable' retainer is actually DRAINING your evenings, one 9pm reply at a time… like a slow puncture you keep pumping instead of replacing."
3. "Every invoice you send is a little more of your evenings signed away - and you call it 'good client service'."
4. "Your retainer feels like security on the 1st of the month and starts OWNING your weekends by the 15th."

## DNA

- **Segment:** Segment 1, the Burned-Out Agency Owner.
- **Awareness:** Unaware. No AI/product/mechanism mention in any hook.
- **Belief this ad challenges:** "Recurring retainer revenue is the safe, good outcome
  every agency should want." Replaces it with: the retainer itself is the trap — the
  trust token that's secretly the crime (same structure as the sunscreen doc's "the
  protective thing is the harm").
- **Concept (flat):** A retainer that looks like stable income is actually what keeps an
  agency owner reachable and working nights/weekends — the "protection" and the "harm"
  are the same mechanism.
- **Angle:** 2nd-person reframe / trust-token inversion. All 4 hooks are 2nd/3rd-person
  ("your retainer," "you"), not Dan "I" confessions — sidesteps the tense-correction issue
  by construction, not just by convention.
- **Mechanism:** AI + SMS revives a business's dead/old lead database, performance-only
  (30–50% rev share). Withheld in all 4 hooks — Unaware.
- **Problem name:** **"The Vendor Leash"** (shared, see sibling briefs). Retainer = the
  concrete OBJECT that carries the leash; "Vendor Leash" is the coined NAME for the
  pattern once it's stated outright — same relationship as sunscreen (object) to "fake
  gold" (coined crime name) in the source doc's Climb example.
  - **Binary:** Vendor vs. **Partner** (shared across batch).
- **The Image:** A leash clipped from a smartphone to a person's ankle. All 4 hooks are
  pure statement/reframe with no scene of their own, so the image carries the visual load
  in the static execution.
- **Spokesperson:** Dan. Archetype: **the reformed insider**. All 4 hooks are 2nd/3rd
  person by design, so [[dan-truth-constraint]]'s tense-correction doesn't apply to the
  hooks themselves — but the body, once it turns to Dan's own experience of the retainer
  trap, must stay past/resolved.
- **CTA:** $14.40 book + bonuses + 30-day guarantee → `book.flexxable.com/optin`.
- **Format:** Static — bold-typography card OR Notes-app style (either fits; Joey's call
  in creative). Given 4 hook variants, this is a natural hook-test: same body, same image,
  4 static executions differing only in the opening line.
- **Source route:** Regular path (hook bot → body → headlines) — with an amplification
  pass on top, sourced from Joey's Genesis Hook Amplification training doc. Not a swipe,
  but the sunscreen-batch lines were used as literal structural templates line-for-line —
  worth banking that doc as a primer for future hook passes (flag for /reflect).
- **Guidelines:**
  - Write ONE body that works after any of the 4 hooks — don't write four separate
    bodies. The body should assume the reader just had the retainer-as-villain reversal
    land, and continue from there.
  - Body should transition from the 2nd-person reframe into Dan's own past-tense
    experience of the same retainer trap — don't let it stay abstract the whole way
    through.
  - No mechanism/AI mention until well past the reframe.
  - Keep the crime-verb / trust-token structure alive into the body where natural
    (DRAINING, OWNING, BLEEDING) rather than dropping into generic language once the
    hook's done its job.
