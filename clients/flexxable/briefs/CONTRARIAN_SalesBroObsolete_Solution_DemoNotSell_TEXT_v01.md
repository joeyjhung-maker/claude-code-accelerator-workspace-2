# CONTRARIAN_SalesBroObsolete_Solution_DemoNotSell_TEXT_v01

Controversial/us-vs-them variant of the "you don't need to be a sales bro"
weekly angle. Sibling of the other 3 sales-bro entry points from this week's
run (declaration, confession-stat, old-plan-expose) — this is Dan's own add,
explicitly asking to "hit harder... we ARE better than them."

## DNA
- **Segment:** Cross-segment — anyone AI-agency-curious (Segment 1-4, but
  hits hardest for the self-doubting "I'm not a salesperson" reader — closest
  to Cautious Carl / the broad opportunity-seeker who's ruled themselves out
  because they think closing = being good at sales).
- **Awareness:** Problem → solution. The reader already knows AI agencies
  exist; the ad turns the belief "you need sales skills to close" via a
  belief-callout hook, then reveals the mechanism.
- **Concept (flat):** Nobody needs to be a "sales bro" to close AI clients,
  because the close isn't a sales call at all — it's a demo. You still get
  on a call. You just don't sell on it. The AI/mechanism does the selling by
  showing itself working live.
- **Angle:** Contrarian / controversial, us-vs-them. NOT pain-led. Calls out
  the "sales bro" identity/skillset as the thing that's become irrelevant —
  outcome claim ("tech guys are closing more"), not a blanket insult of
  salespeople as people. See guideline 3.
- **Mechanism:** Demo-led, risk-free commission-only test (ROYA way, per
  Travis). Old Plan (huge promises, compete with 5 agencies, proposals/bids/
  negotiation gauntlet/4 Zoom calls, maybe get the deal, mountain of work to
  deliver, needs years of credibility) vs New Plan (demo first, risk-free
  test, commission only, no upfront, results sell it, they ask you for more
  work after). Do not use Travis's or Dan's exact private wording verbatim
  (that transcript is internal strategy talk, not cleared copy) — paraphrase
  the SHAPE of the mechanism in Dan's own voice.
- **Problem name:** none locked yet — working label "the Sales Bro Trap" (the
  belief that closing AI clients requires being a natural salesperson). Not
  a hard requirement for this ad; don't force it if it doesn't fit naturally.
- **The Image:** N/A — text ad only, no static/video brief attached.
- **Spokesperson:** Dan, mostly second-person/observational per
  [[dan-truth-constraint]]. The one first-person-safe proof point is Harry
  (below) — do not invent any new personal Dan backstory about sales or being
  a "sales bro" himself.
- **CTA:** Client default — The Instant AI Agency book, $14.40
  ([[flexxable-strategy-map]] line 8). Top-of-funnel, no VSL, no retargeting.
- **Format:** Text ad (Meta primary text + headline). **UPDATED 21 Jul:** this
  version is being recorded by Dan as a voiceover/talking-head ad, capped at
  ~2 minutes spoken — hard limit **200 words max** for the body. Write for
  the ear (spoken cadence), not just the eye — short lines still apply, but
  cut every beat that isn't load-bearing. Keep the hook verbatim, keep Harry
  as the only proof point, keep the CTA shape.
- **Guidelines:**
  1. **Hook is LOCKED, organic source route — use verbatim, do not regenerate:**
     "The highest-paid people in AI right now have never sold anything on a call."
  2. Resolve the "do they get on calls at all?" ambiguity FAST — within the
     first 2-3 lines after the hook — or it reads as a bait-and-switch once
     the demo/call mechanism shows up later. Something in the shape of "they
     still get on calls, they just don't sell on them" needs to land early.
  3. Superiority claim stays on the OUTCOME (tech guys / non-salespeople are
     closing more, right now), never a blanket insult of salespeople as
     people. Keep it "we found a better way," not "sales bros are bad."
  4. No swearing, no censored profanity (f**k/s**t etc.) — Meta ad-policy
