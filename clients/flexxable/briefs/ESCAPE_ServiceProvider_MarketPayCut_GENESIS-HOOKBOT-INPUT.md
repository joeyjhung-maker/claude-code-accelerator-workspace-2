# Genesis Ad Hook Bot 1 input — alternate hooks for one fixed ad

## The task

Write your native batch of **10 candidate hooks**, then rank the strongest three.

These are replacement openings for one existing Facebook video ad.

They are not ten different ad ideas.

After judging, five finalists will be selected for the user to narrow to two tests.

## Replacement boundary

Each hook replaces the ad's full current opening:

> Your clients aren't paying you less because your work got worse.
>
> They're paying you less because AI made the OUTPUT feel cheaper to them… even when you're still the one doing the thinking.

The unchanged shared body begins immediately after the new hook:

> Budgets shrinking, scope creeping, and more briefs landing with that little sting of "can't AI just do most of this now?"

Every hook must flow naturally into that exact body line.

## Product

The Instant AI Agency book, priced at $14.40.

It teaches agency owners and online service providers to stop selling output on upfront retainers and instead use AI + SMS to reactivate a business's old leads, create measurable sales and get paid from the results.

Do not mention the book, mechanism, old leads, SMS, performance pay, price or solution in the hook.

## Target reader

Existing agency owners, freelancers and online service providers, especially copywriters, funnel builders, SMMA managers and SEO providers.

They fear AI will replace them.

The immediate wound is subtler: even when buyers still hire them, AI has lowered what new buyers expect skilled output to cost.

## Locked idea

AI does not need to replace a service provider to cut their income.

The going price can be lower before the provider ever quotes, even while the provider still carries the judgement, revisions, responsibility and blame.

This ad focuses on **new prospects, incoming briefs and new pitches**.

It is not an existing-client renewal or discount ad.

## Human moment

A new brief lands.

The buyer still wants skilled work and a commercial result, but the budget already feels thinner before the provider has named a price.

## Open loop

Each hook should leave one question burning:

> How did AI cut what new buyers expect to pay me without replacing me?

Show the consequence and withhold the explanation.

The next body line reveals the lived symptoms.

## Hook requirements

- One or two spoken lines, ideally 8-24 words.
- All hooks must attach to the exact same unchanged body.
- Consequence before mechanism.
- Aim for `wait… WHAT?`, but sound like a real market observation rather than a copywriter performing violence.
- Use blunt, simple language.
- Vary the entrance: paradox, contrarian belief, new-brief moment, direct call-out, emotional threat and curiosity.
- Do not merely paraphrase the control hook.
- Do not explain that AI made output faster, cheaper or easier inside the hook.
- Do not use crime-scene, knife, blood, biting, guts, robbery or clearance-rack metaphors.
- Do not drift into existing clients, renewals, discounts, fuller calendars or workload multiplication.
- Do not invent a result, statistic, exact decline, timeframe, quote, study or Dan backstory.
- Do not claim every buyer or provider behaves this way.
- Do not write body copy, headlines or CTA.

## Existing control — do not paraphrase

> Your clients aren't paying you less because your work got worse.
