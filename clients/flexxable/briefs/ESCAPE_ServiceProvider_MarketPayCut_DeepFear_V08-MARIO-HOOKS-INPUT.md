# Hooks only — 15 alternates for a finished, locked ad

The ad at the bottom is finished and locked. Dan Wardrope, spoken to camera, Facebook video, selling The Instant AI Agency book.

Write **15 alternate hooks** to test against the control. Hooks only. Do not rewrite any body line.

Each hook replaces the two control lines and hands straight into the first body line, which does not change:

> The same work you used to charge $5K for…
> Is now “worth” $2K.
> Not because your skills got worse…
> But because clients now think:
> “Can AI do this cheaper?”

Say your hook out loud, then say those five lines. If there is a bump, the hook fails.

## The control (do not rewrite it, do not return near-copies)

> AI doesn’t have to replace you to take your money.
> It just has to make your work look like a five-minute job.

## Already written and off the table (do not repeat or paraphrase)

- Nobody told you your rate dropped… they just stopped saying yes to the old one.
- Your best client is about to haggle you down… and they won't even feel bad about it.
- You sent the same quote you always send, and for the first time the client said "let me think about it."

## Who is watching

Agency owners, freelancers, copywriters, funnel builders, SMMA managers, SEO people. They sell skilled work online.

They are braced for AI to replace them one day. What is actually happening is quieter and already here: the same job now gets a smaller budget, and they are blaming one cheap client at a time.

Their belief: "If my work is good and I explain the value well enough, I keep my old rate."

## What a hook has to do here

The hook is most of the ad. Too polite is a FAIL.

1. **The worst true thing.** Not the loudest claim. The worst TRUE one, in the most charged true wording.
2. **Their moment.** A scene from their own week they can see: the quote going out, the reply, the invoice, the discovery call, the client's face.
3. **A face on it.** Who is doing it to them, or what it's called.
4. **One itch.** They must be left holding ONE question, and the body answers it. Give A and Z, hide B through Y.

They should think "wait… WHAT?" and need the next line.

The body is what explains WHY (clients now think AI can do it cheaper). So the hook must NOT say that. A hook that explains its own twist is dead.

## What killed the last batch (do not do these)

- Blaming a cheaper rival or another agency undercutting them. The problem is in the CLIENT's head, not the competition.
- Invented timeframes and numbers ("signed in a day", "two weeks", "three other quotes", "half the price").
- Invented Dan claims ("every agency owner I know", "I've watched a thousand…").
- Giving away the body's line "Can AI do this cheaper?".
- Repeating the body's "not because your skills got worse" in the hook.
- Tidy "you used to be X… now you're Y" flips and op-ed sentences about "the game changing".
- Non-payment and ghosted invoices. That is a different problem.

## Shape

- One or two short spoken lines. 25 words at the very most across both.
- A statement. Never open on a question.
- Tradie words. Blunt, physical, Germanic: pay, cut, cheap, broke, sweat, haggle, quote, invoice, bill. Not "commoditised", "margin", "perceived value".
- It has to sound like Dan saying something true to a mate, not like an ad.

## Spread

I need different doors, not 15 versions of one move. Tag each hook with its vehicle and give me at least three hooks in each of these:

- **Call-Out** — names their exact situation
- **Emotion** — a felt moment or a confession-shaped line (second person, not a Dan story)
- **Curiosity** — withholds, promises a reveal
- **Urgency** — a clock already running
- **Contrarian** — attacks a belief they trust (their own "explain the value" belief is fair game), but NOT in the control's "AI doesn't have to X to Y" shape

## Truth rules

- No numbers at all in the hook. The $5K and $2K live in the body.
- No Dan backstory, no student proof, no testimonial.
- Do not claim every client or every freelancer.
- Do not mention old leads, SMS, the book or the offer.
- No crime-scene, knife, blood, guts or clearance-rack imagery.

## Output

Numbered 1 to 15. A two-line hook stays on one number with " / " between the lines. Vehicle tag in square brackets at the end. No commentary, no preamble.

## The locked ad, for context

AI doesn’t have to replace you to take your money.

It just has to make your work look like a five-minute job.

The same work you used to charge $5K for…

Is now “worth” $2K.

Not because your skills got worse…

But because clients now think:

“Can AI do this cheaper?”

So here’s how to flip that:

The people crushing it in the new AI economy aren’t trying to COMPETE with AI…

They’re simply charging for RESULTS, not output.

I’ve taught over 1,000 everyday guys and gals how to do this - then use AI to handle the work.

And it’s stupidly simple.

All we do is find a business with old leads.

Then ask:

“Hey Mr Business Owner, if I could turn those old leads into fresh sales… would you give me a cut of the profits?”

No upfront payment.

ZERO risk for them.

That’s why it’s so easy to say yes.

And because you’re paid on results, the upside can be huge.
