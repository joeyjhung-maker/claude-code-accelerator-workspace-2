# Flexxable testimonial-video copy lessons

## Joey correction — 2026-09-07

- For testimonial openings, make the first proof point a mini-story: specific outcome, then the apparent disadvantage or avoided work, then the simple action that produced the result.
- Keep three distinct proof-led hooks feeding one unchanged body so Dan films the shared section once.
- In the shared stack, use named results with a concrete contrast (for example, revenue versus profit) before revealing the common system.
- State the deal mechanics plainly: old leads, no upfront cost, then a 30–50% revenue split.
- Describe the bot as doing the lead work while the operator collects monthly commissions; pair it with the explicit release from ads, funnels, and coding.
- Close the capability objection directly: it can work for someone who knows nothing about AI or sales, anchored to named proof rather than a generic reassurance.
