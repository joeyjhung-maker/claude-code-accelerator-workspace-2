## The client

- 450K people in their database.
- Sells a $1,500 one-time-purchase product.
- Spends about $6M a year on ads (Jack's comment).
- Jack's fractional CMO side brings in 15K+ new leads a month (post) / 500 to 800 new sales leads a day (comment). Channels: TikTok, Meta, Google, Bing, CTV, LinkedIn.
- Leads who don't buy get folded into the DR campaign after a short sales cool-down, with a $300 off offer.
- It took 5 to 6 months to convince the client "they had gold in their database."

## The deal

- Old: $5K/mo management fee for the DR campaign.
- New: 10% of gross sales on the DR portion. "Agreement amendment in the works."
- Client pays for the SMS: about 4,000 net new SMS a day plus follow-up backlog.
- Jack's cost: about $2K to $2.5K a month, blended human and AI.
- Jack's forecast: $250K/mo average for the DR campaign, netting him about $22K/mo profit, "if the sales team can keep up performance."
