# Dan's reels: what separates the winners from the flops

*2026-10-14. 349 Instagram reels from Jan 2025 to Oct 2026, exported from Business Suite. The top 15 and bottom 10 by views against Dan's normal were watched and broken down with Gemini. Average watch time was pulled per reel from Business Suite.*

Related: [[Flexxable]] [[hooks]] [[pain-is-amplifier-not-opener]]

Data: `data/ig-ranked-2025-01_2026-10.csv` (every reel, ranked). Teardowns: `teardowns/T01.json` to `T15.json` (top) and `B01.json` to `B10.json` (bottom), each with the full transcript.

## Boosting check

No sign of paid views on the 21 Sep 2026 reel or the 23 Sep 2025 reel. Neither post's insights show any ad views. Treat the numbers as organic.

## The account in one line

Posting went from about 5 reels a month (2025) to about 30 a month (2026). Typical views per reel fell from 1,000–3,000 to about 400.

## What the top 15 have that the bottom 10 don't

**1. Something to look at in the first frame.**
9 of the top 15 open on an object in Dan's hand or a graphic: the locked bottle (5 reels), a tier-list graphic, a basketball, a "Payment Received" notification.
0 of the bottom 10 do. They open on Dan with his hands clasped, or on generic b-roll.

**2. The opening text names a thing or a scenario, not a principle.**
- Winners: "NOBODY IGNORES A BOTTLE THEY CAN'T OPEN" · "SKILLS TO LEARN IN 2026" · "SO WHAT BUSINESS WOULD YOU START IF YOU HAD UNLIMITED FUNDS?"
- Flops: "SUCCESSFUL AGENCIES ALWAYS BEGIN WITH A VERY PAINFUL PROBLEM" · "CHASING AI TOOLS GUARANTEES AGENCY FAILURE" · "3 CHECKPOINTS BEFORE YOU PITCH"

**3. People stay twice as long.**
Most of the top reels hold 12–20 seconds on average. Six of the bottom 10 hold 5–9 seconds.

## The cleanest test in the data: the locked bottle

The same idea was posted six times.

- With the bottle in frame 0, all five beat Dan's normal.
- The one version without it was one of the worst reels on the account. It also opens on pain ("you're getting ignored on cold email…") before showing the bottle.
- T01 and T08 are nearly the same script, word for word, yet one did 51,000 views and the other 1,500. The formula gets above normal reliably; the size of the hit is partly luck. Don't promise anyone 51K.

## Other formats that work for Dan

- **Tier list** ("rank these", "skills to learn in 2026"): 7.6x and 9.2x, with the graphic on screen from frame 0. A spoken list without a graphic ("3 checkpoints") flopped.
- **"What would you do if…" scenario** ("unlimited funds", "Hormozi's $1,000", "just a laptop and Wi-Fi"): 2x to 8x. The same clip was posted twice and beat normal both times.

## What doesn't work

- **Business-model maxims to camera.** Every one in the bottom 10 is a principle about how to run an AI agency, said by Dan sitting or standing with nothing in frame. They consistently land at 0.4–0.6x.
- **A "Comment KEYWORD" CTA doesn't fix reach.** Three of the bottom 10 had one. Keep keyword CTAs for leads (in 2025 they drove 35–50 comments and 10–26 follows per reel), but they don't rescue a weak opening.

## Dan's principles so far (v0, from his own numbers)

1. Frame 0 shows a thing: an object in hand, a graphic, a screen. Never just Dan.
2. The opening text names that thing or a scenario. No principles or maxims.
3. Show the object before any pain.
4. Re-run the bottle. It has beaten normal 5 times out of 5 when it's in frame 0. Vary the story around it (a new business owner, the call that came in, the letter, the result), not the opening.
5. Tier lists and "what would you do if" scenarios are the second and third formats.
6. Drop the maxim reels.

## Limits

- 25 reels from one account. These are patterns to test, not laws.
- Gemini's own verdicts were ignored: it rated the flops as "highly effective". Only what it observed was used (frame 0, opening text and line, format, cuts, CTA), checked against the real numbers.
- No skip rate or retention curve. Average watch time is the only retention number available.
