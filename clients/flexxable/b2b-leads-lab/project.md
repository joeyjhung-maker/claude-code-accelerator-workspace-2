**Mechanism to own: "AI Enriched Lumpy Mail."** That phrase is the whole thing — physical gift as the pattern interrupt, AI as the enrichment/personalisation layer. Everything upstream sells that mechanism.

Year Wealth Shortcut" naming pattern, but flipped — names the NEW fast time-to-execute rather than
the old slow cost being escaped. **Verified fact: 4 hours is Joey's real time-to-execute for one

**Avatar / TAM (2026-08-20, Joey's correction): anyone whose job is landing meetings with
high-value prospects — NOT just agency owners or people trying to quit a job.** This includes

employed 9-5ers who'd use this to hit their own numbers, not escape anything. Very big TAM.

## Dallas Mastermind — pre-event "guinea pig" strategy (2026-08-26, from a call/voice note, source unconfirmed)

*Transcribed from a voice note Joey shared — speaker not confirmed (reads like Dan riffing, not
verified). Bank the STRATEGY, treat the specific wording as paraphrase, not verbatim Dan quote.*

**The idea:** sell out the Dallas 2-Day Mastermind over the next 7-10 days. Once someone buys a
ticket, reach out immediately (before the event) with "pre-event goodies" — the promise being
they can land a client before their plane even leaves the tarmac. Ticket buyers become
"guinea pigs" for a fast, simplified version of the system:

- **A short-form letter with a QR code linking to a calendar booking page** — NOT the full
  Dream 100 + AI research + long letter system used elsewhere in B2B Leads Lab. Sourced from
  "what Johnny said his CRM friend is doing." Distinct, faster mechanism, specific to this
  pre-event push.
- UK buyers get this directly; US buyers get offered UK/US client access if they want to start
  now too (detail thin — worth confirming exact US mechanics before building anything).

**Why:** Travis apparently told Dan that a past event of his (Big Ticket Email Mojo) failed to
give pre-workshop goodies, so attendees arrived without a specific, personal problem to dig into
during the event — e.g. someone shows up with coffee dates already booked but no idea what to
SAY on the call. Pre-event goodies manufacture that specific problem on purpose, so the live
event has real material to work with instead of teaching in the abstract.
