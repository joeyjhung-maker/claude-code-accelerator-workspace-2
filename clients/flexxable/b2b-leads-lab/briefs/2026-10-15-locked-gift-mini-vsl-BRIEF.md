## The job

Write the SPOKEN SCRIPT for a 10 to 15 minute video sales letter.

Dan Wardrope reads it to camera, holding the locked bottle. An editor cuts it later and adds the visuals. You write the words only.

It is the video version of Dan's free 5-part written guide, "How We're Getting CEOs Of $20M-$80M Companies To Call US… For Just $40 Bucks… Using LOCKED GIFTS!" The full guide is pasted at the bottom. It is the source of truth. The VSL tells the same story in the same order, in Dan's voice, for the ear.

You are NOT inventing a new pitch. You are turning a signed-off written guide into something that sounds great spoken out loud, and cutting it to length.

## Length

1,800 to 2,000 spoken words. Hard cap 2,100. Read at about 150 words a minute, that is 12 to 13 minutes.

The guide is longer than that. Cut by tightening, not by dropping beats. The 14-item "everything I've tried" list gets SAID as one or two quick lines (the editor puts the full list on screen).

## Output format

First, give me 4 alternative OPENINGS. Each is the first 20 to 30 seconds (50 to 75 words). Label them O1 to O4.

- O1: lead with the 10 sent, 6 calls, 3 closed result and "we're getting more clients than most people get replies".
- O2: lead with Dan holding up the locked bottle: what it is, what it does to the person who gets it.
- O3: lead with Mateusz: 14 bottles, 4 coffee dates with $20M to $80M a year businesses, for $40 each.
- O4: your own best idea. Surprise me.

The very first line of every opening must be a statement, not a question. Max 14 words. It must work as the first line on screen at 0:00, over Dan holding the bottle.

Then write the FULL SCRIPT using the opening you think is strongest.

Mark sections with a plain label line in square brackets. These are not spoken:
[OPENING] [WHO I AM] [THE PROBLEM] [THE BOTTLE] [THE TEST] [THE LETTER] [THE RESULTS] [THE 3 PARTS] [OBJECTIONS] [THE 6 STEPS] [THE INVITE]

You may add a very short editor note in square brackets where something should be SHOWN, for example [SHOW: the 14 channels list] or [SHOW: 10 / 6 / 3]. Max 10 of these in the whole script. Never put a note in the middle of a sentence.

## Hard rules

- The $20M to $80M businesses are MATEUSZ'S calls. Dan's own first 10 went to UK indoor adventure parks. Never say Dan's first 10 or his 70 went to $20M to $80M CEOs.
- Never give the wording of the letter. Say what it does and the questions it answers. The exact letters are inside the community.
- No invented results, numbers, names, scenes or backstory for Dan. Nothing Dan didn't describe in the guide. A hypothetical must be clearly a hypothetical ("imagine you're…").
- The "what everyone else does" side is a picture, never a statistic.
- Do not open on a question, on pain, or on a testimonial.
- No three abstract benefits in a row.
- Banned words: leverage (except inside the mock bad pitch in Step 6), solution, deliverable, onboarding (onboarded is fine, it's Dan's word for clients), streamline (except inside the mock bad pitch), game-changer, unlock your potential, supercharge.
- No fake urgency, no deadline, no price for anything. Everything offered is free.

## Write for the EAR

- This is SPOKEN. Dan says every word out loud to camera. If it only works on a page, cut it.
- One sentence per line. Blank line between each.
- But NOT machine-gun fragments. Mix short lines with fuller spoken sentences. Never three or more lines in a row under six words, except one deliberate punch moment.
- CAPS on the words Dan would stress out loud. Ellipses for pauses.
- No emojis, no ":-)" (it can't be said), no bullet points, no headlines, no "TAP HERE", no "next page", no "part 3 of 5". It's one video.
- Swap page talk for video talk: "read that again" becomes "let that sink in"; "see below" becomes "the link's under this video".
- Dan is an Aussie living in the UK. Casual, cheeky, direct. Like he's telling a mate who owns a business. "Yo yo!! Dan here" can open the WHO I AM section. "bloody", "mate", "peeps", "luuuuv" are all fine. "ya" at most four times in the whole script.
- Keep his jokes from the guide: catnip for marketers, INBOX ARMAGEDDON, I blame Hormozi, desperate side chick, a very expensive mug, the bugger got me, business owners' strange obsession with combination locks.

## Output

The 4 openings, then the full script. Then one line: the total spoken word count. No other notes.
