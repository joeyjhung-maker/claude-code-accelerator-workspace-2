# Brief: squeeze page for "The Locked Gift Strategy" guide

Related: [[client]] [[cap-equals-cdcdcd]]

Run Step 1 and Step 2 in one go. No HTML.

- **Step 1:** 6 variations. Every one must follow this shape (Joey's direction, 2026-10-12):
  - **Headline = the RESULT.** Lead with what we got, in real numbers.
  - **Subheadline = the PROCESS.** Say plainly how it works, step by step: who we send to, what we send, what they have to do. Do not mention the letter.
- **Step 2:** then write the Full Opt-In Page Text Pack for whichever variation you rate strongest. Copy only.

## Core inputs

- **page_type:** download
- **audience:** Agency owners, consultants and B2B sales people who sell to business owners and want meetings with bigger companies. NOT for people who want to hide behind a laptop and never pick up the phone.
- **traffic_sources:** Paid social video ads and Dan's own short videos, where he holds up a bottle of whisky with a lock on the cap. Mostly strangers, some followers. Mostly mobile.
- **relationship_context:** Mostly strangers from ads. They have just seen the locked bottle, so they know there is a locked gift. They do not know why it works or what the results are.

## The lead magnet (page_type = download)

- **D_asset_title:** The Locked Gift Strategy
- **D_benefit:** Get the decision makers at the companies you most want as clients to call YOU, for about $40 a package.
- **D_format:** Free guide in 5 short parts, plus free access to a 350M+ contact lead database.
- **D_size:** 5 parts
- **D_author:** Dan Wardrope. He has spent 7 figures+ on advertising directly to business owners and coaches 1,288+ students on getting in front of B2B businesses.
- **D_takeaways:**
  1. Why the gift is NOT the clever bit, and the one thing in every package that actually gets them to call.
  2. How a bottle of champagne Dan couldn't open got him to phone a stranger inside 3 minutes.
  3. What happened with our first 10 sends (6 calls booked, 3 clients closed).
  4. "But I can't send booze": the non-alcohol gifts we're testing.
  5. The $40 maths: why this costs more than an email and why that helps you.
  6. The 6-step playbook, in the order we run it: niche, Dream 100, data, gift, letter, follow-up.
  7. Free access to our 350M+ contact lead database, to build your list.

## Message to match

The guide's own headline, which the page should echo:

> How We're Getting CEOs Of $20M-$80M Companies To Call US… For Just $40 Bucks… Using "LOCKED GIFTS"!

## Mechanism

A physical gift arrives on their desk. It is locked. They have to call you to get it open. You stop chasing them and they call you.

## Proof you may use (all real, use only these numbers)

- 10 gifts sent. 6 calls booked. 3 clients closed. 60% response rate.
- 20 gifts sent. 7 replies. 35% response rate.
- 70 gifts sent. 25% response rate. 6 new clients.
- A mate of Dan's in the US sent 14 and booked 4 calls with businesses doing $20M to $80M a year.
- A whole package costs around $40.

## Top 2 objections

1. "I can't send alcohol." (You don't have to. The guide covers other gifts.)
2. "Isn't this expensive?" (About $40 a package, against a client worth thousands a month.)

## Frame preferences

- Lead with the result. Do not open on pain. Do not lead with curiosity alone.
- Keep one open question: the gift is not the clever bit, so what is? Do NOT answer it on the page. Do not mention the letter.
- No contrarian-for-the-sake-of-it takes.

## Hard rules

- Use only the facts and numbers above. No invented testimonials, logos, reviews, member counts or stats.
- No fake urgency. No countdown timers, no "only 7 spots left".
- No three abstract benefits in a row.
- No corporate words: leverage, solution, deliverable, onboarding, streamline, unlock your potential, game-changer.
- Do not mention the software name, coupon codes or the community by name. The page only sells the free guide.

## Voice

Dan Wardrope, the same voice as the samples. Aussie, casual, cheeky, direct. Short lines. CAPS on the words he would stress. Ellipses for pauses. He says "Yo yo!!", "bloody", "peeps" and uses :-).

## Mobile above the fold

Headline, one line of proof (10 sent, 6 calls, 3 clients), the email field and the button.
