# Registered office ≠ real office

Real event, keep verbatim: sent a bottle to Tom Wood (owner, Car & Classic) at his registered
office address. Bottle never arrived — turned out that address wasn't a real working office.
Cold call couldn't recover it after the fact.

Angle: verify the address is a real working office before you send, not just what's listed on
Companies House. Ties to Topa's lead score flagging this as a red flag. Also connects to Check 2
of the Dream 100 Scorecard already shipped (right address, not a stale/registered-only one).

Pure value, no pitch, no waitlist/fulfilment/LinkedIn mention.
