# Brief: weekly Topa 350M+ database click-driver (short)

Related: [[b2b-leads-lab-project]] [[2026-10-06-flexxable-topa-350m-database-optin-headlines]]

## DNA
- **Room:** AAA Ninjas, ROYA, Million Dollar AI Deals. NOT B2B Leads Lab.
- **Job:** harvest. Mop up leads for the Topa free-database opt-in. Runs weekly, so it has to hold up when repeated.
- **Tap position:** T3 (direct ask, one link, no hedging).
- **Mechanic:** click-driver. One link to the opt-in page.
- **The Ask:** click the link, create the free Topa plan.
- **Avatar:** agency owners and freelancers in the AI/automation groups who sell to local businesses and B2B. They already know Dan. Some already have lists, some are still hunting.
- **Proof:** none. Do not cite the 250 opt-ins (internal number). No member results.
- **Format:** Skool post, headline + a few lines + link. NO `Dan` sign-off. No P.S.

## Hard shape
- UNDER 60 words including the headline. As short as it can be and still make someone click.
- One link, on its own line, near the end: [OPTIN LINK]
- No comment keyword. The link is the CTA.
- Lead with curiosity or the size of the thing, not pain.

## Facts allowed (verbatim, nothing else)
- 350M+ contacts across 50 million organisations.
- Free Topa plan. Unlimited access to the database.
- Contact info in there: mobiles, emails, addresses.
- Say "Topa". No bracketed explainer.

## Not allowed
- No deadline, no "limited spots", no fake urgency.
- No "verified" / "enriched" accuracy claims.
- No download counts, no coupon code, no prices.
- Nothing that says only members of one group get it. It is free for anyone.
- No claim Dan personally used it for a result.

## Style
Dan, relaxed, Aussie-inflected. Short lines. Ellipses fine. Never "Quick one", "Fair question", "here's the thing".
