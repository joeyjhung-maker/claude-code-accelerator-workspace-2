# Skool brief — The Sunday Dream Five

Room: B2B Leads Lab.

Job: Turn Sunday preparation into one small action that helps members start the week with a deliberate prospecting sequence rather than random outreach.

Tap position: T2. Practical assignment for members already familiar with the Dream 100 idea.

Mechanic: Domino assignment. Reduce the Dream 100 to five prospects and ask members to map four useful touches for each before Monday.

The Ask: Choose five Dream 100 prospects for next week, write down their first four useful touches before contacting anyone, then report back with the work. No promotional CTA.

Avatar: Anyone responsible for landing meetings with high-value B2B prospects. Do not use escape-the-9-to-5 framing.

Proof: None required. This is a planning exercise grounded in the existing Dream 100 and multi-channel follow-up teaching.

Core idea: A Dream 100 can feel too large to act on. A Dream Five makes the next week manageable. Planning the first four touches in advance prevents members from sending one message, hearing nothing and disappearing. Useful touches can include a locked gift, LinkedIn connection or genuine engagement, a relevant case study or idea by email, and a phone call while the sender is still familiar. The exact order can vary, but every touch should add something rather than repeat “just checking in.”

Format: Skool community post from Dan. Maximum 200 words including the headline and sign-off. One sentence per line with varied sentence length. Plain text only with no Markdown bold formatting.

Guidelines:
- Make it feel easy enough to complete on a Sunday, not like homework.
- Use “Dream Five” as the memorable simplification.
- Keep the four-touch examples compact.
- Reinforce that the sequence should be useful and multi-channel, not four repeated nudges.
- Do not mention research statistics; this is the action post following the deeper fourth-touch lesson.
- End with a decisive instruction members can act on immediately and ask them to report back with the completed work.
- Dan voice: direct, cheeky, energetic and practical.

Cross-seed check: No conflict. Pure engagement inside B2B Leads Lab with no product or cross-group promotion.

Seed verbatim: The Sunday “Dream Five” — choose five prospects for next week and map their first four useful touches.

User constraint: Keep it under 200 words.
