# Brief — Dan's YouTube About, modelled on the Think Media swipe

## The swipe (structure only — do NOT reuse its sentences)
Watch our FREE YouTube Masterclass 👇

Think Media is a team of content creators committed to bringing you weekly conversations around building your YouTube channel, personal brand, and business with online video. Over the last 10 years, we've created and refined the #1 YouTube Strategy Course in the world - Video Ranking Academy.

Our mission is to help ONE MILLION purpose-driven people go full-time doing what they love and making an impact through the power of video.

Work with us 🤝

## What each section does (map the job, not the nouns)
1. Line 1: free offer + 👇 — the CTA comes FIRST, because YouTube only shows the opening line before "more".
2. Para 1: who we are + what this channel gives you every week + credibility (years/track record + a flagship thing).
3. Para 2: a big mission statement with a big number, aimed at a type of person and an outcome they want.
4. Last line: a short secondary "Work with us 🤝" CTA.
