# Skool brief — Our offer gets stronger in bad markets

Room: AAA Ninjas.

Job: Reorient members who are absorbing doom-and-gloom headlines and restore conviction that a performance-based AI agency offer becomes more attractive when businesses are risk-averse but still need sales.

Tap position: T1. Broad mindset and opportunity post with a strong rallying close, not a product promotion.

Mechanic: Philosophy stance. Start with the current doomscrolling atmosphere, pivot from fear to commercial logic, then end with a determined identity-level rally.

The Ask: Stay focused, keep building and do not give up. No external CTA or generic engagement question.

Avatar: AAA Ninjas members and Instant AI Agency book buyers who want more clients and may be questioning whether economic uncertainty or AI disruption makes now a bad time to start.

Verified market backdrop:
- The September 2026 OECD outlook says near-term G20 inflation is expected to rise and its assumed gas-price path is around 60% higher than the June prolonged-disruption scenario.
- The IMF describes an uneven global outlook shaped by war and technology, with stalled disinflation and downside uncertainty.
- NOAA’s September outlook gives a 75% chance of a historic El Niño event exceeding the strength of previous events dating back to 1950 during October–December 2026. Do not call it the worst in centuries.
- The ILO’s June 2026 evidence review says large-scale AI job displacement remains limited. Therefore, frame job destruction as a fear/headline, not an established mass outcome.

Verified offer mechanism:
- Flexxable’s locked positioning is not “selling AI”; it is selling “more customers with ZERO risk.”
- The model is performance-based: the business pays on results rather than carrying a standard upfront retainer risk.
- DBR uses the business’s existing lead database to generate sales without requiring fresh ad spend.
- Sources: clients/flexxable/offer-doc-ROYA.md, clients/flexxable/strategy-map.md and clients/flexxable/book-mining-instant-ai-agency-2024.md.

Core argument: Businesses facing uncertainty cut overhead, delay retainers and become more cautious with marketing spend, but they cannot stop needing sales. That makes a credible performance-based offer more attractive: more customers, no upfront spend and payment tied to results. A bad market weakens expensive speculative promises but strengthens a de-risked revenue offer.

AI stance: AI is not the replacement enemy in this argument. Members are combining AI with offer-making, sales and commercial judgment to deliver outcomes faster and capture market share. Do not claim AI can never replace any task or job.

Opportunity stance: Dan believes major fortunes will be built over the next five years by people who understand and act on this shift. Phrase this as conviction or prediction, never a guaranteed income claim. Finish with confidence: this can be a great time, we are positioned to win, and members must not quit because the headlines feel frightening.

Format: Skool community post from Dan. Roughly 250–350 words. Headline, body and Dan sign-off. One sentence per line with varied sentence length. Plain text only with no Markdown bold formatting.

Guidelines:
- Let the opening feel recognisably current: war fears, historic-weather warnings, recession/inflation anxiety, energy prices and AI job fears.
- Do not litigate the news or make political claims; it is the emotional backdrop.
- Do not say recessions are universally good or celebrate real suffering.
- Move quickly from fear to the specific commercial mechanism.
- Clearly explain why the offer strengthens: businesses still need sales, but want less upfront risk and overhead.
- Use Dan’s “more customers with ZERO risk” language.
- Make the post motivating without drifting into empty hustle slogans.
- End with a short rally: use AI, sharpen the offer, keep moving, do not give up.
- Dan voice: direct, cheeky where appropriate, conviction-led and personally reassuring.

Cross-seed check: No conflict. This is native AAA Ninjas philosophy and reinforces the core Instant AI Agency opportunity without redirecting members into B2B Leads Lab.

User steering: There is a lot of doom and gloom right now, but recessions and economic fear strengthen what we do. Businesses cut marketing and overhead but still need sales. We offer more customers with no money down and no risk, using AI plus offer-making skills to capture market share. Millionaires will be made over the next five years by people who understand this. We will win; do not give up.
