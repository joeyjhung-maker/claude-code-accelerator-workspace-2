# Brief: B2B Leads Lab poll, "Have you ever posted something physical to a prospect?"

**Job:** get B2B Leads Lab members to put a hand up and say where they are with physical outreach (locked gifts, bottles, letters), so we can point each one at the right lesson in the free classroom. This is the room's "tiny, specific thing to type": a vote plus one line.

**Why this shape (Joey's own study, 20 Oct):** in B2B Leads Lab handraisers and guess-games get 40 to 135 comments. Teaching NAPs with nothing to reply to get 0 to 3. Cross-posted polls got 0. So this one is written for THIS room, in this room's language, and ends on something easy to answer.

**Avatar:** B2B Leads Lab members. Mixed bag: some have sent a gift, most haven't. BizOp / make-money-online types and small agency owners trying to land B2B clients. They are in the free group and the free classroom. NOT an "escape the 9-to-5" audience. Keep AI automation out of it.

**The idea:** a box on a desk beats another email in a spam folder, and the only way to start is to send ONE. The poll finds out who has, who hasn't, and who's scared.

**Facts allowed (nothing else):**
- B2B Leads Lab teaches sending a locked gift: a physical gift plus a letter to a decision maker (the "lumpy mail" idea).
- The classroom is free for everyone in the group now. It has the Locked Gift Strategy course (niche, Dream 100 list, decision makers, verify the list, the letter, gift and lock).
- The reason to vote: Dan will point each person at the right lesson to start with, based on their answer. (Joey to confirm Dan or the team will actually do this.)
- Dan's own current work is "sending locked gifts" (do not say bottles of whiskey; wine and gifts are fine).
- A gift is cheap to send (the course title says "for just $40 bucks"). Do not claim any result, reply rate, coffee-date count or client win.

**Do NOT:** invent a member story, a stat, or what "most people" do. No price for Luke's training. No mention of the Rainmaker, tiers or prices. No result claims.

**Poll options (Mariobot writes them):** 4 or 5 options. Plain, friendly, mutually exclusive, each one something a real member could honestly pick. Include one funny hot option for the people who do it all the time, and one funny but honest option for the people who've never done it and are nervous. Emoji at the front of each option, like the Offer Goggles poll.

**Reply prompt (Travis shape):** after the poll, a tiny specific thing to type. Something like: vote, then tell me in one line what you sent (or what you WOULD send). ONE ask, not two.

**Hard rules (Joey):**
- Lead with curiosity or the scene, not pain. No student proof opener.
- Headline: start with "POLL:" like Dan's earlier polls, then a loop. No stat stack.
- No filler label lines ("Serious question.", "Quick one", "Real talk", "Listen"). No "Fair enough", "Fair question", "So yeah". No "here's the kicker", "changed the game", "nobody talks about".
- No filler intensifiers (absolutely, genuinely, literally, honestly).
- Dan's voice. Casual, Aussie, max two "ya". One sentence per line. No sign-off. No keyword close. No P.S. unless it earns its place.

**OUTPUT:** Skool post. HL (45 to 70 chars) + body 70 to 140 words + the poll options as a bullet list + the one-line reply prompt. Then 5 alternate headlines, each a different curiosity lens. Label clearly. No commentary.
