# Brief — Dan's Facebook profile post: the champagne in the locked cage

Platform: Dan's personal Facebook profile. NOT a Skool post, NOT an ad. Must read like Dan telling an interesting story to his friends and followers, then almost naturally revealing at the end that this accidental discovery has become his newest business. Not polished, not corporate, not a "launch post."

Length: a BIG post. 350–450 words. One sentence per line, blank line between. Plain text, no markdown, no headline.

## The story — Dan's own facts. Use them, don't embellish, don't add scenes or dialogue he didn't give.

- About four months ago, a very interesting package arrived at Dan's office.
- It was a nice bottle of champagne, but the bottle was locked inside a cage. There was a letter with it.
- Everyone in the office was intrigued. "WTF is this thing? How do we get into it?"
- The letter was from a guy called Jonathan Matthews. He was trying to get Dan's attention.
- Well… it worked. It didn't take Dan long to pick up the phone and call him.
- Jonathan and Dan have since done some really cool business together. But one of the biggest things to come out of it was realising the METHOD he'd used to get Dan's attention was incredibly powerful.
- So they started testing it themselves — sending gifts/bottles to business owners they wanted to work with, to sell their AI automation services.
- The results have been ridiculous.
- Dan has spent multiple seven figures on paid advertising over his career. He's spent basically his entire entrepreneurial career trying to figure out better ways to get conversations with motivated business owners. And for them, nothing has worked like this.
- When they send the right business owner the right gift, with the right message, an extraordinary percentage of them actually WANT to speak to them.
- Instead of chasing someone who doesn't know who they are, they're getting conversations with business owners who are already curious and engaged.
- That discovery has now turned into an entirely new business.
- They've built the infrastructure around it, secured a deal with a UK distribution warehouse that gives capacity to send up to 500 bottles/gifts per week, and they're building out the data/enrichment and outreach side around it.
- They've now launched a FREE Skool community around the bigger idea: Generate B2B Leads On Demand.
- The community is about much more than sending bottles. It's B2B lead generation — finding the businesses you actually want as clients, getting their attention, and creating conversations with people ready to buy.

Optional real proof (verified, use exactly if used): "10 gifts sent. 6 calls booked. 3 closed." / "20 gifts sent. 7 replies."
