## MY PICK AND WHY

**Variation 5** is the strongest for this page.

Here's the thinking: the guide's own headline is the message-match anchor — visitors have just seen Dan hold up a locked bottle in a video, so the headline they already half-know lands like confirmation rather than a cold pitch. It echoes the source creative exactly. The subheadline then does the structural job Joey specified (process, step by step, no mention of the letter). The body keeps the open question alive — *the gift is NOT the clever bit* — without answering it. Proof numbers are present but not front-loaded in a way that makes the headline feel like a stat dump. And the tone is closest to Dan's own voice from the samples.
