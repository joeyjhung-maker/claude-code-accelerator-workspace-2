# Skool brief — Running people's LinkedIn for $200–300 a month

**Room:** B2B Leads Lab (Skool, about 788 members).

**Job:** Nurture / validate. Seed the idea that "automating other people's LinkedIn" is a real,
simple business, and test whether the room wants it. Joey, 2026-10-16: "seed the idea that this is
possible and see if it resonates."

**Tap position:** T2. A handraiser that sorts the room. No selling, no price for Topa, no link.

**Mechanic:** NAP (Nurture & Picks). Paint the opportunity, make it feel simple and real, then
the keyword handraiser. Comment count is the resonance test.

**The Ask:** Standing CTA, exact shape: "If you're ready to [outcome tied to this post], comment
"[KEYWORD]" and we'll get the deets to ya." One themed word in caps. Make the keyword fun, not
logical (Joey rejected AUDIT as boring and picked GOLD on another post). Do not reuse GOLD.

**Avatar:** B2B Leads Lab members. Agency owners, AI automation agency folks, people selling DBR,
people who want a B2B income stream. They already believe LinkedIn outreach works. Many have never
thought of selling it as a service.

**Angle (Joey's words):** Agencies charge $200–300 a month per account just to automate people's
LinkedIns. People doing this make a solid $5–10k a month.

**What the service actually is (true, from Jonny Matthews on the 13 Oct Topa buyer call):**
- You plug the client's LinkedIn account into an automation tool.
- You pick who to reach out to and write the messages.
- The tool sends the connection requests and follow-ups every day, on its own.
- The client doesn't even need to know which tool you use. They just see messages going out from
  their own LinkedIn and reply to people in their own LinkedIn.
- Jonny: "it's big business", "a lot of agencies do very well doing that."

**Proof / numbers allowed (use only these):**
- $200–300 a month per LinkedIn account (Jonny, 13 Oct call).
- People doing this make a solid $5–10k a month (Joey). Say "people doing this", NOT Dan. Dan does
  not claim he runs this service himself.
- Simple maths is fine (e.g. 25 clients at $200–300 lands in that range).
- No named people, no invented case studies, no invented reply rates. A hypothetical example
  client (e.g. a local accountant, a recruiter) is fine if it is obviously hypothetical.

**Do NOT say:** that automation breaks LinkedIn's rules; anything about renting accounts; cold
SMS; Topa's seat price; "white label" jargon; that Dan personally does this.

**Topa:** Optional. At most one light mention near the end as the tool that does the sending. It
must not read as a pitch.

**Format:** Skool community post as Dan. Headline, then short spoken lines, about 180 to 280
words. No "Dan" sign-off. Aussie, playful, confident.

**Source route:** Internal (13 Oct Topa Q&A call) plus Joey's supplied angle. Write fresh.

**Cross-seed check:** Last few posts in this run were poll, invite, story, and a video NAP. This
one is a new topic (a service people can sell), so no overlap. Third keyword-comment ask this week;
Joey asked for it.
