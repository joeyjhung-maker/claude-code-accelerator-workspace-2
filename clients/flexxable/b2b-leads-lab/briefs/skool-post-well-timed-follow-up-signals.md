# Skool brief — One well-timed follow-up vs ten generic ones (signals)

**Room:** B2B Leads Lab.

**Job:** Engage. Pure value after the Topa LinkedIn campaign. Joey, 2026-10-09: "no CTA, pure
value post."

**Tap position:** T1. Teaching post. No pitch, no link, no comment keyword, no bolted-on question.

**Mechanic:** NAP. Principle, then how to do it, then "you can do this now".

**The Ask:** (Changed by Joey in his final, 2026-10-09.) Homework reply: think about the signals you want to watch, then comment your niche and your signal(s). Originally briefed as no ask.

**CTA type:** None.

**Avatar:** Anyone whose job is landing meetings with high-value prospects. B2B sellers, agency
owners, GTM people, recruiters, sales teams. No escape-the-9-to-5 framing.

**Angle (Joey's words):** "What a single well-timed follow-up can do that ten generic ones can't."

**Core argument (supplied by Joey):**
- A follow-up lands when it arrives while the prospect is already in motion.
- So look for signals that show they are in motion:
  - are they hiring
  - are they actively growing
  - are they looking for help
  - have they opened a new site
  - are they engaging or commenting on posts that hint they need help or are looking for info
- The LinkedIn tell: plenty of people post a lead magnet or info to help get X result. Anyone who
  commented wants help with that thing.
- Reference the signal in your DM follow-up and you have a much higher chance of a response.
- If you joined Topa recently and have LinkedIn Signals, this is all much easier. Otherwise you can
  do it manually.

**Proof:** None used. No stats, no reply rates, no results. "Much higher chance of a response" is
Joey's own line and stays as Dan's conviction, not a measured claim.

**Tool facts allowed** (from `clients/flexxable/campaigns/2026-09-topa-linkedin-automation/pricing-and-competitor-proof.md`,
"LinkedIn Signals" section): Signals watches two things daily on autopilot. Who interacts with a
named person's posts, and who posts a chosen keyword. Do not say it tracks hiring, growth or new
sites. Do not say it finds addresses.

**Format:** Skool community post as Dan. Headline, short lines, one sentence per line, Dan
sign-off. Around 250 to 330 words.

**Guidelines:** One mention of Topa / LinkedIn Signals near the end, for people who already have
it, followed straight away by the manual route. It must read as a tip for existing members, not a
pitch. Any example DM must be obviously hypothetical.

**Source route:** Internal doctrine plus Joey's supplied argument. Write fresh.

**Cross-seed check:** No conflict. Follows the fourth-touch post (24 Sept) and extends it: that
post said map the touches, this one says time them. Topa is mentioned for existing buyers only.

## One-breath lock

In B2B Leads Lab, Dan teaches that one follow-up sent when a prospect is visibly in motion beats
ten generic nudges, shows the signals that reveal that motion (hiring, growing, a new site, asking
for help, commenting on someone's lead-magnet post), and tells the reader to name that signal in
the DM. Topa members with LinkedIn Signals get it done faster, everyone else can do it by hand,
and nothing is asked of the reader.
