## The job

Write 6 different versions of what fills the gap: the final lines between "You don't need to compete against a million other peeps anymore." and "I'll see you inside."

Each one is 3 to 8 spoken lines (about 25 to 70 words). Label them C1 to C6.

The close has to make the viewer CLICK the link under the video, now. It should land the whole video in a way they remember.

Make them properly different:
- C1: call back to the opening ("more CLIENTS than most peeps get REPLIES").
- C2: screen vs desk. End on the strongest version of that idea.
- C3: a picture of the viewer's near future. A hypothetical scene: their phone rings, it's the CEO from their Dream 100 list, asking how to open the thing. Must be clearly a picture ("imagine…"), not a promise.
- C4: Dan's own story as the bookend. He doesn't call strangers back, but he did. Now it's their turn to be the one who gets the call.
- C5: a cheeky, fast close. Short and punchy, but no fragment machine-gunning.
- C6: your own best idea.

Optional: one version can remind them the letter templates and the database are waiting inside the community (both are promised earlier in the video).

## Hard rules

- Truth only. No new numbers, results, deadlines, scarcity, bonuses or guarantees. Nothing is paid; everything is free.
- Don't promise they'll close clients or that CEOs WILL call. Pictures must be clearly hypothetical.
- No "Fair enough", "So yeah", "here's the kicker", "game-changer", "leverage", "solution".
- No "absolutely", "literally", "honestly", "genuinely".
- Don't open on a question. Don't recap the whole video in a "the point is simple" block.
- One sentence per line. Spoken words only. CAPS on stressed words. Ellipses for pauses. Dan is an Aussie in the UK: casual, cheeky, like he's talking to a mate.
- Each version must flow from "You don't need to compete against a million other peeps anymore." and into "I'll see you inside."

Output only C1 to C6.
