**Redirect from Joey after v1 (2026-10-09):** "just focus on a locked gift strategy as an A pile

or YETI tumbler (from the locked-gift lead magnet brief and project.md).

**Proof / facts (checked 2026-10-09, secondary sources):**
- Gary Halbert taught that everybody sorts their mail into two piles, standing over the bin.
- A-pile: letters that are, or look, personal.
- B-pile: everything else. Bills, catalogues, brochures, anything that obviously contains a sales
  message.
- All A-pile mail gets opened. B-pile mail does not always.
- His A-pile envelope: no ads, no teaser copy, a real stamp.
- Do NOT say which book or letter it comes from. The searches did not confirm it is in The Boron
  Letters. "Gary Halbert used to say" is as far as it goes.
- "A package is never B-pile" is Dan's stance, picked by Joey. No stats, no open rates, no results.

**Format:** Email. Body only, no headline. Short lines. Ends with a sign-off (emails keep it).
