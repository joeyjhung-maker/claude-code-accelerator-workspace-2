# Jonathan (Topa/b2bgifts.ai) + Nat call — launch logistics & Topa feature status

*Friday 2026-09-04 call between Joey, Jonathan Matthews (CEO, Topa.io / b2bgifts.ai fulfilment
partner), and Nat Ottley (running the direct mail agency at Flexxable). Full transcript below the
summary. Companion to [[travis-call-b2bgifts-strategy]] — that call covered strategy/positioning,
this one is operational: launch dates, pricing, and what Topa's tool can/can't do yet.*

Related: [[b2b-leads-lab-project]]

## Summary

**Launch timeline, now dated:**
- **14th Sept**: internal launch — fulfilment goes live for the wait list, Nat fulfils manually,
  used as a smoke test for one week.
- **21st Sept**: official UK launch of B2B Gifts with the **Yeti Rambler only** — not the wine,
  since they're not licensed to ship alcohol to the UK. Landing page flips from "wait list" to
  live-for-UK, still wait-list for other regions until ready.
- Sunday 2026-09-06: wait-list email goes to all Skool groups (first "sign up to wait list now"
  send). Held until JV sends finish — Rich Schefren runs to Saturday, Matt Leitz/BotBuilders the
  following week, so B2B Gifts group-seeding push properly starts Monday 14th.

**Pricing, locked:** £39.99 for the wine, £54.99 for the Yeti Rambler. (Pounds, not dollars —
confirmed explicitly on the call.)

**Dallas mastermind buyers get priority access, not "first 10."** Nat deliberately avoided
promising Dallas ticket-holders they'd be the literal first 10 people live, since a chunk of them
are US-based and the US send isn't ready — copy uses "priority access" instead. They do get
cost-price fulfilment and Jonathan's 1:1 support.

**US Dallas buyers — proposed "guinea pig" pre-launch cohort (not yet actioned):** Nat's idea —
since the done-for-you fulfilment isn't ready for the US yet (Matthias is reportedly doing his own
US sends already, described on the call as "illegal" re: alcohol), do a hand-raise with US Dallas
buyers asking who wants to start early as a guinea pig/collaborator, working 1:1 with Jonathan
(calls every couple of days, help with letters). Pick one, not all five — Jonathan is protecting
his time until the rest of the build is live. Purpose: get early US case-study results before
public launch.

**New Topa features confirmed/in progress:**
- **Ad-spend checker** (shows whether a prospect is running Facebook/Google ads) — Jonathan is
  **rushing this** after two people DMed him on Skool asking when it ships, off the back of one
  email Joey sent. Nat frames this as the biggest content/growth lever: it captures the large
  share of the audience who have zero intention of doing lumpy mail but still want better
  targeting — support calls currently walk people through Facebook Ad Library + Google
  Transparency Center manually.
- **Lead-ranking (green/amber/red)** — gated behind signing up for the **$99/mo LinkedIn
  automation**, not the free lead database. Deliberate value-stacking move: Jonathan's read is
  that people who sign up only for the ranking feature (without intending to send mail) are more
  likely to cancel, but bundled with LinkedIn automation they keep paying because they're also
  automating LinkedIn.
- **LinkedIn automation** — running a bit behind, targeting live **week of 21st Sept** (same week
  as UK launch). Jonathan plans to launch the ad-spend + ranking features at the same time as
  LinkedIn automation.
- **Duplicate-send check** — flags if a given address has already received a bottle before (not
  full CRM-style history, just a flag). Explicitly framed by Dan as a differentiator/selling
  point: prevents multiple B2B Gifts users unknowingly targeting the same lead in the same niche.
- **Letter upload per-address** — each order gets its own letter field (not one templated letter
  fired across a batch), because Nat flagged that Johnny (a member) manually reviews every
  AI-drafted letter before sending — the AI sometimes pulls in competitor names, including
  competitors that have closed down, so a blanket auto-letter would ship errors.
- **Lock codes** — Jonathan is standardizing to one shared code per batch (changed roughly
  monthly) rather than per-customer, to keep the fulfilment company's setup cost down. Security
  tradeoff accepted for now ("we'll keep the same code until it becomes a problem").
- Postcode look-up is live for the address form now; LinkedIn-URL address lookup + phone-number
  verification prompt planned as a fast-follow, not ready for the 14th.

**Lead database — current limitation, relevant to member support/expectations:** Topa's lead
database is LinkedIn-scraped (~350M licensed records) and strongest for LinkedIn-native niches
(insurance, mortgage advisors). Weak for niches with little LinkedIn presence, e.g. adventure
parks — Nat's example (Johnny's brief) showed searching a company or director name returned
nothing useful unless you already had the LinkedIn URL. Jonathan's plan: add a Google
Business-listing database as a second source over time. He separately licenses a database called
**Ocean.io** for look-alike prospecting (put in a website, get similar businesses via full-web
scrape, not just LinkedIn) — too expensive to build into Topa's own database yet, but he
recommended it as a paid tool for members doing heavy list-building, over LinkedIn Sales
Navigator (Nat doesn't think Sales Navigator is worth paying for if you can search company names
on free LinkedIn directly).

**AI voicemail / SMS features exist in Topa but Jonathan doesn't want them promoted for this use
case** — those were built for B2C/bulk outreach. For B2B Gifts' one-bottle-at-a-time model, his
advice is just call and leave your own voicemail; no benefit to routing through Topa/Twilio for
low-volume 1:1 outreach.

**Cross-sell structure, as Jonathan frames it:** Topa and B2B Gifts are two entry points into the
same account — sign up for either, you see both in the dashboard and get pushed to the other.
Once fulfilment is live, members don't need to be moved to a different Skool group to buy — they
can just order from within the existing group.

**Nat's course (mentioned, no new date beyond the existing "8 weeks" already banked):** designed
as mini-courses/modules rather than one long course, per Dan's direction. Nat's proposed content
angle — teach list-building fundamentals (Apollo, Ocean.io, Hunter, etc.) inside it, and use the
ad-spend checker as a hook to pull in AAA-Ninjas members who have no intention of doing lumpy
mail but want better-targeted outreach generally.

**Marketing/content plan discussed (Joey's side):**
- Two separate webinars planned: one for the LinkedIn automation product, one for the B2B Gifts
  done-for-you fulfilment — kept separate since combining them is too much to explain in one
  post. Each ~40 min, not necessarily a full hour. Possible special deal at the close of each.
- Jonathan will record two Looms (one per product: B2B Gifts, LinkedIn automation) to post in
  "start here" — full walkthroughs, refreshed with a new Loom each time he ships an update, doubling
  as a standing feature-announcement channel.
- Waitlist onboarding email flow doesn't exist yet — Joey is building it: welcome email
  confirming wait-list position + a link to join the B2B Gifts Skool group while they wait.
- Explicit rule surfaced by Nat, worth remembering for any B2B Gifts/Topa post that references
  ROYA: **don't make free features feel conditional on being in ROYA** — position free perks as
  something ROYA members have simply had access to for longer ("you've had this for six months"),
  not as a paywalled ROYA-only benefit, since that reads as a bait-and-switch to ROYA buyers who
  already paid.
- Consequently: Jonathan is **not** pushing the "200 free leads/month" hook to the main AI
  Automation group yet — wants LinkedIn automation + fulfilment live first, so the free-lead
  funnel converts into automation/gifts signups rather than landing on a half-built feature set.
  His stated funnel logic: roughly 1 in 5 free-lead signups converts to a paid feature (LinkedIn
  automation or the $99 voicemail tier, by his rough numbers), and buyers of either product tend
  to cross into the other once they see it.
- Group-seeding cadence concern (Nat, echoing an existing internal worry already banked in
  `project.md`): audience is already fatigued from stacked offers (Roya, then Dallas, then Rich,
  then Matt Leitz) with little value-first content between them — flagged as working against B2B
  Gifts group-seeding timing, not a new issue but a live one this month.
