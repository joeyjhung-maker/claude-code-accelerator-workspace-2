## 1. Narrowing Down Your Niche — Finding Your Sub-Niche
- Start with "What industries do I have professional experience in?" Client work, a 9-5 job or contract work all count.
- Why it matters: you already know the jargon, the painful problems, and how clients get won and lost.
- Tools: a **Sub-Niche Finder** (a Claude artifact), a Niche Research prompt, a blank results table plus a worked example.
- **Niche Sanity Check:** prior experience? existing connections? too crowded? the main pains and does your offer fix them? do they hire outside providers? margins? cost-sensitive? market big enough?

## 2. Your Dream 100 Businesses (two methods)
- Pre-game: decide what signals a dream client (sales team size, runs ads, premises size, online checkout…).
- **Method 1, Claude:** a $20/m Claude Pro plan + Chrome + the Claude in Chrome extension. About 5 minutes of setup. Paste the List Build & Enrichment prompt (change the yellow bits). Tip: on long lists, split into chunks of about 100 companies per chat.
- **Method 2, TOPA:** sign up free (affiliate link). "350 million contacts across 50 million organisations"; "replaces Sales Navigator, Apollo, etc."
  - **Redeem code DANWARDROPELEADS** under Billing → Manage Plan = **200 free lead downloads a month** (decision makers' mobiles and direct emails).
  - Use TOPA's AI search in plain English, or have Claude turn the TOPA prompt template into a detailed search. Download all pages as a CSV.

## 3. Your Dream 100 Decision Makers
- Target four LinkedIn seniority levels: Owner/Partner, CXO, VP, Director. Pair them with job titles (long list in the "Who To Target" doc).
- TOPA users already have the names. Claude users run the Decision Maker Finder prompt on their CSV (being logged into LinkedIn helps).
- Get mobiles and direct emails in TOPA via "Get Cell Phone" (paste the LinkedIn URL). Check the numbers with a phone-validation ping tool (Data8).

## 4. Verify Your List For Sending
- **Business:** registered office vs the best mailing address · what days they're open · still trading or in administration? · **are they hiring salespeople?** · have you already contacted them?
- Business registers: OpenCorporates lists each country's "Companies House" equivalent.
- **Decision maker:** still there? is the LinkedIn URL really them? mobile? direct email? **do they live close enough to actually go into the office?** · does their seniority make sense for the company's size?

## 5. The Letter, Gift & Lock
- Four templates: Long 1 (windows & doors), Long 2 (blinds & shutters), Short 1 (generic), Short 2 (blinds & shutters).
- **The lock mechanic in the letter:** "There's a nice bottle of wine in this box… call me… even if it's just to tell me to go away… I'll give you the code to unlock it."
- **The long letter is Dan's own Flexxable AI-agency letter**: AI Sales Androids reviving old leads. It names the sales gap (paid-for leads that didn't buy), includes the "316 dialled-to-death leads → £448,803+ in 90 days" client result, and closes with a P.S.: only 2 windows & doors businesses per city, "if that's not you… I'll just give you the lock combo… No hard feelings."
- **The short generic letter:** fill in [ideal outcome] and [USP], offer a free trial call, QR code to a booking link.
- **Sourcing:** gifts from wherever (a wholesaler is best for volume); bottle lock tops, cable locks and shipping boxes from Amazon.
- **Tips:** test that the lock fits your gift before buying a batch · letter goes where they see it first · seal well, write clearly · decide on a return address.
- **DFY upsell inside the lesson:** "short on time?" → b2bgifts.ai/lander (sourcing, printing, packing and tracked posting done for you).
- Resource: a B2B Gift Send tracking sheet.
