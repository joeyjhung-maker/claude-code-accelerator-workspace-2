## What's new vs the 15 Oct plan

The 15 Oct call had Joey building ONE appointment-setting site on flexxdigital.com, for Nat and
Gav only, as the template-to-be. This call extends it: **every Dallas attendee** gets their own
site, on their own subdomain — e.g. `joey.b2bgifts.io` — not just the first two affiliates.

- The GHL event happens straight after the Flexx Dallas event, so attendees get trained and
  prepped on selling TOPA / B2B Gifts *before* Dallas, then go meet people and sell at the event
  itself.
- Framed as **their own business** — their site, their pics of bottles and gifts, their "how it
  works." Not branded as Dan's or Flexxable's.

## Site structure (one template, per-rep subdomain)

Main page sections:
1. **TOPA** — a page/section for the done-for-you lead gen service.
2. **Skool** — a section for B2B Leads Lab (probably Skool only, not the wider group list).
3. **DIY** — link out to the b2bgifts.ai site, with the rep's own affiliate link, plus their
   Skool affiliate link so people can follow along with B2B Leads Lab themselves.

Positioned as an appointment-setting website using bottles + gifts as the visual identity —
multiple ways to help a client with lead gen, from fully done-for-you to fully DIY.

## Two avatars (confirmed, matches the existing project file)

1. **Want leads given to them.** DFY — Topa does the sending and research.
2. **Want to send bottles and get meetings for themselves.** DIY — they run it, Topa/b2bgifts is
   just the fulfilment layer.

Sub-split surfaced on this call (new nuance):
- People who already have their own offer and just want traffic → LinkedIn + sending bottles
  themselves, OR hand sending to b2bgifts/Topa.
- People with no offer who want to make money → sell TOPA itself for commissions.
