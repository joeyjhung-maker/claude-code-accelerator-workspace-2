## The voice DNA (grade the draft against this)
- **Cold-open income claim** — a concrete number + short time + "with AI". No wind-up.
- **Objection stack, killed fast** — no experience / no product / no investment / not technical / AI does the heavy lifting.
- **"Boring but lucrative"** — low competition, one client = thousands/mo, few hours/week.
- **Pre-empt disbelief** — "I get it, sounds too good to be true. I had my doubts too."
- **Overwhelm → simple** — name the tutorial-hell, then "this is different / not another course."
- Plain, spoken, first-person, one idea per line. NO agency jargon, NO "serenity-sucking client list", NO burnout-confession register — that's the OTHER segment.
