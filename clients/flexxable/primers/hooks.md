# Flexxable — Hook Primer

Source: Top 10 winning ads, last 18 months. All promoting The Instant AI Agency ($14.40 book).

---

## The winning hooks (verbatim)

1. I copied this agency model from what Hedge Fund Managers do…
2. I now make 10X what I earn from clients by installing A.I. into their business… plus… A.I. does all the work!
3. Rob Brown earned $341,000 dollars in just 90 days from a single client using my AI system.
4. I've been in the game for a while, and I've never seen ANYTHING with the easy revenue potential of an "Instant A.I. Agency"
5. What Does It Take To Be #1? 🥇

---

## What makes these hooks work — patterns extracted

**1. The borrowed credibility move**
Hook 1 opens with a prestigious reference (Hedge Fund Managers) before saying a word about AI.
Transfers authority instantly. Reader thinks: if hedge funds do this, it's real.

**2. The ratio flip**
Hook 2 leads with 10X — not a dollar amount, a multiplier.
Then immediately answers the "how": A.I. does all the work.
Two punches in one line.

**3. Named result + tight timeframe**
Hook 3 names a real person, a real number ($341k), a tight window (90 days), and a single client.
Every word earns its place. No vagueness.

**4. Industry veteran signal**
Hook 4 opens with "I've been in the game for a while" — establishes credibility before the claim.
"ANYTHING" in caps does the heavy lifting. Superlative without hyperbole.

**5. Universal question hook**
Hook 5 asks a question every entrepreneur has asked themselves.
Doesn't mention AI, agency, or the offer at all. Pure emotional entry point.

---

## Voice rules for Flexxable hooks

- Short. One or two lines max. Never a full sentence if a fragment will do.
- Ellipses (…) are a core rhythm device. Use them to create forward pull.
- Named real people with real numbers beat vague claims every time.
- The "I" voice is Dan's — first person, personal, matter-of-fact.
- Never lead with "how to" or a list. Lead with a result, a question, or a status signal.
- Timeframes make results feel real: "90 days", "2 months in", "just in November".
- The offer ($14.40 book) never appears in a hook. Problem or result first. Always.
