## 4. What already looks live — provisional, not the audit

These are recon-level observations. Every one needs verifying before it goes near a pitch.

1. **The ladder stops at £399/mo.** Top rung is £3,990/yr. For a man positioned as a seven-figure entrepreneur running a marketplace institute, there's no high-ticket rung, no 1:1, no done-with-you, no mastermind above The Hive. `[INFERRED]` Classic backend gap.

2. **Two rails that appear to collide.** A Kingdom Partner giving £99/mo gets *less* than a member paying £49/mo. Generous money is being routed into the lower-value experience. `[OBSERVED prices, INFERRED conflict]`

3. **Nothing between free and £49/mo.** Ebook → Telegram → £49 subscription. No low-ticket entry, no paid front-end, no self-liquidating step. `[OBSERVED]`

4. **The hero CTA on thewatchglobal.com points to `/prophet-school`, which returns 404.** The nav item labelled "PROPHET SCHOOL" routes to `/store` instead. `[OBSERVED — verify again before citing; sites change]`

5. **Four brands, four audiences, one man.** Church / prophetic school / entrepreneur institute / conference. `[INFERRED]` Key-man dependency looks total — he is the product in all four.

6. **The TAP Institute's website is effectively offline.** `[OBSERVED — hard-verified]` Probed from two independent clients (browser engine, plus curl/openssl) over ~20 minutes:
   - `www.thetapinstitute.org` — **no A record, no CNAME. Does not resolve at all.**
   - `thetapinstitute.org` (apex) — resolves to 162.159.140.166, but TLS handshake fails with alert 40 (`handshake_failure`) on TLS 1.2. Plain HTTP returns **409 Conflict**.
   - Nameservers: `ns0[1-4].squarespacedns.com` + `dns[1-4].p03.nsone.net` — Squarespace-hosted, domain not correctly attached.

   **Every Google-indexed URL for the commercial arm sits on the `www` subdomain** (`www.thetapinstitute.org/the-winners-circle`, and the Trustpilot listing). All of them are dead links. Someone searching "TAP Institute" finds results that go nowhere.

   *Caveat: tested from one network. Worth a re-check from another connection before it's cited to him — though a missing `www` DNS record isn't a transient fault.*

## 5. Flag — reputational allegations

`[UNKNOWN / UNVERIFIED — treat as allegation only]`

Search-tool summaries of it surface reviewer claims that: testimonial photos are stock imagery and testimonials are fabricated; prior ventures include Empire Q, Wealth Nation, and Basilica Capital; and an NFT project raised ~£500k which was placed with a broker that has withheld client funds for roughly two years.

**I could not verify any of this.** Trustpilot blocked direct fetch (403) and then served a bot-check I did not attempt to clear. These are anonymous reviewer claims summarised by a search tool — the weakest possible evidence class.

**Why it's in this file anyway:** it is material to whether you pitch at all, and to *which entity* you pitch. If the commercial arm carries live disputes, the church/prophetic side is a very different proposition from the entrepreneur side. This needs proper verification — Companies House, the Charity Commission, and a direct read of the reviews — before it informs anything.
