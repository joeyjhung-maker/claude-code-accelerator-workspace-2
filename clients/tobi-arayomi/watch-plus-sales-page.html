If this sounds familiar

You're not unsure whether God speaks. You're unsure whether it was Him

  • "I get impressions — but I can't tell what's God and what's me."

Before you join

This is built for a specific person

It's for you if…

  • You believe God still speaks and you want to grow in hearing Him

Before you decide

Questions people ask

What if I'm not sure I'm called to be a prophet?

Most people who join aren't sure. The school is about learning to hear God clearly — which every believer is invited into. What you're called to becomes clearer as you learn to listen, not before.

I've never done anything like this. Am I too far behind?

No. The courses start from the beginning, and the library means you can go at your own pace rather than the group's. You are not joining halfway through something.

What if I can't make the live session?

Sessions are kept in the member library, so you can watch afterwards. The live room is better if you can make it, but nothing is lost if you can't.

Can I cancel?

Yes — it's a monthly membership and you can cancel any time from your account. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for.

How is this different from watching the free teaching on YouTube?

The public teaching shows you that this is real. The school teaches you how to do it — in order, with practice, and with somewhere to ask when you get stuck.

I'm outside the UK. Does that matter?

No. The school runs online and members join from every continent. If cost is the barrier rather than distance, see the Emerging Nations section above.
