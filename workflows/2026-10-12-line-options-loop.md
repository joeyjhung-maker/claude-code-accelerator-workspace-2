# Line-options loop (polish an ad one line at a time)

Banked 2026-10-12 after about ten runs on the Primal Viking "52-year-old Viking" ad. Not yet a skill: see observation 46 in `skills/observations.md`.
Related: [[Promotion]] [[2026-06-24-mariobot-genesis-body-copy]] [[show-dont-tell-decisions]]

## When to use
The ad is written and one line is weak. Joey can say what's wrong with it but doesn't want to rewrite it himself.

## The loop
1. **Joey names the line and the complaint**, in his words ("too long and waffley", "why do I need it", "frozen sand doesn't make sense").
2. **Write a one-line brief for Mariobot** with six parts:
   - the weak line, and the lines either side of it
   - why it fails, using Joey's complaint
   - the only facts allowed (from the brand's own material)
   - hard limits (word count, banned words, what must not be named)
   - how many options (6 to 15)
   - how the options must differ from each other (list the angles)
3. **Run Mariobot without the style contract.** Save the options next to the ad.
4. **Check every option against the facts** before showing them. Drop or flag any that invent a detail or promise something the ad doesn't deliver.
5. **Show the best few in place**, inside the sentence, with one pick and the reason.
6. **Joey picks.** Swap the line in and save a new version of the ad.

## What we learned running it
- Ask what size of change he wants. "Maximise this idea" once meant a new sentence and once meant swapping two words.
- For a phrase swap, say "same length, same rhythm" in the brief or the options run long.
- An open loop must point at something the ad pays off, in the place the loop says it will.
- Story colour can be embellished; product facts can't. See [[story-liberties-ok-if-not-jarring]].
