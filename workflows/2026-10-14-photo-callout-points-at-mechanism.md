# Photo callouts point at the mechanism

Related: [[Promotion]] [[client]]

## The rule
- The arrow on a hero photo points at the thing that makes the idea work.
- On Locked Gifts that's the locked bottle, not the letter.
- If the page already has a headline, the photo doesn't get its own. Two headlines fight.
- Keep the photo to one callout: a short line ("It's locked!"), an arrow, and an icon if it helps (padlock).

## Why
- Dan wanted the arrow on the bottle, not the letter. The lock is the hook.
- "Books meetings w/ 8-fig CEOs" on the photo clashed with the page headline. Joey: remove it and "let the headline do the selling."
