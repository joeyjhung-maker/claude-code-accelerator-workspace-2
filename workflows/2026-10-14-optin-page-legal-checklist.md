# Workflow: Opt-in page legal checklist

Related: [[Promotion]] [[client]]

First run 2026-10-14 (Locked Gifts opt-in). Not legal advice. Link Flexxable's existing policies where they exist.

## When to use this
Any opt-in or landing page that collects an email or gets paid traffic.

## The steps
1. **Consent line, straight under the button.** What they get, who emails them, unsubscribe anytime, and a Privacy Policy link.
   - Used: "Free. You'll get the guide plus emails from Dan. Unsubscribe anytime. Privacy Policy"
2. **Footer.** © Flexxable · Privacy Policy · Terms · Disclaimer. Meta and Google reject ads whose landing page has no privacy policy.
3. **Results disclaimer**, in small text in the footer, whenever the page claims results (calls, clients, $).
   - Used: "Results shown are not typical. They depend on your offer, your market and the work you put in. We don't guarantee you'll get the same results."
4. **Cookie banner** only if the Meta pixel or other tracking fires (UK and EU rules). That's a build call, so leave it out of mockups.
5. **Alcohol heads-up.** If the whisky bottle is the hero, Meta may class the ad as alcohol content. Target 18+ (25+ in some countries).
