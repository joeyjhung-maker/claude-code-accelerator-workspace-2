## The top ad is the template for AD 70
**Rank 1, Library ID 1836867677282688, running since 8 Jul.** The "modern male body is powering
down" ad.

- **It never says "you".** Not once. Every line is about "the modern male body", "the body",
  "today's men", "most men".
- **It's a timeline.** Day 7, Day 20, Day 45.
- **Each timeline beat is a feeling, not a measurement.** Fog clears and morning energy returns.
  The body stops hoarding fat. Drive and focus come back. No pounds, no lab values, no days-to-gone.
- **The mechanism is one metaphor carried all the way through.** The body is electrical, ions
  are spark plugs, the modern body is unplugged, beef-organ boosters are an AA battery on a
  truck, reindeer organs reconnect the power.
- **No medical chain.** No thyroid, no aromatase, no estrogen.
- **No testimonials, no customer count, no price, no guarantee.** Mechanism and timeline only.
- **Narrator is a researcher voice** ("in my research, I've analysed hundreds…"), not a customer.

## What they say across the account

| Topic | How they handle it in ad copy |
|---|---|
| Who it's about | Top ad: third person only. Mid-rank ads: "you" freely, often with "not your fault". |
| The villain | The environment. Dead food, plastics, chemicals, low nutrition in food. Never the man. |
| Doctors | "Doctors tell you it's just aging" appears in several. Third-person version is common. |
| Mechanism | Metaphor first: power outage, dead fire, estrogen flood, a system that's unfed. |
| Thyroid | **Never mentioned in any live ad.** It lives on the landing pages. |
| Aromatase / estrogen conversion | Not in any of the 20 texts read today. (The 8 Oct pull had one "Aromatase Trap" ad.) |
| Lab values (ng/dL) | Not in ad copy. One research-style ad cites a population average. Michael's 370 to 800 is page-only. |
| Named customers | **None in ad copy.** Named testimonials are page-only. The founder speaks as himself in one ad. |
| Customer count | "100,000+" in ads. "157,579+" on the site. |
| Population stats | Used a lot: testosterone down 20%, 40%, 50% versus past decades. |
| Timelines | In four ads. Beats are feelings: fog, energy, sleep, mood, drive, "wife notices". |
| Fat | Blunt in the short offer ads ("melter", "gone"). In the long-form ads it "starts" or "shifts". |
| Verbs for the product | support, helps rebuild, restore, reactivate, feed, fuel. Aggressive ones (melt, skyrocket) sit in headlines and statics. |
| Hedges | Present in the newer ads: "men report", "some men", "often", "one capsule doesn't flip a switch", "I'm not saying it's magic". |
| The back-stop | The 30-day money-back guarantee is attached directly to the timeline promise. |
| TRT | Two ads. Framed as "try this first", plus a list of TRT's downsides. |
| First-person stories | Three ads (the 48-year-old, alcohol after 30, the grandfather research). |

## Where our four shortlist ads go further than they do
- Thyroid and aromatase chains in the ad itself.
- Michael's lab numbers, and Chris, Niko and Matthew by name, in the ad itself.
- "That's low testosterone" as a flat diagnosis. Their nearest line is softer: several problems
  are "usually one", testosterone doing less than it used to.
- "The entire hormonal axis breaks down."

## Where they go further than we do
- Size claims in inches.
- "Gone in 9 days", "melt 27 lbs or money back", "forever gone".
- A percentage of customers who feel it within six weeks.
- "Nature still holds the cure" and "every ingredient heals".

## The register to mirror for AD 70
1. Third person all the way down, like their rank 1. "You" only in the CTA.
2. One metaphor for the mechanism. AD 70 already has the fireplace.
3. Timeline beats a man can feel or see, never a number.
4. Environment is the villain. Food lost its nutrition; men stopped eating organs.
5. No thyroid, no aromatase, no lab values, no named customers in the ad.
6. One honest hedge, and the guarantee as the back-stop to the timeline.
7. Product verbs: feed, fuel, support, rebuild, restore.
