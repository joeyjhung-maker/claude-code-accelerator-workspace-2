**STEP 3 — QUALITY GATE**

**TEST 1 — WEAKEST INTERPRETATION:** Hook 4 at its weakest reads slightly like "take this supplement and here's a timeline," which is borderline. But the specificity of "not synthetic pills, not powder in a tub" and the promise of a timeline keep it from collapsing. All others hold strong under worst-case reads — reindeer testicles doubling, wife noticing before you speak, bloodwork jumping from 370 to 800 — these are too specific and too charged to flatten.

**TEST 2 — COMPRESSION:** Hook 6 tightened from an earlier version that over-explained the fireplace metaphor. Hook 9 compressed from three sentences about aromatase to two punchy ones. Hook 4 is the longest but every clause earns its spot by stacking specific rejections (synthetic pills, powder, tub). All hooks wrung dry.

**TEST 3 — ESCALATION:** Hook 5 escalated from "his body changed" to "his wife said something she hadn't said in years" — far more vicious because it weaponizes the relationship. Hook 8 escalated from a clinical explanation of reindeer biology to "testicles double in size" which is visceral and impossible to scroll past. Hook 9 escalated from "stubborn belly fat" to "man boobs are your body converting your own testosterone into estrogen" — that reframe is devastating.

**TEST 4 — FRAGMENT CHECK:** All hooks verified. No sentence fragments. Every sentence has a subject and predicate. Thoughts flow naturally with commas, conjunctions, and dashes where appropriate. No neo-noir chop.

**TEST 5 — VICIOUSNESS CHECK:** Hook 1 is the softest of the set but still lands because "your wife will notice before you say a word" implies she'd stopped noticing entirely. Hooks 8, 9, and 10 all produce genuine flinches — testicles doubling, man boobs reframed as estrogen conversion, and a bloodwork number jump so dramatic a doctor assumed TRT.
