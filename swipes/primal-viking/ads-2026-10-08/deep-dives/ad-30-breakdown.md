## Hook analysis (first 3 seconds)
- VISUAL: gym guy hammering battle ropes. Motion is the only thing moving. Aspirational body contrasted against the list.
- VERBAL: none.
- TEXT: header plus the full bullet stack. Text layer does all the work.
- Type: call-out ("LOW TESTOSTERONE MEN:") plus shock list (taboo words) plus curiosity gap ("if you want to know why").

Hook effectiveness score: 8 to 9 out of 10 (Gemini gave 9, then 8; call it 8.5).
Why: names the avatar in the first line. The taboo words (m*sturbate, p*rn) are the flinch. Every bullet is a private shame the viewer can self-diagnose in a second. One cause is implied for all eight symptoms. Works sound-off.
Cost: it is a text wall at 360px. The bottom lines are small and the "why" curiosity sits at the very end of a long read.

## Target avatar
Men who suspect or believe they have low T. Specifically men with porn/masturbation habits, anxiety, procrastination, low drive, poor dating results, weak emotional control. Assumes they already believe low T is real and that these symptoms are connected to it. Fed through the page to a 35-60+ reindeer organ pitch.

## Awareness stage
Problem-aware, leaning unaware-to-problem. It re-labels behaviours (porn, procrastination) as a hormone problem. It does not name a product or a solution. The "why" is withheld to the click.

## Angle and mechanism
- Angle: all of a man's private failures are one root cause, low testosterone. Shame to explanation.
- Mechanism: not named in the video. Implied hormone root cause. The named mechanism sits off-ad: "Secret Superfood", "Secret ancient information passed down from the Vikings", reindeer organs, arctic thriving.
- The ad is a pure click-driver; the story is on the landing page.

## Script structure
Listicle / problem-agitate, entirely in text.
- Hook 0:00: "LOW TESTOSTERONE MEN:"
- Body 0:00 onward: the 8 bullets
- Close: "Link in bio if you want to know why"
No timing shifts because everything is on screen at once.

## Proof and objection handling
- Proof: none. No numbers, testimonials, credentials, product shown. The fit man is implied "what you could look like".
- Objections pre-empted: only "is this really me?" by listing specific symptoms. Inference: the market is skeptical of generic self-help, so the ad sells "the real reason" rather than a fix.

## Emotional angle
Shame and frustration first, then curiosity. Carried by the text (shame) and by the contrast with the jacked guy (envy / aspiration).

## Visual approach
Native-looking stock/organic fitness clip with a text overlay. Looks like a repost meme, not a commercial. No logo, no product, no captions needed (no speech). Passes the "doesn't look like an ad" test.

## CTA
"Link in bio if you want to know why." Verbal-free, on-screen text. Low friction (content click). No urgency, no scarcity. The cost of inaction is the list itself.
Oddity: "Link in bio" is a social-organic CTA. On Meta the button is what is clicked; this reads as a repurposed organic/Reels creative.

## Format
- 1:1 (360x360), 25.8s, no VO, music only.
- Sound-off fully viable.
- Low resolution (360px), which makes the small bullets hard to read in feed.

## What makes it work
1. Shame list as hook. Eight private, taboo, self-diagnosable behaviours collapse into one label. The viewer who ticks two is hooked.
2. "Why" curiosity gap with a soft CTA. It asks for a click to get an explanation, not a purchase.
3. Native, no-ad look plus zero-audio dependence. Cheap clip, cheap to produce, cheap to test in volume.
4. Reframe. Porn habit, anxiety and procrastination become a hormone problem with a purchasable fix, which converts guilt into a product need.
5. Longevity signal: the ad has run since 3 Jun 2026 (about 4 months), so this format is probably a working one.

## What could be improved
1. Everything appears at once, so the viewer has no reason to keep watching. Stagger the bullets, one per beat, so retention carries to the CTA.
2. Resolution and type size. 360x360 with a dense list is hard to read.
3. No sound hook. A voice reading the list would add a second layer.
4. Disconnect between video (low T, porn) and primary text (beer belly, Viking secret). Message match to the landing page is loose.
5. "Link in bio" is the wrong CTA language for a Meta ad. A button-matched line would fit.
6. Generic gym stock. A visual tied to the product or the reindeer / Viking mechanism would build brand memory.
7. No proof anywhere.

## Swipe skeleton
- [0-3s] "[AVATAR LABEL]:" plus the first 2 bullets that are the most taboo or private
- [3-20s] 6 more bullets, one idea each, every one a self-diagnosable shame or failure, all implicitly traced to one cause
- [last 3-5s] "[Link/CTA] if you want to know why"
- Visual: unrelated aspirational athletic or on-brand B-roll loop, muted, music bed, no logo

Example fill: "[PEOPLE WITH X]: - [private shame 1] - [private shame 2] ... - Link in bio if you want to know why"
