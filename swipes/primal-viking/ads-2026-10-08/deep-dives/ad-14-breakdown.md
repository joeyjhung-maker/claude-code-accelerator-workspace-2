## What makes it work (consolidated)
- Call-out in the first line, then a list that reads like a confession. Self-diagnosis in under 10 seconds.
- Taboo bullet leads the list ("m*sturbate"), censored so the platform lets it through but the viewer still reads it. Scroll-stopper.
- One cause for eight problems. Reframes anxiety, procrastination, focus, motivation, dating, porn, emotions as a single fixable thing.
- Curiosity gap close: "if you want to know why" withholds the answer. Primary text then supplies the stat ("20% lower testosterone than men 20 years prior", "down about 40% since the 80s") and the page "10-reasons-beef-long" delivers the reasons.
- Looks like organic gym content, passes the not-an-ad test, fully sound-off.
- Long runner (live since 8 Jul 2026) is a longevity signal.

## What could be improved
- No proof in the video, relies on the primary text for the stat.
- "Link in bio" is a mismatch for a Meta ad (there is a CTA button), reads as repurposed organic.
- No hint of solution type or product, no brand on screen.
- Single looping shot, no pattern interrupt through the 15s, drop-off risk after the list is read.
- Over-attribution of eight issues to testosterone can read as oversimplified.
