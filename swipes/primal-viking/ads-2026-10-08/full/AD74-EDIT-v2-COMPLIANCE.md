a diagnosis, and "your" thyroid / testosterone lines recur through the body.

the product fixes, sets it against TRT, and rests on unverified testimonials and numbers.

## Net impression
A man over 35 with a poor morning appetite, man boobs, a beer belly or low drive has low
testosterone caused by a failing thyroid that his doctor missed. Primal Viking repairs that
thyroid, roughly doubles testosterone, removes man boobs and 20-plus pounds, and does the job of
TRT without drugs. Vikings and reindeer biology are the proof.

→ L1, L2, L10, L12, M3 → **LAW NEVER / META NEVER.**

Moving "you" to "men" brings the META column down. It does not move the LAW column while the ad
still promises to fix low testosterone.

## Proof that would unlock claims
- Raises testosterone / reduces chest and belly fat / supports thyroid function: a human trial
  on this product or an essentially identical one. Even then, worded as normal healthy function.
- Michael, Matthew Kingston: signed release, lab reports or weight records, typical buyer results.
- Chris R., Niko D.: original reviews verbatim, with release. The man-boobs claims stay NEVER.
- 157,579 men: order records.
- 80% nutrition loss: a citable study.
- 2x beef organs: a defined, measured comparison.
- Vikings: a historical source. Reindeer facts: zoology sources.
- Third-party tested: the lab report. Made in the US: sourcing breakdown. Guarantee: refund terms.
- Free quiz and personalised plan: the quiz itself.
