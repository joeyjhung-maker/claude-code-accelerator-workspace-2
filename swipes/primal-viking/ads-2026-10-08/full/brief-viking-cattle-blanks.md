FILL IN TWO BLANKS. Write 14 options, numbered. Each option is the two finished lines. Nothing else, no explanations.

THE LINES:
"Vikings ate the reindeer, packed with ___
You eat the cattle, deprived of ___"

CONTEXT: a raw Facebook ad for Primal Viking (freeze-dried reindeer organs). The lines just before these are:
"Cattle stand in a field, get fed, barely move, and break down young.
Reindeer run 5,000 km a season through minus-40 cold and never quit."
These two lines are the turn of the whole ad. They must hit like a slap.

WHAT THE BLANKS MUST DO: the two blanks mirror each other. What the reindeer is packed with is exactly what the cattle (and so the reader) has none of. Each blank is 2 to 7 words. Short enough to say in one breath.

MATERIAL YOU MAY DRAW ON: the reindeer earned what's in it by surviving minus 40 and running 5,000 km a season. Cattle stand still and get fed. Reindeer organs are dense in iron, B vitamins, zinc, carnitine. The brand says they are free of the toxins factory-farmed cattle carry. You can go literal (nutrients) or go for the bigger idea (everything the animal fought for, everything a hard life puts into meat, a wild animal versus a penned one).

TONE: a man telling this at a bar. Savage, dry, a bit funny. Concrete over abstract. No lab words like "bioavailable" or "micronutrients". No numbers.

Give a spread: some literal about nutrients, some about what a hard life builds into an animal, some that are really about the reader, a few darkly funny.
