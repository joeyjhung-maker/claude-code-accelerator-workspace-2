WRITE AN EARLY PAYOFF INSERT FOR AN AD. Write 5 versions, labelled A to E. Each is 3 to 6 short lines, 35 to 70 words. Nothing else, no commentary.

THE AD OPENS:
"A 57-year-old Viking rowing for days through frigid open water, hauling blood-crusted axes up jagged shoreline could easily fight hand to hand against men half his age and conquer half of Europe.
But today, that same 57-year-old modern man needs ibuprofen to get out of bed.
The difference wasn't genetics. It was what they ate.
[INSERT GOES HERE]
That kind of body doesn't run on chicken breast and multivitamins.
Vikings ate the WHOLE animal, liver, heart and kidney."

THE PROBLEM (feedback from a senior media buyer): the ad is entertaining but never tells the reader, in the first few lines, what is in it for HIM. The core benefit and pain point are missing early, so people will enjoy the story, then drop off and not buy. It only needs to be TEASED, not explained.

THE READER: a man 40 to 55+. PAIN: he hits a wall at 3pm, the fat on his belly and chest won't move whatever diet he tries, the drive he used to have (gym, work, bedroom) has gone quiet, and he's been told it's "just aging". BENEFIT: energy that lasts all day, a body that starts leaning out again, drive back, his wife noticing. Without needles, prescriptions, or another shelf of single-ingredient supplements.

THE JOB OF THE INSERT: right after "It was what they ate", tell him what he is about to find out and what it will do for him, then hand back to the Viking story. Tease the benefit and name the pain in his words. Do not mention the product, reindeer, organs or capsules yet, the story reveals those.

USE THESE FIVE PROVEN STRUCTURES, one per version (fill the blanks for this reader, keep the skeleton recognisable):

A - THE PAYOFF PROMISE: "In the next [short time], I'm going to show you ___ that will ___ and free you from ___ so you never have to ___ again… all without ___."
B - NOTHING TO DO WITH YOU: "And it has nothing to do with your ___ or ___ or ___… it has everything to do with [one thing outside of you, so it's not your fault]."
C - INSTEAD OF: "Instead of ___, you can ___. Instead of ___, you can ___. And instead of ___, you can ___."
D - WHAT THEY TELL YOU: "They'll say you must ___, have to ___, and at all costs ___. But following that advice only keeps you stuck with ___ and never addresses ___. So if you're ready to finally stop ___ and get back your ___…"
E - THE FORGOTTEN THING: "So in the next [short time], I'll show you what ___, why ___ stopped, and how to ___ without ___."

RULES: same voice as the ad, a man telling this at a bar, dry and direct. It is a text ad, so "the next 60 seconds" or "the next minute" not "7 minutes". No numbers of pounds, no lab values, no named conditions, do not say "low testosterone", "cure" or "treat". Do not promise a result by a date. One sentence per line. Ellipses or commas, never a dash. Each version must end in a way that flows into "That kind of body doesn't run on chicken breast and multivitamins."
