REWRITE ONE LINE OF AN AD. Write 6 options for this single line, numbered 1 to 6, nothing else.

THE WEAK LINE: "The kidney loads you with zinc and selenium, the minerals most modern men are quietly starving for."

WHY IT FAILS: it tells the reader he is short of something but never tells him WHY HE NEEDS IT. The reader's reaction is "I don't care that men are starving for it. What does it do for me?" The line must answer that with a benefit he can feel or see.

THE ONLY FACT YOU MAY USE (this is the brand's own claim): zinc and selenium from reindeer kidney support nitric oxide production, which is blood flow, and zinc supports testosterone. Blood flow is what a man feels in the gym as a pump, in his hands and feet in the cold, and in the bedroom.

THE LINES AROUND IT (match this pattern, organ then the job a man feels):
"The liver delivers B vitamins and iron that carry oxygen through your blood… so your energy doesn't die at 3pm like it does now."
"The heart is one of the richest sources of carnitine on earth, and carnitine moves stored fat into the cell to be burned as fuel… not stored around your gut."
[THE KIDNEY LINE GOES HERE]
"Beef testicle supplies the raw cholesterol and androgenic peptides your body actually builds testosterone from… that's where drive comes from."

RULES: one sentence, 18 to 30 words. Start with "The kidney". Name zinc and selenium. End on the benefit, said plainly, a bit savage, like a man at a bar. Do not say "starving", "deficient", "missing" or "modern men". Do not use the words nitric oxide. No inches, no numbers, no "cure". Plain hyphens only.
Make the 6 options genuinely different: two about the bedroom, two about the gym or physical work, one about the cold (tie to the Viking), one that covers blood flow in general.
