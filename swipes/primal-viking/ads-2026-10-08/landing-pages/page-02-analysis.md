Above questions: three VERIFIED BUYER quotes with age and a number ("27lbs in the 2 months"), plus "#1 Testo Support For Men" strap with a specific blood result: "total T jumped from 370 to 800 ng/dL." ## 6. Objection map
Quiz itself pre-handles "is this for me" via personalization. Handled on result page: side effects, TRT comparison, cholesterol/PSA/kidney FAQs, guarantee. Unhandled: why reindeer (mechanism is thin until the founder story near the bottom).

## 7. Copy strategy
Light micro-copy in a personalizing voice ("No judgment. This only shapes your recommendation."). Sensitive questions (morning erections) used as qualifier and commitment device. Result copy mirrors answers ("built for men in their 40s who want steady energy all day. The afternoon crash isn't on you").

## 8. CTA & friction
9 single-tap questions (2 multi-select with CONTINUE), ~6s fake "Building Your Primal Viking Profile..." load, then email gate (first name, email) with "Skip & see my results" (soft gate), then result with "CLAIM OFFER". Urgency: "UNLOCK 60% OFF AT THE END", "PRICE INCREASES IN 00:08:33", "LOW STOCK NOTICE (36 LEFT)" - manufactured.

## 9. Visual design
Dark mode, minimal, one question per screen, big tappable rows with radio icon, thin orange progress bar. Mobile-native.

## 10. Funnel position
Mid-funnel quiz -> BOFU offer on the same domain. Email capture happens before the offer (skippable). Likely backend: abandoned-quiz email/SMS, subscription retention, Libido Fire Drops cross-sell.

## 11. What works
- Quiz-as-discount-unlock ("UNLOCK 60% OFF AT THE END") gives sunk-cost momentum.
- Soft email gate with "Skip" keeps conversion while harvesting emails.
- Personalized result blurb mirrors the answers.

## 12. Weaknesses
- Product and reindeer mechanism hidden until the end. Fix: one-line mechanism teaser between questions.
- Result page drops visitors into a long generic PDP below the fold, diluting the recommendation.
- Evergreen timers + "36 left" repeated across pages.
