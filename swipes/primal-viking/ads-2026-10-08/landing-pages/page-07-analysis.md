## 12. Weaknesses
- Roughly 25 screens before any price; high drop-off risk. Fix: shorten or show reward earlier (as quiz-t1 does with "UNLOCK 60% OFF").
- Result data are incoherent with answers (age Under 30 -> chronological 27; T-score 25 vs 58 in the same screen; belly "Flat" -> "Beer Belly" copy) which undermines trust if noticed.
- Offer reveal shows a $403 list price with no bundle choice or detail on what's in the Ritual.
