# Organizing Swipes

A swipe is a winning ad or landing page worth modeling. Two branches, two different reasons you reach for each:

- `named-mechanism/` — ads, organized by MECHANISM, not brand. You reach for a mechanism when you ideate.
- `landing-pages/` — landing pages, organized by PAGE-TYPE (`events/`, `low-ticket/`, `workshops/`, `webinars/`). You reach for a landing page by what it's *for*, not by the psychological lever it uses.

## Folders to create as you go (named-mechanism/)
- curiosity-gap/    ads that withhold the answer
- cost-reveal/      ads that expose a hidden price or fee
- us-vs-them/       ads that frame a clear enemy
- demonstration/    ads that show the product working
- identity/         ads that speak to who the buyer wants to be

## What to capture per swipe
For each swipe, save a short note: the mechanism (or, for a landing page, the structure), why it worked, and the one thing you would steal. Not the whole ad theory. One line you can act on.

The screenshot itself lives in the swipe-type's `_assets/` folder and gets embedded in the note with `![[filename.png]]` — the image should be visible in Obsidian, not just linked out. That's the whole point: it survives even if the original source (an ad, a tool, a webpage) goes offline.

## How a swipe gets in
1. Screenshot it (full-page tool for landing pages, manual/clipper for ads).
2. Drop it in `inbox/`.
3. Ask Claude to file it — it reads the image, drafts the note (mechanism/structure → why it worked → what to steal), moves the image into the right `_assets/` folder, and embeds it.
