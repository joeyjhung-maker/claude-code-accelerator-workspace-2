## How a Mario launch runs

He ran seven launches in seven months. The shape barely changes:

| Launch | Dates | Price | Emails |
|---|---|---|---|
| OpenClaw for Creative Strategists bootcamp | 28 Mar – 2 Apr | $97 | ~17 |
| Elite CS mentorship (6 seats, application) | 11 – 15 Apr | high-ticket | 5 |
| Claude Code Accelerator | 27 May – 3 Jun | $297 (or 2 × $165) | ~20 |
| Creative Cartel (6 seats, NDA, application) | 19 Jun | high-ticket | 3 |
| Creative Diversity: Decoded (free class) + Luke's AI CS Masterclass (cold list) | 27 Jun – 1 Jul | free | ~25 |
| Copy Instincts | 3 – 9 Jul | $97 | ~20 |
| Scaling With Statics (free class) | 7 – 14 Aug | free | ~10 |
| WINNING Ad Workflows (free class) | 28 Aug – 3 Sep | free | ~10 |
| Long-Form Narrative Ads | 24 – 30 Sep | $97 | ~17 |
| Luke's fundraiser ($20 donation unlocks classes) | 2 – 7 Oct | donation | ~10 |

Between launches, almost one email a day: **story, then lesson, then a soft call to book a Genesis call.**

**The skeleton:**
1. **Open with a reason, not a pitch.** Partner "went too far" (#6), a reply-to-vote temperature check (#144), "this one's on us" after a run of paid offers (#108), or his own conversion story (#60).
2. **One idea per day, every one tied back to the offer.** Each email takes one angle on the same promise: an objection, a feature, a proof point, a story. The offer recap at the bottom barely changes.
3. **Price framed by an anchor that's real** ("Day 2 alone is a $3,500 mentorship people paid for", #72), or by a reason it's cheap (the partner insisted, #6, #15).
4. **Live-only bonuses** to get people to show up (a skill file, a swipe file, a bot), not in the replay.
5. **Real limits stated as real:** a 500-seat Zoom cap with live counts ("420 gone", #124). "No fake-scarcity games."
6. **Deadline day: three to five emails**, each a different last-call frame (two futures, takeaway, ultimatum, cost of waiting).
7. **Delivery emails carry the upsell** (the Genesis discount inside a Day 3 reminder, #89).

**House style:** short lines, one random word in CAPS per line, parenthetical asides, "Yo," to open and "Peace, Mario" to close, swearing, self-deprecation ("self-admitted lazy fuck"), and a P.S. that restates the deadline or the bonus. Joey's rules (Dan's voice, no corporate language) differ; take the moves, not the style.
