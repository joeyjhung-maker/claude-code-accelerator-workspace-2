## Universal formats (long-lived)

### 1. Red flag / three signs
**Why:** turns a vague fear into specific symptoms the viewer can check.
**Steal:** the count ("three signs") plus one vivid image per symptom.

Red-flag call-outs:
- [High blood sugar](https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/985013383630734)
- [Damaged nerves](https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/1051551083643630)
- [Kidney inflammation](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/377773828348823)
- [High cholesterol](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/1647041709176659)
- [Kidney inflammation v2](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/1026976251956349)
- [Numbness in hands or feet](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/1748873235893095)

Three signs:
- [Inflamed prostate](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/815279630486327) (the one read aloud in part 2; ~48M views per Kam)
- [Colon](http://facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/990487605822991)
- [Inflamed gut](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/313694457699212)
- [Toxic overload](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/223817104045533)
- [Three foods for the liver](https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/987039019317487)

### 2. Timeline ("what would happen if…")
**Why:** maps the result week by week, so the promise feels inevitable. Makes no direct claim.
**Steal:** lead with a deadline the viewer already has, then run the timeline.

- [Version 1](https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/1029270802538325)
- [Version 2](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/818263277002956)
- [One cup a day](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/459545116563389)
- [If you drink](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/1581521333246829)
- [One cup, another cut](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/1946703979115601)

### 3. Transformation
**Why:** a result gets past scepticism. Best with a number and a time frame.
**Steal:** "it's not [the two things you're trying], it's actually…"

- [This to this in two weeks](https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/931576945641045)
- [One inch off the waist](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/1369201757363191)
- [This to this, under two weeks](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/890514362774632)
- [Fasting sugar 156 to 117](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/949821523559782)
- [14 lbs in three weeks](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/429469969925923)

### 4. Lifestyle
**Why:** hangs a consequence on a habit the viewer already has.
**Steal:** open on the habit, not the condition.

- [Natural way to reduce blood sugar](https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/1064649432333795)
- [Waking at night to pee](https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/1037666841698721)
- [Alcohol and the liver](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/637394579148011)
- [Sex life](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/1108546333973751)
- [Italians, pizza and pasta](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/645591224525881)

### 5. Branded "us vs them"
**Why:** names the thing the market has settled for and shows it quietly failing them.
**Steal:** this is the ad for people already buying the category.

- [Not all coffee is equal](https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/982238323130890)
- [Why mushrooms](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/652372096472013)
- [Long "wholesome" VSL](https://www.facebook.com/ryzesuperfoods/videos/1421406945734410)

## Trendy formats (expect ~6 months)

### 1. Pixar / claymation
**Note:** Kam says it tends to sit at middle of funnel with a much higher new-customer cost than its popularity suggests.
- [Example 1](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1233372785282864) · [Example 2](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=2002480013683036) · [Example 3](https://drive.google.com/file/d/11gP-_ExK_ks_RYpWs3z-y80ttwziPHba/view)

### 2. Talking-head AI
**Note:** UGC without the creator. Cheap, so profitable. Doctors, historical figures.
- [Example 1](https://www.facebook.com/reel/1398946548933731) · [Example 2](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ADpGm7w9hSxFglDig4pCPrK_-BC8REBJ/view) · [Example 3](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1oNJ9luG0Wm3oB2OeIEehEgnnwd-JmKg1/view)

### 3. "Hidden knowledge" storytelling
**Note:** novelty. A secret or forgotten discovery tied to a culture or a historical figure.
- [Example 1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1tCUnF9Vzl2kN456LwwkOMEnMJewfRQbU/view) · [Example 2](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1pSzCyGbbuIQjNHMCyjqx6U1O9b9-q0f1/view) · [Example 3](https://www.facebook.com/61563899071618/posts/122193088388463302/)

### 4. Sliding text
**Note:** a long native ad delivered as scrolling text. Reads like a confession.
- [Example 1](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=690712510704043) · [Example 2](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1749953329324749) · [Example 3](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1613809683190722)

### 5. Long-form native
**Note:** forum-confession post with a provoking image. The raw, embarrassing ones perform. Kam calls natives penny stocks.
- [Example 1](https://docs.google.com/document/d/19jKdrINudFsb4nTK8VybGL372uAqmz2hAo85mxNu4Rg/edit) · [Example 2](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1StdL9aI0P4tzTQ2ds8kvs_ZT9m7uWt02AfCBHAb-was/edit) · [Example 3](https://www.facebook.com/permalink.php?story_fbid=pfbid02vA66tSZWmsQyvB3ngwYniVJwwoWY7RE5nKddBsWhv4g4ixCHhjadddLgDci7vERpl&id=61566176792424)
