# Made You Look - 527 Email Subject Lines

Source bank by Lawrence Bernstein and Art Crowley (2009), organised around curiosity, big benefit, compelling questions, controversy, qualification, fascination, free, news, numbered lists, savings, universal problems, and urgency.

![[2009-made-you-look-527-email-subject-lines.pdf]]

## How to use it

- Primary use: extra inspiration for email subject lines when the first ideas are flat or repetitive.
- Secondary use: selective inspiration for Skool post headlines when the underlying hook shape fits the post.
- Borrow the mechanism or tension, then rewrite from the actual brief, audience, offer, and voice.

> [!warning] Back-pocket source, not a template bank
> Do not blindly adapt these for Skool. Many are old, email-specific, hype-heavy, personalised, deadline-led, or dependent on context inside the email. Never import a name, number, result, deadline, or claim that is not true for the client. Mariobot may produce a stronger, more native headline; use this bank to widen options, not overrule better work.
