# Swipe: Pishuw DreamCradle — problem-checklist → product → benefit-checklist (structure only, off-niche)

Related: [[_organizing-swipes]]

**Seen running:** 2026-08-05

**Mechanism:** Two stacked checklists sandwiching the product. First checklist is pure pain, one fragment per line: "No Sleep, Constant Holding / Makeshift Sleep Solutions / Parent Guilt & Exhaustion." Product drops in the middle with its one differentiator stated as a spec ("15° Incline for Reflux Relief"). Second checklist is the benefit mirror: "Anti-Roll Safety Design / Hypoallergenic & Cozy / Better Sleep for Baby & You."

**Why it works:** the two checklists are mirrored 1-for-1 — three pains, three matching relief points — so the product visually resolves every line the reader just nodded along to. The single spec number (15°) in the middle sells "this is engineered," not "this is nice to have."

**The one thing to steal:** this is a baby-product ecom ad, not infoproduct/coaching — the niche doesn't transfer, but the shape does. Pain-checklist / product-with-one-spec / mirrored-benefit-checklist is a clean skeleton for any offer with 3 concrete pain points and 3 matching resolutions. Worth stealing the STRUCTURE for a Flexxable or JV ad, not the copy.
