# Swipe: "Copy My AI Agency" — $999 → $37 (old-price/new-price urgency)

Related: [[_organizing-swipes]] [[2025-12-23-zalo-kabche-old-way-vs-new-way-3-step-system]]

**Seen running:** 2025-12-23

**Mechanism:** Opens with a status-quo callout — "Still charging $500 for websites?" — aimed straight at the exact prospect (agency owners doing manual web/service work), then pivots to the model to copy: "the AI agency model that pays me $5K for 1-hour AI chatbot installations." Price block does the rest: $999 struck through, $37 landed, "LIMITED TIME" stamped over both.

**Why it works:** the opening question does instant self-selection — anyone still charging $500 for websites reads that as a callout, not an ad. The offer swap ("$500 for a website" → "$5K for a 1-hour install") reframes the entire unit economics before price ever comes up, so the $999→$37 drop lands as access to a system, not a discount on a product.

**The one thing to steal:** lead a price-drop ad with the OLD business model you're helping the reader escape, not the new price. The struck-through number only works because the reader has already been sold on "I do not want to be doing what I'm currently doing" one line earlier.
