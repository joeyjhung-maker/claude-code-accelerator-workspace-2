# Swipe: Client Accelerators — "Stuck at $2,000/Day (Not Because Your Ads Suck)" (ceiling reframe + trapped-money math)

Related: [[_organizing-swipes]]

**Seen running:** 2026-03-18

**Mechanism:** Names the ceiling the prospect is already living at ("$2,000/DAY") then immediately removes the obvious blame ("NOT BECAUSE YOUR ADS SUCK") before he can defend himself. Visualizes the ceiling as a literal number problem: "$2,000 x 40 DAYS = $80,000 TRAPPED" — turning a vague feeling of being stuck into a specific dollar figure sitting on the table. Names the real cause (waiting 40 days for backend sales while cash flow dies) and the fix (liquidate 80% same-day, scale to $18K/day). Closes with scaled social proof: "like 240+ others did" / "The Engine That Prints $53M a Year."

**Why it works:** most "you're leaving money on the table" ads make the reader feel dumb. This one pre-empts the defensive reaction ("not because your ads suck") in the same breath as the callout, so the reader stays open instead of arguing back. The $80,000-trapped math is the real hook — it's not a claim about the future, it's a reframe of money the reader already has sitting in their current funnel, which hits differently than a growth promise.

**The one thing to steal:** when you name a ceiling, immediately rule out the reader's own obvious self-blame theory before offering yours. And where you can, translate "stuck" into an exact number sitting on the table right now (not a future projection) — trapped money is a stronger visual than missed opportunity.
