# RYZE: "dude who smokes weed" animated timeline ad

Related: [[2026-10-03-video-ad-rules]] [[2026-09-29-kittyspout-sung-animated-ad]]

![[2026-10-04-ryze-weed-timeline-contact-sheet.jpg]]

No transcript is stored here (their copy). This is the structure and the compliance moves only.

## Structure

1. **Hook, on frame 0:** a third-person character with a vice (a weed smoker) starts the product every morning. Open loop: results will surprise you.
2. **Honest dip:** first sip tastes like a latte, nothing changes, he feels scammed.
3. **Gradual, experiential change:** mornings are less groggy, the fog lifts, he's locking in faster.
4. **"After a few more weeks":** ingredient by ingredient, each one framed as supporting a function.
5. **Expectation cap:** not superhuman, just feels like it.
6. **Identity payoff:** "the version of me that wasn't running on empty". He didn't quit the vice, he added one thing.
7. **Product proof:** full-spectrum blend, doses disclosed, third-party tested, organic, grown in California, 200,000+ daily drinkers.
8. **Offer:** 50% off, 30-day money-back, send the empty bag back, stock scarcity.

## Why it survives review in a hard category

- **Claims are hedged structure/function verbs** ("helps support"), never cure, treat or fix. No disease words.
- **Results are subjective feelings over weeks,** not measurable outcomes. No numbers attached to the result at all.
- **It caps expectations out loud,** twice: nothing changes at first, and you won't be superhuman.
- **Third-person character.** Nobody tells the viewer they have a condition (personal-attributes safe).
- **The visuals show the product and the process,** never a transformation. The bag, the pour, the cup, the ingredient, him working, cooking and running. No before/after.
- **The only numbers on screen are about the offer and the proof** (50% off, 30 days, review count, label). Never the outcome.
- **On-screen text is plain small captions.** No giant claim headlines.
- **The edginess is in tone** (weed, swearing), not in the claim. So the risky part of the ad is not the part reviewers police.

## What to steal for our ads (bizopp / income category)

- **Show the mechanism and the product, not the payoff.** Our equivalent of "no before/after" is no cash rain, coin bursts, cheques or money fountains.
- **Result numbers live in the voice and normal-size captions only.** No giant "$1.2M" or "$225,000" art.
- ~~Put the product (the book) on screen early and often.~~ Tried as a corner badge on 2026-10-04 and Joey killed it: it doesn't work for us. The book stays hidden until the reveal. (RYZE's product is the story; ours is the payoff of the story.)
- **Cap expectations inside the ad:** one client's result, an honest "this is not magic" beat.
- **Lean on product and credibility proof:** readers, guarantee, what's inside the book. Not outcome proof.
- **Timeline structure** is available to us too (/timeline-ad).
