# Swipe: RYZE mushroom coffee — "Red Flags" symptom self-diagnosis (video, 1:52)

Related: [[_organizing-swipes]] [[2026-08-05-pishuw-dreamcradle-problem-checklist]] [[flexxable-winning-ads]]

**Source:** https://www.facebook.com/100063662435276/posts/985013383630734
**Seen running:** 2026-10-03. Posted 29 March 2024. Asset is ~918 days old and still up.
**Engagement on the post:** 10K reactions, 9.2K comments, 13K shares.
**Format:** 9:16, 1:52, UGC creator, captions on every line (works sound-off).

**Mechanism:** Self-diagnosis. The ad opens by naming a condition and showing four everyday symptoms, acted out, two seconds each. The viewer ticks them off and qualifies themselves before anything is sold. Everything after that is a straight chain: cause, the fix that fails, a discovery, the mechanism, his own result, risk reversal.

This is the "Red Flag" format in Johnny Kam's five universal structures (Second Brain: `marketing/wiki/universal-ad-structures.md`).

**Why it works:**
- The hook is a checklist, not a claim. Nobody argues with a symptom list. They count.
- The symptoms are common enough that most people tick two of four. That's the trick.
- Symptoms are shown, not said. Five cuts in nine seconds, new room and new shirt each time.
- The solution is held back on purpose. "Simple fix, but first why this happens" buys ~40 seconds.
- Everything mirrors. Symptom → cause → mechanism → result line up one-for-one. Tired at the start, steady energy at the end. Cravings at the start, cravings gone at the end.
- The enemy is the default fix, not a person. It "masks symptoms" instead of fixing the root.
- It's found, not sold. A peer stumbled on it and hedges ("in my opinion").
- No price. The offer shows up at 1:44 as risk reversal only.

**The one thing to steal:** open on the symptoms, acted out, and make the result beat answer those same symptoms line for line.

**Don't copy:**
- The disease claims. Our version of that line is income claims.
- The 17 seconds of anatomy in the middle. That's the likely drop-off.
- The vague "browsing forums" discovery. Dan has a real one.

## Template (fill in the blanks)

```
1. HOOK (0–2s)
   Caption: "[CONDITION] RED flags." 🚩🚩🚩
   Visual: close-up of ONE object that screams the category. Slightly uncomfortable.

2. SYMPTOM RUN (2–9s) — no talking, acted out
   🚩 [Symptom 1]   (location A)
   🚩 [Symptom 2]   (location B)
   🚩 [Symptom 3]   (location C)
   🚩 [Symptom 4]   (location D)
   Rule: everyday, specific, most of the avatar ticks at least two.

3. REASSURE + OPEN LOOP (9–17s)
   "Don't panic. There's a simple way to fix this.
   But first, here's why it's happening."

4. CAUSE (17–30s)
   "See, [ONE NAMED CULPRIT] is supposed to [job].
   But when [what's broken], [symptoms follow]."
   Stakes: "And it's only a matter of time before [worse outcome]."
   Visual: one simple diagram. Not a lecture.

5. FALSE FIX (30–40s)
   "Most people [DEFAULT FIX 1] or [DEFAULT FIX 2].
   All that does is mask the symptoms. It never touches [culprit]."

6. DISCOVERY (40–48s)
   "So when I [true story of how I found it], I came across [new way]."

7. REVEAL + MECHANISM (48–65s)
   "It's this. [PRODUCT in hand]. Here's why it works."
   [Mechanism in one breath] + [one number] + [why it's been proven].
   Must answer the culprit from beat 4.

8. CALLBACK + OBJECTION SWEEP (65–75s)
   "So if you've got any of these red flags, [recommendation]."
   [Easy] + [cheap/quick] + [none of the false fix's downside].

9. RESULT (75–92s)
   "I've been doing this for [time]."
   [Symptom 1, reversed] / [Symptom 2, reversed] / [Symptom 3, reversed]
   [Third-party nod.]

10. ADVICE CTA (92–98s)
    "If I were in your shoes, I'd [get it] and [track one thing]."

11. RISK REVERSAL (98–108s)
    "[Guarantee in plain words]." + closing nothing-to-lose question.
```
