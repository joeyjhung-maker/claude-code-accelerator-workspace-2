# Swipe: "Chalkboard Native" structure — Question → Show Problem → Break Belief → Educate

Related: [[_organizing-swipes]]

**Seen running:** 2026-07-20 (captured as a structure breakdown post, not the raw ad — the poster flagged it as top-spend in their account for two weeks running)

**Mechanism:** This is a swipe of someone else's teardown of a native-style ad (handwritten chalkboard aesthetic, health/supplement niche), but the structure they extracted is the real asset: **1. Question → 2. Show problem → 3. Break belief → 4. Educate.** The example they broke down opens with "WHY THE LUMP WON'T LEAVE," lists what the reader has probably already tried and why it failed ("not the acid," "PPIs don't reach it," "70-80% get no relief"), then reframes the cause (a worn-down "shield" that takes 90 days to rebuild) before pointing at the fix.

**Why it works:** step 3 — "break belief" — is the piece most ads skip. Before this ad educates the reader on the real mechanism, it first kills the reader's CURRENT theory of why they've failed (the acid, the PPIs). That belief-break is what makes the educate step land as a revelation instead of a lecture — the reader has to let go of their old explanation before the new one can replace it.

**The one thing to steal:** don't jump from problem straight to mechanism. Insert a belief-break step that names and dismisses the reader's current (wrong) explanation first — "you've probably been told it's X. It's not X." — THEN reveal the real mechanism. The reveal only feels earned once the old theory is gone.
