# DR Craft Bench

Not competitors. Not weekly-tracked. A **swipe pool** of proven 7–9 figure direct-response brands, kept for the CRAFT they donate — hooks, mechanisms, formats — when a Tier-1 gap calls for a shape you don't have. Narrowed from Joey's 130-brand DR list, weighted for copy/mechanism transfer (he writes copy, not ecom visuals).

The weekly sweep skips this list. It spot-checks it on the **first Monday of the month** and surfaces anything scaling hard. Reach for it on demand when `/storm` needs a format donor.

| Brand | Craft it donates | Reach for it when… |
|-------|------------------|--------------------|
| Gundry MD | Advertorial / VSL — curiosity + cost-reveal | you need a long-form or advertorial angle |
| Dr. Marty's Pets | VSL: fear → mechanism → solution | building a problem-first VSL structure |
| Dr. Kellyann | Advertorial, 40+ weight loss, avatar-driven | writing to an older / specific avatar |
