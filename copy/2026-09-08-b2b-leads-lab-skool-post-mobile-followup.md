HL: Mobile numbers vs NO mobile numbers

FYI on mobile numbers:

Having one makes a BIG difference once your gift lands.

You can call 'em directly…

And we’ve found this helps a TON with booking more coffee dates.

No mobile?

Not the end of the world.

You can still follow up by email and LinkedIn.

They can both work.

Just not as powerful as picking up the phone.

It’s not easy… you need some cojones to do it…

BUT they will be some of the EASIEST “cold” leads you chat to - bc the gift has already opened the door

Just gotta ring and book that coffee date

So… when building your Dream 100, prioritise prospects with a verified mobile.

Not a total dealbreaker if you can’t find one…

But it definitely tips the odds in your favour.
