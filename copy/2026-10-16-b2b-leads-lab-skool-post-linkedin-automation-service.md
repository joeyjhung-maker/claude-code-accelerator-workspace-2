**The most boring business model I've ever been excited about**

Something came up on a call recently that kinda blew my mind.

And not 'cos it was complicated… but 'cos it was the OPPOSITE.

There are people out there charging $200–300 a month per client… just to run their LinkedIn outreach for 'em.

That's it.

That's the whole business.

You plug their account into an automation tool…
Pick who they should be reaching out to…
Write the messages…
And the tool does the sending every day on autopilot.

The client doesn't need to learn anything.

They don't even need to know what tool you're using.

All THEY see is conversations showing up in their LinkedIn inbox from people who actually wanna talk to 'em.

Look at it from the client's side…

Say you're a local accountant.

You KNOW LinkedIn could bring you clients.

But you're not gonna sit there sending 25 connection requests a day between tax returns.

So when someone says "I'll handle all of it for $250 a month"… that's a no-brainer.

Now do the maths on YOUR side.

25 to 35 clients at $200–300 a month… that's $5–10k a month, recurring.

And people doing this are making exactly that.

No big proposals or fancy funnels.

Just a simple service people need… that runs mostly on autopilot once it's set up.
