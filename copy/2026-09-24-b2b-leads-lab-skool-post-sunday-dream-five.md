**HL: Your Sunday “Dream Five” challenge**

Your Dream 100 is probably sitting in a spreadsheet gathering dust.

100 names feels like a LOT…

So most peeps do nothing.

Here’s your fix:

Pick FIVE prospects from that list right now.

Just five.

These are the peeps you’re going after this week.

Now map out four useful touches for each one.

(Not four “just checking in” messages)

Here’s what that might look like:

Touch 1: Send a locked gift or something physical they can’t ignore.

Touch 2: Pick up the phone while your name still rings a bell.

Touch 3: Connect on LinkedIn and genuinely engage with their stuff.

Touch 4: Email a relevant idea, win or case study that shows ya understand their world.

The order can shift.

The channels can vary.

But every touch needs to ADD something.

Not repeat the same message louder.

Five prospects.

Four touches each.

That’s 20 deliberate moves mapped out before Monday.

Go do it while your Sunday coffee’s still warm.

And report back with your work ;-)

Dan
