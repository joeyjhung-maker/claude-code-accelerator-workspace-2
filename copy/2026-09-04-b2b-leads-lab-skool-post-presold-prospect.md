HL: The call isn't where they decide

You ever notice how thunder always shows up AFTER the lightning?

The sky already cracked open… the energy already discharged… then a few seconds later, the boom.

That's what a sales call looks like when lumpy mail does its job properly.

By the time you're on the call with someone… they've already opened the box, read the letter, and had that little moment where they thought "huh, nobody's ever done this before."

The decision pretty much got made at their desk, alone, holding a gift they weren't expecting.

So when you hop on the call and they're smiling and asking YOU questions about how it all works…

You're not convincing anyone of anything… you're just the thunder.

The lightning already struck days ago when that package landed.

Most people spend all their energy getting better at the call itself… tighter scripts, sharper objection handling, better closes.

But what if the smartest move is making the call almost irrelevant before it even starts?

What would you change about your current outreach if you actually believed that?
