## Body

Yo,

I put the retainer model and the performance model side by side on five dimensions… and the retainer column reads like a list of reasons your spouse never sees you.

Have a look at the image.

Left column is the model you're probably running right now.

Right column is what happens when you stop selling your time and start taking a cut of revenue instead.

I'll walk you through it quickly because the table kind of speaks for itself.

**Who pays upfront?**

Retainer model, your client writes you a cheque before they've seen a single result… and they're already half-resenting you for it.

Performance model, they pay nothing upfront because you're working their EXISTING leads, so there's zero risk on their side.

**Who re-earns trust every month?**

Retainer, that's you… on a never-ending audition where one bad month means "we need to talk."

Performance, the results re-earn it FOR you… because every sale the bot books is proof you belong there.

**Who carries the risk?**

Retainer, the client carries all of it… and they KNOW that, which is why they watch your ad account like a hawk and ping you at 9pm on a Sunday.

Performance, YOU carry the risk… which sounds worse until you realise it's the reason they stop micromanaging you and start rooting for you.

**What's the income ceiling?**

Retainer, it's whatever you negotiated three months ago… and good luck raising it without a PowerPoint deck and a weird conversation.

Performance, it's 30-50% of every sale the AI employee generates… so your ceiling is their database, not your day rate.

**What happens when you go on holiday?**

Retainer, you're still "reachable" because the client is paying whether you're at the beach or not… so you never fully leave.

Performance, the bot keeps texting old leads, keeps booking calls, keeps closing while you're gone… because it doesn't need annual leave.

That's the whole table.
