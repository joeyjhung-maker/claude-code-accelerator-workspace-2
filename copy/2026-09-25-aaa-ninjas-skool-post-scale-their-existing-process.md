HL: Stop demoing what YOU built

Eric jumped on a call with an ecommerce owner doing 20–40 sales a day with a database of over 20,000 customers.

Solid business… and he wanted a demo within the next couple of days.

His REAL need was customer feedback.

Right now he speaks to customers one-to-one to learn what they love… what they hate… and what they’d change about the product or process.

And here’s the important bit…

He actually ENJOYS doing it because it gives him a real connection with his customers.

Most peeps hear “manual process” and immediately rush to automate the whole darn thing away.

But if Eric rolls up and demos a generic reviews bot or starts rattling through every shiny feature inside Wingman… he’s missed the point.

He’s prescribed the medicine BEFORE diagnosing the patient.

This is why we do a Doctor’s Diagnosis on the call.

First, ask the owner for his current feedback process or SOP.

What does he ask?

How does he open the conversation?

What makes the replies feel honest rather than like some corporate survey nobody wants to fill out?

Then use THAT as the knowledge base to train the Android.

The opening could be as simple as:

“Is this the same Dan who bought a pet food package from us a few months ago?”

“Phew! Didn’t wanna spam ya… I’m looking for some honest feedback on your order. Would it be okay if I asked a few questions in exchange for a 50% discount code?”

Now the Android can run his existing process across far more customers… then send him the transcripts so he can still read every juicy bit of feedback.

We haven’t removed the thing he values.

We’ve helped him do it at SCALE.

That prospect has basically handed Eric the product brief already.

So before your next demo, stop asking “Which bot should I show them?”

Ask what they’re already doing manually… what they REFUSE to lose… and how you can help them do more of it.

Don’t demo what YOU built.

Demo a faster, bigger version of what THEY already want.
