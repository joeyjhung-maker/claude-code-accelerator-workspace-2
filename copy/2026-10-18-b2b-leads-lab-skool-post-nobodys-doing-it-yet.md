HL: "Aren't we all just selling the same thing?"

If you've thought this lately… you're not the only one.

Because when you look around this group… a lot of us are leading with DBR.

So the worry is… isn't everyone doing this already?

Here's the thing.

Talk to 100 businesses… and you'll be lucky to find one that's actually running a proper AI qualification.

Zero.

Maybe one.

Saturation just isn't a thing yet.

Picture a roofing company that's been running ads for 3 years.

They've got thousands of old quotes just… sitting there collecting dust.

Nobody's following up on 'em… nobody's even thought about it.

And that's MOST businesses right now.

Which is why DBR is still the offer of the century.

It gets replies.

It starts conversations.

And once you're actually TALKING to a business owner… you can look under the hood and figure out what else they need.

DBR is the door opener.

Oh and the "aren't we all selling the same thing" bit?

Yeah, a lot of us in here are doing DBR… but across totally different niches and different countries.

So there's barely any overlap anyway.

The market isn't full.

It's basically empty.

So stop worrying about whether someone else is already doing it…

And go have the conversations this week 👊
