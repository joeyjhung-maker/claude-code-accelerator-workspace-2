HL: The 3 things that decide if your lumpy mail WORKS

Sending lumpy mail is a bit like fishing.

You can buy the fanciest rod in the shop…

But use the wrong bait… in the wrong water… at the wrong time…

And you’re coming home hungry.

Same goes for lumpy mail.

There are THREE things ya need to dial in.

**1. THE BAIT**

This is your gift, letter and reason to respond.

Everyone loves talking about WHAT gift to send.

But a fancy gift won’t rescue a boring generic letter.

The gift gets their attention.

The letter gives them a reason to chat.

**2. THE WHERE**

Most peeps are fishing in the same crowded holes.

Cold email.

LinkedIn DMs.

Paid ads.

Everyone is fighting for attention in the SAME places…

And somebody else owns the pond.

Google can chuck ya in spam.

LinkedIn can change the rules overnight.

Ad platforms can charge ya more whenever they fancy.

Meanwhile… lumpy mail puts you somewhere most of your competitors completely ignore.

Their DESK.

No spam folder.

No promo tab.

No ad auction standing between you and the prospect.

**3. THE WHEN**

This is your follow-up timing.

Track the delivery.

Then follow up while the gift is still fresh in their mind.

Calling their mobile is the strongest option.

Email and LinkedIn can work too.

Most peeps obsess over ONE piece and forget the rest.

Fancy gift… crowded channel… zero follow-up.

All three need to work TOGETHER.

Which one do ya want the most help with next? 👇

🎣 **BAIT** – the gift, letter and offer

🌊 **WHERE** – escaping the same crowded channels

⏰ **WHEN** – your follow-up timing and sequence
