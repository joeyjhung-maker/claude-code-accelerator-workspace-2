## Final shortlist

1. AI isn't just coming for your clients. It's coming for the number on your invoice.
2. AI has two ways to kill a service business: take the work… or crush what the work is worth.
3. Losing clients to AI is the threat everyone's preparing for. Nobody's preparing for what happens when you keep them.
4. If you're still selling output, AI doesn't have to beat you. It only has to make you look expensive.
5. AI replacement is only one jaw of The Service Provider Squeeze. The other one hits your rates.
6. Proving you're better than AI can work. The client stays. The squeeze hits your rate instead.
7. Retainers aren't getting harder to sell because clients got cheap. They're getting harder because AI made the work feel cheap.
8. AI doesn't have to replace you to hurt you. It only needs a seat in your client's head when they review your invoice.
9. The worst place to be in the AI economy is valuable enough to keep—and easy enough to lowball.
10. Stop worrying about what happens if AI replaces you. Start worrying about what clients will pay if it doesn't.
