There’s a big difference between TELLING a prospect their follow-up sucks…

And SHOWING ‘em.

One gets the door slammed in your face…

…the other gets you listened to.

So this is something you should be doing as much as possible: 

Audit your prospects!

Step 1: Wait until it's the weekend or after hours. 

Step 2: Go to their website. Put in an enquiry under a fake name.

Step 3: Wait.

See how long it takes for them to get back to you… IF they get back to you at all.

Step 4: Log your findings and then tell them what their follow up process is like. 

There’s a ton of sales data out in the wild already - anything over 1hr response times KILLS deals.

Help them understand how much a lost customer is worth - 5k? 10k? 30k?

Then book the coffee date and solve their problems.

What this does is put them in a problem state - they now feel the pain of lost sales every single day when their sales team clocks off

And now your solution feels more urgent by the minute.

Does it feel a bit sneaky? Maybe.

But all you did was ask the question a real customer would ask

The only thing you tested is whether anyone bothered to answer.

Try it on one prospect or two this weekend.
