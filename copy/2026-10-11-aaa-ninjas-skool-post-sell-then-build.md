HL: Sell THEN build (the ONE belief I see holding students back)

I've coached thousands of students at this point.

And the ONE belief that kills more momentum than anything else is this…

"I'll start booking coffee dates once everything is ready."

Ready means the demo is built.
The tools are set up.
The systems are in place.
The niche is locked in.
The website looks right.

And it FEELS like progress… which is exactly why it's so dangerous.

Because you can spend weeks in that mode and never talk to a single business owner… which means no feedback and no money coming in.

Just… dabbling.

And eventually a lot of students in dabbling mode quit… not because they failed… but because they never actually STARTED.

I get why it happens.

Reaching out before you feel ready is uncomfortable.

Your brain wants everything polished before you put yourself out there.

But our ethos has ALWAYS been sell THEN build.

You don't need a finished system to have a conversation.

Pitch database reactivation… the client only pays when you get results… so there's ZERO risk on their side.

Which means you don't need a fancy demo or a perfect website to get that first yes.

You just need to get stuck in and make mistakes fast.

Nothing else matters until you've got money coming in.

ESPECIALLY if time matters to you and you can't afford to burn weeks tweaking things nobody's asked for yet.

So this week… close the tabs… stop building stuff no one's seen… and book a coffee date.
