## Loop line options (mariobot)

Turn line, between Day 1 and Day 3. The script uses TURN 2, moved to present tense by the judge.
- TURN 1: Then day three showed up and something shifted.
- TURN 2: That's when things started to get interesting.
- TURN 3: And then, quietly, it began.
- TURN 4: But the next morning told a different story. (FAIL: the next stage is day 3, not the next morning.)
- TURN 5: Then something small happened that she almost didn't notice.

Line before Day 21. The script uses LAST 1.
- LAST 1: This is the part you were waiting for.
- LAST 2: And then day 21 hit.
- LAST 3: Now here's the morning that changed everything. (FAIL: promises more than day 21 delivers.)
