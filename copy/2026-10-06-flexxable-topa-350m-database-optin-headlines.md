# Flexxable / Topa 350M Database Opt-In Headlines

Date: 2026-10-06
Source: Genesis `opt-in-page-optimization-bot`, narrowed and judged against the local copy rubric.
Traffic: Warm SMS from Dan to the Flexxable list.

---

1. Get FREE Access to 350 Million B2B Contacts

2. Here’s the FREE 350 Million Contact Database Dan Just Texted You About

3. Create Your Free Topa Account to Search 350 Million B2B Contacts

4. Find Your Dream Prospects in a Database of 350 Million B2B Contacts, FREE

5. Search 350 Million B2B Contacts for FREE, With No Credit Card Required

---

## Recommendation

Lead with **#1**. It is the shortest, clearest continuation of the SMS and puts the two strongest conversion facts first: `FREE` and `350 Million B2B Contacts`.

Use **#2** as the first challenger. It has the strongest message match for SMS traffic, but it is longer on mobile.

## Rubric scorecard

| Gate | Result | Note |
|---|---|---|
| One clear idea per headline | PASS | Each option makes the offer obvious without adding a second argument. |
| Plain over clever | PASS | No metaphors, wordplay, or hidden offer. |
| Specificity | PASS | Every option names the 350 million B2B contact database. |
| Free offer stated explicitly | PASS | `FREE` appears in every option. |
| Message match | PASS | All five continue the promise made in Dan's SMS; #2 matches it most explicitly. |
| No invented proof, result, feature, or guarantee | PASS | Only supplied offer facts are used. |
| No false urgency | PASS | The uncertain Friday deadline is not turned into a hard claim. |
| No banned corporate language | PASS | No `leverage`, `solution`, `deliverable`, or `onboarding`. |
| No AI dashes or stacked abstract triplets | PASS | None present. |
| Mobile readability | PASS | #1 is strongest; #2 is the only option likely to wrap beyond two lines on a narrow screen. |

## Watch-outs

- #2 depends on all traffic coming directly from Dan's SMS. Do not make it the evergreen headline for other channels.
- #4 is more benefit-led, but `dream prospects` is softer than the literal database promise.
- The deadline should live in the subheadline or CTA only after Friday is confirmed.
