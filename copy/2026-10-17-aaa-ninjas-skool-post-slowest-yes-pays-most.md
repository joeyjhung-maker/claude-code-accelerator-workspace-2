HL: The slowest yes pays the most

Most of ya have had this happen…

You pitch a biz owner on reactivating their old leads.

You KNOW there's money sitting in their database.

They say "maybe."

Or "let me think about it."

Or they just… go quiet.

And most people take that as a no.

They move on… next prospect, fresh pitch.

I get it.

But here's what I've learned…

"Maybe" is not "no."

"Maybe" is "I don't believe you yet."

And… can ya blame 'em?

You're telling someone there's a pile of cash hiding in a list they wrote off years ago.

That's a big claim.

Belief takes time.

Jack Brandt took 5 to 6 months to convince ONE client they had gold in their database.

Five. To six. Months.

That client had 450K people sitting in their list doing nothing.

7 weeks after going live… $432,252 from 372 sales.

Read that again.

That's what a slow yes can turn into.

So what do you do while they make up their mind?

Follow up like a dog with a bone.

Don't let a good prospect go until they actually say no.

Mix it up… a call, a short Loom showing a result, an email, a WhatsApp.

And lower the bar to yes… offer a small no-risk test on a slice of their old leads, paid on results.

Make it stupid easy to say "go on then."

Keep it about THEIR money sitting in THEIR list.

Not about AI… not about you.

So here's my question…

Who's your slow yes?

The one you gave up on a few weeks ago cos they went quiet?

Go back to 'em this week 👊
