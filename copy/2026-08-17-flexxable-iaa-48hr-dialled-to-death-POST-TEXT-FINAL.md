## What this is

The argument is the Sidekick Hijack stacked with zero-downside — see [[argument-bank]]. The client's own sales team is the wave; the ad farms what it discards instead of competing with it. *"Those leads were dead to them"* is the line carrying it.

The mechanism arrives as a four-line sequence rather than the ellipsis ladder the template used. Priority → hammer → bin → my bot picks up. Shorter, and the bin is the pivot.

## Two things that changed from the 1-vs-10 template

**The promise dropped from ten retainer clients to three.** This makes the maths hold in the reader's head. Dan's own hook has agency owners struggling to land $3k/month retainers, so three of those is $9k against the $10k here. Ten was a claim; three is arithmetic.

**The "Ready to…?" question before the CTA is gone.** The template ran price → question → tap. This runs price → tap. Noted so it can be tested back in rather than rediscovered.

## Swapping the opener

Hook 1 is baked in. The other two hooks from [[2026-08-17-flexxable-iaa-48hr-dialled-to-death-TEXT-FINAL]] can take its place, with one caveat:

- **Hook 2** works as-is. It ends on *"I turned their trash into a $10k per month deal"*, which sets up "Here's how:" cleanly.
- **Hook 3** does not. It ends on *"this ONE deal-killing mistake"*, so "Here's how:" answers the wrong question. It would need "Here's what I stopped doing:" or similar.
