## The three I'd run

**1** is the best pure feed title. It promises a specific moment, then undercuts it in the
parenthetical — which is exactly Dan's format from the webby post, and the undersell is what makes
you click.

**2** is the shortest and the most Dan. It coins a phrase, and a coined phrase is the thing members
start repeating back to you in comments. If you want one that outlives the post, it's this.

**3** does the most work in the fewest words. Names the mechanism and the payoff, and "show up for
coffee" is the group's own language.
