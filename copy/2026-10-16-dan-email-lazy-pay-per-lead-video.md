Subject: The lazy way to run pay per lead (no ads)

Back in 2016 a client asked me to switch from a retainer to pay per lead…

And it was one of those decisions that totally changed my life.

Because PPL paid for my house and my wine cellar… all in about two years.

And today I still get paid per lead… without running a single ad. (More on that in a sec.)

But the bit everyone asks me about?

How do ya actually PRICE the leads.

Turns out it's stupidly simple.

All you need to do is take your client's average order value… and charge about 1% of it per lead.

So if a Mortgage broker's averaging $5,000 a sale?

That's a $50 lead.

Done.

There's no awkward negotiation.

No pulling numbers out of thin air.

Just a clean rule of thumb that makes the client go "yeah… that's fair."

In one deal we did… the client wanted 100 leads a week at $100 each… we then generated on Meta for about $25 each.

Which means we walked away with roughly $7,500 PER WEEK in gross profit.

From ONE client.

Now…

Is pay per lead perfect?

Nah.

There's risk.

Ad costs swing.

And don't get me started on sales teams that sit on a red-hot lead for three days then tell ya "your leads are crap."

But I figured out a way around ALL of that.

No ads. No funnels. I don't even generate a single lead anymore.

100 times easier.

And still paid on performance though so the upside is juicy.

Here's exactly how we run it today 👇
