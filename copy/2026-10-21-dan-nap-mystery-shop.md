## A) SKOOL POST (AAA Ninjas + MDAD)

HL: Turn a prospect's own website against them (nicely)

There's a difference between TELLING a prospect their follow-up sucks…

And SHOWING them.

One gets you argued with.

The other gets you listened to.

So here's a cheeky move Nat shared on one of our coaching calls.

Step 1: Go to the prospect's website. Put in an enquiry under a fake name.

Step 2: Pick a really sociable time. Like 9pm on a Saturday night.

Step 3: Wait.

See how long it takes them to get back to you… IF they get back to you at all.

Step 4: Go back to 'em and tell 'em what one lead like that is worth. Nat's example is 10 to 30 grand.

Then say what you'll charge per month to stop it happening.

Now it's not a claim they can argue with.

It's their OWN data… staring 'em in the face.

Does it feel a bit sneaky? Maybe.

But all you did was ask the question a real customer would ask.

The only thing you tested is whether anyone bothered to answer.

Try it on one prospect this weekend.

## B) EMAIL

Subject: The 9pm Saturday night test

Nat shared a cheeky move on one of our coaching calls.

Instead of TELLING a prospect their follow-up is broken…

You show them.

Here's how:

Go to their website. Put in an enquiry under a fake name.

Pick a really sociable time… like 9pm on a Saturday night.

Then just… wait.

See if anyone replies. And how long it takes.

Now when you go back to 'em you're not making a claim they can argue with.

You're holding up a mirror.

Tell 'em what one lead like that is worth (Nat's example is 10 to 30 grand) and what you'll charge per month to stop it happening.

Hard to argue with their own data.

Does it feel a bit sneaky? Maybe.

But you only asked the question a real customer would've asked.

The only thing you tested is whether anyone answered.

Pick one prospect and try it this weekend.
