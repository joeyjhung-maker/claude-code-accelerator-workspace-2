HL: Stop pitching the whole bloody pharmacy

Most agency owners walk into a pitch and empty the whole medicine cabinet on the table.

“We do chatbots, voice AI, workflow automation, reputation management, database reactivation, CRM buildouts…”

And the prospect's eyes glaze over somewhere around item three.

Not because the stuff isn't valuable.

But because they came in with ONE headache… and you've asked them to diagnose themselves and choose between six different pills.

That's backwards.

You're starting with YOUR toolbox.

Not their problem.

They don't know what they need.

They just know something HURTS.

Your job isn't to list everything you COULD do.

Your job is to find the thing that's bleeding… and stop the bleeding.

That's it.

That's your entire first conversation.

One painkiller.

Not a supplement stack.

So how do you pick the right painkiller?

Simple.

Find the problem that's closest to REVENUE.

Not the sexiest bot.

Not the most technically impressive automation.

The thing that puts money in their pocket the fastest.

Because when a business owner sees revenue show up that wasn't there before…

They stop viewing you as a “vendor” and start viewing you as a partner.

And partners get asked to solve MORE problems.

Here's a clean example of how this can play out…

Say you're working with adventure parks.

They've got a database full of past customers who came once… had a great time… and then just… vanished.

Nobody followed up.

Nobody invited them back.

That's money sitting in a spreadsheet collecting dust.

So instead of walking in and pitching four different services…

You lead with database reactivation.

“Let me wake up the customers you've already paid to acquire and get 'em back through the doors.”

That's a painkiller.

It's urgent.

It's obvious.

And the result is measurable in dollars.

Now here's where it gets interesting…

Because once you reactivate that database and customers start flowing back in…

The business owner starts noticing OTHER leaks.

“We're getting more traffic now but our reviews are outdated…”

Cool.

That's your second conversation: reputation management.

“We're getting more enquiries but nobody's responding fast enough on the website…”

There's your third: live chat.

“We're missing calls after hours and losing bookings…”

Fourth: voice AI.

See what happened?

You didn't PITCH four services.

You solved ONE problem…

And the result EXPOSED the next bottleneck.

Each new service becomes the logical next step…

Not because you shoved it down their throat…

But because THEY felt the gap.

That's the difference between being a pushy vendor with a laminated menu…

And being the person they call every time something breaks.

The first way gets you ghosted.

The second gets you embedded in their business.

So before your next pitch…

Grab a notes app or a napkin or whatever you think on…

And map three things:

**1)** What's the ONE problem closest to revenue for this prospect?

That's your painkiller.

That's the ONLY thing you pitch.

**2)** If you solve that problem successfully… what's the NEXT bottleneck that will naturally surface?

That's your second service.

**3)** And what does solving THAT expose?

There's your third.

Now you've got a logical expansion path.

Not a menu.

A SEQUENCE.

Where every step earns you the right to offer the next one.

Stop trying to sound impressive by listing everything you can build.

Start being useful by fixing the one thing that actually hurts.

Land with the painkiller.

Expand when the next bottleneck appears.
