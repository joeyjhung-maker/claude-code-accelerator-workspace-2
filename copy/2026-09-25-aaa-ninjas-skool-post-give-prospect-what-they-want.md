# AAA Ninjas — Give the prospect what they want

Date: 2026-09-25 · Client: Flexxable / AAA Ninjas · Status: DRAFT

---

HL: Your prospect will tell ya what to sell next

Most peeps go into a prospect call with ONE thing on their mind.

DBR.

And that makes total sense because DBR is our bread and butter.

It puts more money in the client’s pocket… which is usually the MOST important problem we can solve for any biz.

So yeah… lead with DBR.

But don’t let that stop ya from listening to everything else the prospect says.

Because quite often they’ll tell you EXACTLY what to sell them next.

Eric had a call with an ecommerce owner doing 20–40 sales a day with a database of over 20,000 customers.

DBR is the obvious first play and he’s gonna offer it.

But the owner also told him about another pain.

He wants more customer feedback… and right now he’s getting it by having one-to-one conversations with customers himself.

He enjoys the connection and gets honest answers about what they love, hate and want changed.

The problem is he can only do so many of those conversations manually.

So give the man what he wants!!

We don’t need to build some fancy new bot from scratch.

Ask for his feedback process or SOP… then use it as the knowledge base to tweak our Social Replies Android.

It can reach out to past buyers, ask permission to get some feedback and offer a discount code in exchange for a few answers.

Then we send the transcripts back so the owner still gets all those juicy insights… without personally holding every conversation.

Simple.

DBR makes him more money.

The feedback Android solves another problem he actually cares about.

Eric sells another useful solution… and the client feels like someone has FINALLY listened to him.

Win win.

That’s how ya build great clients who have a reason to stick around for YEARS.

Not by dumping every bot you own onto the table… but by keeping your ears open and solving the problems they hand you.

If a prospect tells ya what they want… and you can deliver it by easily tailoring something we already have… give ’em what they want.

Be helpful.

Solve problems.

Get paid.
