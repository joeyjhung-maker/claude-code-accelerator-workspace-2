HL: How much should you charge per lead? (my 1% rule)

Back in 2016 a client asked me to switch from a retainer to pay per lead…

And pay per lead paid for my house and my wine cellar in about two years.

But the bit everyone asks me about…

How do ya actually PRICE the leads?

Turns out it's stupidly simple.

Take your client's average order value… and charge about 1% of it per lead.

Mortgage broker averaging $5,000 a sale?

That's a $50 lead.

Done.

No awkward negotiation.

No pulling numbers out of thin air.

Just a clean rule of thumb that makes the client go "yeah… that's fair."

One real deal we did… 100 leads a week at $100 each… generated on Meta for about $25 each.

That's roughly $7,500 a week in gross profit.

From ONE client.

Now…

Is pay per lead perfect?

Nah.

There's risk.

Ad costs swing.

And the one that used to drive me mad… the sales team sits on a red-hot lead for three days then comes back with "Dan, your leads are crap."

But that last one taught me something.

No ads. No funnels. I don't even generate a single lead anymore.

Still paid on performance though.

100 times easier.

I broke the whole thing down here 👇

https://youtu.be/D_TRoO0EWiU

P.S. On average our clients closed about 5 out of every 100 leads. Watch what we do with the other 95… that's where the real money is hiding.
