HL: We built a LinkedIn outreach tool that sends your REAL pitch

The philosophy behind this thing is dead simple.

Send a plain, honest pitch that sounds like YOU actually wrote it.

There's a website-scanner feature that pulls real info from the prospect's actual site, so the opening line is genuinely relevant to their business.

The flow is about as simple as it gets.

Connect your LinkedIn, build a campaign, it sends the connection request, waits, and follows up if there's no reply.

Under the hood it's rate-limited to roughly 25 connection requests a day, around 500 a month per account.

Timing between actions is randomised, and everything's routed through a detection-avoidance layer that geo-matches the IP to your real location… so your account stays safe.

On accounts reaching active LinkedIn posters, Jonathan's seeing 60-70% connection-acceptance rates and about a 50% reply rate.

You also get lead-ranking built in, green amber red, plus an ad-spend checker at the same tier.

It goes live the week of the 21st Sept… same week as the B2B Gifts UK launch.
