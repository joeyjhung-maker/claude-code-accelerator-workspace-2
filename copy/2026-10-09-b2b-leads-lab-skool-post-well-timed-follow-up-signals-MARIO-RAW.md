HL: What a single well-timed follow-up can do that ten generic ones can't

Most follow-ups fail before you even hit send.

Not because the message sucks.

Because the TIMING is irrelevant.

"Just bumping this up" is code for "I have nothing useful to say but I want something from you."

Your prospect can smell that a mile away.

And ten of those in a row doesn't make you persistent… it makes you annoying.

Here's what actually works.

ONE follow-up that lands while the prospect is already IN MOTION.

Meaning they're actively thinking about the problem you solve… right now… today.

So before you type a single word, look for signals.

Are they hiring for a role that hints they need help?

Have they just opened a new location or launched something?

Are they commenting on posts about a topic YOU can help with?

That last one is the juiciest.

Because LinkedIn is FULL of people posting lead magnets and free guides… "here's how to fix X" type stuff.

If your prospect commented on one of those posts…

They just raised their hand and told the entire internet they want help with that exact thing.

Now imagine your follow-up says something like…

"Saw your comment on [name]'s post about [topic]… we actually help companies sort that out. Worth a quick chat?"

That's not a cold message anymore.

That's a RELEVANT message landing at the exact right moment.

Completely different energy.

The prospect feels seen instead of stalked.

If you've got access to LinkedIn Signals inside Topa, it can watch for this stuff automatically… tracking who interacts with a chosen person's posts and who posts a chosen keyword.

Otherwise you can absolutely do this manually.

Scroll through the comments on a few big posts in your space every morning.

Takes ten minutes.

The point is simple.

Stop trying to follow up MORE.

Start following up at the moment it actually MATTERS.

One well-timed message beats ten lazy ones every single day of the week.
