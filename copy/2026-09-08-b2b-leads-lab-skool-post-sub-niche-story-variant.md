HL: The narrower your pitch, the HARDER it hits

Imagine walking into a room of 500 biz owners

and yelling “WHO WANTS MORE CUSTOMERS?”

Everyone kinda nods… but nobody feels like you're talking to THEM.

Now…

Imagine pulling one trampoline park owner aside and saying “I can fill your Saturday slots with birthday parties so you’re not relying on rainy days to fill the place”

That's the difference between broad vs narrow.

When ya pick a tight sub-niche, your entire message changes.

The outcomes get specific…

The pain points hit harder…

And the person hearing it feels like you ACTUALLY understand their world.

Because you DO.

You've taken the time to learn what keeps THAT type of owner up at night, not just “business owners” in general.

Generic outreach gets treated like junk mail because it reads like junk mail.

Narrow outreach reads like someone who gets it… and that's the person they wanna
jump on a call with.

So before ya send another campaign, ask yourself… could I make this MORE specific to ONE type of business?

If yes, do that first - even if it FEELS restrictive. Your results will be better
