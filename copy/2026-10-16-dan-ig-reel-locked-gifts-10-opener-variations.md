# Dan IG reel: 10 opener variations (locked gifts, comment "B2B")

Built from the "1,000 Viral Hooks (PBL)" template shapes. Hook bot drafted 20, I took 10 and cut the lines it invented (call back "within an hour", "handwritten", "single afternoon", "same day", "paying us monthly", "less than a week of ad spend", "Royal Mail", "instead of an email like everyone else"). Every number is from Dan's 7 Oct post.
Each replaces the first lines of the script, up to and including "There was a letter with it". Frame 0 is the caged bottle in Dan's hand for all of them.

1. [YOU KNOW THIS INBOX]
You know when you get a cold email and delete it before the second line?
This guy sent me a cage instead.
(shape: "You know when…" pattern interrupt. Mildly pain-led, so use it as the one pain option)

2. [SEVEN FIGURES ON ADS]
I've spent multiple seven figures on ads trying to get in front of business owners.
Nothing has worked like a gift they can't open.
(shape: "I've done big thing, nothing worked like small thing")

3. [60% RESPONSE RATE]
This is what a 60% response rate looks like in cold outreach.
Not an email. Not a DM.
A bottle I couldn't open.
(shape: "This is what X looks like")

4. [WOULD YOU CALL BACK?]
Would you call a stranger back if they sent you champagne you couldn't open unless you rang them?
I did.
(shape: "Would you…? Because I did")

5. [WE COPIED IT. 70 SENT.]
We copied one thing someone did to get my attention.
We sent 70 of them to business owners we wanted to work with.
What happened next is why I'm making this video.
(shape: cliffhanger "what happened next")

6. [HE LOCKED THE BOTTLE]
A bloke called Jonathan Matthews wanted my attention.
So he sent me champagne I couldn't open unless I rang him.
(shape: "Person wanted X, so he did Y". Drop the Jonathan line later in the script)

7. [WHOLE OFFICE BUZZING]
The whole office crowded round a cage with a bottle of champagne inside.
Nobody could open it.
Then I read the letter.
(shape: mini-story scene)

8. [10 GIFTS → 6 CALLS → 3 CLOSED]
We sent 10 locked gifts.
6 calls booked.
3 closed.
(shape: "this is what X looks like", numbers first)

9. [BEAT SEVEN FIGURES?]
If I told you a locked bottle of champagne has worked better than seven figures of ads, you'd think I'd lost it.
(shape: "If I told you… you'd think I'd lost it")

10. [WHEN EMAILS STOP WORKING]
Everyone tells you to send more emails and more DMs.
Nobody tells you what to do when that stops working.
A bloke called Jonathan showed me.
(shape: "Everyone tells you X, nobody tells you Y")
