# The Pay Cut Before the Pitch — 10 hooks

All 10 open the same body: the market has already cut the going price of output-based service work before the provider quotes. The hook shows the wound and withholds the explanation.

## Shortlist

### 1 — Contrarian scene

You’re not walking into a negotiation.

 

You’re walking into the scene of your own pay cut.

**Open question:** How can my pay have been cut before the negotiation began?

**Why it pulls:** It turns a normal pitch into a crime scene, gives the cut physical reality and stops before naming what did it.

### 2 — Lived-moment curiosity

The client hasn’t lowballed you yet.

 

But your fee was already bleeding when the brief landed.

**Open question:** What wounded my fee before the client even named a price?

**Why it pulls:** It breaks the expected order of a lowball and puts the wound inside a moment the reader already knows: a new brief hitting the inbox.

### 3 — Urgency / visual intrusion

Open the next brief carefully.

 

There may already be a knife buried in its budget.

**Open question:** What put the knife there before the brief reached me?

**Why it pulls:** It makes the very next brief feel dangerous without explaining the mechanism, so the shared body gets to reveal what the knife is.

## Remaining seven

### 4 — Curiosity

Somewhere between “new enquiry” and “send your quote,” a chunk gets ripped out of your fee.

**Open question:** What happens in that gap, before I name my price?

### 5 — Call-out

Agency owners: the next brief can arrive with chunks already bitten out of your fee.

**Open question:** What took the bite before I entered the pitch?

### 6 — Curiosity

Your next client may never lowball you.

 

The brief will do it for them.

**Open question:** How can a brief lowball me before I quote?

### 7 — Contrarian belief attack

“Charge what you’re worth” won’t stop a knife that lands before your quote does.

**Open question:** What cuts my fee before my pricing advice can matter?

### 8 — Curiosity / compression

The new lowball doesn’t wait for your quote.

 

It cuts first.

**Open question:** How can the lowball land before I name a number?

### 9 — Call-out

If you sell services, your fee can be gutted before your name even touches the brief.

**Open question:** What guts the fee before the buyer knows who will do the work?

### 10 — Emotion / inbox scene

A full brief can hit your inbox with your fee already picked clean.

**Open question:** Who or what picked the fee clean before it reached me?
