## Closing passage, two options (mariobot, two passes, judged)

Modelled on RYZE's "You're not superhuman… / Eventually, you catch yourself… / You didn't overhaul your whole life…". Three jobs: the cap, the catch, the shrink.

### Option A: end of the day (78 words)

You're not some brand new version of yourself, you just feel like the one your family remembers.

[DAY 21]

Eventually, it's the end of a full day, everything handled, house finally quiet, and you're still standing.

Not dragging yourself to the couch, not running on whatever was left in the tank.

You're just there, and you go, "There she is."

You didn't rebuild your life from scratch, you just changed what was in the cup for the first ten minutes.

### Option B: the kitchen, the first ten minutes (80 words)

You're not glowing or floating or any of that, you just feel like you again.

[DAY 21]

Eventually, you're in the kitchen, ten minutes into your morning, holding your cup, and you notice something weird.

The day hasn't even started yet but you already feel ready for it.

It just feels like a hug you can drink.

And you go, "So this is what I had in me the whole time."

You didn't give up your coffee routine, you changed one cup.

### Judge

- PASS: both lint clean, one sentence per line, no swearing, no health claim, no ingredient names.
- PASS: both bring back the hook's ten minutes. A does it in the last line. B sets the whole scene there.
- PASS: "a hug you can drink" and "surviving/running on what was left" trace to real reviews. "There she is" comes from "I've found my self again".
- Judge cuts: ", calm, clear, present" from A (three abstract words in a row, hard rule). ", warm and quiet and yours" from B (triplet).
- FLAG: both run about 80 words. The model passage is 58.
- FLAG: the hook promises the last stage "will blow your mind". Both options are quiet. RYZE's own payoff is just as quiet.
- Dropped: two other mariobot versions. One had a garbled quote. One never brought the ten minutes back.
