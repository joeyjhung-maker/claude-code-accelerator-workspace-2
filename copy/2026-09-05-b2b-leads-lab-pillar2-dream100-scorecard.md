If you followed that process, you should be sitting on a decent shortlist of companies.

But a list of company names isn't the same as a list of companies worth spending money on.

Lumpy mail costs real money… and more importantly, it costs you TIME to put together.

So before you send a single piece, every company on your list needs to pass a simple scorecard.

Do not skip this step!!

5 checks, 1 point each.

CHECK 1: Right PERSON - must be an individual with actual buying authority. Senior but not TOO senior (depends on size of company). Owner, marketing director, sales director… someone who can actually say yes. Not "info@company.com", not reception, not a generic contact form.

CHECK 2: Right Address - a current physical address that reaches THEM personally. Not a PO box, not a co-working front desk, not a stale address off a 3-year-old directory. (If their Linkedin says they live in Scotland but the company head office is in London - I would not send)

CHECK 3: Verified Follow-Up Info - mobile AND email confirmed BEFORE you mail. Same rule as last week, repeating it word for word because people keep ignoring it… if you cannot find their mobile number, DO NOT SEND. Use Topa to find this.

CHECK 4: Buying Signal - are they running ads, collecting leads, running retargeting, are they hiring, have they been in biz for 5+ years?

CHECK 5: Budget Fit - does the site, reviews, and team size say they can actually afford you? A 2009-looking website with 4 Google reviews and 2 employees on Linkedin is probably a size mismatch.

I would only mail to peeps with all 5 points checked.
