Happy Sunday.

Old school direct mail legend Gary Halbert had this thing about how people sort their mail.

Standing over the bin, everybody makes two piles.

The A-pile is stuff that looks personal… and it ALWAYS gets opened.

The B-pile is anything that screams "I want to sell you something."

And the B-pile mostly ends up in the bin.

Honestly… most of our outreach lands in the B-pile before anyone even reads a word.

Which is why I love the locked gift so much.

You FedEx someone a box.

Nobody is binning a FedEx box.

They open it… and it gets even MORE intriguing, because there's a genuinely valuable gift sitting inside.

A bottle of whisky… or a YETI tumbler.

But here's the kicker…

The gift is LOCKED.

They literally have to call you to get it unlocked.

Think about that for a sec.

You've skipped the bin, skipped the "delete" button, and given them a reason to actually pick up the phone.

That's about as A-pile as it gets.

Dan
