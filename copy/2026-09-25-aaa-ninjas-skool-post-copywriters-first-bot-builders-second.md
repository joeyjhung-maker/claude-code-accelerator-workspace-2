# AAA Ninjas — Copywriters first, bot builders second

Date: 2026-09-25 · Client: Flexxable / AAA Ninjas · Status: JOEY FINAL

---

HL: Did 10,000 leads fail… or did the CAMPAIGN fail?

One of our members was chatting with the MD of a large claims company recently.

They’d already handed 10,000 old leads to another provider who used AI voice calls, WhatsApp and messaging to reactivate them.

The result?

Virtually NO uptake.

The provider’s verdict was that the leads had been worked too hard and there was nothing left.

Most peeps hear that and walk away.

Fair enough on the surface… but what ACTUALLY failed?

Was it the list?

Or was it the campaign?

Those are two very different diagnoses.

These leads were all from within the last year and originally came through Facebook ads, Google ads and door-to-door activity.

Now they COULD be exhausted.

But one failed campaign doesn’t prove it.

Maybe the message was rubbish.

Maybe the offer gave them no reason to reply.

Maybe the “conversation” felt like 10,000 peeps getting blasted by the same robot at once.

This is why we’re copywriters and direct-response marketers FIRST… and bot builders SECOND.

It doesn’t matter how clever your AI is if the lead reads the message and feels absolutely nothing.

So when this objection lands, don’t argue… and don’t chuck the previous provider under the bus.

Ask how the leads were generated.

Ask whether the business is still getting fresh enquiries every day.

Then propose a small, safe test.

Take a few thousand leads… run them through a different approach using simple SMS conversations… and take the risk away from the client.

If nobody responds, you’ve got far better evidence that the database really is cooked.

But if they DO respond… you’ve just uncovered an asset everyone else had written off.

So don’t blindly accept someone else’s diagnosis.

Test it properly and EARN the conclusion.

Dan
