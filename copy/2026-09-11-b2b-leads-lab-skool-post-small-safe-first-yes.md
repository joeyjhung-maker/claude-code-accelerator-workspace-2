HL: The TINY offer that unlocks BIG deals

I’ve never seen a gent get down on one knee at a coffee shop with someone they just met…

Yet most peeps jump on their first coffee date and pitch a chunky monthly retainer.

Thousands of dollars.

6m contracts.

A YUGE leap of faith from a prospect who barely knows ya.

Even if they liked the gift…

Even if they’re interested in the offer…

That’s still a BIG first yes.

So we do the complete opposite.

Make the first yes small and safe.

For us, that might be a $500-$750 PAID test.

Enough that they’ve got skin in the game…

But not so much that it needs three meetings and a board vote.

Then we structure the deal around performance.

If they don’t get paid… we don’t get paid.

That removes a ton of risk from their side.

Here’s important bit for those paying attention:

Small does NOT mean free.

Free requires zero commitment.

Paid gets their attention.

And when the test works… the next yes becomes much easier.

This is how we upsell, crosssell, upsell

And it’s possible to turn a $500 client into $50K.

So on your next coffee date…

Don’t try to sell the marriage.

Sell the second date.
