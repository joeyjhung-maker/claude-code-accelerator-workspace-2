## PRIMARY TEXT

The wall between you and big AI clients isn't your skills… it's that your pitch sounds like "I want you to fire your people."

And honestly, you're not wrong to worry about that.

Because that IS how most business owners hear it.

You walk in talking about AI and automation… and the guy across the table is thinking about his sales team of eight, wondering which ones you're about to make redundant.

So he says no, and not because he doesn't believe AI works.

He's just not going to be the one who fires Dave.

Fair enough.

But here's what I worked out when I kept hitting that same wall with bigger clients.

I'd been trying to get at their FRESH leads, the ones coming in from Meta, Google, whatever.

The clients with real sales teams wouldn't budge.

They had humans working those leads already, and they weren't about to replace a process that was working.

So I asked a different question.

"How long do your sales guys actually call a lead before they move on?"

The client told me: "After 48 hours, we've dialled them 16 times."

"Then we move on."

I asked: "So after 48 hours… they're pretty much dead to you?"

"Yeah, we might try them a few times a year, but they're dead to us."

"OK… so if they're dead to you, would you API those leads to us after hour 48, and just pay per appointment set?"

"We cover all costs."

"Hell yeah."

That was it.

Nobody got fired.

The sales team keeps working every fresh lead for the first 48 hours, same as always, and they keep their jobs, their pipeline and their whole routine.

Our AI doesn't show up until hour 49, on leads the humans have already given up on.

And this is the bit most people miss about why it works so well.

After 16 dials in two days their interest hasn't disappeared, what's died is their willingness to pick up the phone from that number again.

They've been dialled to death.

Which is exactly why a text works where call number 17 never would.

A short SMS lands in their pocket, kicks off a real back-and-forth convo, qualifies them, and books a sales call.

The AI handles all of it, built on 2.5 years of testing that gets 30-60% response rates.

The client isn't risking anything, because these leads were already written off.

No ad spend involved, no creatives to build, no campaign to manage.

And because you're only touching leads they'd abandoned… there's almost zero resistance when you pitch it.

One client on this model pays me $10k a month plus commission.

From leads his business had already decided were worthless.
