## PRIMARY TEXT

A client told me his team dials every lead 16 times then bins them… so I asked "can I have your dead ones?"… he said hell yeah, and now that deal pays me $10k a month.

Let me walk you through how that convo actually went.

I wanted the fresh leads, the ones coming in hot off Meta and Google.

But this client had a full sales team already working those leads, and he wasn't about to fire anyone so some AI bot could take their place.

Fair enough.

So I asked him a simple question: "How long do your guys actually call a lead before they move on?"

He said: "After 48 hours, we've dialled them 16 times."

"Then we move on."

Sixteen times in two days, then they're done.

I asked: "So after 48 hours, those leads are pretty much dead to you?"

He said: "Yeah, we might try them a few times a year, but they're dead to us."

So I asked one more question.

"If they're dead to you… would you API them over to us after 48 hours, and just pay per appointment we set?"

"We cover all costs."

He said: "Hell yeah."

And that was the whole pitch.

Nobody got fired, nobody's job was threatened.

His sales team keeps every fresh lead for the first 48 hours, same as always.

Our AI starts at hour 49.

Now here's what most people miss about leads that have been "dialled to death"…

Sixteen calls in two days doesn't kill someone's interest in what you sell, it kills their willingness to pick up the phone.

They see the number, they already know it's another sales call, and they just… stop answering.

Which is exactly why a text works where call number 17 never would.

A short SMS feels completely different.

It's low pressure, they can reply in their own time, and it kicks off a real back-and-forth convo that qualifies them and books a sales call automatically.

We've been building and testing this AI plus SMS system for over 2.5 years, and it pulls 30-60% response rates on leads the client had already written off.

And here's why this is probably the easiest thing you'll ever pitch to a business owner…

You're not asking them to trust a bot with their fresh leads, and you're not asking them to make anyone redundant.

You're just asking for the leads they've already given up on, and offering to cover ALL the costs yourself.

The only time they pay you is when you book a real appointment their sales team can close.

There is almost no way to say no to that.
