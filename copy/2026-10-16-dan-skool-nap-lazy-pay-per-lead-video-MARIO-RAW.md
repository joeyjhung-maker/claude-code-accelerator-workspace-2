**HL: The dumbest pricing hack I ever stumbled into**

Most agency owners overthink pricing.

I used to as well.

Then a client asked me to switch from retainer to pay per lead back in 2016…

And that one conversation basically paid for my house and my wine cellar in about two years.

But here's the bit nobody talks about…

How do ya actually PRICE the leads?

Turns out it's stupidly simple.

Take your client's average order value… and charge about 1% of it per lead.

Mortgage broker averaging $5,000 a sale?

That's a $50 lead.

Done.

No awkward negotiation. No pulling numbers out of thin air.

Just a clean rule of thumb that makes the client go "yeah… that's fair."

One client we ran this with… 100 leads a week at $100 each… generated on Meta for about $25 each.

That's roughly $7,500 a week in gross profit. From ONE client.

Now…

Is pay per lead perfect?

Nah.

There's risk. Ad costs swing. And don't get me started on sales teams that sit on a red-hot lead for three days then tell ya "your leads are crap."

But I figured out a way around ALL of that.

No ads. No funnels. I don't even generate a single lead anymore.

Still paid on performance though.

100 times easier.
