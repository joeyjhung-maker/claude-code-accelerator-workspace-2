HL: Stop sending gifts to companies (they can't open a box)

Dan here…

Most people start their outreach list with company names.

"I wanna land Adventure World… I wanna get in front of Timezone… I wanna work with that big resort group up the coast."

And yeah, that's a fine place to START thinking.

But it's a terrible place to stop.

Because a company doesn't open your package.

A company doesn't read your letter over their morning coffee.

A company doesn't pick up the phone and say "mate, that was brilliant… let's chat."

A person does.

And not just ANY person…

The one who can actually say yes.

So before you spend a cent on a gift or write a single word of your letter…

You need to answer one question with a specific name and face.

"Who is the individual human being that has the authority to buy what I'm selling… and would personally care about the outcome I deliver?"

That second bit matters just as much as the first.

Because sometimes the person with buying authority technically COULD say yes… but they've got zero skin in the game for the result you create.

They'll nod politely on a call and then ghost you forever.

You want the person who FEELS it.

The one who's getting pressure from above when that metric dips… or who looks like a legend internally when it climbs.

THAT person reads your letter differently.

They don't see a cold pitch… they see someone who actually understands their world.

So if you're building your outreach list right now, give every entry a quick gut check.

Can you name the actual human you're sending to?

Do they have the authority to say yes without needing three layers of approval?

Would they personally benefit from the outcome you deliver?

If you can't tick all three, you're basically mailing a gift to a logo and hoping it finds the right desk.

Sometimes it will… but you're leaving a LOT to chance.

Or are you still working at the company level and need to drill down to the actual person? 👇
