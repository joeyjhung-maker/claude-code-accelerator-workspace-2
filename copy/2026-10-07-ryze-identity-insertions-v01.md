## Strongest negative insertion

A woman in her 30s who calls six hours of sleep "enough" and swears by her morning coffee replaced her first cup with RYZE for 21 days.

## Negative alternatives

1. who needs another caffeine hit by 3pm
2. who feels dead by 3pm and wired at bedtime
3. who treats her morning coffee like life support
4. who wakes up already thinking about her first cup
5. who needs coffee before she feels like herself

## Strongest positive insertion

A woman in her 30s who calls her morning coffee the best ten minutes of her day replaced her first cup with RYZE for 21 days.

## Positive alternatives

1. who protects her first quiet cup like sacred ground
2. who has the same mug, chair, and coffee ritual every morning
3. who always gets up first and makes coffee before anything else
4. who has built her whole morning around that first cup
5. who refuses to start the day without her coffee ritual

## Recommendation

Use the positive version.

It recognises her through a ritual she loves, so replacing the cup creates tension without insulting the identity the ad needs her to bring with her.

The negative version is sharper if the ad needs to enter through exhaustion, but it narrows the identity to women who openly admit six hours is not enough.

- PASS: the negative line uses an existing body fact, while the positive line uses the existing ten-minute morning scene.
