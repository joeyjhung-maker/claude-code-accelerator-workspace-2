Happy Sunday!

Old school direct mail legend Gary Halbert had this thing about how people sort their mail.

And it’s fantastic at helping us understand how to get our outreach OPENED.

He says… 

Standing over the bin, everybody makes two piles.

The A-pile is stuff that looks personal… and it ALWAYS gets opened.

The B-pile is anything that screams “I want to sell you something.”

And… if we’re honest… most outreach done today sits squarely in the B-pile…

…and this pile?

Mostly ends up in the bin before its ever read

This is why I love the locked gift strategy so much.

You FedEx someone a box.

Firstly…

Nobody is binning a FedEx box.

They open it… and it gets even MORE intriguing, because there’s a valuable gift sitting inside.

A bottle of whisky… or a YETI tumbler… an airtag…

And before they can get their hands on it…

The gift is LOCKED.

The letter shows they gotta call to get the code.

For THEM…

It’s a fun way to do outreach and give someone value in exchange for wanting to do biz with them

And for US…

You’ve skipped the bin, skipped the “delete” button, and given them a reason to actually pick up the phone.

A-pilers win the prize

B-pilers get the bin

Be an A-piler using what we’re teaching in here :-)

Dan
