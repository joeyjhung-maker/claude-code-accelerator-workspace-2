HL: Most deals die on the FOURTH touch nobody sent

Most peeps think they lost the deal because their pitch wasn't good enough.

Nah.

They lost it because they disappeared.

6sense studied nearly 4,000 B2B buyers and found the winning vendor averaged 16 interactions per person before the deal closed.

SIXTEEN.

That does NOT mean sending 16 “just checking in” emails lol.

Gartner found 73% of B2B buyers actively avoid suppliers who send irrelevant outreach.

So it’s not about pestering ‘em more.

It’s about staying useful… relevant… and hard to forget.

This is why I LOVE starting with your Dream 100.

By the time you reach out, you don’t just have a company name.

You’ve got their email, mobile, LinkedIn AND physical address.

That gives ya options.

Touch one might be a locked gift.

Touch two could be a useful case study via email.

Touch three might be engaging with something they posted on LinkedIn.

Touch four could be a call while your name’s still fresh.

You can send wins, ideas, information and even different offers… without hammering the same channel over and over.

This used to be a nightmare to manage.

Now we’ve got tools to make it WAY easier.

The person who sent one cold email and heard nothing back didn’t necessarily get rejected.

They got forgotten.

So before you contact anyone on your Dream 100…

Map out the next four or five useful touches FIRST.

Your opening pitch doesn’t need to do all the heavy lifting.

Your sequence does.
