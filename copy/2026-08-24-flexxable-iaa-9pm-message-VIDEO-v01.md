mariobot (see [[2026-08-24-flexxable-iaa-retainer-leash-TEXT-v01]]). The raw draft scored
4 FAIL / 34 FLAG on `copy_lint.py` — mostly AI-written em-dashes (this bot has no

binary were all correct in the RAW draft — only sentence-level craft needed the pass.

**Pacing trim (2026-08-24):** the raw draft had 15 [BEAT]/[PAUSE] markers, nearly one per
paragraph. Trimmed to the 9 that mark a real structural pivot (the "I got out" turn, the
revenue-paradox beat, the mechanism reveal, the payment-model reveal, the vendor→partner
payoff, the callback to the hook's own image, and the CTA transition) — cut the ones that
were just pacing filler between continuing thoughts. These 9 also now double as safe
places to stop and restart filming — Dan doesn't need to nail this in one continuous take.
See [[dan-on-camera-coaching]] for the full filming approach, since this is his first time
doing this style of ad.

**Runtime trim (2026-08-24):** the cleaned draft above was 1,114 spoken words — at Dan's
actual pace of ~100 wpm, that's ~11 minutes, roughly double-to-triple the brief's 3-6 min
target. Trimmed to 577 words (~5.8 min at 100 wpm) by cutting redundant elaboration —
the extended retainer-model list, the multi-example lead-database list (restaurant/gym/
dental/estate agent/car dealership down to one line), and the "this isn't passive income"

## Script

[VISUAL: Dan, evening lighting or natural indoor light, relaxed, not performing urgency, speaking like he's telling a friend something true.]

[OPENING: QUIET, SPECIFIC, IMMEDIATE]

You know the feeling.

It's 9pm.

You've had dinner.

You're finally off.

And then your phone lights up.

[PAUSE]

Client name.

And before you've even read the message,
something in your chest just... tightens.

[VISUAL: Dan, not dramatic, just honest]

I used to get that feeling every single evening.

Not because anything was usually wrong.

Just because the line between work and not-work had disappeared,
and my nervous system stopped believing the day was ever actually over.

[BEAT]

I'm not telling you this as someone who's still there.

I got out.

And I want to tell you how.

The revenue was fine.

Sometimes it was great.

By every measure, the agency was working.

Which made the exhaustion feel like my own fault.

Like the answer was to get more organized,
or hire better people,
or just... toughen up.

I tried all of it.

And the 9pm messages kept coming.

Because it was never a people problem, or a boundaries problem.

[VISUAL: Dan, the turn]

It was a model problem.

Every retainer you sign puts someone else's hand on your calendar.

Every month, you re-earn it.

Every result is something you have to explain or defend.

That's not a flaw in how you run things.

That's just the model.

And the model was the problem.

[VISUAL: Dan, calm, building toward the mechanism]

So I went looking for a different structure entirely.

One where the income didn't require me to be available.

[BEAT]

What I found is something I've now put into a book called The Instant AI Agency.

[VISUAL: Dan, the key insight]

Every business you've ever worked with has a database of old leads sitting there, ignored.

People who raised their hand, then went quiet, months or years ago.

Written off completely.

Here's the part most people miss.

Those leads aren't cold the way a stranger is cold.

They already said yes once.

The timing was just off.

An AI-powered SMS system can go into that database and start real conversations with every one of them, automatically, booking appointments and generating sales from a list the business owner had already given up on.

[BEAT]

And here's what changes everything: how you get paid for it.

You don't charge a retainer.

You don't charge setup fees.

You don't pitch and wait for a budget conversation.

[VISUAL: Dan, this is the crux]

You go to a business owner and say: "Give me your old database, and I'll deploy this system at no cost to you.

If it makes money, I take 30 to 50 percent of it.

If it doesn't, you've lost nothing."

[PAUSE]

No upfront cost.

No monthly retainer.

No performance reviews.

[VISUAL: Dan, quieter, letting this settle]

You're not a vendor anymore.

You're a partner.

They're rooting for it to work, because they only pay you when it does.

[BEAT]

There are no 9pm messages in that model.

Not because you've set better boundaries.

Because the structure just doesn't produce them.

[VISUAL: Dan, closing in on the offer]

I put the whole approach, how to find the business owners, how to structure the deal, how to deploy the AI, into that book.

It's $14.40.

Not a stripped-down version of something bigger.

That's genuinely the whole thing.

[VISUAL: Dan, final, unhurried]

If you run an agency right now and you recognize that 9pm feeling, not as a crisis, just as something that's become normal, I want you to read it.

There's a 30-day guarantee.

If you don't think it's worth your time, you get your money back.

[BEAT]

But what I hear from most agency owners who read it is the same thing.

"Why didn't anyone show me this sooner?"

[BEAT]

Click the link below.

It's $14.40.

And it might be the most useful thing you read this year.
