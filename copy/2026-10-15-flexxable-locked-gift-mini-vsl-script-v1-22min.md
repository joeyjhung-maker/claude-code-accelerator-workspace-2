## FULL SCRIPT

[OPENING]

We're getting more CLIENTS than most peeps get REPLIES using this weird campaign.

And the way we did it is almost embarrassingly simple.

It starts with this bottle right here.

But the bottle isn't actually the clever bit.

Something ELSE gets a CEO running a twenty, forty, eighty million dollar company to pick up the phone and call YOU… like it was THEIR idea.

There's a lot more that goes into it.

I'm gonna break the whole thing down right now.

[WHO I AM]

Yo yo!! Dan here.

Quick bit of context so you know I'm not just some random bloke waving a bottle around on the internet.

I've spent seven figures plus advertising directly to business owners over the last couple of years.

And I'm coaching over twelve hundred students on how to get in front of B2B businesses.

I've tried pretty much EVERYTHING to generate leads.

Facebook ads, Google ads, cold email, LinkedIn DMs, AI agents, webinars, community building…

Manual AND automated versions of most of those.

[SHOW: the 14 channels list]

You name it.

I've done it.

And here's the thing…

They all worked!

But there's a small problem.

[THE PROBLEM]

EVERYONE is fighting for the same six inches of screen.

Let me give you a real example.

One of my best mates is Dr Moradi… Ziggy to me.

He's a plastic surgeon in Sydney.

Which also means he's basically CATNIP for marketers.

Think about it.

He's rich, he's in a valuable niche, and he sells a high-ticket service.

So every man and his dog wants to sell him something.

His inbox?

Forget Inbox Zero.

INBOX ARMAGEDDON.

Every single day someone's emailing him.

Sliding into his LinkedIn DMs.
Cold calling him.
Retargeting him.
Shooting over another "personalised" AI message telling him how much they LOVED his latest post.

And someone is probably asking him to download another bloody PDF.

[SHOW: caption "My mate Ziggy: Plastic surgeon. Professional deleter of cold outreach."]

And now AI has made this problem a hundred times WORSE.

Because suddenly everyone can mass blast emails and DMs at higher and higher rates.

The funny thing is… these AI messages are SUPPOSED to look clever.

They're supposed to look personal.

But I can spot them a mile away.

So can Ziggy.

Archive… archive… archive.

More bloody NOISE.

And what do most marketers do when this stops working as well as it used to?

They do MORE of it.

I blame Hormozi.

So we flipped the script entirely.

We stopped trying to get onto their SCREEN…

And started trying to get onto their DESK.

[THE BOTTLE]

A few months ago, a lumpy mail package arrived for me.

Inside was a nice bottle of champagne.

Which is always a good start.

There was just one problem.

I couldn't crack the bubbly open.

The bottle was locked away inside a cage.

There was also a personalised letter addressed to me.

Everyone in the office was buzzing.

Who had sent it?

Why was it locked?

And most importantly… how the HELL do we open it?

So I read the letter.

It was from a British dude called Jonathan Matthews.

Jonathan wanted to get my attention.

And there was something I needed to do to unlock the bottle.

Call him.

Now… I DON'T call strangers back.

Ever.

Guess what I did within about three minutes.

I picked up the phone and dialled the number on the letter.

The bugger got me.

Jonathan and I have since done quite a bit of biz together.

But here's the thing I couldn't stop thinking about…

Jonathan had managed to do something that marketers spend BILLIONS trying to do every year.

He got the attention of the EXACT person he wanted to do business with.

Me.

[THE TEST]

So I started wondering… what happens if WE send some bottles and try to land clients for our own agency?

Now at around the same time, my brother had launched a business in the indoor adventure park space.

I'd invested in it too.

And through my agency, we'd been helping him scale to three locations inside twelve months.

Which got me thinking…

If we can do this for my bro… why don't we go and speak to a load of OTHER adventure parks?

So we built a list of indoor adventure parks in the UK we absolutely MUST have as clients.

Not a thousand random leads.

Definitely not spraying and praying.

This was a list of a hundred adventure parks we'd luuuuv to work with.

We found the decision makers.

Sent the gifts.

And every single one went out with the same ONE thing inside that did all the heavy lifting.

It's not the gift.

And it's not the lock.

[THE LETTER]

It's the letter.

The "how to unlock" letter is the GLUE that holds the whole thing together.

The letter explains why we'd specifically chosen THEIR business.

What we thought we KNEW we could help them with.

And most importantly… what they needed to do next.

Now imagine being on the receiving end for a second.

You're the owner of an indoor adventure park.

You've already deleted twenty-seven cold emails today.

You've ignored another fourteen LinkedIn messages.

Then the postman knocks on your door and hands you a lumpy package.

There's a nice bottle of something inside.

You can SEE it.

You WANT it.

But you CAN'T open it.

There's a letter with your name on it.

And to find out what the hell is going on… and unlock your gift… you need to call the number at the top of the letter.

It's almost the complete OPPOSITE of what everyone else is doing.

Normally… YOU are chasing THEM.

With this… THEY start calling YOU.

[THE RESULTS]

And that's exactly what started happening.

[SHOW: 10 / 6 / 3]

Our first ten sends… six calls booked… three closed.

Let that sink in.

We didn't send a thousand emails or need five hundred replies.

We sent TEN physical packages to businesses we actually wanted to work with.

Six turned into conversations.

Three turned into sales.

That's a sixty percent call rate from TEN packages.

So we kept going.

And across our first seventy packages… we closed and onboarded six clients.

That's an eight point six percent package-to-close rate.

Now I knew I was onto something… so I showed this to a mate of mine in America.

Mateusz decided to send a few bottles of his own.

He picked the home improvement niche, selling AI automation.

He sent fourteen bottles.

And booked four coffee dates… you'd probably call 'em sales calls…

With twenty to eighty million dollar a year businesses.

[SHOW: 14 bottles, 4 coffee dates, $20M to $80M a year businesses]

And he basically said… he didn't know another cold outreach method that could get the CEO of an eighty million dollar a year company to call HIM.

And it cost him forty bucks.

[THE 3 PARTS]

So why does this actually work?

It's NOT because there's something magical about a bottle of wine.

Or because business owners have a strange obsession with combination locks.

It works because the gift is only ONE of three moving parts.

The gift gets attention.
The lock creates curiosity.
The letter creates the conversation.

And it's the lock in the MIDDLE that flips everything.

Because normally… you're the desperate side chick trying to get THEIR attention.

With this… now THEY'RE curious about YOU.

Copy just the bottle without the other two pieces… and all you've done is send a stranger a free drink.

Get all three right… and a CEO calls you.

[OBJECTIONS]

Now I know what some of you are thinking.

"But Dan… I can't send booze."

I know.

Especially if you're in the US, where sending alcohol can be a pain in the arse.

Good news… you don't NEED to send alcohol.

Because it was NEVER really about the booze.

It was about putting something on somebody's desk that was desirable, different, physical, and difficult to ignore.

We're now testing all sorts of other gifts.

YETI tumblers.
AirTags.
JBL speakers.
Premium products people actually WANT.

In fact… some of these options MAY turn out to work even better than alcohol.

Because not everyone drinks.

Pretty much everyone likes getting cool stuff.

"Okay Dan… but isn't this expensive?"

Yep.

Compared with sending an email?

Absolutely.

But that's the WRONG question.

The right question is… expensive compared to WHAT?

Your entire package costs around forty dollars.

Gift, packaging, postage, everything.

Send ten and you're roughly four hundred bucks in.

Now four hundred dollars is quite expensive if your objective is to acquire ten nice mugs.

But what if you're trying to get a meeting with the owner of a business worth twenty million?

Forty million?

Eighty million?

And what if ONE of those businesses could become a client worth five thousand a month… ten thousand a month… or more?

What if the lifetime value of that client is twenty thousand, fifty thousand, a hundred thousand dollars plus?

Suddenly forty bucks doesn't sound quite so scary.

And there's ONE more reason I actually LIKE the fact this costs money.

There's a barrier to entry.

Anyone can send an email or a DM with zero effort.

But when someone receives a physical package… they can SEE you've invested thought, effort, and money into getting their attention.

It feels different.

Because it IS different.

[THE 6 STEPS]

So how do you actually DO this?

Six steps, in the exact order we run them.

Step one.

Pick your niche.

Don't start with "every business owner in America."

Pick ONE market… HVAC, dentists, roofers, accountants, manufacturers… whatever makes sense for what you sell.

Get specific.

We call these subniches.

Your list gets better.
Your letter gets better.
Your offer gets better.
Your conversations get better.

Step two.

Build your Dream Hundred.

Find a hundred businesses in that market you'd genuinely LOVE as clients.

Don't just grab the first hundred businesses Google spits out.

Then find the PERSON inside each business who can actually say YES.

Owner, founder, CEO, managing director, head of sales, head of marketing.

Because there's no point spending forty dollars getting the attention of someone who can't buy from you.

Step three.

Get the data.

Finding the company is easy.

Finding the right person along with their mobile, email, LinkedIn, and physical address… not so much.

But I've got a present for you here.

I'll give you access to our lead database for FREE.

This isn't a hundred dodgy leads in a spreadsheet.

It's over three hundred and fifty MILLION contacts.

Create your free Topa plan and use the code DANWARDROPELEADS to get unlimited access.

The link's under this video.

[SHOW: Topa logo + code DANWARDROPELEADS]

Step four.

Choose your gift.

You don't need a five hundred dollar gift.

Something in that thirty to fifty dollar range is more than enough.

What matters is it feels valuable enough that they WANT it… and interesting enough that they notice it.

Then… lock it.

Now you've got the curiosity piece.

Step five.

Write the letter.

Pleeease don't skip this bit.

The gift without the letter is just a nice gift.

But not particularly useful if your goal is to land a client.

Your letter needs to answer a few very simple questions.

Why them?
Why are you contacting them?
What do you believe you can help them with?
Why should they care?
And what do they need to do NEXT?

It doesn't need to be fourteen pages long.

It doesn't need clever copywriting tricks.

It needs to make the recipient think… "Okay. This person clearly chose ME for a reason."

The exact letters we send are inside the community, which I'll tell you about in a second.

Step six.

Send it… then follow up.

This isn't magic.

Some people will call immediately, some won't.

So follow up… call them, email, connect on LinkedIn.

But NOW you've got a pretty useful reason to start the conversation.

"Hey John… Dan here. Quick one… did that package I sent you arrive?"

That is a VERY different opening from…

"Hi John, we help businesses like yours leverage AI to increase revenue and streamline operations."

You aren't another random in their inbox anymore.
