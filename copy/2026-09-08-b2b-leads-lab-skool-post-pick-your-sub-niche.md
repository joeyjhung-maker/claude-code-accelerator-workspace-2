HL: Most people pick a niche that's way too broad… and then wonder why their lumpy mail gets binned

Dan here…

Before you build your Dream 100 list or write a single word of your letter, you need to nail your SUB niche.

Not just "solar."

Not just "home services."

Those are categories, not niches.

And when your letter reads like it could've been sent to ANY business in a massive industry… it lands with a thud because the owner doesn't feel like you're talking to THEM.

So take your broad niche and break it down one level further.

Solar alone could be residential installers, battery storage companies, commercial and industrial, solar financing, or lead gen aggregators.

Each of those has different pain points, different language, different reasons they'd want what you're offering.

That's exactly how we landed on kids adventure parks… we didn't just pick "entertainment venues" and hope for the best.

If you're stuck, chuck your broad niche into ChatGPT and ask it to break it into sub-niches.

Takes about 30 seconds and it'll probably spit out options you hadn't even thought of.

Pick the one that gets you fired up, THEN build your Dream 100 around it.
