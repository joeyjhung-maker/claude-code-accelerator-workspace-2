Happy Sunday.

Old school direct mail legend Gary Halbert had this thing about how people sort their mail.

Standing over the bin, everybody makes two piles.

The A-pile… stuff that looks personal. That ALWAYS gets opened.

The B-pile… anything that screams "I want to sell you something."

B-pile goes straight in the trash.

And honestly… most of our outreach lands in the B-pile before anyone even reads a word.

So here's something that changed the game for me.

You FedEx someone a box.

Inside is a genuinely cool gift… like a bottle of whisky or a YETI tumbler.

But here's the kicker…

The gift is LOCKED.

They literally have to call you to get it unlocked.

Think about that for a sec.

Nobody is binning a FedEx box.

They open it… and now they're CURIOUS.

And curiosity turns into a phone call… because they WANT what's inside.

You've skipped the bin, skipped the "delete" button, and given them a reason to actually pick up the phone.

That's about as A-pile as it gets.
