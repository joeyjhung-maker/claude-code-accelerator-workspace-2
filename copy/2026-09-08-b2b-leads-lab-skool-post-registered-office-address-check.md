HL: We wasted a bottle on a building nobody works in

So this one stings a little…

We sent a bottle to Tom Wood, owner of Car & Classic.

Pulled his registered office address off Companies House… sent it out… and then nothing.

Crickets.

Cold-called to recover it.

Turns out that address isn't a real working office — just what's filed with Companies House.

Tom never saw the bottle.

Never opened it.

Dead on arrival, and a cold call can't fix that.

Here's the lesson…

Companies House gives you AN address.

It won't tell you if anyone actually SITS there.

Loads of businesses use a registered office that's just a legal formality — an accountant's office, a virtual mailbox.

Nobody's opening packages there.

Verify it's a real, staffed workplace before you send.

Don't just copy-paste the public record.

This is exactly why the Dream 100 Scorecard has a "right address" check — and why Topa flags registered-office-only addresses as a red flag.

A bottle on the right desk = a coffee date.

A bottle at a ghost address = an expensive donation to nobody.

Don't be like us on this one 😂

Verify the address FIRST.
