## Left alone on purpose

- Every hedge: "the way you normally do", "the way you used to", "as often", "starts happening less". Cutting them saves words and turns a hedged line into a flat claim.
- The hook. All three lines are Joey's and carry the ten-minutes payoff.
- All four ingredient lines and all four Day 3 signals. No pain point or benefit was removed.

## Flags (Joey's lines, flag only)

- There is no call to action. The ad ends on the refund line.
- "Send it back for a full refund": RYZE's policy only refunds an opened bag on a first subscription order, one bag. Their own wording is "If you don't love it, you get your money back."
- The script has no [DAY] markers in this version. Fine if they are on-screen only.
- Still open from earlier: nothing after the first day says the taste gets better.
