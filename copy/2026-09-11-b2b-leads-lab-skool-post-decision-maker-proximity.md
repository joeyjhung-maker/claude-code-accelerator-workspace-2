HL: Stop chasing the biggest logo in the room

There’s a TINY targeting mistake that can cost ya MONTHS of work…

And potentially $100s of dollars worth of gifts down the drain

Matt sent 14 bottles recently.

Booked FOUR coffee dates.

GREAT hit rate.

His targets ranged from $20M all the way up to $80M companies.

BUT…

There’s a problem with this.

He learned pretty quickly that bigger isn’t always better.

At the $50M-$80M level…

The person you speak to may still need to sell your idea to a COO or CMO.

Now YOU’RE not pitching anymore.

Someone else is half-remembering your pitch and passing it up the chain like Chinese whispers.

The level of red-tape and bureaucracy increases DRAMATICALLY the bigger the company.

In my experience, it’s not worth it

Especially when we can go after smaller companies and STILL make bank.

I reckon the sweet spot is closer to $5M-$20M.

Still a juicy WHALE…

But you’ve got a much better chance of speaking directly with the person who can write the cheque.

Decision-maker proximity beats company prestige.

So before ya add someone to your Dream 100…

Ask yourself one question:

“Can this person actually say YES?”

If not… find a smaller whale.

Dan
