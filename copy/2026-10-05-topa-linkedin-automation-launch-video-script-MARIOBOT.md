## Final script v3 (as voiced)

**[HOOK]**
Right now your dream client is deleting a DM that sounds exactly like the one you sent this morning.

**[LEAD]**
The $250k to $1m+ deals are sitting on LinkedIn.
The decision makers with real budgets… who can sign one contract that completely changes your year.
Everybody knows they're there.
Everybody's fighting for their attention in the DMs.
And almost nobody is closing them.

**[PROBLEM]**
Their inbox is a graveyard of copy-paste pitches from strangers.
And if someone does reply… you're trapped in a chat thread.
You don't have their number.
You don't have their email.
The conversation just goes cold.
The DMs are where deals go to die.

**[TURN]**
The people who actually close these deals do two things every single day.
They keep adding the right decision makers to their network… and they pull meetings and sales out of the connections they already have.

**[MECHANISM]**
That's why we built Topa.
Topa does both for you, on autopilot, from your own LinkedIn account.
You pick the decision makers you want… and Topa sends them connection requests every day, then messages the ones who accept.
For the connections you already have, Topa sends one short message with no pitch in it… we call it the hand raiser.

**[PROOF]**
Jonathan runs Topa, and these are two of his own campaigns.
In the first, 30 people were messaged and 22 replied… a 73% reply rate.
In the second, 12 new connections accepted, and 11 of those 12 replied.
That's 92%.

**[HOW IT WORKS]**
Connect your account, set your hours, and it runs.
Every reply lands in one inbox… click "Get Cell Phone", and for about seven in ten people, their mobile number comes back in seconds.
Now you're ringing someone who just replied to you.

**[DATABASE]**
And you've got access to almost every LinkedIn member sitting inside Topa's internal database.
That's over 350 million potential leads.
You can search all of it, and download 10,000 contacts a month with their full contact info… mobile, email, address, and more, included in every Topa plan.
So you can follow up by phone, by email, and for the hottest names, with direct mail.
That's how a reply turns into a meeting… and a meeting into a big deal.
And best of all: You can delete Sales Navigator because this replaces it.

**[OBJECTION]**
LinkedIn is strict, so Topa keeps it slow on purpose… randomised send times, a residential IP in your own location, and low daily limits.
You still write every reply yourself, and no tool can promise what LinkedIn will do.

**[PRICE]**
It's $79 a month for one LinkedIn account, or $99 for two.
One deal covers that many times over.

**[CTA]**
Go to topa.io and sign up.
Because that dream client isn't going to find your DM in a graveyard of copy-paste pitches.
