# **THE MARKETING INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM**

*A Complete Framework for Organizing Facts & Concepts Into High-Converting Ads*

## **What This Is**

A systematic way to collect, organize, and deploy facts about your audience, market, and product to create compelling ads. Think of it as your "fact pantry" - raw ingredients you can pull from and wrap in different angles (breaking news, confession, discovery, conspiracy) to create messages that stop the scroll.

## **The System Components**

**AVATAR FACTS** - Raw truths about your audience

* Their pain expressions, daily reality, core wounds  
* What they see/hear/feel/think/do  
* Their resistance patterns and buying triggers

**MARKET FACTS** - Intelligence about the competitive landscape

* What solutions they've tried and why they failed  
* Cultural influences and tribal beliefs  
* How they evaluate and decide

**PRODUCT FACTS** - The truth about your solution

* Discovery story and unique mechanism  
* Transformation milestones and proof  
* Implementation details and results

**PSYCHOLOGY TOOLKIT** - Techniques to reshape understanding

* Ways to reframe beliefs (metaphors, paradoxes, etc.)  
* Ready-to-use concepts (boundary violations, attention mechanics)  
* Proven psychological triggers

# **AVATAR FACT COLLECTION SYSTEM**

This system captures the discrete psychological, emotional, and behavioral facts about your target audience that become the raw ingredients for your marketing. These aren't messages or angles - they're the unprocessed truths about how your audience experiences their problem, what they want, what stops them, and how they navigate their world. Each fact is a building block you can deploy through different angles to create compelling ads, emails, and content.

Think of these as your ingredient pantry - the specific pains, desires, beliefs, behaviors, and patterns you've discovered about your market. You'll pull from these facts and wrap them in different angles (breaking news, confession, discovery, conspiracy) to create messages that stop the scroll and compel action.

## **A. IDENTITY: WHO THEY ARE**

### **1. Demographics**

The basic factual framework of who this person is in the world - their age, gender, occupation, location, and life circumstances that create the context for everything else.

* *Sarah, 47, married mother of two teenagers, marketing director at tech company, lives in Denver suburbs, household income $145,000*  
* *Marcus, 34, single, senior software developer, rents apartment in Austin, makes $95,000 but has $67,000 in student loans*  
* *Jennifer, 52, divorced three years ago, adult children live out of state, owns small consulting business, recently moved to new city*

### **2. Self-Concept Deep Beliefs**

The fundamental "I am someone who..." statements that form their core identity and guide every decision they make.

* *"I am someone who used to be athletic but now my body is betraying me"*  
* *"I am someone who's smart but can't figure out money like everyone else seems to"*  
* *"I am someone who gives everything in relationships but always ends up alone"*  
* *"I am someone who should have it all figured out by now but I'm still faking it"*  
* *"I am someone who's running out of time to make something of myself"*

### **3. Core Problem Statement**

The one-sentence encapsulation of their primary struggle that they would use to describe their situation to someone who truly understands.

* *"I've tried everything but nothing stops this exhaustion that's stealing my life"*  
* *"I make good money but I'm further behind than when I made half as much"*  
* *"I know exactly what I don't want but keep attracting the same type of person"*  
* *"My business should be thriving but I'm drowning in tasks that don't make money"*

### **4. Cultural Touchstones**

The shared references, events, and cultural moments that shaped their worldview and create instant recognition and connection.

* *Remembers when fat-free was everything, lived through opioid crisis coverage, watched parents deal with statins*  
* *Graduated into 2008 recession, watched crypto friends get rich, remembers when house prices made sense*  
* *Grew up when divorce became common, experienced dating apps revolution, navigated Me Too revelations*  
* *Started business during dot-com boom, survived multiple "disruptions," remembers when a handshake meant something*

### **5. Meaningful Symbols**

Objects, images, or concepts that carry deep emotional weight beyond their literal meaning - the things that represent their hopes, fears, or identity.

* *Empty Advil bottle representing another failed attempt, unused gym membership card, bathroom scale hidden in closet*  
* *Screenshot of bank balance they can't look at, friend's vacation photos on Instagram, parents' worried expressions*  
* *Empty side of bed, phone with no messages, wedding photos turned face-down*  
* *Untouched business books on nightstand, competitor's success story in Forbes, laptop still open at midnight*

**Core Wound Prediction:** *"Being sidelined from life - a burden who watches others live while they merely exist"*

This manifests as the terror of becoming irrelevant, watching from the sidelines as life passes by. They imagine family gatherings where they're the one in the corner chair while others play. Friends who stop inviting them because they always cancel. A partner who becomes a caregiver instead of a lover. Children who learn not to ask them to participate. The core wound is the complete loss of agency and participation in their own life - not death, but a living death where they exist without truly living.

# **MARKET FACTS COLLECTION SYSTEM**

This system captures the discrete facts about your market's landscape - what solutions they've tried, what influences them, what they believe, and how they make decisions. These aren't opinions or strategies - they're the raw truths about the competitive environment, cultural context, and decision-making patterns that shape your audience's choices. Each fact becomes ammunition you can deploy through different angles to position your solution as the obvious choice.

Think of these as reconnaissance intel - the specific competitive weaknesses, cultural moments, failed approaches, and tribal beliefs you can leverage. You'll pull from these facts to create messages that tap into existing conversations, counter prevailing wisdom, or align with emerging movements.

# **PSYCHOLOGY TOOLKIT**

This toolkit provides the advanced psychological techniques for transforming beliefs into compelling marketing narratives. While PRODUCT FACTS give you the raw material about your solution, the PSYCHOLOGY TOOLKIT shows you how to reshape prospects' mental models so they see your product as the inevitable answer. These aren't manipulative tricks - they're cognitive tools that help people see past their limiting beliefs and recognize the true nature of their problem and your solution.

Think of these as transformation tools - specific techniques that take a prospect's current belief (usually a constraint or misconception) and reshape it into a new understanding that naturally leads to your solution. Every technique creates a cognitive shift that makes your mechanism feel like the discovery they've been waiting for.

## **Core Concepts**

**TECHNIQUE:** A cognitive tool or framework you apply to reshape understanding (like using a metaphor to make something abstract feel concrete)

**TARGET:** The specific belief or mental model you're transforming (usually beliefs about the problem, solution, vendor, or self)

**FACT/CONCEPT:** The resulting marketing asset created when you apply a technique to a target (this becomes usable copy, hook, or narrative)

You can apply any technique to multiple targets - the same metaphor framework might illuminate your unique mechanism, explain why past solutions failed, AND demonstrate your personal authority. The key is matching the right technique to the belief that needs shifting.

## **The Belief Architecture Context**

Every sale requires belief transformation across three core models:

* **PROBLEM MODEL:** What's really wrong and why it matters  
* **SOLUTION MODEL:** What actually works and how  
* **VENDOR MODEL:** Why you specifically can deliver results

Within these models, you're performing belief operations:

* **UNINSTALL:** Remove limiting beliefs ("this is just aging")  
* **INSTALL:** Insert new empowering beliefs ("there's a hidden mechanism")  
* **ACCOMMODATE:** Work with existing beliefs rather than fight them ("yes, diet matters, but there's something else...")  
* **REPLACE:** Swap one belief for another ("it's not willpower, it's brain chemistry")

## **THE 7 CRITICAL BELIEFS (PRIMARY TARGETS)**

These are the most powerful beliefs to transform because they directly drive purchase decisions:

1. **UNIQUE MECHANISM BELIEF**  
   * OLD: "My problem is caused by [surface symptom/common belief]"  
   * NEW: "My problem is caused by [specific mechanism] that only this addresses"  
2. **FAILED ALTERNATIVES BELIEF**  
   * OLD: "I've tried everything, nothing works" or "I just need more willpower"  
   * NEW: "What I tried before was doomed because it didn't address [mechanism]"  
3. **ESCALATING COST BELIEF**  
   * OLD: "This is annoying but manageable" or "I can live with this"  
   * NEW: "This is actively getting worse and costing me [TIMER losses] daily"  
4. **CONSTRAINT REMOVAL BELIEF**  
   * OLD: "I don't have the time/money/discipline/genetics for this"  
   * NEW: "I already have everything needed to succeed with this"  
5. **PERSONAL AUTHORITY BELIEF**  
   * OLD: "Another guru selling snake oil" or "This person doesn't understand"  
   * NEW: "This person discovered it through struggle and has helped thousands"  
6. **RISK REVERSAL BELIEF**  
   * OLD: "I'll probably waste my money like always"  
   * NEW: "This either works or costs nothing - the mechanism guarantees it"  
7. **NOW-OR-NEVER BELIEF**  
   * OLD: "I can think about it" or "Maybe later when I'm ready"  
   * NEW: "This specific opportunity won't remain available"

# Angles

# **ANGLES: THE PSYCHOLOGICAL FRAMES OF PERSUASION**

**Core Principle:** Angles are the psychological entry points through which information becomes persuasive by aligning with specific mental states and cognitive biases. They transform raw concepts/facts into compelling narratives that hijack attention and create urgency.

## **WHY ANGLES MATTER**

We don't change minds with new information - we change HOW people attend to information they already have access to. The angle shapes attention, attention shapes perception, perception shapes belief, belief shapes action. An angle is the psychological premise that makes someone CARE about information they would otherwise ignore.
