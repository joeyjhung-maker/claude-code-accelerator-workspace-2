# TIMELINE brief: the 25-year coffee drinker, 21 days

Related: [[research]] [[2026-10-04-ryze-weed-timeline-animated-ad]] [[2026-10-03-video-ad-rules]]

**Hypothesis:** RYZE's own timeline ads all say the first cup "tastes like a creamy latte". 65% of their 1-star reviews say otherwise. Saying the odd first cup out loud should buy belief for every rung after it. If it misses, either the honest dip costs more attention than the trust it buys, or a coffee lover is too mild an avatar next to their vice avatars (weed, energy drinks).

## Avatar
A woman who's had coffee every morning for 25 years.
She swaps her first cup. She does not quit coffee.

## Frame
Hypothetical, third person ("she").
Who, the habit swap, the 21 days, then a tease of the last day.

The taste beat is rung 2 only, set up by her objection on Day 1. It is one beat, not the theme.

## Villain
It was never the coffee she loved. It was the size of the caffeine hit: a spike at 8 and the bill at 3.
Named on Day 1 (half the caffeine). Explained at rung 3.

## Spikes
1. **3pm (rung 3).** It passes, because there's less to come down from. Unlike the second coffee, which borrows from tonight's sleep.
2. **The can (rung 4).** The false fix is still sitting there, unopened.

## Seal
The 3pm can and the wired-at-night feeling both came from one oversized morning dose.
She never quit coffee, because this is coffee.
The ask is one spoon, once a day, for 21 days.

## Sales block (this order, these facts only)
1. Six mushrooms, 2,000mg per cup, organic, grown in the USA.
2. No sugar, 20 calories.
3. 219,573 reviews, 4.8 stars.
4. 90 cents a cup.

No stock or sell-out line. RYZE doesn't publish one.

## Claims rules
- "Supports" only. Never gives, fixes, boosts, cures.
- Banned phrases (RYZE dropped them in September 2025): all-day energy, sharper focus, healthier digestion, better immune support, better sleep.
- No disease or condition words. No weight, bloating, blood sugar, inflammation, hormones.
- No number on any result. Numbers only on the offer and the proof.
- Guarantee is "30-day money-back guarantee" and nothing more. No "send back the empty bag", no "no questions asked".
- No invented testimonial, count or stock line.
