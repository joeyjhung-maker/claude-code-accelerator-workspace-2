# PART 0 — THE META-PHILOSOPHY (why build it this way at all)

## 0.1 The one sentence that governs everything
> Making is cheap now. The creative strategist's real job is **choosing what to make** from a near-infinite space — and that choice is **creative diversity**, steered by **judgment + taste**, wired into a **flywheel** that compounds into **instinct**.

## 0.2 The new paradigm — build software for AGENTS, not apps
**Claim 1 — Think in PROCESSES and sub-processes, NOT roles.** The old way builds a team (or buys software) by *role*: a strategist, a copywriter, an editor, a media buyer — each a black box you hand a job to. The new way decomposes the work into **processes → sub-processes → sub-processes**, discrete observable tasks you point an agent at. A "copywriter" isn't a role; it's *pull seed → prime with winners → generate hooks → push to Level 3 → write body → editing passes → claim-check.* Seven tasks, each automatable, improvable, and **yours**.
> *Say it like this:* "Don't ask 'how do I replace my copywriter.' Ask 'what are the twelve things a copywriter actually does.' Then you build twelve small, sharp tools — and you own all twelve."

**Claim 2 — Building for AGENTS (CLI) beats using APPS.** An app is someone else's process, frozen; you get their average answer. Building for agents (Claude Code + scripts + skill files) flips it:
- **It's personal to you.** The primer holds *your* winners; the skill holds *your* voice rules. Same machine, different brand output, because the inputs are yours.
- **Your data is the moat, not the model.** Everyone has the same model; the edge is the proprietary stuff you encode — winning ads, corrections, judgment.
- **It compounds.** A prompt is worth one answer; a setup is worth every answer after. Six months in it knows your accounts better than a new hire after a year.
- **You can open the box.** When a step is wrong, you fix that step.

**The honest caveat (which is also the pitch):** building for agents is more powerful AND more work than downloading an app. You wire the nodes, feed the banks, tend it weekly. That's real — and it's why the done-for-you path (Exodus app + Genesis) exists. *The map is free; the build is the choice.*

*(Resource backbone: Hormozi — Ep 963 "Stop Ignoring AI" — processes-not-roles + data-as-moat.)*

## 0.3 Don't be a nerd
Every model in this doc is a **thinking tool, not a procedure.** Hold the map so you have *facility*, then move fast on the direct path. The instant you're optimizing the map instead of shipping, you've fallen into the trap the map was meant to prevent.

# PART 1 — THE FLYWHEEL (the system at a glance)

## 1.1 The Core: the Dual Flywheel (judgment + taste → instinct)
```
        JUDGMENT ──────────►
       (is this right?)       \
                               ►  INSTINCT  ──► makes the whole system "1 of 1"
       (is this good?)        /   (compounds)
        TASTE ──────────────►
```
- **Judgment** = is this *correct / strategically right* for this brand, segment, decision?
- **Taste** = is this *good* — does it have the angle, the edge, the thing only you'd see?
- Every correction feeds judgment + taste back in. Over cycles this compounds into **instinct** the agent carries — the part competitors on the same model can't copy. **The human instinct is its own flywheel.**

## 1.2 The six processes in a loop
```
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │                                                              ▼
STRATEGY ──► ANALYSIS ──► IDEATION ──► COPY ──► EDITING ──► CREATIVE ──► [SHIP]
 (what to    (what's      (STORMING)   (hook+   (passes)    (statics+      │
  make)       working)                  primers)             video)        │
   ▲                                                                       │
   └─────────────── winners + results feed back in ◄──────────────────────┘
            (banks, primers, templates, hypotheses, instinct all self-update)
```
> **Production-flow order (locked in the circuit):** Analysis → Ideation → Copy → **Editing → Creative**. Strategy governs from the top; Memory/Foundation feeds from the bottom; the loop closes along the bottom (Ship → results → Analysis).

## 1.3 The "feed it" rule (what makes it a flywheel, not a pipeline)
Outputs become inputs: **Winners** (Analysis) → swipe banks + templates (Ideation) + new segments (Strategy) + **primers** (Copy/Editing). **Your corrections** → the instinct core. **Skill files self-update** with whatever you feed them.
> Nothing keeps working on its own. The flywheel needs a **weekly hour**: feed new winners, pull dead ones, fix the voice where it slipped. *That hour is the system.*

# PART 2 — STRATEGY (what to make · governs everything)

> *The one line:* Strategy is **what to make**. The goal is a **healthy, diverse ad account** — stocked with many *winning types* of ads, not one winner cloned 50 times. **Creative is the biggest lever** on the whole account. **Net-new beats optimization** — Andromeda rewards genuinely new creative; even a **swipe counts as a net-new ad.**

## 2.1 The Creative Diversity formula
> **Creative Diversity = Segments × Visual & Creative × Awareness Levels × Angle & Messaging**

Descending weight (Segments + Visual are the two heavy hitters, roughly co-equal):

| Factor | Weight | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Visual & Creative** | heaviest | What **Andromeda** premiums on — style, feel. Black box, but undeniable. |
| **Segments** | heaviest | "Segments are the biggest unlock." New segments win even with imperfect copy — no competition. |
| **Awareness Levels** | matters, less | Real but secondary. Often collapses to 2 buckets. |
| **Angle & Messaging** | last lever | Angles/concepts/features. Important, but the last thing you reach for. |

Multiply → **hundreds of thousands** of possible ads. That explosion is the whole point, and the reason the job is *selection*.

## 2.2 The Segment Ontology — two layers people constantly conflate
### Layer 1 — DIMENSIONS (a person's attributes; the axes you slice on)
| Dimension | What it is | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Outcome** (desire + pain) | what they want / what hurts — the deep, primary axis | joint, heart, weight, sleep, energy |
| **Demographic** | observable *who* | gender, age, race, location |
| **Identity** | how they self-identify (high leverage — Resonance Hierarchy) | Christian, Republican, athlete |
| **Mechanism / belief** | the cause they believe drives the problem | cortisol, inflammation, hormones |
| **Condition** | a factual restriction (eligibility) | vegetarian, diabetic, pregnant |
| **Value** | a soft preference/belief | organic-only, natural, budget |

**The clean rule for the blurry ones:** *Demographic = what they ARE · Identity = who they SAY they are · Condition = a fact that limits fit · Value = what they PREFER.* "Constraint" was a bad single bucket — it secretly meant two things (a *value* like "organic" vs a *condition* like "vegetarian"). Split them.

### Layer 2 — SEGMENT TYPES (named slices; sizes, not attributes)
| Term | What it is |
|---|---|
| **TAM** | the whole market — everyone |
| **Segment** | any slice (generic) |
| **Outcome segment** | one outcome — the chunkiest, primary cut (vertical slice) |
| **Super segment** | spans *multiple* desires — often a mechanism that bundles them (cortisol → joint+heart+hormones) |
| **Sub-segment** | *beneath* an outcome — narrowed by demo/mechanism/condition (a line) |
| **Cross segment** | demographic-only, *across* outcomes ("just women") (horizontal slice) |
| **Mechanism segment** | one mechanism across everything |
| **Persona** | a single fully-instantiated slice, characterized as a named person — the thinnest slice |

> **A persona is not a dimension.** It's any slice (usually thin) dressed up as a concrete person.

## 2.3 The shape: SPINE + FACETS (not an N-cube)
You can't put 5–6 dimensions on spatial axes, and you don't need to:
- **Outcome is the one true vertical axis — the spine.** A vertical slice = a segment. Several stacked = a super segment. All = TAM.
- **Everything else is a facet** — lay **one** facet across as rows, keep the rest as **filters** that thin the slice. A **persona** = outcome × a specific combination of facets.
- Two directions to find new segments: **new outcomes → add columns** (grow the spine) · **new conditions/identities → add facet values** (grow the qualifiers).

**The cube (3D expression of the same model):** X = demographic · Y = outcome · Z = a chosen facet (mechanism/condition/value). Whole cube = TAM · horizontal plane = cross segment · vertical layer = outcome segment · depth plane = mechanism segment · a line = sub-segment · one cell = persona. **Coverage** (real ads) shades the cells — and *that's where gaps appear.*

## 2.4 The worked example (Ground & Co. / grounding)
- *To everyone:* "Here's why grounding makes you healthy."
- *Outcome segment:* "Fix your joints with grounding."
- *Mechanism segment:* "Fix cortisol — which fixes joints, heart, and hormones."
- *Demographic:* "Christians and grounding."
- *Super sub-segment (thin, high-relevance):* "Christian women struggling with menopause because of cortisol."
- *Cross segment:* "Women with grounding."

## 2.5 Most important segments + finding NEW ones
- **Start with pain point + dream outcome** — highest-leverage. Then experiment on the other axes (gender, etc.) to find the *hidden* ones.
- **Finding a new segment is the single biggest unlock** — biggest recent winners came purely from a segment nobody targeted; the copy didn't even have to be great because there was no competition.
- **The new-segment process (concrete):** ① pull **tons of reviews** (Amazon, via **Apify**) ② combine reviews + call transcript + your own understanding ③ **Whisper voice-note** dump into Claude Code ④ extract all specific outcomes & desires → segments ⑤ build a **master list of new segments.** New outcomes grow the spine; new conditions grow facets.

## 2.6 Awareness layer
Five classic levels: **Unaware · Problem-aware · Solution-aware · Product-aware · Most-aware.**
**Working simplification — collapse to 2 buckets:** A = (Unaware/Problem-aware) · B = (Solution/Product-aware). **Most-aware** usually isn't segmented — goes to everyone (deals/offers). Why collapse: the real difference is mostly *the hook*; the ad *structure* is largely the same. **Awareness is NOT a segment facet — it's the layer above.** Keep it out of the cube or you double-count.
**Visual ↔ awareness mapping (best guesses):** Native → Unaware/Problem · Templated → Solution/Product (sometimes Problem) · Branded → Product/Most (bottom of funnel).

## 2.7 Coverage & Gaps
Two separate things, deliberately: **the map/cube = the space of what's possible** (fully populated, no holes) · **gaps appear only when you overlay your actual ads** (coverage). A gap = a cell with no ads. Classify each whole ad → one persona (net impression) → accumulate coverage → empty cells = gaps → rank gaps by **importance × reach**.

## 2.8 Judgment + Taste — the dual flywheel (how you actually choose)
### Judgment — "Chad logic" (the explicit equation)
For each candidate gap, weigh: **Gap size** (how big is the hole? no unaware ads → do unaware) · **Urgency of outcome** (pain or desire, usually pain) · **TAM** (how big is the slice?) · **Competitors / what's already working** (proven demand) · **Zeitgeist** (what's trending you're genuinely obsessive about). This isn't throwing out logic — it's **direct-path logic**: find the big gap, go straight at it. Bonus: a clean story to tell clients, and ammunition for your positions.
### Taste — the intuition beneath words
**Taste = experience + intuition — "the thinking beneath words"** (cf. *Blink*). Built by **saturating**: living in ad accounts, studying winners and competitors, being obsessive. Then you get ideas you can't fully justify — *"I don't know why I want to test this, but I'm going to."* That's taste firing.
### The dual flywheel → instinct
Judgment (logic) **+** Taste (saturated intuition), fed back every cycle, compound into **instinct** — the **1-of-1** AI and competitors on the same model can't hand anyone. The model in your head makes judgment fast and clear so you go direct-path with reasoning underneath and taste layered on.

# PART 3 — ANALYSIS (read the account → form hypotheses · closes the loop)

**Purpose:** look at media buying first — read the ad account, see what's working. This is where the human **saturates their brain with winners** → the **instinct firewall**.

**Components (node types):**
- **Ad Account → Meta API** *(Connection)* — Facebook/Meta reporting + comments (or a reporting export).
- **Classifier** *(Code)* — scores ads by **ROAS + spend** into types.
- **Instinct firewall** *(Human)* — the human reads/saturates on winners; taste develops here. "Saturate on winners."
- **Hypotheses** *(Database)* — tests + log. May be a **RAG system** hypotheses get fed into.
- **RAG store** *(Database)* — retrieval.

**The 4 ad classes** (working framework — **NOT canonical**, to stress-test with media buyers; Luke got these elsewhere and will make his own universal framework):
- **Breakthrough** — crazy from launch (e.g. ~7 ROAS after a few hundred $ spend).
- **Spend Winner** — getting lots of spend but not hitting the KPI.
- **KPI Winner** — hits everything (usually the best).
- **Loser** — doesn't work (still a learning).
- Any class can convert into another. If something's getting spend, there's a reason → **form a hypothesis** and keep testing to find the real reason.
- *Simpler alternative cut:* **Winners · Traction · Losers** (by ROAS + spend).

**Flow:** Ad Account → Meta API → Classifier → [4 classes] → Instinct firewall → Hypotheses.
**Loops:** Hypotheses ↔ RAG (store / retrieve) · Hypotheses → firewall (re-test) · firewall → Ideation (human judgment).
**Output:** new hypotheses → net-new ads (into Ideation); firewall's human judgment also feeds Ideation; iterations/variations may branch off hypotheses.

# PART 5 — COPY (brief → hooks → body → headlines)

## 5.1 Intake / the Brief
- Ideation produces **raw seeds** (sketches). A seed carries the **source** (swipe/template/organic/etc.) + **specific links**, and *sometimes* a pre-determined **segment** + **awareness level**.
- That becomes the **Brief**, where the **user adds additional context**.
- **Swipe seed** → copy it almost **verbatim, like Mad Libs.** **Organic seed** → take just the hook.
- **All seeds get saved** (Seed Bank) for the future.

## 5.2 Primers = foundational onboarding docs *(Memory)*
**A primer = top-performing ads + a light instruction wrapper.** The ads ARE the brand truth; the wrapper is light scaffolding (villain focus, mechanism style, "use original language"). The set:
- **Hook primer** (winning hooks)
- **Body copy primers** — **S1** = Unaware/Problem-aware · **S2** = Solution/Product-aware · + more to add (chosen by the brief's awareness level)
- **Headline primer**
- (Hooks can just be hooks; headlines can just be headlines.)

**The 2-step bot-call protocol** (how a primer is used — the single most important technical concept): the bot server is stateless. **Call 1 (Priming):** send the FULL primer text → bot responds "I understand, I've absorbed the patterns." **Call 2 (Instruction):** replay primer + bot's response + the actual instruction (hook prompt, body instruction…). The quality of the primer directly determines the quality of the output.

## 5.3 The three sub-nodes
**1 · Hooks** — **Hook bot** = the "ad book," an empty file/prompt holding **all your book rules + examples.** Feed the **hook primer** to prime it → feed the **idea** (brief) → **push it 2-3×** to make it better (2-3 is ideal) → out: a bunch of hooks → **human selects** (optional gate). New winning ads feed back into primers (flywheel).

**2 · Body copy** — **Writing bot** (Mario bot or custom). Feed **body copy primer** → the **selected hook** (body must start from the hook) → the **rest of the brief** → it writes the whole thing. Run **in parallel** with different bots/primers ~2-4× (one tends to beat the others) → **human selects.**

**3 · Headlines** — Immediately from each body copy → feed into headline gen with the **headline primer** → "give me headlines for it." **Human chooses** the body-copy + headline pair for each → saved into a **doc.**

**Node-type clarity (Luke flagged explicitly):** Bot = skill that writes · Primer = foundation doc that primes the bot · Human = the selection point · Doc = saved output.

**Output:** Selections doc → Editing.

# PART 6 — EDITING (refine, then re-hook / re-headline → Google Docs)

**Architecture (Luke's words):** a writing **canvas/bot** + **primers** + the **ad to edit** + **human judgment via Wispr Flow** + **drop-in steer prompts** to push it in directions until it's right. You can also just manually edit.

**Surface vision (Exodus app):** paste an ad *or send it from Claude Code* → toggle between Genesis bots (Mario, Envy VSL, AdHook, custom + BYO) → primer pre-loaded by a button → steer "buttons" per direction → user adds their own commands → **save good edits as rules.**

**Components:** Ad to edit *(Doc)* · **Primer** *(Memory — structured INSTRUCTIONS → EXAMPLES → GUIDELINES)* · **Editing bot** *(Bot — Mario · Envy VSL · AdHook · custom; open 3-4 in parallel; push "until right")* · **Wispr Flow** *(Human — voice-note judgment)* · **Saved edit rules** *(Database — before/afters; feeds back into steer prompts)*.

## 6.1 Steer prompts — the "directions" master list (drop-ins / buttons)
- **Natural language / anti-AI** — too AI → natural; too staccato → natural; too-short sentences → natural; conversational.
- **More bounce** — conversational, musical flow.
- **Shorten (to a word target)** — read through and **cut specific lines**; do **NOT** collapse/compress sentences into "little turds."
- **Cut** — be anal; remove fluff lines that don't move the argument forward.
- **Simplify (reading level)** — swap high-falutin Latin words for concrete, earthy **Germanic-rooted** words; shorten structure. ~8th grade → 3-5th grade. *Not* dumber, *not* less intense — just brute-simple.
- **Make better** — "weave" in (don't bolt on) proof / believability / logic / insight; concrete visual language; intensify pain or benefit so they *feel* it.
- **Fix transitions** — add "and / because / so" connective tissue.
- **Fix logic** — does this make sense; stress-test specific ideas.
- **CTA** — explicit (formula below); clear characterizations.
- **Characterizations** — fun, clear.
- **Find & replace (semantic)** — e.g. "20 seconds" → "30 seconds" across all ads.
- Other: flavor, idiosyncratic/specific fixes, read-primer (more in style).

## 6.2 Editing knowledge to encode — Luke's VERBATIM style rules
**Core sentence rules:**
- **One sentence per line. One idea per line.** Each line stands alone as a complete thought; break separate ideas onto separate lines (keep as one line only if tightly connected and flowing).
- **No staccato fragment phrasing** ("No this. No that." / "Not X — it's Y") as a rhythm trick. Only break for rhythm if it genuinely serves the story.
- **Write ACTUAL sentences, not fragments.** If it wouldn't make sense read aloud, it's not a sentence ("Empty." → "It's empty.").
- **Bounce + natural transitions** — break up compound thoughts so each idea breathes, but guide between them with **And / Because / So / But** so it reads like someone telling a story, not a list.

**Story structure:**
- **Tell it linearly.** Open on a **specific night / specific moment**, unfold in real time. Don't open vague or jump in time (paramedics shouldn't appear before we saw them called).
- **Story FIRST, explanation SECOND** — let the emotional beat land before pulling back to research/mechanism.
- **Don't cite specific years** — say "researchers found" / "a study showed." Keep it timeless.

**Endings & CTA:**
- End on **intrigue** with a **fun, relatable characterization** of the solution (feels like a discovery, not a pitch) — e.g. "a little 30-second thing I do every morning with my coffee."
- Also support an **explicit CTA**. **Formula:** *[explicit action phrase] + [casual characterization] + [brief benefit tease (2-4 lines, mechanism/transformation)] + [personal proof payoff (first-person, specific, before→after)]* — e.g. "Check out this weird thing I mix into my water bottle every morning…" then mechanism, then "By month two, my wife reached for me on a Tuesday night… and I was."

**Anti-AI style guidelines (avoid):**
- *Structural:* predictable "statement: then list"; balanced "This isn't X, it's Y"; three-part lists (use irregular numbers); formal transitions (However, Moreover, Indeed, Subsequently, Furthermore, Additionally).
- *Red-flag phrases:* "Not just X, but Y," "Whether X or Y," "This means that…," "Let's explore/dive into," "Picture this/Imagine this," "The key/truth is," business jargon (leverage, drive results, optimize), intensifiers (ultimately, essentially, simply), "One might argue…," "It is worth noting…"
- *AI words:* hurdles, harnessing, unveil, realm, delve, dive in, embark, navigate, tapestry, craft, dazzle, evoke, transform, profound, game-changing, insights, synergy, leverage.
- *Embrace instead:* varied paragraph lengths; fragments-as-speech starting with And/But/So; conversational asides & parentheticals; small imperfections; clear opinions without hedging; pattern interrupts; spontaneous questions; deliberate tangents; big sentence-length variation; specific personal details; intentionally broken grammar; conversational flow over perfect structure.

## 6.3 Then → Finish (Google Docs)
Make **new hooks** (AdHook/Mario ← hook primer + the ad) → select → **headlines** (← headline primer) → select → format into a **Google Doc** → done → Creative.

# PART 7 — CREATIVE (words become assets)

> **The governing thesis ($100M-on-Meta).** The new *mechanism* isn't a new claim — it's the **creative format itself**. A never-seen format **resets the market's sophistication clock** on two levels: the **human brain** (novelty hijacks attention; a format it's seen 1,000× is invisible) and the **machine** (Meta can't slot it next to your existing winners → forced to find a fresh audience). **The desire is the permanent payload; the format is the vehicle and must change constantly.** The DR skeleton underneath never changes. **Every format has a hidden expiration date** — copied within weeks. So the real asset is *a system for manufacturing novelty faster than the market copies it.* (Schwartz 1966 sophistication Stage 3 → "find a new mechanism"; Whitman *Cashvertising* Life-Force 8 = the permanent desires.)

## 7.2 Video — the CAST grammar (the generative engine)
Old families ("Human-first / VO+B-roll / AI animation") aren't MECE — each keys off a *different axis* (who / delivery / render). Replace with a faceted grammar: **every video = a point across 4 axes.** Decode any ad → its 4 coordinates (**reverse**). Pick a novel combination → a new format (**generate**).

**CAST = Conceit · Actor · Style · Terrain**
| Axis | Question | Values (open) |
|---|---|---|
| **C — Conceit** | What social shape / skit / premise? | talking-head/yapper · podcast · street interview · customer call · cue cards · whiteboard · green-screen react · tutorial/demo · listicle · skit · text-thread · thumb-fight · news/doc … *(where new types are born)* |

**Cut on purpose** (failed the "type-definer or variation-knob?" test): **Delivery** (sync/VO/text) → already an Iterations knob (VO↔text); **Fidelity/polish** (ugly→branded) → that's the native↔templated↔branded awareness wrapper, and it's *emergent* from Actor+Style+Terrain.

**Discipline (same as CASHED):** hold most of CAST stable, turn one. **Novelty (resets the clock):** new **Conceit** or new **Style**. **Diversity (varies a winner):** swap **Actor** or **Terrain**. **The DR skeleton is NOT a CAST axis** — it's the constant payload underneath; CAST is the vehicle.

## 7.3 The Conceit Engine (the creativity bot)
Conceit is the one axis where novelty lives → the only thing to point a creativity bot at.
```
   what's working  ──┐
   (organic + swipes  │
    + your own ads) ──┤──►  CONCEIT BOT  ──►  new conceits  ──► each becomes its own PRIMER
                       │   (abstracts the *shape*,   (cue-card, customer-call,
   formats you've ────┘    not the copy)              thumb-fight, + net-new)
   already unlocked
```
① **Feed** — a screen on what's winning organically + in ads (STORMING/swipe sources). ② **Conceit bot** — abstracts the *format/shape* (not the words), then remixes/invents. ③ **Output** — each new conceit becomes its **own primer** (winning examples + light wrapper).

## 7.4 How Conceit shapes the script + the two-layer primer model
A conceit script is a *different animal* from a long-form-unaware copy ad → **different primer types:**
- **Payload primers** *(exist today)* — cut by **awareness × length**. Teach *what to say / where to enter* = the DR engine. Stable per segment.
- **Conceit primers** *(new)* — cut by **format**. Teach *what shape to pour it into* = the vehicle. ("Story-based" is already one hiding in the awareness list — it "cross-cuts awareness," the tell that it's a different axis.)

**Apply in sequence — don't multiply into slots** (awareness × conceit = explosion):
```
payload primer (what to say) → conceit primer (what shape) → Style/Actor/Terrain (production) → scripted ad
```
Hold the payload stable, **swap the conceit** → a net-new-*feeling* ad with zero new research. One payload × 5 conceits = 5 clock-resetting ads = the novelty machine.

**A conceit primer encodes:** (a) beat→structure mapping · (b) register/voice · (c) the conceit's native hook type · (d) visual-direction cues (where Style/Terrain plug in). Examples: *Cue-card* = one beat per card, ≤8 words, terse; *Customer-call* = two-voice turns, overheard, hook = a friend's confession; *Street-interview* = Q&A, respondents stack proof; *Thumb-fight* = beats wrap a UI interaction, 4th-wall-breaking.

## 7.5 Output
static + video + iterations → **Creative gate** *(Human, optional)* → **Ship to Meta** → results feed back into Analysis (close the loop), and **winning ads feed back into the primers.**

# PART 8 — ITERATIONS · VARIATIONS · SCALING (the CASHED engine)

Three tracks for a winning ad: **NET-NEW** (new core idea — explore) · **VARIATIONS** (freeze the winner, turn one knob) · **SCALING** (amplify, change nothing). The variation engine runs on one model: **CASHED.**

## 8.1 CASHED — the knobs (freeze every letter, turn exactly ONE)
- **C — Concept / Claim** — what you assert: core idea + facts, proof, mechanism.
- **A — Angle** — the psychological frame you wrap the claim in.
- **S — Style** — visual style / medium / who delivers it.
- **H — Hook** — the opening.
- **E — Edit** — tone & format dials: length, valence/intensity, register, pacing, CTAs.
- **D — Demographic / Who** — the whole segment (biggest swing — borders net-new).

**The decision test — "what's the smallest box that changed?"** Ask big→small, stop at first yes: new WHO → **D** · same who, CLAIM/facts/mechanism changed → **C** · same claim, new FRAME → **A** · same claim+frame, new LOOK/medium/presenter → **S** · same everything, new OPENING → **H** · only tone/length/pacing → **E**. Each knob is a spectrum — a small turn is cosmetic, a full turn becomes net-new.

## 8.2 VARIATIONS (the "Re-" moves · 🔴 high / 🟡 medium priority)
**Re-Aim — turn D (new WHO):** Awareness-level test 🔴 · Mechanism-segment 🔴 · Different sub-avatar 🟡 · Condition/value slice 🟡 · Stack into a thin persona 🟡 · Self-discrepancy 🟡.
**Re-Concept — turn C (new claim/content):** Mechanism re-explain/swap 🔴 · Proof/credibility swap 🔴 · Overcoming objections (from comments, woven, not a list) 🔴 · Extended problem section 🔴 · New supporting facts 🟡.
**Re-Angle — turn A (new frame):** Reframe the claim 🔴 · Negative framing ("honestly, don't buy this…") 🟡. *The multiplication engine.*
**Re-Skin — turn S (new look):** Swap who delivers it (UGC/founder/authority; vary age/gender/energy) 🔴 · VO ↔ text-on-screen 🔴 · Organic/ugly edit 🔴 · New medium/port the format 🔴 (Static extraction 🔴 · First-frame static 🟡 · Native static 🟡 · Static + congruent LP 🔴) · VSL expansion 🔴 · AI animation 🟡 · Podcast style 🟡 · New location/setting/vibe 🟡.
**Re-Hook — turn H (new opening):** Volume hook-swap (proven body + 5–10 openings) 🔴 · Winning-hook remake 🔴 · Comment-reply hook 🔴 · In media res 🟡.
**Edit — turn E (do INSIDE a bigger turn; Andromeda collapses these):** Valence/intensity 🔴 · Dumber version 🟡 · Longer/shorter 🟡 · Soft CTAs/pacing 🟡.

## 8.3 SCALING (amplify, change nothing)
Budget scale ladder 🔴 ($100 → 200 → 350 → 550 → 750 → 1,000; don't wait) · Creative-volume flood 🔴 (feed 10–20 *diverse* creatives) · Stack-winners mega-ad 🟡 (3–4 winning clips/hooks into one compilation).

## 8.4 Angle examples (Re-Angle source — a taste)
New discovery · Accidental discovery · Root cause · Conspiracy/hidden · Breaking news · Story/case study · Contrarian/myth-busting · Enemy admission · Geographic · Confession.

## 8.5 IDENTIFY — decode the winner FIRST (each question = one CASHED letter)
**Desire** → D (ladder with 5 Whys) · **Avatar** → D · **Awareness level** → D · **Problem/Positioning** → C *(their doc calls this "angle"; we reserve Angle for the frame)* · **Mechanism** → C · **Authority/proof** → C · **Emotion** → E (valence+intensity) · **Frame** → A. *Then turn one knob.*

## 8.6 The split (adjustable per person)
Budget default ≈ **70 scale / 20 iterate / 10 net-new.** Production effort rides account maturity: cold ≈ 85% net-new → mature ≈ 25–30% (never zero — winners fatigue). Real driver is the matrix: big gaps → bias net-new · well-covered + fresh winners → bias iterate.
> **Net-new is prioritized over iterations** in *production* (writing new ads beats optimizing one hook). The 70/20/10 is *budget*. Don't confuse the two.

# PART 11 — COPY BLOCKS + PERSONA FRAMEWORKS (the writing IP)

## 11.1 The 5 Copy Blocks (+ bonus) — **P3C2** (Pain · Promise · Proof · Constraints · Curiosity + Conditions)
1. **Pain** — the urgent problem they want gone. Use *problem tokens*. **Pain Chain:** General → Specific → Cinematic → Emotional → Core Wound.
2. **Promise** — the desired outcome/identity gain; live on the edge of believability; be generous; anchor to the **Core Transformation. Promise Ladder:** General → Specific → Cinematic → Emotional → Core Transformation.
3. **Proof** — reason to believe. *"Never make the claim bigger than the proof."* 5 categories/22 types: Psychological · Experiential (strongest) · Empirical · Credible · Social. Pair every Promise with Proof ("Siamese Twin"). Psychological proof = most underused, best strength-to-effort.
4. **Constraints** — objections/limits blocking the sale. Big Three: **Money, Time, Effort. Resonance Hierarchy:** Experiences → Beliefs → Values → **Identity** (formed bottom-up, interacted top-down; appease Identity, shift Experiences/Beliefs). **Dissolve via A.W.E.:** Acknowledge → Wedge (counterexample → "both/and") → Elaborate.
5. **Curiosity (Mechanism)** — the unique bridge Pain → Promise; the differentiator; most important. **Curiosity Quadrant:** External/Internal × Problem/Solution × General/Specific. **Insight** = Simple · Easy · Explanatory · Novel (Epiphany Threshold ~6-8/10). **Characterization** = Evocative Naming · Idea Caricatures · Intuition Pumps (e.g. "Joint Drought").
6. **Conditions (bonus)** — offer terms: Qualifications · Conversion Triggers (urgency/scarcity) · Risk Reversal (guarantees) · Value Adds · Terms & Structure.

**Persuasion equation:** `VALUE = PROMISE × (PROOF × CURIOSITY) / CONSTRAINTS × CONDITIONS`
**CRAVES** (make any block stronger): Clear · Relevant · Accurate · Visual · Expressive · Specific.
**Copy Velocity** = #blocks / #words × 100 — drench copy in blocks, cut filler.

## 11.2 Persona framework (Todd Brown) — 6 dimensions + extras
**Demographics** *(targeting params, not limits)* · **Wants/Desires** (Primary drives purchase + Secondary shapes messaging) · **Emotions/Feelings** (Dominant drives the hook + Supporting) · **Beliefs/Identifications** (core values respected in all messaging) · **Behavior Profile** (prior purchases, info sources, online presence) · **3D Psyche** (Seeing · Hearing · Saying · Thinking · Feeling · Doing — Saying/Thinking → pull copy; Feeling → hooks; Doing → timing/placement) · **Marketing Thesis** (one core proposition) · **Unique Mechanism** (the Curiosity block, per persona) · **Objections & Buying Motivations.** Then layer **Copy Blocks per persona** — **each persona has its own mechanism**; Proof/Pain/Mechanism can be **pulled directly from the ads** targeting that persona.

## 11.3 The ad→persona classification rule (coverage)
Classify each **whole ad → ONE persona** (its NET IMPRESSION). **Hard rules:** whole-ad judgment, never keyword matching · **default every field to null**, assign only when clearly built around it · **Gender is high-criteria** — "Men"/"Women" only on explicit callout ("Men —", "Ladies,", "for women over 50"); a male/female character ≠ a gendered ad · at most one value per dimension · outcome is usually clearest but still null if unclear. *(Full prompt in `context/ad-classify-prompt.md`.)*
