# Example swipes (practice material for cold start)

These are **generic, made-up example ads** — not real winners — so a customer with nothing yet can
practice the swipe-rewrite (`../../templates/cold-start.md`) entirely inside this folder. Replace them
with real competitor ads you admire (Meta Ad Library, scrapecreators.com, Hookd, Get Hooked) as soon as
you can. Don't ship these as ads — they're just structure to Mad-Libs against.

---

## Example A — long-form, unaware/problem (story → mechanism → offer)
> Are you not hungry for breakfast in the morning? That might not be discipline — it might be a warning.
>
> For two years I blamed my age for the weight that wouldn't move and the afternoons I could barely keep
> my eyes open. Clean eating, more walks, less sugar — nothing touched it.
>
> Then a tired doctor at a walk-in clinic asked me one odd question about my mornings. And it all clicked.
>
> It turns out a stress signal in the body can stay "stuck on," keeping blood sugar high and energy low
> no matter how well you eat. When I finally calmed that one signal, the weight around my middle started
> letting go — and my energy came back by the third week.
>
> Here's the strange 30-second thing I now do with my morning coffee. [CTA]

## Example B — short-form, problem-aware (punchy, comparison)
> Most "energy" supplements just dump caffeine on a broken system and call it a fix.
>
> The problem was never that you needed more stimulation. It's that one quiet signal is keeping your body
> in survival mode — so the fuel never reaches the tank.
>
> Calm that one signal and the tiredness lifts on its own. No jitters, no crash. [CTA]
