# Winner: High Ticket Sales Hook

**Hook:**
Here's why most coaches aren't generating enough high ticket sales from their audience & list

**Why it worked:**
Opens on a pain-point the audience already feels — not enough high ticket sales — without leading with proof or student results.
Positions the coach as someone with the answer, not someone selling.
"Most coaches" creates instant identification for the reader without alienating them.
World-level problem hook before any credibility or proof.
