- Hook: "Your client's CRM is probably worth more than their entire ad budget this year, and nobody's told them yet."
- Why it's promising: reframes the client's DEAD lead database as the asset — world-level, not proof-led, and it makes the reader (the agency owner) the one who spots the goldmine. Sidesteps the tired "escape the grind" open by leading with the prospect's money that's already sitting there.
- Reuse it for: cold agency/lead-gen audiences on the AI-employee / DBR play. The "existing asset they've forgotten" reframe travels to any DBR offer.
- Do NOT reuse for: JV partner lists without reworking — this leads with the reader's clients, which is fine cold, but partner framing is additive (see [[jv-additive-framing]]).

## Two edit-patterns from Joey's pass on the MarioBot draft (the transferable lessons)
- **First-person credibility bridge over a neutral connective.** Joey swapped MarioBot's "where this whole model starts" → "where I've made a ton of money." Plants the operator right before the mechanism. Watch for neutral bridges in AI drafts and earn them back.
- **Ladder the mechanism pivot** (now also a writer-side rule in [[mariobot-style-contract]]): MarioBot crammed "instead of a retainer → battling CPLs → you install an AI employee" into one line; Joey broke it into a 3-line ellipsis ladder + added "(competing with hundreds of agencies)."
