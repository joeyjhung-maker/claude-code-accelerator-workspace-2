# Winner (process): prime mariobot with real primers, never a from-memory summary

*A process win, not an ad win. Named without the date prefix because two files already link to it as [[prime-mariobot-with-real-primers]] — the filename has to match for the link to resolve. Banked 2026-07-06.*

- The move: before mariobot writes, prime it with REAL locked winning copy pasted verbatim — Joey's WINNER edits and Dan's locked ads — pulled from `clients/flexxable-primers/`. Never re-type a "summary of the voice" from memory as the prime.
- Where it proved out: 2026-07-06, Ryan/Leadbase IAA ads. The thin-primed run came out flat ("~70% there, register flat, proof wrong" — Joey's words on the WINNER). The same day, the 222k ad primed on the hooks primer + locked winner + real testimonial-bank proof came out visibly closer to voice.
- Why it wins: mariobot is stateless and only as good as what it absorbs on that call. A hand-typed summary carries none of the rhythm, register, or proof texture of the real copy — so the draft averages out to generic.
- Reuse it for: every mariobot run, any client. Build each client's body primer in `clients/{name}-primers/` and feed it via `scripts/run_mario.py --primer`.
- Do not: prime on old locked copy alone without the style contract — Dan's 06-29 ads contain staccato patterns the rubric has since banned, and the prime teaches them. The contract (`rubrics/mariobot-style-contract.md`) rides along in the instruction to override that.
