## The skeleton
> I hate to break it to you, but [3–5 unglamorous fundamentals, listed] will [deliver the outcome they want] better than anything else.

## Why it works
- The opener sets up a flinch — the reader braces for bad news.
- What lands instead is a boring truth. That inversion is the whole engine: the "bad news" is that the answer was never a hack, it's the dull work they've been avoiding.
- Stacking 3–5 specifics does two jobs — concrete enough to be credible, long enough to feel exhaustive.
- The absolute closer ("better than anything else") forces the reader into agreement or argument. Both are engagement.
- Passes the no-shaming rule: nobody is made the villain, and the tone is regretful rather than superior.

## The bigger lesson — cross-niche structure transfer
This is the second confirmation in one session that **post structure travels between unrelated niches**:
1. Joey's best post is a fitness structure ported to faith.
2. Joey's "Definition Flip" shape ("X isn't what you think, it's Y") turned up independently in the *discipline* niche, written by someone who has never heard of him.

Structure is niche-agnostic. Only the nouns change. Swiping shapes from health, money, recovery, stoicism and writing is legitimate fuel for the faith and entrepreneurship lanes.

## The mistake to not repeat
**Used exactly once in 403 posts.** There is a draft of a second version sitting unposted in the Notion archive. The account's best-performing structure was found, pinned, and then abandoned for six months.

Reuse it. That is the entire point of banking it.
