# Make 'Em Beg to Buy from You (Travis Sago, 2024)

Travis's core pre-selling book — the "5 Ps" framework already referenced at one remove in the
Hopper Millions digests (`../hopper-millions/digests/00-foundations.md`) turns out to originate
here, in far more depth. Where Hopper Millions is about *group/community* mechanics (crock pot vs
microwave, the domino, comment-gating), this book is the underlying *copy/persuasion* engine those
mechanics run on.

## Book structure (for orientation, not a task list)

1. Introduction — the 5 Ps of Pre-Selling, framed as the "Pre-Sell Sandwich."
2. Ch1: The Pre-Sell Sandwich Method — the golf-course metaphor, why pre-selling beats
   convincing.
3. Ch2: Present Pain — symptomatic messaging, the index-finger test, Hell Island/Heaven Island.
4. Ch3: The Plan — the "meat" of the sandwich, the Pitch Slap, TIMER, qualifying for commitment
   before cash.
5. Ch4: Personality & Philosophy — the condiments; includes the actual origin of "30-Year Wealth
   Shortcut" as Travis's own named philosophy.
6. Ch5: Price — pre-selling price as a TIMER trade-off, not just a dollar figure; harpooning
   objection "sharks."
7. Ch6: The Idiot's 3-Step Plan to Get Rich — find something good to say (ask/listen), say it
   well, say it often.
8. Bonus Chapter — a full interview with Andy Hussong on "endorsed traffic" (JV/affiliate
   outreach): why it compounds where paid/organic traffic doesn't, and the concrete mechanics of
   landing and keeping JV partners.

## Why this matters here

This book is the deepest source yet for: the exact mechanics of "Present Pain" and symptomatic
messaging already used in B2B Leads Lab copy, a sharper objection-handling framework (TIMER) than
anything currently in the rubric, the real origin story behind the "X-Hour Dream 100 Shortcut"
naming convention already locked for B2B Leads Lab, and a concrete JV-outreach playbook directly
relevant to the underdeveloped JV lane in `clients/flexxable/b2b-leads-lab/project.md`. See
`synthesis.md` for the full breakdown and where each piece plugs into existing work.
