## Structure (the skeleton to steal)

1. **Personalization** — opens by naming their exact offer/niche back to them, so the pitch
   reads as "we solve exactly that problem," not a broadcast.
2. **Overview** — logistics of what's happening (dates, format), framed to feel different
   from anything they've seen.
3. **Unique promise** — solves their present pain in a way that sounds impossible
   ("6th grade simple... WITHOUT VSLs, Webinars or phone calls").
4. **Proof statement** — one concrete number from the last cohort ("$484,000 in sales,
   everyone but one made a sale").
5. **What's been stopping them (general)** — names the market's current broken approach
   and WHY it fails (phone-first before the offer is dialed in → burns leads, feels awful).
6. **What's been stopping them (specific)** — narrows to the ONE missing piece (a dialed-in
   Easy-Yes offer + pre-selling), not a vague "you need help."
7. **Why now** — scarcity that's about the seller's own constraint, not manufactured urgency
   ("I only teach when I feel like it, don't know if I'll run this again").
8. **CTA + risk reversal** — plain ask ("in, out, or a question?"), deposit + backend
   pricing, and a self-serve guarantee mechanic (pay the balance only after you've banked
   $10k+ — "honor system").

## Why it worked / what to steal

- **The proof number is a RESULT, not a promise** — "$484,000... everyone made a sale" is
  something a prospect can't argue with, dropped early, before the pitch even fully unfolds.
- **The pain diagnosis has two layers** — first the market-wide broken pattern (phone before
  offer is dialed in), then the SPECIFIC missing piece for THIS reader (no Easy-Yes offer,
  no pre-selling). Two passes, general then personal, is what makes it land as
  "you specifically" rather than "people in general."
- **Risk reversal ties to a number the reader controls** — "$4k balance after you deposit
  $10k+" makes the guarantee self-evident math, not a policy they have to trust.
- **The CTA has zero pressure language** — "in, out, or a question" is the lowest-friction
  close in the entire swipe. No countdown, no "spots filling up."
