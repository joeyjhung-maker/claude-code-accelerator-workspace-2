Two-step cold-outreach templates plus the "Columbo Response" for handling objections. Candidate motion for landing Heuromi's own performance-based clients — read before the next agency new-biz outreach push.
- **`2026-09-03-netflix-model-training.md`** — live training call: how to keep a paid Skool tier converting after launch via continuous small content drops ("Netflix Model"), zero-CTA premium-only posts, VIP/Shogun tier design (negotiating contributor pay via "what would be dreamy for you"), and how to assess an existing community's real value before acquiring/bolting onto it. Directly informs B2B Leads Lab's Anchor Course drip plan — read before building the Sep/Oct mini-course sequence.
- **`psm-btem/`** — Travis's "Big Ticket Email Mojo" email-copy course (plus an unlabeled earlier course, "PSM"). The Circle of Money (60% list/30% offer/10% copy), the Triangle of Insight NAP-email template (Symptom → Wisdom → Metaphor), NAP vs. Harvest campaigns, the CAP Child/Adult/Parent buying-psychology model (same CAP as Hopper Millions — see that folder's corrected entry), the Rainmaker Campaign launch structure, and a fully annotated real NAP email swipe.
