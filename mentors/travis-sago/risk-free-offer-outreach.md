# The Risk-Free Commission-Only Offer (Travis Sago)

Not a hopper/group-growth play like `hopper-millions/` — this is a **client-acquisition**
strategy. Joey's use case: getting more performance-based clients for Heuromi Media, not
building or monetizing a Skool group.

Related: [[travis-sago]] [[joey-runs-heuromi-agency]] [[travis-sago-make-em-beg-synthesis]]

Source: Travis Sago post, ingested 2026-08-28. Verbatim templates preserved below — do not
paraphrase them when using this play, the exact wording is the point.

## The core move

Find a crowd that HATES doing something. Offer to take it off their plate on a
**risk-free, commission-only basis**.

For agency new-biz specifically: don't lead with the tools (Sales Savers, Auctions,
Rainmakers, or in Heuromi's case: media buying, creative systems, etc). Lead with *taking
the thing they hate off their plate*, risk-free. Most business owners hate generating
leads and hate doing sales — that's the crowd this targets.

## The two-step approach (for cold/new relationships — "Ronin")

**Pre-step:** 5–10 minutes of research per prospect. The goal is that they feel a REAL
human reached out, not AI or a canned blast. Skip this and the whole play collapses.

**Step 1 — find something personal they're proud of, then send a short, warm, no-ask
email:**

> Subj: Land University is on fire
>
> I saw your Land U Skool is #9 in the Skool games!
>
> Are you still running Land U with Jeff?
>
> -Travis

Wait for a response. No pitch in step 1 — it's purely a genuine, specific compliment that
proves a human looked closely.

**Step 2 — once they reply, be upfront and make the offer.** Respond to what they actually
said, then land the ask:

> "It says a lot that you and Jeff have been partners for 15 years...
>
> We haven't been introduced yet...
>
> I'm emailing because...
>
> I'd like to generate 100 qualified leads for Land U and enroll new clients into your Lot
> Flipping Class.
>
> ...in 7 days, give or take
>
> ...on a commission-only basis
>
> Would you be open to giving it a try?
>
> -Travis"

Deliberately light on detail. That's a filter, not a gap: most owners who hate lead gen/sales
either won't respond, or say "I'm good" — filtering out the ones who'll be a pain to work with
before any time is spent. The ones worth having will respond **with questions**.

## The Columbo Response — handling questions

No matter what question comes back ("what's the commission %?" etc.), don't answer it in
text. Play dumb-ish and pre-sell a call:

> "That's a fair question. I'm not smart enough to answer it by email because there are so
> many factors. Your Lot Flipping Class is $5k. I wouldn't even want to do this if it
> doesn't put at least $25k in your pocket after commission in a 7-day test.
>
> Would you be open to discussing it?"

The pattern: acknowledge the question as fair → claim you can't do it justice by email →
anchor a concrete, favorable number specific to their offer → redirect to a call. Every
objection gets this same shape, not a bespoke answer.

## Why it works

- The risk-free/commission-only framing removes the biggest objection (cost, trust in an
  unproven agency) before it's raised.
- Targeting what they *hate* doing (not what they need) finds owners who are already
  motivated to say yes — pain they're already avoiding, not a pitch they have to be sold on.
- Doing 10 of these a week, consistently, is what turns into coffee dates and partners —
  it's a volume/consistency play, not a one-off script.

Travis notes variations exist (e.g. sending to a Loom before the email) but says not to
layer those in before the foundation above is solid.

Joey runs Heuromi Media ([[joey-runs-heuromi-agency]]) — this is a candidate outreach
