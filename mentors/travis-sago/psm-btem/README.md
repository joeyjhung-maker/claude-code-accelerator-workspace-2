## Why this matters here

This is the deepest source yet on TWO things not well covered elsewhere in this workspace: (1) how
to structure an actual NAP email from scratch (the Triangle of Insight: Symptom → Wisdom →
Metaphor), and (2) a Child/Adult/Parent lens for judging whether a finished piece of copy actually
works on all three levels a buyer processes it through. Both are directly usable for B2B Leads
Lab's weekly NAP cadence and for tightening `copy-rubric.md`'s judging checklist. See
`synthesis.md` for the full breakdown and where each piece plugs into existing work.
