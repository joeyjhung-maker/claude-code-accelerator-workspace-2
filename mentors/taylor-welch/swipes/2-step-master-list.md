# Taylor Welch — 2-Step master list (verbatim swipe reference)

*Source: Google Doc linked from Notion "Creating CTA & Bookings"
(https://docs.google.com/document/d/17b4nFbf7QfqDzMTdqBJjWipZjUZ4y-yJ0exbKc0vwwk/edit), saved
2026-09-02. Taylor's own claim: each of these did "at least 500 leads per, and grossed $100k per
post" — his number, not verified against any Flexxable data. Never cite these figures as fact in
Flexxable copy.*

**Mad-Lib the SHAPE only.** Steal the qualifier conditions, the scarcity framing, the "team is
standing by" mechanic, the two-rules structure. Never reuse the actual language, offer names, or
claimed numbers — this is a competitor/mentor swipe, not house copy.

## Recurring skeleton across all of these
1. A real, substantial teaching/insight/story FIRST (never skip this — matches the "value before
   ask" rule in `../synthesis.md`).
2. A qualifier list (2-3 conditions: "you're capable and willing," "you promise not to share
   this," "you'll actually implement") — does double duty as a soft filter AND an exclusivity
   signal.
3. The literal ask: "Comment '[KEYWORD]' and we'll send it to you."
4. A scarcity/urgency close: a deadline, a limited number of spots, or "by the end of the
   weekend."
5. Sign-off in the sender's own voice.

## Raw examples (verbatim, for structure study only)

---

**"THE GOAL isn't 'money' it's wealth"** — opens on a reframe (money vs. wealth), names 3 stages
(Validation / Infrastructure / Wealth Building), offers a book that teaches all 3. Qualifier: A)
capable and willing to follow through, B) hungry for growth, C) promise not to share it outside
the group. Ask: comment "Money secrets."

---

**"How many of you feel like you have superior offers... but are seeing clients take the bait of
hypey marketing"** — opens on a felt injustice (better than the competition, losing anyway),
promises a training ("The Holy Grail") with no hype/no ad spend/no bending the truth required.
Scarcity: "ready in 5 days." Ask: comment "HOLY GRAIL."

---

**"READ IF YOU CHARGE LESS THAN $8,000"** — a segmented headline that pre-qualifies by a concrete
number. Teaches the actual offer-packaging lesson in full (Offer A vs Offer B, lawn-care example,
"packaging is 50% of what you can charge") before the ask. Two rules: don't share, implement.
Ask: comment "CHARGE MORE." Closes with the math made concrete: "$8k per client at 10 clients a
month = a million dollars a year."

---

**"Sup fellow entrepreneurs... here's how to get more sales"** — personal origin story (his first
awkward sales call) as the value, positions the free asset as an extension of that story ("Sales
Master Bundle"). Two rules: don't share, watch it this week. Ask: comment "SELL ME."

---

**"HEY HEY HEY HEY (this is awesome but I gotta vet you first)"** — offers a write-up (not a
video), explicitly limits it ("probably 10 or 15 of you"), and stacks a SECOND value-add inside
the ask itself: reply and I'll also diagnose your biggest bottleneck. Ask: comment interest,
DMs follow.

---

**"I surpassed them all" / "0" / origin story from a spare bedroom** — full founder-story arc
(from $6k/month to consistent 6-figure days), pivots to "would you sign up for that class, if it
cost you nothing but time?" Two rules: apply it, don't rip it off/share it. Ask: comment "PICK
ME."

---

**"A report on charging more"** — a near-identical re-run of the $8k offer-packaging post above,
months later, same lawn-care example, same math, same two rules, same core teaching — confirms
Taylor's own stated method of re-running a proven post/insight rather than always inventing new
material (matches /storm's "if a post/insight worked once, revisit and re-run variations" rule).
Ask: comment "OFFERS."

---

**"Can I show you how to write GOOD ADS?"** — leads with a real, dated proof number ("It's
September 24th and YTD we're just around $8,000,000 in paid ad spend"), names three past brand
launches as track record, offers a masterclass "on the house." Three rules: ready to implement,
be nice to the team, don't share. Explicitly states WHY he's giving it away (a past student who
consumed free content and later joined a paid room). Ask: comment "ADZ PLS." Closes with real
urgency: "by the end of the weekend this post is locked up."

---

**"HOW I EARN $19,000 PER HOUR"** — the longest example, a full origin story ($3.60/hr church job
→ $19/hr real estate → $19k/hour now) building to a private internal sales-team training offered
as a gift. Explicitly says why he's motivated to give it away (helping people speed up their
journey). Ask: comment "20k."

---

**"Welp lol" — the annual purge post** — NOT a lead-gen 2-step; this is the "stick" version of the
engagement-boost mechanic from `../synthesis.md`. States a real operational fact (group has grown
too big, engagement metrics have dropped), sets three self-qualifying criteria, asks for a bare
comment to "stay," and states plainly that non-responders get removed in an announced house-
cleaning. Useful as a reference for what the stick mechanic actually looks like in practice, not
as a template to run on B2B Leads Lab without Joey/Dan explicitly deciding to.
