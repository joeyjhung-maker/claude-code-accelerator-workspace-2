## Book structure (for orientation, not a task list)

1. What's a Money Model? — a Money Model is a deliberate *sequence* of offers, not one offer. Four offer types, built in this order: Attraction → Upsell → Downsell → Continuity.
2. Attraction Offers — turn strangers into customers: Win Your Money Back, Giveaways, Decoy Offer, Buy X Get Y Free, Pay Less Now or Pay More Later.
3. Upsell Offers — get people to spend more, immediately: The Classic Upsell, Menu Upsell, Anchor Upsell, Rollover Upsell.
4. Downsell Offers — turn a "no" into a "yes": Payment Plan Downsells, Trial With Penalty, Feature Downsells.
5. Continuity Offers — keep them paying: Continuity Bonus Offers, Continuity Discount Offers, Waived Fee Offers.
6. Make Your Money Model — build one stage at a time, in order, never all four at once.
