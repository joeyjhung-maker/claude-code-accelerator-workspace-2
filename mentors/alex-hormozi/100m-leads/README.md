## Book structure (for orientation, not a task list)

1. Get Understanding — what a "lead" actually is (engaged lead, not just a name), and how offers/lead magnets turn a lead into one.
2. Get Leads — the **Core Four**: warm outreach, posting free content, cold outreach, paid ads. Plus "More Better New" for scaling any of them.
3. Get Lead Getters — getting other people to advertise for you: customer referrals, employees, agencies, affiliates.
4. Get Started — Rule of 100 / Open to Goal (volume discipline), and the 7-level roadmap from first lead to $100M+ machine.
