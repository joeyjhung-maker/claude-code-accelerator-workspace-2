# Alex Hormozi

Founder of Acquisition.com. Not a personal mentor of Dan's the way [[travis-sago]] is — this is a book ingestion, kept in the same `mentors/` shape because the material is a reusable external framework, not client work.

Related: [[Memory Map]] [[Promotion]]

## What's here

- **`100m-leads/`** — his book *$100M Leads*, on lead generation: the Core Four (warm outreach, content, cold outreach, paid ads) and the lead-getters (referrals, employees, agencies, affiliates). Ingested 2026-08-24 from Joey's own epub. Read `100m-leads/synthesis.md` for the distilled framework.
- **`100m-money-models/`** — his book *$100M Money Models*, on offer sequencing: Attraction → Upsell → Downsell → Continuity offers, how to turn a single sale into a 30-day-payback money machine. Ingested 2026-08-24 from Joey's own PDF. Read `100m-money-models/synthesis.md` — this is the most directly actionable of the three for auditing Flexxable's offer structure (ROYA) and JV front-end pricing.
- **`100m-offers/`** — his book *$100M Offers*, the value/offer-construction side: the Value Equation, the five-step Grand Slam Offer build (problems → solutions → Delivery Cube → trim & stack), and the five enhancers (scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantees, M-A-G-I-C naming). Full book ingested 2026-08-24, superseding an earlier pass that turned out to be a mislabeled 13-page companion workbook, not the real ~206-page book. Read `100m-offers/synthesis.md` — this is the actual source of the "value equation" vocabulary the other two syntheses reference at one remove.

No raw transcripts or full-text copies kept in the vault for any of the three; the source files stay wherever Joey keeps his ebooks (`~/Downloads` at time of ingestion).

## Why keep this at all

The Core Four, the lead-getters, and the four-stage Money Model give a shared vocabulary for lead-gen and offer strategy across every lane in this workspace — Flexxable client ads, B2B Leads Lab's cold-mail funnel, JV partner deals, and Heuromi Media's own agency positioning all touch some part of this. See each book's `synthesis.md` for where it matches, extends, or is already implicit in what this workspace does.
