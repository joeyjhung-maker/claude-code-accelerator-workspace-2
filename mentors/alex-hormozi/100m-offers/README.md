## Book structure (for orientation, not a task list)

1. How We Got Here / Grand Slam Offers — the core concept: an offer priced, valued, guaranteed, and named so it can't be compared to anything else in the market.
2. Pricing — the commodity problem, picking the right market (pain, purchasing power, targetability, growth), charging what it's worth, the virtuous cycle of price.
3. Value: Create Your Offer — the Value Equation (Dream Outcome, Perceived Likelihood, Time Delay, Effort & Sacrifice), then a five-step build: problems → solutions → delivery vehicles (the "Delivery Cube") → trim & stack.
4. Enhancing Your Offer — scarcity, urgency, bonuses, guarantees, and the M-A-G-I-C naming formula.
5. Execution — wrap-up and next steps.
