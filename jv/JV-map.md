# JV Partnerships Map

The department map for JV (joint venture) partner deals — separate from the core Flexxable retainer work in `clients/`, `copy/`, `creatives/`.

## The play

Every partner deal runs two directions:

1. **Sell their offer to our audience.** The partner's product/service/course goes out to the Flexxable list/FB audience.
2. **Sell our offer to their audience.** The Instant AI Agency book or the ROYA coaching program goes out to the partner's list/audience.

Each partner needs its own research (their avatar, their product, their sales page) before copy gets built in either direction. Treat each partner as its own mini-client — don't blend avatars or angles across partners.

## Per-partner structure

Each partner folder holds:

- `partner.md` — who they are, their offer, their audience avatar, voice/rules, what's working, what died.
- `research/` — drop zone for raw research before it's synthesized into `partner.md`.
- `copy/sell-their-offer/` — copy selling the partner's product to the Flexxable audience.
- `copy/sell-our-offer/` — copy selling IAA book / ROYA to the partner's audience.

## Rules that carry over

Same rules as the core Script (`CLAUDE.md`) apply here — hook first, no invented results, run the judge before showing copy, short punchy paragraphs, no corporate language. This is a new vertical, not a new rulebook.

## Hard rules learned (the JV ladder)

Earned from the Ryan Magdanz Rainmaker post-mortem ([losers/2026-07-16-jv-webinar-straight-to-highticket-no-ladder.md](../losers/2026-07-16-jv-webinar-straight-to-highticket-no-ladder.md)). Do NOT re-run the dead motion.

- **Never sell high-ticket (ROYA) cold off a webinar to a partner's audience.** They're cold to us, low-buyer, and a link with no conversation converts ~0. ROYA is the *ascension*, not the webinar offer.
- **The webinar always sells a $500–$1K reversible front-end** (currently **$497–$997** for Buildy; possibly two packages — undecided). High enough to make a real buyer who consumes it; low enough to be a safe, reversible yes at emotional peak. Needs a real (Dan-true) guarantee.
- **Never sell the book over a webinar.** Too low-ticket — wastes the event and barely indoctrinates. The book's job is pre-frame (free lead magnet) or an order bump/bonus, never the headline offer.
- **Price point dictates the close mechanism.** Cheap rung ($500–1K) = self-serve cart off the webinar (price matches trust). High rung (ROYA) = conversation + **partner co-closes** (the only thing that converted for Ryan — engineer it, don't leave it to chance).
- **The front-end's real job is indoctrination, not revenue:** first dollar to *us* (trust transfers to Dan), belief in the mechanism before the big ask, a real win that qualifies them for ROYA, and self-selection of the serious.
- **Add to the partner's model, never ask for a pivot** ([[jv-additive-framing]]) — and match the bait to the offer (deliver what the webinar promised).
