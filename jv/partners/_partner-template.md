# JV Partner: {Name} — {Business}

Copy this file to `jv/partners/{name}/partner.md` for each new JV partner, alongside a `research/` folder and a `copy/sell-their-offer/` + `copy/sell-our-offer/` folder. This is the context Claude loads before doing that partner's work, in either direction.

## Who they are
One or two lines. Who the partner is, what they're known for, what they sell.

## Their offer (what we sell to our audience)
- Product/service/course name, price, mechanism.
- Sales page link.
- Proof we're allowed to use — only what's documented. If it's not here, ask Joey.

## Their audience (what we sell our offer into)
- Who follows them — niche, sophistication level, pain points, objections.
- Why the IAA book or ROYA is a fit for this audience specifically.

## Voice and rules
- What this partner sounds like, or is sensitive about.
- Approval process — who signs off before anything goes out.

## What is working right now
- Current winning angles and hooks, either direction. Update after each review.

## What died
- Angles that flopped for this partner, so we don't re-pitch them.
