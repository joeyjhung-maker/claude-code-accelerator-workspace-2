## Their audience (what we sell our offer into)
- **Solo founders / non-technical first-timers.** Both the homepage ("Join Thousands of Solo Founders Who Made the Leap") and the webinar page confirm a **beginner / aspiring** skew — sold on "AI does it for you." NOT established agency owners.
- Their owned audience likely lives in the **Skool community** (`skool.com/buildy`) — that's probably the list the JV webinar hits. Bill also funnels from a big **YouTube channel** + **free monthly webinars**.
- They're **webinar-trained** (used to run 2–3/month) and have been through auction/Beamer-style campaigns — Russell says they've "seen it work," which de-risks a webinar launch.
- Two doorways they run: homepage sells the **product** ("your AI team / 7 agents"); the webinar funnel sells the **money result** ("$233,496 in 14 days"). Know which one a given segment has seen.
- **Why ROYA fits (sharpened by Travis Sago, our mentor):** the Buildy audience can now *build* anything — their unanswered pain is **how do you actually SELL AI / get paying clients.** ROYA answers exactly that. Buildy = the build side; ROYA = the sell side, the missing half.
- **The angle to LEAD with for this audience:** the **anti-retainer / commission-only** play — *not* $25k to build a tool, *not* $3k/mo to automate; instead demo → risk-free, commission-only test → split the upside, low delivery hassle. Travis: it's old to ROYA's core list but **new to the JV audience**. Combine with Russell's read (lead with outcomes + curiosity, stay blind on the mechanism) and the DVR **Sleeping Beauty → Prince Charming** path as the way in. See `research/2026-07-15-travis-sago-mentor-advice.md`.
