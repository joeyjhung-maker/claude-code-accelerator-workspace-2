## Russell's marketing insights — the gold (apply to the Buildy JV, and back-apply to Ryan)

**1. Lead blind. Outcomes + curiosity, not the named vehicle.**
Going into the first webinar, lead with outcomes and benefits and stay "blind" about the mechanism. Heavy curiosity. If you come out direct and name the thing, people go "oh I already know this, I've seen this" → attendance drops.

**2. Nobody wakes up wanting to start an agency.**
They wake up wanting an outcome — more leads, more sales, more clients, today. Sell the outcome; the agency/tool is just the vehicle. (Same reason "automation agency" is hot but shouldn't be the lead.)

**3. The webinar IS the pre-sell/framing — especially straight-to-Rainmaker.**
When you're not warming with a Beamer/auction campaign first, the webinar has to do the pre-framing. The audience needs a frame for what this is before the Rainmaker hits.

**4. Match how the audience is used to being sold.**
Buildy's list is trained on webinars (they used to run 2–3/month), and they've been through auction/Beamer-style campaigns — so they've "seen it work," which lowers Russell's risk worry. If they *hadn't*, he'd be more cautious.

**5. Slowing webinar cadence helped.**
They eased off frequency lately and it worked in their favour — freshness/scarcity.

**6. Curiosity in the invite drives the room.**
For Dan's folks: "they've never seen anything like this," play it up, talk about *why Dan's excited* and the benefit. That's what fills the webinar.

**7. Stack the back end: encore → final encore → recording → sales team.**
Run an encore (final encore if needed), then the recording, then a sales team does all follow-up. Let the partner keep following up in the community long after — max sales, max win both sides.

**8. Make them experience the funnel while they're in it.**
In the 24/7 Sales Engine webinar, attendees actually move through the personalised funnel as they watch — demo-by-doing, not just tell.
