## The frame — "borrow our brand"
Badge: **"CHARTER OFFER: AVAILABLE FOR A LIMITED TIME."**
Headline (verbatim):
> "Borrow Our Brand + $200M-Proven Playbook To Turn AI Into A High-Ticket Offer Businesses Are Hungry To Pay You For (Even If You're Starting From Zero)"

Sub: a Charter Membership = 1-on-1 consult, group coaching, software, tools, training, DFY assets. Core promise repeated: **"Partner with us and borrow our brand, authority, trust, credibility, social proof, and case studies."**

→ The whole offer is a **white-label / borrow-authority agency-in-a-box.** That's the real product: not a tool, a *done-with-you agency you run under their brand and case studies.*

## Risk reversal — SOFTER than the webinar
- Page: **"Performance Risk Reversal Policy — if you follow the training, use the software, and don't see the value, we'll work with you until you do."** = a *keep-working-with-you* guarantee, **not a refund.**
- The webinar (Bill, live) promised **money back + $500 on top.** The page does **not** repeat that. Worth knowing which one is actually on the table — they don't match.

## What this changes for the JV
1. **Real anchor for our positioning:** their audience is being sold a ~$5k "borrow our brand, run an agency" charter with a $299/mo backend. That's the price/complexity ROYA is measured against.
2. **BAPP is a white-label agency-in-a-box, ROYA is a client-getting system** — genuinely different products, which is good (less cannibalisation, cleaner "the missing half" story). BAPP hands them the agency; ROYA is how you actually fill it.
3. **"Wasn't designed as a JV offer" makes sense now** — the whole page is "partner with *us*, borrow *our* brand." For a Dan/Flexxable JV that framing has to be reworked (whose brand are they borrowing?). This is the "needs slight adjustment" Gavin flagged.
4. **Guarantee mismatch (money-back+$500 vs. work-with-you)** is a live question — pin it down before any copy leans on risk-reversal.
