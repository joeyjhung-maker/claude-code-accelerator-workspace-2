# Buildy.ai homepage — teardown (15 Jul 2026)

Source: `https://getbuildyai.com/` (main site, not the webinar funnel). **Key finding: the homepage sells a different angle than the webinar opt-in.** Homepage = "your AI team builds the business" (product/mechanism-led). Webinar page = "$233,496 in 14 days" (money/result-led). Two different doorways into the same platform — useful to know which one their audience has actually seen.

## Positioning (verbatim)
- "You Bring the Vision. Your AI Team Brings Everything Else."
- "Your 7 AI agents handle strategy, design, code, marketing, and operations. Automatically."
- "This Isn't Another Builder. It's a Team."
- "We don't give you tools. We give you a team."
- "Other tools help you build a thing. Buildy helps you build a business."
- Social proof stat on page: **"7,321 entrepreneurs are already building with their AI team."** (their claim)

## The mechanism — 7 AI agents
1. **Strategist AI** — market/competitor research, ideal customer.
2. **Designer AI** — logo, palette, page design.
3. **Engineer AI** — production code, front/back/DB ("idea to working checkout in 47 minutes").
4. **Marketer AI** — email sequences that nurture leads to buyers.
5. **Operator AI** — business phone number, AI voice agent, 24/7.
6. **Analyst AI** — *coming soon* — tracks visitors/leads/sales.
7. **Optimizer AI** — *coming soon* — reviews data, suggests improvements.

## What you get
Business blueprint · designed site/web app · all the code · ready-to-sell store w/ payments · automated sales+marketing funnels · 24/7 AI workforce · unified growth/analytics dashboard.

## Feature one-liners (benefit framing)
"Get Paid Instantly" · "Never Miss a Customer Question" · "Your Phone Gets Answered" · "Leads Get Called Back in Minutes" · "The Repetitive Stuff Runs Itself" · "Always Online, Always Fast."

## Testimonials on page (names, their claims)
- **Nikki Summers** — "Got my 1st app built and published… gave me extra features I hadn't thought of."
- **James Bailey** — "made building funnels and online assets fast, simple, and actually fun."
- **Bridye Smith** — "What used to feel overwhelming… became simple, fast, and actually fun."
- **Angela Roberts** — "I did build my app and it is published… only took a few minutes."

## CTA
- **"Start Building Free"** (repeated). Free-to-start SaaS motion — no price shown on the homepage.

## Avatar signal
- **Solo founders / non-technical first-timers.** "Join Thousands of Solo Founders Who Made the Leap." Confirms the beginner/aspiring skew from the webinar page — this is NOT an established-agency-owner list.

## Read for the JV
- Their audience is bought into "AI builds it for me." ROYA has to answer the next question: *okay, I built the thing — now how do I actually get paying clients?* That's the bridge (matches Russell's "sell the outcome, not the vehicle").
