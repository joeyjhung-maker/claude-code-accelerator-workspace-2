# Travis Sago (mentor) — advice on the Buildy/Russell JV (15 Jul 2026)

Source: Dan Wardrope's post in **The MOJO Mastermind** (Facebook), asking mentor **Travis Sago** what to put in front of Bill & Russell's audience. This is the sharpest strategic steer we have for the JV — it names the exact angle to lead with. Travis is the "whiskey bottle" / commission-only-JV guy (which is where the Asana "whiskey bottle starter" option comes from).

## Dan's ask
- "Overwhelmed with JVs… quite lost on how to adapt offers to other people's audience."
- "Going out to Russell DeCorte & Bill's audience in a fortnight. Not sure what to put in front of them."

## Travis's answer — the money angle
**The biggest, most painful, unanswered problem in the whole AI-tool / agency niche: HOW DO YOU SELL AI?**

- You **don't** sell it with a retainer or upfront fee.
- Why ROYA is crushing it: you're **NOT asking for $25,000 to build an AI tool**, or **$3,000/mo to automate a business**.
- **OLD PLAN** (what everyone else does): huge promises → compete with 5 other AI automation agencies → proposals, bids, the negotiation gauntlet, 4 Zoom calls → *maybe* get the deal → then a "mountain of sh*t you have to deliver."
- **ROYA way:** Demo → propose a **risk-free test on a commission-only basis** → when the money floods in, split it, and *they* ask **you** how else they can work together. **Low delivery hassle.**
- "You've got the ULTIMATE PLAN, Dan."

## The unlock for the Buildy JV
Travis's follow-up nails the positioning: the commission-only / risk-free angle is *"not new for you and ROYA, but **new to your JV peeps**."*

- The Buildy audience can now **build** anything (that's what Buildy does). Their unanswered problem is **selling it / getting paying clients**. ROYA answers exactly that.
- So the lead for this audience = **the anti-retainer angle**: no $25k build fee, no $3k/mo. Demo → risk-free, commission-only test → split the upside. It's fresh to them even though ROYA's core list has seen it.
- Buildy = the **build** side; ROYA = the **sell** side. Frame ROYA as the missing half, not a competing pitch. (Lines up with Russell's "nobody wakes up wanting to start an agency — sell the outcome.")
