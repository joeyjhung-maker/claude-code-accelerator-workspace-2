# BAPP evergreen webinar — marketing teardown (15 Jul 2026)

Companion to `2026-07-15-bapp-webinar-transcript.md` (full 28k-word transcript). This is Buildy's own evergreen webinar selling **BAPP (Buildy AI Profit Partnership)** — the offer they'll run to the Flexxable audience. Hosts: **Bill McIntosh** + **Daniel** (Daniel runs chat/ops). Runtime 2h39m. **Every number below is theirs, unverified — sign-off before any goes in our copy.**

## The hook (title card)
> "How Did He Land His First Ever High Ticket AI Business Client The First Day He Started & Immediately Get $3,750... Without Any Prior Experience?"

Classic result-in-advance curiosity hook. Named-character, specific number, day-one speed, "no experience" objection killed up front. Opens with chat engagement ("give me a big warm hello… all caps, exclamation points") — standard webinar warm-up.

## Positioning / authority
- Bill + partners: **"over $200 million in combined online revenue"**, thousands of clients, 25+ years. Repeated 5+ times as the credibility spine.
- Proof examples flashed: a jewelry store to **$33,502**, a personal **$31,212 in commissions** running the automations.

## What they actually sell (the mechanism)
- **Find local businesses that don't have a website** (they literally search for businesses with no site), then build one + AI automation for them using **Buildy**.
- Framed as high-ticket AI services, **not** "$19–$29 products." One saved client = $5–6k, "pays for itself."
- Two ways to earn: **do it yourself and keep 100%**, OR **let Buildy's team help close + fulfil and split the revenue 50/50.** Close it on your own = you keep it all.

## The story (Colin)
- Colin = "a dabbler," not much money, no runway, no experience. Bill built him a plan.
- **Landed his first client day one: $3,750 + $250/mo residual.** Used as the "if he can, you can" proof and the whole webinar's spine.

## The offer stack
- **BAPP** = the partnership program: step-by-step training + done-with-you + done-for-you, all inside Buildy.
- **First 12 months free** (platform access) when you join.
- Value anchor: **"normally $33,000"** → early-bird discount on the webinar. (Actual price ladder, per Gavin's call notes: **Decoy $997 / $1,497 premium / $5k ultimate** — webinar sends buyers to `profitpartnership.ai/invite`.)

## Risk reversal (strong — worth stealing)
- **"Put up the investment today. If you do the work and it isn't working, you get your money back — and I'll reward you $500 on top."** A money-back *plus* $500 rebate for your time.
- Reinforced with: **"just one client pays for your investment."** Cost justified before price is even the conversation.

## The close
- Urgency + scarcity ("act fast, get all the bonuses, head over there").
- Response trigger: type **"lock me in"** in chat.
- A **winner/giveaway** announced at the end to hold attendance to the last minute.

## What this tells us for the JV
1. **Their offer and ROYA are cousins, not twins.** BAPP already sells "get your first client" to beginners via a commission/rev-share model. ROYA's Rainmaker is the *bigger, sharper* version of the same promise → the bridge is easy, but we must **differentiate**, not echo. (Ties to Travis's "sell the outcome / commission-only" steer — they already speak this language.)
2. **Steal the risk-reversal muscle.** The "money back + $500 on top" and "one client pays for it" framing land hard with this exact audience. Our offer into their list should carry an equally concrete risk-flip.
3. **Their proof is result-in-advance + one named character (Colin).** When we write ROYA copy for their list, mirror the format they've trained the audience on: one believable hero, day-one/first-client speed, "no experience."
4. **Price expectation is set low-to-mid** ($997–$5k ladder, "one client pays for it"). ROYA Rainmaker's price/positioning has to account for what they've been anchored to.
5. **Do NOT reuse their $200M / $33k / $3,750 numbers as ours.** They're Buildy's claims. Ours must be Dan-true.
