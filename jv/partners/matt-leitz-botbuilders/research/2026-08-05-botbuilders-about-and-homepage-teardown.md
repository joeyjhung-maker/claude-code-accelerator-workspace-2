## Origin story (their brand narrative, useful for JV framing / rapport)
- Matt Leitz started a board-game ecommerce company with a warehouse and a small team. Razor-thin margins + shipping costs killed it, right as he and his wife had their first baby on the way.
- Down to his last option, he hired an unpaid intern off Craigslist — **Nic Frachon** — who is now his co-founder. Together they built a chatbot for the game business that actually sold games. That chatbot was the pivot.
- **BotBuilders founded 2018** "from the ashes" of the failed board game company. Nic Frachon is co-founder, still active (shows up in their "how it works" training videos alongside Matt).
