## Travis's verbatim points
- *"This has a wonderful DEMO... I love that more than 'selling.'"*
- **#1 lever: STAY ON LONGER.** *"If there are 100, 50, even 25 peeps still on when you END the webinar, that is your biggest improvement window. Keep throwing harpoons into sharks and re-offering."*
- **The order is off.** Correct sequence: **OPPORTUNITY (escape from pain) → CHALLENGE (their "Old Plan") → present the NEW PLAN → then throw harpoons at sharks (objections) for the BULK of the remainder.**
- Loves the **Whiskey Letter** idea, but flags **BIG SHARKS (objections)**: *"I don't wanna spend"* / *"I'm scared to spend $70 a package."* Suggests offering a lower-budget method or another answer to that objection.
- **The real pain point is NOT "how to do AI."** It's **how to get on coffee dates** (i.e. get appointments/prospecting), **then how to sell it**, and **only then** how to deliver.
- *"This is part of the order restructure of the webbie, IMO."*
- **Content ratio: sell at least 3-to-1** — "getting on coffee dates" training over delivery training.
- *"Your 6 AI solutions... is two or three steps ahead of where most of the market is mentally."*

## What this means, unpacked
1. **The demo is the strongest asset — keep it, but it's currently mispositioned.** It's being led with / over-weighted, when it should be supporting proof inside the NEW PLAN beat, not the headline.
2. **Narrative arc must run Pain → Old Plan (why it's broken) → New Plan (the mechanism) → Objections (the bulk of remaining time).** Objection-handling isn't a short segment at the end — it's the majority of the back half, repeated ("harpoons"), with re-offers, for as long as people stay.
3. **Content ratio is inverted.** The webinar over-indexes on AI/delivery mechanics (the androids showcase) and under-indexes on the actual felt pain: getting appointments and selling. Travis wants ~75% prospecting/selling, ~25% delivery.
4. **The tech is ahead of the market mentally.** 6-7 AI "androids" is impressive but abstract to an audience whose real question is "how do I get in front of someone and get them to say yes" — not "how sophisticated is the AI."
5. **Stay-on-longer is the single highest-leverage, lowest-effort fix.** Don't end when engagement is still there — keep re-offering and handling objections live.
