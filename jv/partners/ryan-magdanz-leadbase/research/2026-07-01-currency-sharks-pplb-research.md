# Raw research — "Currency & Sharks.xlsx" (2026-07-01)

Source: `~/Downloads/Currency & Sharks.xlsx` — Joey's existing avatar/copy research doc for the pay-per-lead business (PPLB) owner market, built around the Currency/Sharks framework (what they're really buying vs. their objections) and mapped to the LF8 (Life-Force 8) + learned/secondary human wants model. Not Leadbase-specific in origin, but directly applicable — compiled here as-is, 4 tabs.

## Tab 1 — Currency (what they're really buying)
Format: Currency | Symptom Expressed (Perceived Motivation) | Applicable LF8

- Better profit margins → building a business around a more profitable model
- Consistent cash flow and better profit margins → scale with a predictable, reliable model [LF8: Survival, enjoyment of life, life extension]
- Buying time/convenience/efficiency/comfort → don't want to learn via trial and error [LF8: Freedom from fear, pain, danger]
- Full control of own performance → making great money while building a tangible asset [LF8: Comfortable living conditions]
- Buying time/experience to do it right first time → a proven system/process to follow [LF8: To be superior, keeping up with the Joneses]
- Consistent cash flow → a sustainable business model [LF8: Care and protection of loved ones]
- Increase income via new skill → generate more leads (PPC/email lead gen too low) [LF8: Social approval]
- Consistent cash flow → paid faster, commission pay is too unreliable
- Low-risk way to increase income → wants a beginner-friendly niche without heavy regulation [Learned wants below]
- Stability/income protection → reduce dependence on one traffic source
- Buying less competition/increased income → move from Red Ocean to Blue Ocean [Learned: To be informed]
- Buying time/margins/lifestyle → work with larger, less demanding clients [Learned: Curiosity]
- Increase margins/reputation → improve media buying skills [Learned: Efficiency]
- Stability/income protection/margins → diversify leads across platforms [Learned: Convenience]
- Buying time without sacrificing income/lifestyle → move from time-based to profit/performance-based [Learned: Dependability/quality]
- Buying time/margins/lifestyle → stop tedious client work and requests [Learned: Economy/profit]
- Increase margins/reputation → take skills to the next level
- Increase reputation/income → learn funnels/strategies that promote quality leads
- Buying time/experience → reduce risk of "haemorrhaging money" doing it wrong first [T.I.M.E.R. framework: Time]
- Increase margins → scale media buying with good CPL [Identity]
- Wants more clients, stop pitching the same tired retainer, increase income → an easier model to sell [Money]
- Wants more clients, stop pitching tired retainer, increase income → promote a better offer [Energy]
- Increase margins → work on a performance basis [Reputation]
- Consistent cash flow → a viable, repeatable system
- Wants stability/full control → wants to own the asset
- Wants stability/full control → wants full control
- Wants to increase income, buying time, reputation → wants to scale efficiently
- Buying time without sacrificing income/lifestyle → wants a model where Time-In vs Profit-Out makes sense
- Wants to be rewarded fairly for their work → sick of making clients loads of money for a minimal fee

## Tab 2 — Sharks (Objections)
Format: Sharks (Objections) | Symptom Expressed (Perceived Hurdle)

- Fear of losing money → having to foot the ad spend
- Lack of confidence a performance-based model can work → "High Risk"
- Fear of losing money / self-judgment → "Can't afford it" / "Is it worth the price"
- Fear of not landing a profitable client → niches saturated/too competitive
- Fear it won't add value (focus on "product" not outcome) → "Do I need your help, I probably already know the small nuggets"
- Lack of confidence in own abilities/model → generating quality leads AND the quantity promised
- Lack of confidence in own abilities/model → getting good CPLs for a good margin
- Fear of not landing a client → no case studies in the niche
- Lack of confidence in abilities/model → being able to deliver
- Lack of confidence in abilities/support → struggling with distribution of leads
- Fear of uncertainty/losing money/not delivering → "chicken or egg — clients or campaigns first?"
- Fear of not landing a client, distrust it's easier than retainer → "why would big whales work with someone new when they're already buying leads?"
- Fear of not landing a client, distrust → "no room in niches for a PPL newbie"
- Not yet convinced → "I want to get my first client myself first to prove this works"
- Fear of losing money, not convinced → "I don't have the time/capacity to branch into PPL right now"
- Fear of losing money, not convinced → "what guarantees do I have I'll get a return?"
- (unattributed) → "Cost for ad spend & social proof"
- Lack of trust in support/process → "I'd like to see testimonials/reviews, speak to students"
- Fear time-vs-effort isn't worth it if forced to change niche → "will it work for my niche?"
- Lack of understanding of model, fear of changing niche → "will my niche work nationally?"
- Fear of landing clients, lack of sales confidence → "how will I land clients?"
- Lack of confidence in model, fear of losing money/rejection → **"how do we charge clients for PPL?"**
- Fear of losing money → "what if lead cost is higher than what we charge them?"
- Fear of reputation/confidence/rejection → **"how do we ask for upfront payment?"**
- Fear of losing money/reputation/rejection → "hard to figure out the proper CPL"
- Lack of confidence, fear of reputation → "hard to know the volume of leads we can generate consistently"
- Fear of wasting time on the wrong model, fear of failing to act → "lack of time"
- Fear of lack of support → "uncertainty around the first few steps"
- "Keeping clients honest on performance-based models, especially hybrid" → keeping track of clients

## Tab 3 — Current Pains/Problems
- Retainer contracts with an income ceiling
- Being a master of none
- Being stuck in a time-based, low-profit model
- Dealing with small local clients that can't scale
- Dealing with demanding clients who "think they own them"
- Building bespoke campaigns per client — too time-consuming for what's paid
- No consistent cash flow
- Trading time for money
- Poor profit margins
- Retainer model too competitive — race to the bottom
- Can't land clients — poor offer
- Constantly creating client content, left with nothing to show for it
- Can't scale without hiring an army of staff
- Doesn't know how to transition to a performance-based model
- Poor lifestyle even as a business owner — long hours to meet client expectations
- Unstable business — constant hamster wheel of landing clients then losing them fast

## Tab 4 — Problems PPLB Solves
(Mapped to LF8 + learned/secondary wants, same framework as Tab 1)

- Focus on the important, not the urgent — not just firefighting [LF8: Survival, enjoyment of life, life extension]
- If fires do break out, this is a rescue service to get things under control [LF8: Freedom from fear, pain, danger]
- **Coaching so no more lone wolf syndrome** [LF8: Comfortable living conditions]
- Feedback/suggestions when things aren't working [LF8: To be superior, keeping up with the Joneses]
- (Care and protection of loved ones — LF8 listed, no line item filled)
- **Collaborate with others who are experienced, help identify weaknesses and where to improve** [LF8: Social approval]
- Stops the most expensive/painful path of trial and error [Learned: To be informed]
- Stops the painful process of going it alone [Learned: Curiosity — likely mis-slotted row]
- No reinventing the wheel [Learned: Efficiency]
- **Playing to each other's strengths** [Learned: Dependability/quality]
- **Access to JV partnerships** [Learned: Economy/profit — likely mis-slotted row]
- Become a specialist, not a jack of all trades
- Being able to generate leads across all platforms, not just the easy ones
- **Not having to do everything yourself — outsourcing etc.**

- "Retainer model too competitive — race to the bottom" / "unstable... hamster wheel" (Tab 3) matches the independent retainer-refugee stories from Karel Stoy, Nathan Lear, and Jack Hunter in the Skool screenshots.
