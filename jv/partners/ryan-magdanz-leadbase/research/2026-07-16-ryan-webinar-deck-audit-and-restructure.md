## Travis's critique, checked against the real deck — every point confirms

**1. "The order is off."** — CONFIRMED, and worse than a simple mis-order: there are **three separate pitches** stacked in one webinar (DBR/Sleeping Beauty → the 9-Android landlord empire → Direct Mail for whale meetings), each with its own mini-arc, none of them following Opportunity→Challenge→New Plan→Objections cleanly. The genuine "opportunity" hook (get whale Zoom calls) is teased at slide 17 then **not delivered on until slide 136** — 119 slides later.

**2. "Sell 3-to-1, appointments/selling over delivery."** — CONFIRMED, and it's the inverse. Content genuinely about *getting in front of prospects* = the opening tease (17–19) + the Direct Mail block (136–146) ≈ **14 slides**. Content about the *mechanism/delivery/tech* (DBR, 48hr, the 9-android catalog, income stacking) ≈ **97 slides**. That's roughly **1-selling : 7-delivery** — not 3:1 the right way, but close to **7:1 backwards**.

**3. "Your 6 AI solutions is 2-3 steps ahead of where the market is mentally."** — CONFIRMED (it's actually 9, not 6 — Travis's count was approximate). Slides 95–131 walk through **all nine androids individually**, full income-stacking math, "landlord" business-model reframe — a 37-slide product catalog dump, for an audience the Challenge section itself establishes hasn't solved basic lead-quality/churn pain yet. The deck even lampshades the mismatch itself at slide 14: *"Some of you are in Ryan's group for lead generation ONLY!"*

**4. "I love the demo more than selling."** — the live demo (slide 94, 107) is real and good — but it's buried inside a 60-slide mechanism block, not positioned as the centerpiece of a tight New Plan section.

**5. No real objection-handling / re-offer block exists.** — CONFIRMED. There is **no scripted "harpoons at sharks" section anywhere in the deck.** The only objection acknowledged is one line at slide 152 (US alcohol-shipping law workaround for the direct-mail bonus). Travis's #1 lever — *stay on longer, keep re-offering while people are still on* — has nothing built into the deck to hang it on. It would only happen ad-lib in Q&A, which is exactly why it likely didn't happen enough live.

**6. The close is fragmented across two unrelated CTA moments.** Slides 132–135 ask for a chat action ("WINGMAN") on the Sales Wingman tool — then the deck **pivots to a brand-new topic (Direct Mail)** for 11 slides — then finally lands the real bonus stack/close at 147. This dilutes urgency and buries the actual ask.

**7. Confirms the [[2026-07-16-jv-webinar-straight-to-highticket-no-ladder]] finding independently.** The built-in CTA here isn't even ROYA directly — it's the **Sales Wingman Lightning Deal** (self-serve cart link, unmanned inbox, no conversation). ROYA/Rainmaker was the separate high-ticket ascension sold afterward via the 24hr auction — which is where the loser file's "$500 top bid on a $3.5k ask" and "0 sales off the deal link" happened. Same root cause as Travis's diagnosis from a different angle: **no conversation, no objection-handling, straight to a cold link.**

## High-level restructure (Travis's model, applied to real assets — mostly reordering/cutting, not inventing new content)

1. **OPPORTUNITY** — promote the buried "get whale Zoom calls for $280 each" tease (currently slide 17) to the actual open, right after a *much* shorter credibility beat (trim Ryan's 19-slide origin story to the essential proof points).
2. **CHALLENGE (Old Plan)** — keep as-is; it already works (20–34).
3. **NEW PLAN — ONE hero mechanism, not three pitches.** Lead with **DBR/Sleeping Beauty only** (simplest, most provable: performance-only, dead leads, first check before they've spent a dollar). Keep the live demo, tightened to this one mechanism. **Cut the 37-slide 9-android catalog down to a single "and there's a whole suite of these for later" mention** — it's the upsell path, not live-webinar content, per Travis's "2-3 steps ahead" note.
4. **THE BULK OF REMAINING TIME = getting appointments + objections + re-offers.** Move Direct Mail (currently exiled to 136–146) into the main body as dominant back-half content — it's the actual pain point per Travis. Script an explicit "big sharks" objection block (can't/won't spend, "I'm PPL only," budget fear) with real answers **and a lower-cost path**, per Travis's own suggestion. Build in 2–3 scripted re-offer beats, not one CTA drop — and instruct the presenter to keep going as long as people are still on.
5. **ONE unified close** — not a chat-action ask followed by a topic pivot followed by the real bonus stack. Single close, single offer.
6. **The offer itself stays untouched** — per Joey's 16 Jul decision: same ROYA program, same angles, same pricing (12-pay $467/mo). The fix is structural (order, ratio, objection-handling), not a new ladder or price change.
