## Video 1 — Boot Camp intro / niche selection / brand building
- Host identifies as "Big Papa Leads" (likely Ryan Magdanz's content persona) alongside business partner "Ryan B" — does ad copywriting/sales/creative; Ryan B does design/landing pages/funnels. 7+ years running the business together.
- Free community (Leadbase Free / School group): 700+ in Discord growing to 12,000+ members by video 2. Paid Pro: 350+ members at $99/mo.
- Core promise/goal stated on-camera: take a beginner from $0 to $10k/mo in lead gen; help existing operators scale past a client-count ceiling.
- **The model, in his words:** pick one niche → build a trustworthy/authoritative brand around it → generate leads via Meta/Google/YouTube/TikTok → sell (lease) leads to buyers with niche exclusivity for 90 days → after 90 days, allowed to resell that same lead database into an adjacent niche → scale to as many buyer-clients as wanted, then repeat in a new niche.
- Explicitly **not** an agency/retainer model: "We do not build accounts for our clients... we own everything... if things don't work out, they [the client] walk away with nothing; we keep the brand, ad accounts, pixel."
- Niche selection framework taught: competitiveness, audience breadth (broad/national vs narrow/local), "money for jam" offer strength, ability to build a resellable database off the niche, and personal edge (sales ability / price / lead quality / creative volume).
- Sets expectations hard: "6 to 18 months" to really crack a niche, explicitly warns against expecting quick money from templates alone — this is a recurring credibility/expectation-setting beat in his content.
- Brand-building philosophy: personified/character brands (e.g. "Time Share Tracy," "Insurance Ian") outperform generic corporate names; brand exists to build trust before handoff to the buyer-client; bootstrap it (templates, stock photos), don't pay a developer.
- Tone/voice: very casual, "guys," first-name energy, self-deprecating ("I suck at building websites"), heavy use of specific dollar figures as proof throughout, teaches real tactics on-camera (FB business manager setup, page-warming with cheap likes, special ad category rules) rather than staying abstract.

## Video 2 — "Why pay-per-lead is the best business model"
- Headline claim: "$5 million worth of leads sold" from one lead-gen website "in the past couple years," "an hour a week" of his own time on it.
- Origin story: ran a $20k/mo retainer agency (10 clients, "endless hours"), lost it when a client fired him during COVID — switched to pay-per-lead, one website did ~5x the revenue of the whole agency.
- Math pitched: buy a lead for $50, sell for $100 → linear profit at volume (100 leads = $5k profit, 1,000 leads/mo = $50k, scaled to ~3,000 leads/mo).
- Core sales pitch to prospective buyer-clients: "I've got people who are ready to buy. Do you want them?" — positions lead-selling as a one-call/two-call close vs. the longer sales cycle of retainer/agency pitches.
- Ownership framing (repeated hard): own the website, the funnels, the ad accounts, the trained pixel, the data — "a trained pixel and a trained account is very valuable," contrasted against losing everything when an agency client fires you.
- Exit narrative: sold his first lead-gen site for $650,000; cites HomeBuddy's $190M exit as proof the asset class is real.
- Lifestyle proof: based in Vancouver, Canada; team is himself + Ryan B + 3 part-time staff; frames it as "10x less work" than agency retainers.
- Named member wins shown from the Pro community "wins" channel: Jacob Watson ($160k in leads sold in April alone, $300k+ total since joining in January, now coaches inside Pro), plus several unnamed/first-name client-closed posts ($5k–$17.5k range).
- Positions the model as "AI-proof" — argues AI won't replace the human-relationship side of lead buying/selling for at least 10 years, and pitches new course content coming on "using AI to run your lead generation business."
- Funnel structure visible across both videos: free YouTube → free Skool/Discord community (top of funnel, big number: 11-12k members) → paid Leadbase Pro at $99/mo (350+ members) → implied future upsells (AI course content teased).
