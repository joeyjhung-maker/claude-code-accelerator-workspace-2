## Who they are
Ryan Magdanz runs Leadbase with a business partner, "Ryan B" (surname unconfirmed) — 7+ years running lead-gen businesses together. Ryan appears on camera under the content persona "Big Papa Leads"; does the sales/copywriting/creative side, Ryan B does design/funnels/landing pages. Based in Vancouver, Canada. Team is just the two of them plus 3 part-time staff.

They teach the **pay-per-lead (PPL) agency model**: build a niche brand, generate leads via paid ads, sell (lease) those leads to businesses at a markup — as opposed to a traditional retainer marketing agency.

## Their offer (what we sell to our audience)
- **Product:** Leadbase Pro — paid Skool community. Live coaching twice/week + the "Leadbase Masterclass" course + community access.
- **Price:** $99/month (per sales page, with evergreen "price increases" urgency copy — treat as standard scarcity framing, not a real deadline).
- **Free front-end:** Leadbase (School group), 11,000-12,000+ members — this is the top of their funnel, not the paid product.
- **Sales page:** https://getleadbase.com/
- **Mechanism, in one line:** pick one niche → build a trust brand → run ads (Meta/Google/YouTube/TikTok) → sell exclusive leads to buyer-clients for 90 days → resell the same database into an adjacent niche after → scale to more clients, then repeat in a new niche.
- **Proof he claims (his own marketing, not verified by us — flag as "Ryan's claimed numbers" in any copy):**
  - 350+ paying Pro members, $2M–$5M+ in leads sold (two different figures across sources — $2M+ on the sales page, $5M+ "from one website" in the newer video; don't average these, cite the specific claim + source if used)

  - Named member result: Jacob Watson, $300k+ in leads sold since Jan, $160k in one month
  - Several member testimonials on the sales page (Nathan L., Shooter M., Michael O., Hatim S.) — see research file for exact quotes

## Their audience (what we sell our offer into)
- Sales page names it directly: **"beginners ready to launch their first lead gen business, and experienced entrepreneurs who want to scale past $100k/month."**
- Two overlapping groups based on the videos:
  1. **Aspiring operators** — currently at $0, want a scalable online business, may have dabbled in marketing/retainers/sales before.
  2. **Existing marketers/agency owners** — currently running retainer agencies or freelance marketing, frustrated by being client-controlled, want to own an asset instead of trading time for a fee that can vanish (Ryan's own origin story: lost a $20k/mo agency when a client fired him during COVID).
- Sophistication: market-savvy on direct response and Meta/Google ads mechanics already (this isn't a "what is Facebook Ads" audience) — comfortable with dollar-figure proof, funnel/pixel/ad-account jargon, "own your asset" framing.
- Explicitly leaning into AI: Ryan is teasing new course content on "using AI to run your lead generation business" and frames the PPL model as "AI-proof." This audience is primed for an AI-agency pitch, not cold to it.
- **Why IAA book / ROYA could fit:** this audience already thinks in terms of "build an owned, scalable asset business" and is already warming to AI as the next lever — the IAA book's premise (an AI-agency as a scalable asset) and ROYA's coaching format (community + live coaching, same shape as Leadbase Pro itself) are structurally close to what they already buy.
