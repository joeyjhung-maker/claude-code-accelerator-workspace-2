"The Lock": a brief is fully locked when every dimension RHYMES — mechanism, image, problem-name, spokesperson, binary, proof, pain/solution all converging on the product as the inevitable answer. Test = say the whole ad in ONE breath; if every clause implies the next and the ending feels inevitable, it rhymes. "And also…" = a dimension isn't rhyming yet.

**Why:** From the "MOST IMPORTANT" section of the 37 Rapid-Fire tips (olive oil / volcanic shower / melatonin examples). It gives /brief a real pass condition — fields locked in isolation vs fields that rhyme.

**How to apply:** Full lock is the DREAM STATE we aim for, NOT a pass/fail gate. Joey's correction (2026-07-10): plenty of good ads never fully lock — do NOT discount an ad, mechanism, or seed for failing to reach it. Note where the rhyme is loose, tighten what's cheap, ship anyway. The one-breath test is a compass pointing at "tighter," not a bar to clear.
