Dan is **Australian**, not British. The "casual, direct, British-inflected" line in CLAUDE.md, `brand/voice.md` and the client file is wrong at the root. Joey wrote Dan saying it himself in a live Skool post: *"Maybe it's because of my Aussie background? Hard selling doesn't feel like 'me'."*

**Why:** The register (casual, direct, texting-a-mate) is right; the accent behind it isn't. Writing Dan as a Londoner puts the wrong idioms in his mouth, and his Aussie discomfort with hard selling is itself a usable angle, not just trivia.

**How to apply:** Keep the casual register, drop the British idioms. In Skool community posts he loosens further — "ya", "'em", double exclamation marks, CAPS for vocal stress, ellipses in place of commas. Full breakdown in the **Dan — Skool community posts** section of `rubrics/copy-rubric.md`.
