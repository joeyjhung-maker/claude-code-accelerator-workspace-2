Don't default to a pain-first hook. Pain is the AMPLIFIER, not the opener. Lead with curiosity, novelty, a weird tangible thing, a promise, or a world-level/identity hook — then use pain AFTER you've earned attention to twist the knife. A hook that opens on suffering is a fail UNLESS Joey asked for a pain-led variation on that specific ad (then grade it on its own merits).

**Why:** AI defaults every hook to pain ("I used to crush mornings, now I'm just surviving them"), so it's become white noise and Meta dislikes it too. From the 37 Rapid-Fire A-lister tips (Lesson 11). Joey confirmed the flip on 2026-07-10 and added the opt-in.

**How to apply:** Lives in CLAUDE.md "Rules that bite" and copy-rubric.md. Grade hooks against it. Reinforced by the vicious-hooks doc's "consequence before mechanism — lead with the wound, not the scalpel." Related: [[the-lock-aim-not-gate]] [[hooks-are-80-percent-vicious-flinch]]
