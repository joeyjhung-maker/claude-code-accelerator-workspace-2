The hook is 80% of the ad — a bad hook can't be rescued by good body copy, and body copy is the part AI does well anyway. So the time goes on the hook. "Vicious" ≠ mean; vicious = grab a fear/shame/dread the reader already feels and SQUEEZE until they can't scroll. The test: read it aloud — if a non-marketer doesn't wince, gasp, or say "damn," it's too tame. Find the version that flinches, THEN dial back for compliance (never the reverse).

**Why:** From the "How to Write Vicious Hooks" training (Luke). 8 principles: relevance in line 1, emotionally charged language, open loops, stakes/rubbernecking, specificity, doesn't-read-like-an-ad, caveman/Germanic words, consequence-before-mechanism. Key moves: protect the charged word (never soften "sexless"→"unfulfilling"), push to the edge of reality, lead with the wound not the scalpel.
