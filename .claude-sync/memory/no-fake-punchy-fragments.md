Do not manufacture "punch" by shattering copy into staccato period-fragments. Joey deletes filler fragments on sight — "Cool.", "Not in theory.", "Same business. Same screen." A short/one-word line must EARN its place with real work: new info, a turn, a concrete image, or soft proof. When it's one thought, a flowing line with a connector ("Every gap AND every crack…", "FROM slow follow-ups TO cold leads…") reads more human than the clipped version. "One sentence per line" bans stacking two sentences — it does NOT mean chop.

**Why:** fake-choppy is as loud an AI tell as a wall of text. Punch comes from a line doing a job, not from adding full stops. Joey caught me over-fragmenting the Revenue Leak Map email (2026-07-02).

**How to apply:** when judging AI/mariobot drafts, flag any short line that exists only for rhythm — cut it or replace with one that works. Prefer connectors over clipped fragments for a single thought. Full detail + dated rows live in the copy-rubric.
