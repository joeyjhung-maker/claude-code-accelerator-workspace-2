For JV campaigns into a partner's audience, a webinar NEVER sells high-ticket (ROYA) cold and NEVER sells the book. It sells a **$500–$1K reversible front-end** (currently $497–$997 for Buildy; maybe two packages, undecided). ROYA is the ascension, sold later to front-end buyers.

**Why:** Earned from the Ryan Magdanz Rainmaker post-mortem (200 reg / 100 live / 24 waitlist / **0 off the deal link**; every close came from the partner DMing warm students, Dan's profile closed nothing; auction top bid $500 on a $3.5k program). Selling high-ticket to a cold-to-us, low-buyer audience with no conversation converts ~0. The book is too low-ticket to justify a webinar and barely indoctrinates.

**How to apply:**
- Webinar offer = $500–1K, real Dan-true guarantee (reversibility is the spine at this price).
- The book's job = pre-frame lead magnet or order bump, never the headline webinar offer.
- **Price dictates the close:** cheap rung self-serves off the webinar (price matches trust); ROYA/high-ticket is co-closed WITH the partner in conversation, never a cold link.
- The front-end's real job is indoctrination not revenue: first dollar to us (trust transfers to Dan), belief in the mechanism before the big ask, a real win that qualifies them, self-selection of the serious.
- Add to the partner's model, match bait to offer ([[jv-additive-framing]]).
