The Instant A.I. Agency / Flexxable mechanism: you take a business's **OLD, DEAD
leads** — the ones already sitting in their database/CRM that they've written off —
and an AI **wakes them back up over SMS** (a casual conversation starter) and turns
them into **new sales**. Revenue split with the business, no upfront cost to them
("found money", easy yes). The "Sleeping Beauty Android" = database reactivation;
the "Prince Charming SMS" = the text template.

**It is NOT new-lead generation, cold outreach to strangers, or "finding new
customers."** The business already HAS these leads — they're just dead. Any copy
that says "find them new leads / lead generation / bring them new customers from
scratch" has the mechanism WRONG and must be fixed.

**Why:** mariobot wrote a biz-opp video body as "AI-powered lead generation… it
finds them new leads" on 2026-07-10. Joey caught it — wrong mechanism. This is the
kind of factual error that silently recurs across drafts if it isn't a standing rule.

**How to apply:** brief the mechanism as old/dead-lead reactivation every time; grade
every Flexxable draft's mechanism against this. Source of truth: [[flexxable-offer-doc]]
(offer-doc-ROYA.md — "take their old leads and turn them into NEW sales").
