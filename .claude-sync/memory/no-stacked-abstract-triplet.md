Never write a stacked abstract triplet — three abstract benefits in a row on consecutive lines, e.g. "More certainty. Higher close rates. You're the pro in the room."

**Why:** It's a dead AI tell. Reads flat, admires itself, gives the reader nothing to picture. Joey deleted exactly this line from a Hulk email and made it a permanent NEVER.

**How to apply:** Don't write it in the first place — not just fix it after. If three benefits want to stack, show ONE as a concrete scene the reader can see, or cut to a single line. Applies to all copy, not just Flexxable. Lives as a hard-FAIL rule in the [[copy-rubric]].
