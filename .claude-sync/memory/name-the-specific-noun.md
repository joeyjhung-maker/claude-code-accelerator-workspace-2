Never write "the thing you were called to carry" or similar empty placeholder nouns in copy — always name the specific noun the source material actually uses.

**Why:** Joey corrected a Threads draft built from `essence-and-identity.md` (Second Brain vault) — "Hidden sin doesn't just weigh on your conscience. It dries up the thing you were called to carry" — with: "We need to be more specific than 'the thing'." The fix pulled the actual term the source note used repeatedly (essence/etsem), not a generic stand-in.

**How to apply:** When drafting from source material (Obsidian notes, briefs, client files), any line with a vague noun placeholder ("the thing", "it", "that stuff") is a sign the draft under-used the source. Go back to the source and pull its specific vocabulary instead of writing around the gap with a placeholder. Applies to [[threads-voice]] work and beyond — the same instinct that produced [[ask-for-proof-dont-write-around-gap]] and [[no-fake-punchy-fragments]].
