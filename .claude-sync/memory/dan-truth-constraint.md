Flexxable ads are narrated in **Dan's** first-person voice. Any first-person claim
must be TRUE for Dan. Do not invent biography to fit an angle.

Known false-for-Dan (do NOT write): Dan was made redundant / laid off; "my company
replaced my team with AI"; "I failed IT in school"; "I make more than my old desk
job." Dan did not have a 9-to-5-escape / redundancy backstory.

**How to apply:** an escape-the-9-5 or "not technical" angle is fine, but write it
**second-person / observational** ("your boss is figuring out how to use AI to
replace you…"), not as Dan's personal story. When a hook needs a personal backstory
Dan doesn't have, reframe to "you"/the market or ask Joey whose story it is.

**Tense matters as much as content.** Agency-owner burnout (client-pleasing,
60-hour weeks, exhaustion despite good revenue) IS true for Dan — but only as a
CLOSED chapter. His current business model is exactly what gives him the freedom he
spent years without. Any first-person burnout/pain content must read as an old,
resolved memory ("there was a year where…", "I used to…", "took me a long time to
work out…") — never present-tense ongoing struggle ("I check my phone every
morning," "I have no idea," "I've run out of things to blame"). Caught 2026-08-24:
Genesis's `unaware-bot-master-bot` produced a strong burnt-out-agency-owner hook
batch that was emotionally sharp but present-tense throughout — Joey corrected all
15 to past/resolved framing before saving to the seed bank. Check every AI-drafted
Dan-voiced pain hook for tense, not just for invented facts.

**Why:** on 2026-07-10 mariobot/hookbot produced "my company replaced my team with
AI on a Tuesday" and "I failed IT in school, now I make more than my desk job" for a
biz-opp ad. Both untrue for Dan — Joey killed them. This is a provenance rule: the
narrator is a real person, claims must match him. Sits alongside
[[dont-verify-user-written-claims]] (grade the machine's claims, not Joey's).
