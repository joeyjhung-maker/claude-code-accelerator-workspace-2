When a piece of copy needs a specific proof point I don't have, **stop and ask Joey for the number and the niche before drafting.** Do not produce a version that works around the hole.

**Why:** On the B2B Leads Lab Skool post I had no details of the sales call, so I wrote three drafts that were carefully vague and technically safe. Joey's rewrite dropped in one line — *"We've closed 5 adventure park clients now"* — and it carried the whole post. I had correctly flagged the gap, then written around it instead of asking. The vague version isn't a safe default, it's a weak one.

**How to apply:** Flagging a missing fact is not enough. If the specific number, niche or name would change the copy, ask for it up front and draft after. Related craft note: the "name and demote, don't scrub" rule — when a brief says *don't position it as X*, naming X and reducing it to one example beats deleting every mention, because scrubbing leaves the reader's assumption intact.
