Joey saved a Travis Sago post (2026-08-28) that is explicitly **not** hopper/group-growth
material — it's a cold-outreach play for landing performance-based clients, which Joey flagged
as usable for his own agency, [[joey-runs-heuromi-agency]].

Core move: find a crowd that hates doing something (most owners hate lead gen/sales), offer to
take it off their plate on a risk-free, commission-only basis. For new relationships, run a
two-step: Step 1 is a short warm no-ask email built on 5–10 minutes of research (proves a real
human, not a blast); Step 2, sent only after they reply, makes the specific commission-only
offer. Any question that comes back gets the "Columbo Response" — acknowledge it's fair, claim
email can't do it justice, anchor a concrete number specific to their offer, redirect to a call
— never answered directly in text.

The exact email/offer/Columbo templates are verbatim and preserved in the workspace file, not
here — re-read them before using this play rather than paraphrasing from memory.

**How to apply:** when Joey is working on Heuromi's own client acquisition/new-biz (not a
Flexxable or JV-partner asset), check `mentors/travis-sago/risk-free-offer-outreach.md` for the
full templates before drafting outreach.
