Dan Wardrope, 2024 — mined into [[clients/flexxable/book-mining-instant-ai-agency-2024.md]] and
[[clients/flexxable/MASTER-CONCEPTS-ROYA.md]]), it's already good to go. Dan wrote it himself and
published it — that's the verification. Don't add an extra "worth confirming with Dan before this
ships" caveat on top of citing the source file.

**Why:** After sourcing the stamp-duty/case-law origin story from the book for a B2B Leads Lab post
and citing the exact file/line, I added an unprompted suggestion to double-check it with Dan
before shipping. Joey corrected this — if it's in the book, it's good to go, full stop.

**How to apply:** Citing the file/line the claim came from is still the right move (traceability
matters, per [[ask-for-proof-dont-write-around-gap]]). The mining file's own caveat about
**numbers** predating and differing from the current offer doc still stands — cross-check dollar
figures/stats against the current doc before using them. But for qualitative story/biography
material (no numbers to drift), citing the book as the source is the end of the verification
chain, not a step toward asking Dan again.
