Joey's PDF (`~/Downloads/Make Em Beg VIP With Cover.pdf`, 108-page extraction, ~212K characters)
was fully read and digested on 2026-08-20. This is Travis's core pre-selling book — the "5 Ps"
framework already known at one remove from the Hopper Millions digests turns out to originate
here, in much more depth: the Pre-Sell Sandwich (Present Pain / Personality / Philosophy / Plan /
Price), symptomatic messaging ("point your finger" — SEE/HEAR/FEEL/TASTE/TOUCH specificity), Hell
Island/Heaven Island, and a new-to-this-workspace objection-handling framework called **TIMER**
(Time, Identity, Money, Energy, Reputation) — map every objection to one of these five buckets and
"harpoon" it before the prospect has to commit, not after.

Two concrete finds worth knowing about directly: (1) **"The 4-Hour Dream 100 Shortcut"** name
already locked for B2B Leads Lab isn't just inspired by Travis's style — it's literally his own
personal-philosophy naming device ("I call this philosophy my 30-Year Wealth Shortcut," from
Chapter 4), reapplied. (2) The book's bonus chapter is a full interview with Andy Hussong on "JV /
endorsed traffic" — concrete tactics (ask what the partner's audience wants before asking them to
promote you, prep a JV invite doc first, book a brainstorm call immediately after a yes, ask for a
second promotion the moment you pay a commission) directly relevant to the still-underdeveloped JV
lane in [[b2b-leads-lab-project]].
