**NAPs = Nurture & Picks.** House term across Flexxable properties (ROYA, AAA Ninjas, B2B Leads Lab). They are content emails that nurture leads and get them hyped and interested — a mix of polls and "handraisers" that either sell, or ask for a comment in exchange for a free resource.

**Why:** They are not direct-response sales emails. The job is to prep a list to be sold to later and to segment by who raises a hand. Treating a NAP as a sales email kills its function.

**How to apply:** When Joey says "write the NAPs" for any property, build warming content with a poll or handraiser mechanic, not an offer close. Name the target group — NAPs into AAA Ninjas recruit for [[b2b-leads-lab-project]]; ROYA NAPs are their own track.
