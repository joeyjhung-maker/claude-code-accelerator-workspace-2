When Joey edits/rewrites something I drafted (mariobot's output, or my own copy), the version he replaced is dead — not a reference point, not history worth preserving, not something to link back to later.

**Why:** During the Matt Leitz/BotBuilders JV campaign (2026-08-05), I was keeping every intermediate version around (v01 rejected, v02, v03, mariobot draft vs. Joey's FINAL edit) — multiple files per email, plus elaborate "history: v01 → v02 → v03 → final" narratives in partner.md pointing back at them. Joey: "if i re-did your drafts, then consider them dead and never to be seen again."

**How to apply:**
- Once a FINAL/Joey-edited version exists, delete the superseded draft file(s) rather than keeping both around.
- Don't write "history: v01 (rejected) → v02 → v03 → final" narratives in notes — just describe the current/final version.
- Don't propose a dead draft for promotion into losers/ or cite it as an example later — the lesson learned from the correction can still be promoted (e.g. into brand/voice.md), but describe it in words, don't pin the promotion to the dead file.
- This applies to versioned copy files (v01/v02/v03 suffixes, -REJECTED- tags, MARIOBOT-vs-FINAL pairs) — once superseded, clean up rather than accumulate.
