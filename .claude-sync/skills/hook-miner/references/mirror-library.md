# The Mirror Library

Identity + specific private struggle hooks. Read this whole file before writing Mirror hooks.

The viewer should think "how do you know that." The struggle must be private, specific, and behavioral. Not "struggles with weight." It's "unbuttons their pants under the desk by 2 PM."

## The 4 sub-patterns

Use these to invent private struggles when the script doesn't hand you one:

1. **The location tell**: bathroom, car, driveway, pantry, hallway, the range. Private spaces where the mask comes off. "Watching this in the car in the driveway because the second they walk inside they have to be fine."
2. **The ritual tell**: checking, rehearsing, screenshotting, deleting history, reheating, arriving early, doing the math. Repeated behaviors that prove the struggle is chronic. "Checks the balance every morning and lets that number decide the whole day."
3. **The witness twist**: the gap between public self and private self. "Acts fine the second someone walks in." "Next to someone they love who has no idea." "You thought nobody noticed."
4. **The double watch**: predict their future behavior. "Going to watch this twice. Once now. Once at 2 AM when [problem] shows up again." Deepest form of "how do you know that."

## Rules

- Never reuse the same template twice in one script's output.
- Invent the private struggle from the script's problem using the 4 sub-patterns. Don't copy the filled examples' struggles into unrelated niches.
- Keep it compassionate, not cruel. The viewer should feel seen, not mocked.
- Health and money niches: the private struggle is the hook, not a claim. No diagnosis, no promised outcome.
