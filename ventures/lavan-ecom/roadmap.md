# Launch roadmap

Nine stages from "no product" to "scale or kill".
Each stage ends with a gate. Don't move on until the gate is true.
Day ranges are rough. The live checklist is the one to tick.

## 0 · Ground rules (Day 0)
- Split the roles: who owns product and supplier, who owns ads and creative
- Set the test budget and the most you'll both lose
- Agree the profit split and who pays for what, written on one page
- Set kill rules before any spend
- Pick the market you'll sell to and its currency
- Shared setup: one Shopify, one ad account, one shared inbox, one decisions log
- **Gate:** budget, split and kill rules signed off by both

## 1 · Find the product (Days 1–5)
- Pick 2–3 niches you know, or a problem you've had yourselves
- Pull ads running 30+ days from the Meta Ad Library in those niches
- Check TikTok Creative Center, Amazon Movers & Shakers and Reddit complaints
- Score each idea: real problem or real delight, shows in 3 seconds on video, 3x markup, ships easily, no regulated claims
- Read competitors' 1–3 star reviews to find the defects and the angle
- Shortlist 3 products
- **Gate:** 3 products pass the filter, and you'd both buy them
- Claude: meta-ads-extractor, ads-analyst, last30days, comment-goldmine

## 2 · Validate and source (Days 5–12)
- Order samples from 2–3 suppliers per shortlisted product
- Use each sample for a week. Check quality, packaging, safety marks (CE / UKCA / FCC)
- Get real shipping times to your market, and a backup supplier
- Work the unit economics: price − landed cost − fees − returns = margin. Breakeven CPA and ROAS
- Tear down the top 3 competitors' ads, pages and offers
- Pick ONE product
- **Gate:** sample is good and breakeven ROAS is under 2
- Claude: ad-creative-analysis, landing-page-analysis, adversarial-review

## 3 · Angle and offer (Days 10–14)
- Find the mechanism: why this works when other stuff didn't
- Map who buys it and why. One main segment, two to test
- Build the offer: price, bundles, guarantee, free shipping threshold
- Name, domain, wordmark, two colours
- Lock the brief: segment, angle, mechanism, offer in one breath
- **Gate:** the brief passes the one-breath test
- Claude: mechanism-finder, segments, brief

## 4 · Build the store (Days 12–18)
- Shopify with a one-product theme
- Product page: your own sample photos and video, benefit-led copy, FAQ, shipping and returns up front
- Policies: shipping, refunds, privacy, terms, a real contact address
- Payments live, business bank account, tax registration checked for your market
- Meta pixel and Conversions API installed
- Email flows: welcome, abandoned cart, post-purchase review ask
- Place a real test order end to end, then refund it
- **Gate:** test order goes through and the purchase shows in Events Manager
- Claude: landing-page-analysis, produce

## 5 · Make the ads (Days 14–20)
- 20–30 hooks across 3 angles, stress-tested before any body copy
- Shoot or source content: demo, unboxing, problem to fix, 2–3 UGC creators
- At least 9 ads: 3 angles × 3 hooks, video and statics
- Hook in frame 0, every word captioned
- Claims check: every claim is one the product can back
- **Gate:** 9+ ads ready and every claim checkable
- Claude: hook-miner, storm, produce, formats-conceits, compliance-check

## 6 · Launch (Days 20–21)
- Business Manager set up, domain verified, payment method on the ad account
- One test campaign, one ad set per angle, broad targeting
- Launch mid-week. Hands off for 48–72 hours
- Support inbox live, replies inside 24 hours
- **Gate:** ads spending and the pixel firing on real traffic

## 7 · Read and decide (Days 21–35)
- Daily read: spend, link CTR, CPC, add-to-cart rate, checkout rate, CPA against breakeven
- Low CTR = hook problem. Clicks but no carts = page or offer. Carts but no buys = price, shipping or trust
- Kill an ad at 2x target CPA spent with no sale
- New hooks on the winning angle, not new products
- Watch fulfilment: tracking, delivery times, refund requests
- Day 14 call: kill, iterate or scale. Log it in decisions.md
- **Gate:** at or above breakeven for 7 days straight
- Claude: account-read

## 8 · Scale, or kill it clean (Day 35+)
- Raise winning budgets 20–30% every 2–3 days
- Lock supply: bulk order, agent, branded packaging
- Collect real photo reviews from real buyers
- Lift order value: bundle, upsell, post-purchase offer
- If it's dead: fulfil and refund open orders, write up what died and why
- **Gate:** a clear call, written down
