## Patterns

- Each persona page almost always runs one brand only. Roosty's, Tavio, Auniva, WANDER+ pages are single-brand. Multi-brand pages are rare: the Korean Beauty Tips pages (Meimei, Smooche, Koriderm) and the Catherine Wright, Diane Kowalski, Alexthevet trio (Kitty Spout plus Doggy Spout).
- One network seems to run many pages per brand with a shared presell template: auniva.co (12+ pages, all started 8 Oct 2026, 10,000 results), Roosty's (4 pages), Kitty Supps (3 big pages), GoodGrove (8 pages).
- Pet is the richest cluster. Cat pages feed Feliway-style pheromone, fountains, supplements, chews, catnip oil. Kitty Supps (taurine) is the biggest new one, running since Feb 2026.
- Many ad pages are new: most started Aug to Oct 2026, so these brands are fresh tests, not long runners. Longest: Kitty Supps (Feb 2026), Wag & Whiskers (Nov 2025 on its own page).
- Several leads use a presell on the store domain itself (koriderm /pages/koriderm-listicle-1, smooche /pages/reverse) and some use a separate presell domain (pawcarelabs, topreviewweekly, bestcatfountains).
- The Ad Library page view only works with the real page ID (found in the search results HTML), not the profile ID in the page URL.
