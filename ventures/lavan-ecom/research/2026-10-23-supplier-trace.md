## 1. Earthly Derm (earthlyderm.com)

**A. Where made and who makes it**
- Site: Shopify, store name Earthly Derm, contact hello@earthlyderm.com. No company address, "Made in", manufacturer or facility wording found on home, shipping, refund or terms pages (confirmed, pages read).
- Shipping policy: processed within 24 hours (up to 3 working days), US standard delivery 2-5 business days "from our warehouse" (confirmed). That points to US stock via a 3PL, which means the product is imported in bulk (inference, not confirmed).
- Brand copy: "100% organic ingredients", eco-friendly, melanin-rich skin focus. Refund terms: 30 days, $7 return fee, per-item refund maths. 60-day results guarantee on the homepage.
- Catalogue is now two products: "Anti-Acne & Blemish Bar" and "Turmeric Fade & Glow Bar" (duplicate listings under variants). Homepage links to /products/african-black-soap and /products/turmeric-glow-soap but both 404 in the built-in browser, so label and bar weight unknown.

- No legal entity name on the site to search further.

**C. Matching wholesale listings**

## What this means for margins

On product cost alone (not shipping, pick and pack, fees or ads):

- **Dairy Pill:** clears 5x on every size on my estimate, and still clears about 5x on 90 ct even at triple the placeholder cost. The 270 ct tub at about 9x is the weakest. Repeat buying is the engine: a tablet before every dairy meal drives refills, and the 10% subscription still leaves big headroom. Shipping is domestic and cheap. Biggest unknown is the real manufacturer quote.
- **Primal Remedies:** clears 3x and 5x easily at single price ($39.95, about 14-16x on estimate). Buy 3 Get 2 Free at $23.99 per bottle still clears 8-9x on estimate. The risk is not margin, it is quality and delivery: 5-10 day delivery on mixed carriers, unnamed maker, no GMP wording, and softgel health claims that Meta and FDA will look at. Raw cost is so low that quality and compliance spend does not break the margin. Cheapest Alibaba benchmark may not be the true 7,500 mg aged garlic spec.
- **Earthly Derm:** single bar at $26 is about 8.7x, 3 bars at $55 about 6.1x, both clear 5x on product cost. The Turmeric 3+2 free deal at $53 for 5 bars is about 3.5x, clearing 3x but not 5x, and that is the deal the site is pushing. A bar is heavier and cheaper than a pill, so shipping per order eats more of the margin here than for the other two. Depends on bundles: the sub-$40 expectation of 5x only holds on the single and 3-bar tiers.

Which of the three really clear 5x for sub-$40 items: Primal single bottle and Dairy Pill 30 and 90 ct clear it comfortably on estimate. Earthly Derm clears it on the single and 3-bar anchor but not on its 5-bar Turmeric deal.
