## Recommended 3

1. **Pet pheromone diffuser, for rescue dog adopters.** The data pick. The only option that passes every gate, has refills built in, and carries no human health claims. A named buyer with a time-limited problem (the first weeks in a new home), reachable through rescue groups.
2. **ADHD sleep, starting with the weighted sleep mask.** The instinct pick, with data behind it: the strongest rising search trend we've found, light to ship, and it lands Lav's ADHD credibility. Merge the pillow idea into this slot: the mask is the cheap front product, a pillow becomes the order-value upsell once the angle works.
3. **ADHD focus supplement as a gummy or powder drink.** The upside pick: biggest market, monthly refill, very high margin. Make it conditional on next week's call with the supplement founder: who formulates, who holds stock, and how he handles claims (you can't say it treats ADHD; "focus" and "calm" are the usual wording).

That's two ADHD slots and one pets slot, roughly the 50% instinct / 50% data split Lav asked for. If the two ADHD tests both work, they become one brand (focus by day, sleep by night).

## Dropped, and why
- **HydroJug for men:** fails the margin rule and needs custom branding and stock, which you both said doesn't fit a lean first launch.
- **Uproot-style pet tool:** fails the margin rule at $24.99 and Lav isn't excited by it. Also, the call notes list Uproot as a client. If it's yours or Lav's, testing a competing product is a conflict worth settling first.

## Before committing
- Confirm a competitor doing $300K+/month on weighted sleep masks (Nodpod is the likely one). This is Mark's demand rule, and it's not proven yet.
- Get unit costs for a pheromone diffuser plus refill vial, and a weighted mask. Check gate 2 with real numbers.
- Ad Library check on each buyer: is anyone already selling a diffuser "for rescue dogs" or a sleep mask "for ADHD"?
