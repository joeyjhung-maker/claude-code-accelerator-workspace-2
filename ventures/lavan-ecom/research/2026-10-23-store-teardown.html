The short version

Seven things the list says

  • Half the list is health, but it isn't the best-paid half.41 of 80 stores are health, 21 of them supplements. Their median revenue is $0.86M. Pets ($1.2M) and home and kitchen ($1.5M) make more per store, from far fewer stores. Less crowded, better paid.
  • A story about why beats no story, but a full scientific mechanism isn't needed.Stores with no mechanism have a median of $0.60M. A light or loose "why it works" line roughly doubles that ($1.1M to $1.3M). A full mechanism comes in at $0.85M. Pick one believable reason.
  • Cheaper sells more.Under $30: median $2.75M. $30 to $49: $1.21M. $50 to $99: $0.56M. Over $100: $0.31M. Part of this is the big range stores sitting at low prices, but the slope holds.
  • The biggest stores started with one product, then built a range.Stores with 11+ products have a median of $2.34M, against $0.65M for stores with 1 to 3. Kitty Spout, Wuffes and Uproot Clean all grew one hero into a range. One product is the way in, not the end state.
  • Lots of ads doesn't mean winning.Revenue per active ad runs from about $121K (HydroJug) down to about $100 (VitalPaws). Stores spraying thousands of ads (VitalPaws 2,473, Tradesmen 3,400, Auniva 1,870) earn far less than tight advertisers like Earthly Derm, Primal Remedies and Dairy Pill.
  • The real winners went vertical in under 6 months.Earthly Derm (26x to $10.5M), Primal Remedies (23x to $9M) and Primal Storm (15x to $7.4M) all went from small to big between April and September 2026, each on one mechanism-led product.
  • Bad reviews don't stop sales.Stores under 3.5 on Trustpilot still have a median of $1.2M. BlackGirlVitamins makes an estimated $25M to $45M on 2.5 stars. Reviews matter for keeping customers, not for the first sale.

Patterns across stores

Moves that keep showing up

Same product, new buyer

A proven product is renamed for one specific person and situation.

Human red light pad → dog joint wrap (Luma Pet). Car door handle → post-surgery leg lifter (Piventra). Castor oil pack → body-area kits (Tavio).

The older buyer

A big cluster sells to people 45+ with a body that's started letting them down: energy, eyes, circulation, smell, joints, menopause.

Auniva, Primal Remedies, Elorita, Neurobella, Her Vitality, rynw, Mirai Clinical, Beminda, Piventra.

Fake experts sell the product

Ads run from persona pages ("Dr. Valerie DVM", "Catherine Wright", "Natural Healing Journal") and lead to a presell article before the store.

Tavio, Roosty's, Kitty Spout, WANDER+, Feliway, Auniva (about 12 persona pages).

The "real reason" headline

The top earners name a hidden cause the buyer hasn't heard: cortisol choking testosterone, raw garlic breaking down, soap pH stripping skin.

Primal Storm, Primal Remedies, Earthly Derm, NaturalRems, Menerals.

Pets graduate to ranges

Pet stores start with one hero then stack refills, supplements and add-ons. Several of the biggest stores on the list are pet ranges.

Wuffes ($15M to $27M), Uproot Clean ($6.2M to $11.5M), Kitty Spout ($4.8M to $7.2M), TheraPetMD.

Refills and subscriptions

The repeat purchase is built into the product: filters, refill vials, monthly chews, plan re-ships.

Curo Skin filters, Kitty Spout filters, TheraPetMD refills, Pestie plans, rynw.

For the Lavan test

Stores worth modelling

One hero product, a price under about $60, a simple "why it works", a fast rise and ads that pull their weight. These are the shapes to copy, not the products to clone.

Earthly Derm · plant-ash acne soap

$29 · $10.5M/mo · 26x · $19K/ad

Cheap, one product, one clear mechanism (normal soap's pH strips the skin; plant ash cleans the pore). The best all-round shape on the list.

Primal Remedies · aged garlic supplement

$39.95 · $9M/mo · 23x · $18K/ad

A "you've been taking the wrong form" mechanism that turns a commodity into something different. 90-day money back.

Dairy Pill · lactase pills

$27.77 · $2.75M/mo · 10x · $24K/ad

A named comparison ("6x stronger than Lactaid") does the explaining for it. Efficient ads, HSA/FSA eligible.

Tavio · castor oil pack kit

$54.99 · $2.3M/mo · 7x · $15K/ad

An old remedy turned into a ritual kit, then split into body-area versions. Sold heavily through advertorial pages.

Kitty Spout · cat water fountain

$38–69 · $6M/mo · range of 50

Pet, refillable, giftable. Shows the path from one hero to a range, and a sister store (Doggy Spout) for the next buyer.

WANDER+ · travel neck pillow

$39.95 · $1.7M/mo · 12x

No mechanism, just a sharp problem and a demo-friendly product. Proof that a plain, physical fix can scale fast.

Read these numbers with care

  • Revenue, visits and ad counts are WinningHunter estimates. Resilia's $125M to $210M a month is almost certainly wrong for a store that started in July, so it's left out of the rankings.
  • Traffic trends come in two shapes: older rows give monthly visits ("35K (Apr) to 913K (Sep)"), newer rows give percentages. Both are turned into a 6-month multiple here, so treat close calls as ties.
  • The 10 TikTok Shop rows have no web traffic or revenue estimate in the same terms, so they sit out of the revenue charts.
  • Two new rows (Peachies, usecadence.com) were still empty when this was pulled, so they're not included.
