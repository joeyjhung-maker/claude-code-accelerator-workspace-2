## How to read it
- **Size:** search volume against "smelly shoes" (= 1.0). So 4.0 means four times the searches.
- **Growth:** last 12 months against the first 12 months, **divided by the median of all terms**. Nearly every term roughly doubled over 5 years, which looks like a change in how Google counts, not real demand. So 1.0 = growing at the normal rate, 2.0 = twice as fast as normal, 0.6 = falling behind.
- **This year:** the last 6 months against the same 6 months a year ago, scaled the same way.
- **Seasonal:** busiest month against the quietest. Under 2 = steady all year. Over 3 = a seasonal product.
- **Score:** growth counts most, then this year, then size, plus a small bonus for steady demand.

Trends shows interest, not sales. A rising term still needs the Ad Library check (are people profitably selling into it?).

## What stands out
- **Rising fast:** #110 descaler (home espresso machines boomed, and the February peak follows Christmas gifting), #4 cat enrichment, #119 slide sheets.
- **Big and steadily rising:** #104 cat water fountain, #10 hard water, #37 burnt pan, #12 shift-worker sleep.
- **Cat > dog.** Three cat ideas are in the top 6. Every dog idea is below the normal growth rate.
- **Bad news for some of my picks.** #83 pet odour, #31 squirrel feeders and #7 washer odour are all flat or falling. They're not dead (steady markets can still sell), but nothing is lifting them.
- **Broad terms warning:** "food allergy", "emergency kit", "bike lock" and "lice treatment" are huge because they catch every kind of search, not just buyers. Treat their size with caution.
