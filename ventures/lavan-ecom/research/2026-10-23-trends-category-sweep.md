# Google Trends category sweep: pet health, pest control, older-buyer body problems, sleep

Run: 23 Oct 2026. US, Google Shopping searches, last 12 months for the rising lists, then 5-year shapes (web search, against the reference) on the best finds.
Same method as the 16 Oct sweep, pointed at the four categories that keep showing up in Joey's winning-store list.
Everything is included here. Supplements and health claims are not filtered out. Joey decides later.

## Headline
**Sleep is the only one of the four categories with real movement. Pet health, pest control and body problems came back thin.**

- **Weighted sleep masks keep climbing.** Nodpod is the brand pulling the lane: 2.2x last year, still 2.3x in the last 3 months, and about a quarter of the reference's size. The generic "weighted sleep mask" is smaller but the same shape (3.5x last year). This confirms the 16 Oct find with a second pull.
- **Cervical pillow is the biggest "body problem" product.** Nearly the size of the reference product (0.81), up 1.4x last year, but dipping over the last 3 months. The rising searches are full of new brands (Elviros, Homca, Tri-Core).
- **Pest control is a service and brand space, not a product space.** The rising searches are Terminix, Viking, a pest control costume and a pellet gun. The product terms (ultrasonic repeller, diatomaceous earth) are flat or falling.
- **Pet health barely shows on Shopping.** "Cat supplement" returned one related search (an NMN human supplement). "Dog joint supplement" returned only Wuffes. "Cat urinary" returned only food.
- **Older-buyer body problems are mostly flat or falling.** Tinnitus relief is down about half over 5 years. Knee pain and circulation are dominated by braces and leg massagers with nothing breaking out.

Size 1.0 means the same search volume as "cat water fountain", a product we know sells.

## Watch-outs
- NMN supplement came from the cat supplement seed but is a human product. Treat it as a separate lane.
- A brand like Nodpod owns the lane in search. A new weighted mask would be competing for "weighted sleep mask" the generic term, which is small.
- Trends shows interest, not sales. The Ad Library check comes next.

## How this was done
- **Browser route.** All data was pulled from inside trends.google.com in the built-in browser, calling Trends' own explore and widget-data endpoints. No paid tool.
- **Level 1.** 21 seeds on Google Shopping, US, last 12 months: cat supplement, dog supplement, cat calming, dog joint supplement, cat urinary, pet water fountain, dog dental, pest control, mice repellent, ultrasonic pest repeller, bed bug, neck pain, circulation, knee pain, eye supplement, tinnitus, dry eye, body odor, sleep, snoring, sleep mask. Top and rising related searches for each.
- **Seeds that came back empty or thin:** mice repellent (nothing at all), cat urinary (food only), dog joint supplement (Wuffes only), dog supplement (omega 3, fish oil; no rising), eye supplement (vitamin D supplement, not an eye term).
- **Level 2.** 15 rising-search pulls on the best candidates. Most came back empty.
- **5-year shape.** 29 US terms in web search against "cat water fountain" in the same request, measured week by week (sum of term divided by sum of reference over each window). This cancels Google's roughly 3x jump in March 2026.
- **UK and Australia.** Six terms (Nodpod, weighted sleep mask, cervical pillow, magnesium glycinate for sleep, sleep patches, NMN supplement) with the reference paired in each country.
- **Reading small terms.** Where a term is mostly zero weeks, a ratio is noise. Those are marked too small to read. The last week of data is a partial week.
- **Rate limits.** About 20 quick calls, then roughly one every 20 seconds or slower. Calls were spaced 12s+ apart with retries after rate-limit errors. The sweep took about an hour.
- **Peak month** is a month-level search level against the reference, shown only where the term has enough volume.
