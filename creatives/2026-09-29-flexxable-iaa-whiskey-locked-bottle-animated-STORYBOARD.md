## Build v2: SFX + motion pass (2026-09-29)

Joey's brief: "more swooshes and sound effects… maximise the motion effects."

**Sound:** all 42 sound types are synthesized in `build/sfx.py`, with no samples or licences involved. There are 251 cues, and they come from the same timeline that drives the animation (`collectCues()` in `anim.html`), so they stay in sync. Every handwritten line gets a pencil scratch and every kinetic word gets a soft tick. Every transition gets a whip or a zoom whoosh.

**Signature sounds:**

- glug on the whiskey pour
- lock clack
- rotary dial
- a riser into an impact plus ka-ching on $1.2M, and again on $225K
- PAID stamp
- tap squeak and a coin stream
- a rising 22-note plink scale as the globe lights up
- the unlock clack, sparkle and warm major-chord swell on the end card

SFX duck ~35% under Dan's voice. The master is at -13.5 LUFS.

**Motion:**

- **Transitions:** zoom-through into the building (A→B), zoom into the offer card (B→C), whip-pans with motion blur (left, up and right variants), and a zoom-out onto the globe.
- **Camera:** a slow push-in on every scene, plus decaying camera shake on 19 impact beats.
- **Kinetic type:** words pop in one at a time with overshoot, tilt and rise, and every pop has squash and stretch.
- **Particles:** a cash and confetti burst on $1.2M and $225K, star sparkles on reveals, spark bursts on the lock, stamp and "tech guru" strike, dust puffs on landings, and a 60-piece confetti blast on the unlock.
- **Peaks:** rotating sunbursts behind the whiskey, SALES?, $1.2M, $225K, the book and the end card. White flash frames on $1.2M, $225K, the book and the unlock.
- **Emphasis:** marker highlights on "one single offer", "already paid for", "ridiculously" and "the easiest AI offer". Red scribble circles on the $10M tag, "immediately", the last pain item and $14.40.
- **Motion blur:** motion blur and ghost trails on the flying package, cheque, wizard hat and refund coin.
- **Life:** idle bob on the android and the bottle, a breathing $1.2M, and a slow drift of faint background doodles for depth.
