## Rules

- Do not invent proof, names, numbers, timelines, skills, or screenshots.
- Use the screenshots as the hero visual on cards 1–4. Add only a short, bold annotation that helps the reader skim the proof.
- Keep card copy short, direct, casual, and Australian-inflected. One idea per line. Preserve punch and personality; do not use corporate language.
- The card 5 mechanism and card 6 offer/CTA must stay materially the same across all three carousels. Cards 1–4 are what vary.
- Give a ready-to-design blueprint for every card: on-card copy, which screenshot to place, and concise layout notes.
- Do not generate generic extra carousel concepts, alternate formats, or a wildcard. Output the requested three carousel blueprints only.
