# Flexxable — Genesis Static Image Generator Prompts

Create three separate 4:5 Meta feed static cover images. These are the cover cards for testimonial carousels selling *The Instant AI Agency* and access to the Sleeping Beauty bot.

Non-negotiables:

- Use the supplied real testimonial screenshots as authentic, unedited screenshot assets — do not recreate their platform UI, faces, names, or quoted wording.
- Preserve claim qualifiers exactly: Erik = `$2k monthly retainer`; Rob = `£20k per month profit, 6 weeks after leaving 9-to-5`; Toure = `$200k run rate in three weeks`; Aaron = `$46,037.05 total revenue last month`; Harry = `£7k+ commission in one week`; Jay = `$20k deal closed (+$1k retainer), from a cold LinkedIn connection Friday morning, $20k in bank Monday`.
- Never turn run rate into revenue or profit. Never call Aaron's revenue profit.
- Purpose: a high-credibility proof wall. Dark background, white direct-response typography, one blue or orange CTA accent; real screenshot crops are the visual proof.
- CTA: `Get The Instant AI Agency + the Sleeping Beauty bot.`

Create these three distinct images:

- primary_psychological_pillar: Ethos.
- core_creative_angle: The money is in forgotten old leads, not a website, ads or a portfolio.
- desired_tone: Blunt, credible, accessible.
- emotional_target: “I could actually do that.”
- required_hook: `$2,000/month from an accounting firm's old leads.`
- required_visual_cue: Erik's authentic screenshot dominant; cropped real screenshots from Aaron, Harry and Jay beneath as a proof wall.
- primary_creative_focus: Testimonial proof and simple mechanism.
- special_guardrail_notes: Include the mechanism line `Old leads. No upfront cost. 30–50% of the revenue.`
- target_concept_style: Native Chat/Text Mock.
