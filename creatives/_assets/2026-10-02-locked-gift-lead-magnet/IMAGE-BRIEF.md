## What the doc is

Dan's free guide for B2B business owners.
The idea is to stop fighting for the prospect's screen and get onto their desk.
You send a gift they want, lock it, and include a letter.
The only way they can unlock it is to call you.

The guide follows the people readers meet on the way through:
- **Ziggy (Dr Moradi):** Dan's mate, a Sydney plastic surgeon whose inbox is "Inbox Armageddon"
- **Jonathan Matthews:** sent Dan a caged champagne bottle, and Dan called him within 3 minutes
- **The adventure-park test:** UK indoor adventure parks, Dream 100
- **Mateusz R:** US home improvement niche

It ends with the B2B Leads Lab (free Skool group) CTA and the coupon code DANWARDROPELEADS for 200 free B2B leads.

Readers arrive from the locked-whiskey video ad, so the guide should look like that ad.

## Style

- **Illustrated scenes (group B): use the Pixar 3D look from the whiskey ad series.**
  - Reference: `creatives/_assets/2026-09-29-whiskey-locked-bottle/pixar/hero-board.png`. Copy its steel-and-glass lock box with the combination dial, warm amber whisky and blue velvet cushion.
  - Dan as a Pixar character: cap and leaf-print shirt, as in the hero-board. Likeness refs are `looks/dan-ref.png` and `dan2.png` in the same folder.
- **Graphics (group A): clean, bold, high-contrast layout** that matches the doc's all-caps headline energy.
  - Big numbers, minimal words, one accent colour (the whisky amber).
  - Readable on a phone.
- **No labels or brand names on bottles.** Keep labels blank, like in the hero-board.
- **Sizes:**
  - Section headers: 1600×900 landscape.
  - Inline images: 1200×1200 square unless noted.

## Hard rules

- **Never fabricate proof.** No fake screenshots of replies, CRM records, calendars, invoices, DMs or ad accounts.
  - Dan's doc comment on the proof strip says "maybe make one up?". The answer is no.
  - Stat graphics built from Dan's stated numbers are fine. Fake "screenshots" are not.
- Don't make realistic photos of real people (Ziggy, Jonathan, Mateusz).
- Use only the numbers listed below. Don't add any new numbers.
