## Two deliberate breaks from a literal swap (flagged, not silently forced)

1. **No "% off" badge.** Flexxable's offer is flat-priced ($14.40 + bonuses), there's
   no discount mechanic to swap the "25% Off" for. Badge swapped to the real price +
   bonuses instead of inventing a percentage.
2. **No "Almost Gone" scarcity line.** The source ad's urgency is real physical stock
   (post-Valentine's inventory, restocking in Spring) — a digital book has no
   equivalent honest scarcity claim. Rather than invent fake urgency, the headline
   was swapped to the copy's own strongest verified line ($341,042 from one client)
   and the bottom italic urgency line was dropped entirely rather than forced.
