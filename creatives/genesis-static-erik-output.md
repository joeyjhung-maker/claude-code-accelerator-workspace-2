### STATIC AD CONCEPT: Blind Curiosity / Social Proof Cascade (Native Chat/Text Mock)

**A. VISUAL DESCRIPTION (For Image Generation AI):**

A dark navy blue background (#0A0F1E) fills the entire 4:5 canvas (1080px × 1350px), evoking a premium late-night mobile feed scroll environment. The composition is structured in three distinct vertical zones stacked cleanly from top to bottom, mimicking the feel of a native iMessage or WhatsApp group thread screenshot dropped organically into a social feed.

ZONE 1 — DOMINANT HERO SCREENSHOT (Upper 50% of canvas): A single large, high-fidelity placed asset — Erik's authentic real screenshot — is positioned centrally and slightly left-weighted, as if naturally screenshotted from a phone and dropped into the ad. It occupies roughly 55% of total canvas width and sits in the upper-center of the frame. The screenshot is rendered with a subtle 2px soft drop shadow (rgba 0,0,0,0.45) and a very slight 1.5-degree clockwise tilt to signal authenticity over polish. A thin white label tag floats above it in a pill shape reading "ERIK — Live Client Win" in 11px uppercase tracking-widened sans-serif, white on a semi-transparent navy overlay (#1A2540 at 85% opacity), anchoring the proof to a real human name.

ZONE 2 — PROOF WALL (Middle 30% of canvas): Three smaller placed screenshot assets are arranged in a tight horizontal row beneath the hero screenshot, each occupying roughly 28% of canvas width with 8px gutters between them. Each screenshot sits flush and unrotated — clean and clinical, like evidence. Each has its own floating white pill label directly above it in the same 11px uppercase style:
— Left pill: "AARON — $46,037.05 last month"
— Center pill: "HARRY — £7k+ commission, one week"
— Right pill: "JAY — $20k deal + $1k retainer, LinkedIn cold"
A faint horizontal rule (1px, white at 15% opacity) separates Zone 1 from Zone 2, reinforcing the chat-log / evidence-thread aesthetic.

ZONE 3 — MECHANISM + CTA (Lower 20% of canvas): Clean typographic lockup centered on the navy canvas. No additional imagery. Pure copy-driven close. A single bright electric blue CTA button (#1D6FF2, 48px height, 320px wide, 8px border-radius) sits at the very bottom center with 32px bottom margin, containing the CTA text in white.

Overall mood: Dark, credible, slightly conspiratorial — like a group chat thread where someone is sharing wins they weren't supposed to. The native chat/text mock aesthetic is achieved through the screenshot placement style, pill labels, and informal tilt on the hero asset rather than through any recreated UI chrome. No fake notification bars, no invented message bubbles, no fabricated UI. The screenshots ARE the social proof. The navy canvas IS the phone screen.

**B. HEADLINE (< 10 words):**

$2,000/month from an accounting firm's old leads.

Font: Bold, white, modern sans-serif (e.g., Inter Bold or Montserrat ExtraBold). All characters rendered at high contrast against navy. No italics. No shadow. Clean and declarative.

---

**C. BODY COPY / PRIMARY TEXT:**

Mechanism line, centered beneath the proof wall and above the CTA:

"Old leads. No upfront cost. 30–50% of the revenue."

Supporting micro-copy directly below mechanism line (optional second line, lighter weight):

"The Sleeping Beauty bot finds the money already sitting in your client's database."

Typography: Inter Regular or Lora Regular, white at 85% opacity, 16–18px equivalent, centered, max width 480px to prevent line breaks beyond two lines.

**D. TYPOGRAPHY & LAYOUT DIRECTIVES:**

* **Headline Position:** Upper 12% of canvas, centered horizontally, above the hero screenshot. 40px top margin from canvas edge. This ensures it reads before the eye lands on the proof.
* **Body Copy Position:** Centered horizontally in the lower third, between the proof wall bottom edge and the CTA button. Approximately 60% down the canvas.
* **Font Style (Headline):** Inter ExtraBold or Montserrat ExtraBold, ~32–36px equivalent for 1080px canvas, white (#FFFFFF), letter-spacing: -0.5px for density and authority.
* **Font Style (Body Copy / Mechanism Line):** Inter Medium, ~17px equivalent, white at 85% opacity (#FFFFFFD9), centered, line-height 1.5.
* **Font Style (Pill Labels):** Inter SemiBold, ~11px equivalent, uppercase, letter-spacing: 1.2px, white (#FFFFFF), on semi-transparent navy pill (#1A2540 at 85% opacity), 4px vertical padding, 10px horizontal padding, 20px border-radius.
* **Color Palette:** Dominant dark navy (#0A0F1E) background. White (#FFFFFF) primary typography. Electric blue (#1D6FF2) CTA button. Pill label backgrounds at #1A2540. Screenshot drop shadows at rgba(0,0,0,0.45). Faint divider rule at rgba(255,255,255,0.15).
* **Native Elements:** No recreated UI chrome. The authenticity signal comes entirely from the real placed screenshots with their informal placement and pill label attribution system. The overall layout mimics the feel of a scrollable chat thread or a shared group message without fabricating any platform-specific interface elements.
* **Call to Action:** Centered electric blue button (#1D6FF2) in the lower 8% of the canvas. Button text: "Get The Instant AI Agency + the Sleeping Beauty Bot" — Inter Bold, white, ~15px equivalent, centered within button. Button width: ~320px equivalent, height: ~48px, border-radius: 8px. 32px bottom margin from canvas edge.
* **Branding Weight:** Light. No Flexxable logo required in primary composition. If brand mark is included, it should appear as a small white wordmark or icon at maximum 60px width, positioned in the bottom-right corner at 20% opacity — present but non-dominant, consistent with a mid-awareness native feed placement.

**E. MANDATE ALIGNMENT CHECK:**

* **Awareness Stage:** Solution Aware / Product Aware — the audience knows lead generation and AI tools exist; this ad closes the gap by showing real revenue proof tied to a specific mechanism (Sleeping Beauty bot + rev-share model), moving them toward conversion.
* **Psychological Pillar:** Ethos + Logos — the dominant driver is credibility through real named proof (Ethos), supported by a concrete, logical mechanism that removes risk objections (Logos: no upfront cost, rev-share only).
* **Emotional Target:** Aspiration spiked with FOMO. The viewer sees peers — Aaron, Harry, Jay, Erik — closing real money from a method they haven't tried yet. The emotional undercurrent is "this is already happening without me."
* **Guardrail Notes:** All screenshot proof assets are real supplied assets and must be placed as-is — never recreated, never approximated, never invented. Income figures (Aaron $46,037.05; Harry £7k+; Jay $20k + $1k retainer; Erik $2k/month) appear only as they exist in the authentic screenshots. No earnings claims are manufactured by the ad design itself. The mechanism line ("30–50% of the revenue") reflects the stated rev-share model and should not be modified to imply guaranteed outcomes. Flexxable brand presence kept minimal to preserve native feed authenticity.
