# Flexxable — multi-testimonial static brief

Generate exactly three 4:5 Meta static-ad concepts that can each serve as the COVER CARD for one
testimonial carousel. Each must use real supplied testimonial screenshots as the proof visual.

## Audience and offer

For aspiring AI-agency operators, 9-to-5 escapees, and agency owners who want a low-risk way to
turn a business's neglected leads into sales. The offer is *The Instant AI Agency* book, including
access to the Sleeping Beauty bot.

## Required concepts

1. **Relatable / Erik:** lead with Erik's $2,000 monthly accounting-firm retainer for old-lead
reactivation and upsells. Primary screenshot: Erik.
2. **Life change / Rob:** lead with Rob's £20,000/month **profit** six weeks after leaving his 9-to-5.
Primary screenshot: Rob.
3. **Big upside / Toure:** lead with Toure's $200,000 **run rate** in three weeks. Primary screenshot:
Toure.

## Supporting testimonial screenshots available for each concept

- Aaron Rowley — $46,037.05 total revenue last month. Revenue, not profit.
- Harry — £7K+ commission in one week from his first client trial; three trials signed after joining
two weeks earlier.
- Jay — cold LinkedIn connection Friday morning → 20-minute demo Friday afternoon → $20,000 in the
bank by Monday, plus a $1,000 retainer. The screenshot's redacted name is Jay.

## Visual and copy direction

- Make the primary proof screenshot dominant and the other three real screenshots visibly stacked as
corroborating proof, rather than rewriting them as fake quote cards.
- Use a short, bold cover headline. Keep the supplied qualifier exact: *profit*, *revenue*, and *run
rate* are not interchangeable.
- The only supporting mechanism line allowed: “Old leads. No upfront cost. 30–50% of the revenue.”
- CTA: “Get The Instant AI Agency + the Sleeping Beauty bot.”
- Direct, casual, Australian-inflected voice. No corporate language.
- Do not invent customer quotes, names, numbers, abilities, timeframes, product visuals, star ratings,
or a founder story.
- Output exactly three designer-ready static concepts. For each: headline, screenshots and hierarchy,
supporting text, layout notes, colour notes, and CTA.
