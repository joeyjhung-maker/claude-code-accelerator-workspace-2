in each — they exist as corroborating proof, not decoration. CTA bar is
the final 8% of the frame.

REVIEW CARD STYLING:
Screenshot-style — all four testimonials displayed as authentic screen
captures with visible platform UI where present. No redesigned cards,
no star ratings added, no invented formatting. The "raw screenshot" look

Dark background (near-black or deep navy) so the screenshots read as
bright, real captures pulled from a phone. Headline in white. Mechanism
line in a muted mid-tone (light grey or off-white at reduced opacity).

with white bold text. The screenshots provide all the colour variation
needed; the background holds them together without competing.

Escalation — Erik's $2K/month retainer is the relatable, achievable
entry point. The supporting stack escalates: Harry's £7K week, Jay's
$20K Monday, Aaron's $46K month. The viewer reads bottom-right last
and lands on the biggest number.
