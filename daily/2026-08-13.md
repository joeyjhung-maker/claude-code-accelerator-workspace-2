- **Craft lesson worth keeping: the long-form unaware ad runs on ONE repeated word.** The swipe says "position" 15x and "flat" 19x - one word hammered until the reader can't see their situation any other way. v1 spread it across "the model" / "the ceiling" / "what you're paid on" and lost the hypnosis. v2 picked **flat vs a cut** (both one-syllable, both already in Dan's mouth, and "flat" carries the same literal-plus-figurative double meaning the swipe gets). Applies to any swipe of this format, not just this one.
- **Dan's tenure and the AI-pivot origin story were both sitting unused in [[book-mining-instant-ai-agency-2024]].** PPL discovered 2016 = 10 years, which killed four `[X]` placeholders. And the stamp-duty case-law campaign (solicitors couldn't qualify against hundreds of case laws by hand, trained the AI on the corpus, ~90% accuracy, saved the campaign) is the perfect structural match for the swipe's paediatric-gastroenterology beat - proof the mechanism WORKS, not proof of results. Has never been in a live ad.
- **The bot re-broke five already-fixed rules on the revision turn** (treadmill metaphor, "No X. No Y." stack, 167 pages, Chesky, "skeptical"). A second turn does not inherit the first turn's judge fixes - re-run the full judge pass on every revision, don't assume the earlier corrections held.
- **New format finding: `copy_lint.py` reports a false clean on any file that uses `---` as a section break.** `body_lines()` splits on `\n---\n` and only lints `parts[1]`, so a 9-section swipe ad linted as "1 FAIL" when the real count was 65. Had to strip the breaks and re-lint. Worth a fix in the linter.
