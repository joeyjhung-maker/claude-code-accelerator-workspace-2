## Ingested: Deep Symptom, Negative Timeline (Claude Code)

- Source: Mario Castelli's Creative Cartel email, in Notion (Second Brain / Marketing): https://app.notion.com/p/3fa78742be6080f595cef85bd1d41ecd
- The idea: the **Symptom Trap**. When an ad sells a cure for each symptom, the sale hangs on "is this annoying enough to pay to fix?" The fix: sell a deeper, real problem, use the symptoms as proof, and walk the negative timeline down. Two wins: it raises the cost of doing nothing, and it reaches people who think everything's fine.
