## Promoted to [[hooks]] — marked UNCONFIRMED, confirm on the ad's numbers

**Beat 2 of a four-beat contrast hook is a gut punch, not an argument.** Round one was strategically correct and structurally dead — a relative clause in that slot makes the reader parse instead of feel. Four to eight words, one idea, no subordinate clause, a negative he has personally lived. The argument is beat 4's job. Candidate for `brand/hooks.md`.

**A contrast hook needs inversion, not just a loser.** Joey's catch, and the real fix. Beat 1 was worthless input → big money. My beat 2 gave them worthless input → no money, which is "I win, they lose" and carries no tension. The version that worked inverted it: good input → no money. Fancy builds for peanuts against dead leads for six figures. Candidate for `brand/hooks.md`.

**Peers in a contrast hook must read as wrong-turned, never useless.** Beat 3 says it isn't talent or effort, so any beat 2 that makes them look pathetic (begging, can't make rent) fights the beat that follows it. Killed several otherwise strong lines on this.
