## What the teardown found

- **The TAP Institute's website is offline.** `www` has no DNS record at all; the apex fails TLS handshake and 409s over plain HTTP. Every Google-indexed URL for the commercial arm is a dead link. Verified from two clients over ~20 minutes.
- **The two revenue rails cross.** A £99/mo Kingdom Partner gets less than a £49/mo member. The most generous money buys the thinner experience, on the same page where a buyer compares them.
- **Nothing between free and £49/mo.** Ebook → Telegram → subscription, in one jump.
- **They already run at trust Level 5** — live prayer, fasting and teaching with 7,500 people weekly — and the store page sells by *telling*. The asset and the ask aren't connected. That gap is the wedge.

## Craft lessons worth keeping

- **A teardown skill needs an evidence-tagging rule or it's worthless.** Auditing from outside means no ad account, no analytics, no P&L. Every claim gets `[OBSERVED]` / `[INFERRED]` / `[UNKNOWN]`. The moment a prospect reads one number they know is wrong, the whole ledger dies. Baked into the skill as non-negotiable #1.
- **Probe the infrastructure, not just the pages.** `dig`, `curl -w '%{http_code}'`, an openssl handshake — that's how the single best finding in this audit surfaced. Nobody else running a funnel audit checks whether the domain resolves. Two minutes, and it's the door-opener.
- **Open the diagnosis on what they're excellent at, and mean it.** Not politeness — a teardown that opens on weakness reads as a stranger insulting a business they looked at for two hours. It's also where the workable levers turn out to live: the wedge here sits directly next to their biggest existing strength.
- **A doctrine page can be the wrong tool, and saying so is the finding.** [[conceptual-god-model]] says bring the god down, compete on similarity. That collides with apostolic authority, which is a theological category and not a marketing posture. Declined it in the audit instead of converting it into advice he'd never take. First deliberate refusal of a wiki page — worth watching whether other authority/identity pages need the same caveat in religious or clinical verticals.
- **Mission-fit has to be a section, not an instinct.** Wrote the boundary into Pass 1 (§10) so it binds the lever list in Pass 3: nothing monetising the congregation, nothing touching the giving rail, no manufactured urgency on spiritual outcomes, Emerging Nations Access protected. A lever list that ignores this reads as extraction and loses the meeting.
