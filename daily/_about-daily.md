# Daily

One note per day (daily/YYYY-MM-DD.md): what you worked on, what you decided, what to pick up next.

Claude reads the most recent note at the start of every session, and logs a short entry here at the end of every session — automatically, not just when you ask. That log is separate from promotion: promoting a lesson into winners/, losers/, brand/, or workflows/ is still a deliberate call, only worth doing when something repeats or corrects a miss (see the-data-dictionary.md).
