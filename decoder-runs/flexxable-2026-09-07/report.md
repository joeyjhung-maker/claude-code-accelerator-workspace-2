## Mechanisms (UMP / UMS)
_18 distinct problem-mechanisms, 22 solution-mechanisms; 82/87 ads name one._
**Problem mechanisms (the cause they blame):**
- old/dead leads sit unworked in a business's CRM or database, paid for once but never followed up on and written off (17, 19.5%)
- old/dead lead databases businesses paid for but never followed up on (15, 17.2%)
- traditional agency/lead-gen service work trades hours for a flat fee with no leverage, unlike an asset that pays out on its own (11, 12.6%)
- traditional high-ticket sales work — back-to-back calls, objection handling, commission swings — is stressful and unscalable (9, 10.3%)
- AI automation services are hard to sell because business owners don't wake up wanting 'an AI automation' — they wake up wanting more customers (5, 5.7%)
- the old sales process makes you compete against other agencies by out-pitching everyone on Zoom, rewarding whoever can sweet-talk the best (4, 4.6%)
- agencies spend all their energy chasing brand-new clients/leads while ignoring the old, forgotten leads already sitting in their current clients' databases (3, 3.4%)
- the retainer model turns you into a ‘vendor on a leash’ — recurring revenue comes with recurring obligation, not real freedom (3, 3.4%)
**Solution mechanisms (their unique fix):**
- AI + a 2-line SMS reactivation bot texts those old leads, qualifies replies, and books appointments/sales automatically (18, 20.7%)
- AI+SMS reactivation system that texts, qualifies, and books the old leads automatically (12, 13.8%)
- copy the hedge-fund-manager fee structure onto old-lead databases: charge a performance cut of the upside an AI+SMS system generates, with tiny cost/time risk (10, 11.5%)
- AI+SMS old-lead reactivation, client pays 30-50% of resulting sales (9, 10.3%)
- performance-fee model copied from hedge funds: 2-line SMS/WhatsApp to old leads, AI handles the rest, paid up to 50% of profit (5, 5.7%)
- skip the pitch — run a live, risk-free demo of the AI bot working on the client's own old leads and let the results sell the deal (4, 4.6%)
- AI turns a business's old leads into sales on a performance basis (3, 3.4%)
- AI+SMS system pointed at a client's forgotten lead database, paid 30-50% of revenue generated instead of a flat fee (3, 3.4%)
