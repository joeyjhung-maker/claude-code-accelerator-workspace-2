Mechanisms - the UMP / UMS read

What they tell the market the real CAUSE is (UMP) and the unique FIX they credit (UMS) - often the highest-leverage part of the ad. 18 distinct problem-mechanisms · 22 solution-mechanisms · 82/87 ads name one. A mechanism they run for one persona but not another is a high-probability gap; a cause nobody in the category names is a category-cracker.
