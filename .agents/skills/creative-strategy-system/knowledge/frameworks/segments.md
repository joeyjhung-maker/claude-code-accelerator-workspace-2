# Segments — the spine + facets model, and finding gaps

Strategy is *what to make*. Segments are the biggest unlock — a new slice nobody targets wins even
with imperfect copy. (Full depth: `../00-full-system-reference.md` Part 2.)

## A segment is a slice; a persona is that slice as a person
Don't let "our 6 personas" cap you — there are thousands of slices.

## Spine + facets (not an N-cube)
- **Outcome is the spine** (vertical) — the desire/pain. The chunkiest, primary cut. Go narrower
  underneath (joint → knee, hip). Add as many outcomes as you want.
- **Demographic crosses** (horizontal) — gender, age, identity.
- **Qualifiers are filters** that thin a slice:
  - **Mechanism** = *universal, mass-market beliefs ONLY* (cortisol, testosterone — what the whole market
    already believes causes it). NOT your product's own proprietary mechanism.
  - **Condition** = a fact that limits fit (vegetarian, GLP-1 user, pregnant). *Stress-test it* — copy can
    fall inside a condition's bucket without the condition being the actual carve-out.
  - **Value** = a soft preference (organic-only, no-needle).
- A **persona** = an outcome × a specific set of facets, named.

Clean rule for the blurry ones: Demographic = what they ARE · Identity = who they SAY they are ·
Condition = a fact that limits fit · Value = what they PREFER.

## Two ways to add a new segment
1. **New outcome / pain point** → add a column (grow the spine).
2. **Go narrower** → add a condition or sub-slice inside one you have.

## Awareness is the layer ABOVE (not a facet)
Unaware · Problem · Solution · Product · Most-aware. Collapse to buckets (Unaware/Problem · Solution/
Product · Most-aware-to-everyone). The difference is mostly the *hook*; structure is similar. Keep it
out of the segment cube or you double-count.

## Coverage & gaps
The map = all possible slices (no holes). **Gaps appear only when you overlay your real ads.** Classify
each whole ad → one persona (its net impression) → shade coverage → empty cells = gaps. Rank gaps with
**Chad logic**: gap size · urgency of outcome (usually pain) · TAM · competitors/what's working ·
zeitgeist. Blend with **taste** (the intuition beneath words, built by saturating in the accounts).
Judgment + taste, fed back each cycle → instinct (the 1-of-1).

## Finding new segments (process)
Pull reviews (Amazon via Apify) + ad comments + competitor swipes → Whisper-dump your read into Claude
Code → extract specific outcomes & desires → build a master list of new segments. New outcomes grow
the spine; new conditions grow the facets.
