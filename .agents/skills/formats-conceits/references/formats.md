# Formats — the proven list

A format is how the argument moves through time. It fixes the beat order and nothing else.

## Proven (running in three or more winning ads across two or more brands)

| Format | The one-second test | When it wins |
|---|---|---|
| **Story / narrative arc** | A character moves symptom → dismissal → reveal → transformation. Is there a turn they didn't see coming? | problem-aware markets that need the mechanism smuggled in under a story |
| **Timeline / day-counter** | Three or more dated rungs (Day 1 / Day 3 / Week 1 / Week 3 / Day 30). Are the rungs dated? Two states with no ladder is a before/after, not a timeline. | any mechanism with a felt progression; sets expectations, inoculates the week-3 dip |
| **Song** | The lyric carries the argument; a chorus repeats a beat at fixed intervals. Remove the music: is there still a chorus? | novelty attention on a proven body; retention through repetition |
| **Long-form letter** | A first-person letter with a week ladder in the body and a P.S. stack at the close, 900–9,500 words. | problem-aware, concept-heavy markets where the claim needs warrant |
| **Listicle / "N things" / "N signs"** | Numbered items, each a self-contained scene, count announced up front. Could you reorder the items? | problem-aware self-selection; "N signs your X is Y" |
| **Historical exposé** | A dated timeline of a villain's decisions ending in today's symptom. Are there real years on screen? | markets with a ready-made villain; identity-heavy offers |
| **Comparison / test** | Ours vs theirs, or "we tested N"; the verdict lands before the reveal. Is there a loser? | solution-aware; lookalike and counterfeit problems |
| **Shock confession → pitch** | A staged confession or dare as the hook, hard cut to a talking-head pitch. Does the first line make the viewer complicit? | product-aware and offer-led |
| **Insider reveal** | "I probably shouldn't tell you this": a leak from inside the category, framed as a favour. Does the messenger have something to lose? | solution-aware, burned buyers |
| **Product demo / routine** | The object shown doing the thing; a routine ("every Sunday I…") carries the interest. Does the demo survive without the mechanism? | utility and gadget categories; product-aware |
| **Testimonial** | One person, one result, with specifics and a time window. Remove the person and their stakes: is anything left? | product-aware; social-proof-led |
| **Re-diagnosis** | "It's not X, it's Y": the relabel lands before the fix. Is there a relabel? | problem-aware markets that have been misdiagnosed |

## Seen once or twice (worth a small test)

Diagnostic "which type are you" · apology / correction letter · "do not buy this" reversal · review read → explain → pitch · scan or specimen walkthrough · 30-day challenge told after the fact · origin story · testimonial train · countdown / deadline · offer stack · "if you're A watch this, if B that" · factory tour · unboxing with a note · free-gift reason-why · one question, answered · hero vs villain three-act.
