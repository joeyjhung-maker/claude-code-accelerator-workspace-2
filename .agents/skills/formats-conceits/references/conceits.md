# Conceits — the proven list

A conceit is what media the ad pretends to be. It fixes who speaks, where, and the genre's conventions. Grouped by who is talking to whom.

## Proven

**One person talks to the viewer**

| Conceit | What it pretends to be | Who speaks | When it wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **UGC confession / review** | A phone clip from a real person mid-thought: car, kitchen, bathroom | a peer, a creator with niche authority, or the founder | unaware discovery; product-aware push; anything that must not read as a brand |
| **Founder direct-to-camera** | The person who built it, straight down the lens, no set | the founder | solution-aware ("I saw every other one fail and built this") |
| **Yapper** | A long, unedited, rambling monologue; the mess is the credibility | a peer or creator | problem-aware markets that distrust polish |
| **Spouse testimonial** | A partner tells the story on the user's behalf ("my husband…") | a witness with a stake | product-aware; male buyers reached through the partner |
| **AI avatar presenter** | A rendered elder or expert speaking to camera | an archetype, not a person | problem-aware supplement briefs; ancestral-wisdom angles |

**People talk to each other**

| Conceit | What it pretends to be | Who speaks | When it wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Podcast clip** | Two mics, a studio, cut in mid-conversation | HOST as the viewer's proxy, GUEST as the substance | problem-aware with an expert; solution-aware with a founder |
| **Street interview** | A stranger with a mic stops a stranger | INTERVIEWER with no stake; SUBJECT caught off guard | fastest public proof; blind tests, hot takes |
| **Doctor / clinic consult** | A clinician at a desk explaining, or defecting from what they used to prescribe | the practitioner | solution-aware indictments; mechanism made credible |
| **Talk-show parody** | A desk, a panel, a villain in the guest chair | host + the adversary | the mechanism as a comedy bit |

**A story is acted out**

| Conceit | What it pretends to be | Who speaks | When it wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fictional drama / skit** | A scripted scene with actors acting the arc | characters; the mechanism arrives as dialogue | problem-aware; sensitive topics a real face won't say |
| **Music video** | A song with visuals acting the lyric out | the singer as narrator | novelty on a proven body |
| **The villain speaks** | The seed oil, the rat, the tub: the problem testifies against itself | the adversary | failed-solutions and mechanism beats delivered by the enemy |
| **Fake organic post** | A long first-person post with one proof photo and a P.S. ladder | a peer writing to friends | concept-heavy problem-aware markets; static placements |

**Something is explained, shown or reacted to**

| Conceit | What it pretends to be | Who speaks | When it wins |
|---|---|---|---|
| **News broadcast** | Anchor desk, lower thirds, a field reporter | anchor + reporter | "this just in" timing angles |
| **Green-screen react** | A creator in the corner reacting to a stat, an article, a clip | a commentator | commentary on proof; a hook-swap chassis |
| **Scan / specimen review** | A doctor reads a scan or a specimen with a cursor | an expert reading evidence | the mechanism made visible |
| **Explainer with flat voice** | A wireframe or pixel-art lesson with an AI voice | no messenger; the diagram is the authority | unaware curiosity |
