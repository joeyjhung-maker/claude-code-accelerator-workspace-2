# Threads Structure Bank

Reusable post skeletons. **Structures, not content** — the shape is what gets reused, the words are always original.

Evidence is from Joey's own 403-post archive (Nov 2025 – Aug 2026).
**Baseline for comparison: median post = 295 views.**

# Part 1 — Mined from Joey's own archive

These are shapes that already worked on this account. Each one is niche-agnostic: nothing in the skeleton is faith-specific.

## 1. The Unglamorous Absolute
**Origin: fitness (swiped) · Used once · Best post on the account**

**Skeleton**
> I hate to break it to you, but [3–5 unglamorous fundamentals, listed] will [deliver the desired outcome] better than anything else.

**Mechanism**
The opener sets up a flinch — the reader braces for bad news. What lands instead is a boring truth, and that inversion is the engine: the "bad news" is that the answer was never a hack, it's the dull work they've been avoiding. Stacking 3–5 specifics makes it concrete *and* exhaustive-feeling. The absolute closer ("better than anything else") forces agreement or argument — both are engagement.

**Evidence** · 10 Feb 2026 — **19,233 views · 2,087 likes · 190 reposts** (65× median)

**Status: criminally underused.** One use in 403 posts.

## 2. The Definition Flip
**Universal · Used repeatedly · Consistently high rate, low volume**

**Skeleton**
> [Thing everyone wants] isn't [what they assume it is]. It's [the reframe].

**Mechanism**
Attacks a definition the reader is holding without examining. Very short, so it reads as an aphorism and gets screenshotted and reposted. The reader has to either accept the new definition or defend the old one — and defending happens in the replies.

**Evidence**
- "True knowledge is not what you know about God, it's how deeply you know Him." — 442v, **12.7%**
- "God cares more about fixing your heart than fixing your problems." — 597v, **12.1%**
- "The true flex is to be known in heaven before you're ever known on social media." — 1,896v, **11.1%**

**Note:** highest *rate* of any structure but rarely breaks big on views. Reliable, not explosive.

## 3. The Absurdly Simple List
**Universal · Two uses, both strong**

**Skeleton**
> [Number] reasons [outcome will happen]:
> 1. [Almost too obvious]
> 2. [Follows inevitably from 1]

**Mechanism**
The setup promises analysis and the payoff refuses to give any. The brevity *is* the argument — it says this is simpler than you're making it. Works because the reader expects effort and gets relief.

**Evidence**
- "2 reasons why you will succeed" — 586v, **16.4%** (2nd-highest rate on the account)
- "How to win in 2026: 1. Spent time with God 2. Do what he says 3. Don't give up" — 1,589v, **12.7%**

---

## 4. The Counter-Intuitive Method
**Universal**

**Skeleton**
> You don't [what everyone tries]. You [what actually works] until [problem dissolves rather than gets beaten].

**Mechanism**
Names the reader's failing strategy, then replaces it — not with more effort, but with a different mechanism entirely. The relief is that they were never going to win the old way, so their failure wasn't a character flaw.

**Evidence** · "You don't fight sin, you fill yourself with so much Holy Spirit that he deletes all desire for it." — 1,339v, **10.0%**, 19 reposts

---

## 5. The Reversed Posture
**Universal**

**Skeleton**
> You're waiting for [X to happen]. [X] is waiting on you to [act on what you already have].

**Mechanism**
Takes the reader's passive stance and flips who's holding things up. Mild accusation, but delivered as news rather than a telling-off — which keeps it inside the no-shaming rule.

**Evidence** · "You're waiting on God to move, but God is waiting on you to move in the authority He's already given you." — 504v, **11.7%**

---

## 6. The Research Reveal
**Universal · Underused**

**Skeleton**
> In [familiar source] there's [a detail everyone skims]. I looked into it — [specific finding]. Which means [reframe of the familiar thing].

**Mechanism**
Rewards the reader with something they can't get by skimming. The specificity is the proof of work, and the payoff reframes something they thought they already understood. Highly repostable because it makes the sharer look informed.

**Evidence** · The Psalm 23 "green pastures" Hebrew study — 904v, **12.2%**

**Status: used rarely.** Strong candidate for repetition — this one scales, because research is infinite.

## 7. The Scene Testimony
**Universal · Your signature, and your biggest asset**

**Skeleton**
> [Drop into a specific moment, sensory detail, no preamble]. [What happened, concretely]. [The turn]. [One universal line the reader can take for themselves].

**Mechanism**
Concrete detail buys attention that abstraction can't. The final line is what makes it shareable — without it, it's your story; with it, it's their story too.

**Evidence**
- The overdose vision — 1,100v, **14.5%**
- The morning spiritual attack + warfare prayer — 632v, **11.7%**
- The brother who fasted after being laid off — 960v, **11.5%**
- Hospital room return (Aug 2026) — 310v, **23 replies** — highest reply rate on the account

## 8. The Blunt Command
**Universal**

**Skeleton**
> Stop [what they're doing]. [Do this instead].

**Mechanism**
Two sentences, no explanation, no hedging. The absence of justification reads as authority. Works only when the speaker has standing — which is why it needs to sit among posts that earned it.

**Evidence** · "Stop negotiating with demons. Cast them out." — 1,511v, **9.9%**, 18 reposts

---

## 9. The Stacked Directive
**Universal**

**Skeleton**
> [Commit to a timeframe]:
> • [8–10 short imperatives, one per line]
>
> [One line naming who they become.]

**Mechanism**
The list is scannable and feels like a checklist the reader can screenshot — which is exactly what drives reposts. The closing transformation line converts a to-do list into an identity.

**Evidence** · "Dedicate 2026 to:" — 1,254v, **10.6%**, **22 reposts** (one of the highest repost counts on the account)

---

## 10. The Confession-to-Principle
**Universal**

**Skeleton**
> [What I did right, on paper]. [Why it still didn't work]. [The thing that actually changed it].

**Mechanism**
Admitting a failure while visibly competent buys enormous trust. The reader recognises themselves in the "doing everything right and still stuck" state, which is far more common than outright failure.

**Evidence** · "I did all the spiritual practices, looked like a good Christian, yet I still struggled to connect with God…" — 506v, **10.7%**

## 11. The Milestone Number
**Origin: recovery + weight loss · The single biggest structure in the corpus**

**Skeleton**
> [Precise number marking distance travelled]. [Where you started, stated flatly]. [No lesson. No moral. Stop.]

**Mechanism**
The number does all the work. No interpretation is offered, which is exactly why it lands — the reader supplies the meaning themselves, and meaning you generate yourself is the kind you act on. Adding a lesson deflates it, because it converts a fact into a sermon.

The top *five* posts in the entire cross-niche corpus are this structure. Nothing else came close.

**Evidence** · 41,077 likes (450→198 lbs) · 29,700 likes (4 years sober) · 21,449 likes (20 years sober) · 12,122 likes (16 months porn-free)

**For Joey** · You already own the strongest possible version — 12 years since the transplant. Your hospital post used it but buried the number under explanation. The pure form is the number and the starting point, and then silence.

## 12. The Rock-Bottom List Promise
**Origin: stoicism · Highest-converting long-form opener seen**

**Skeleton**
> [Time marker] ago, I hit rock bottom. [Two or three clipped fragments of the collapse.] Then I found [the thing]. Here are the [number] [truths/rules/shifts] that saved me:

**Mechanism**
Compresses an entire collapse into about twelve words — the brevity signals that the speaker isn't dwelling, which is what makes them credible rather than self-pitying. The colon opens a loop the reader has to close. The number promises a finite, scannable payoff.

**For Joey** · Your business-collapse-to-million-dollar-campaign story is a direct fit and you've never posted it in this shape.

## 13. The Compressed Aphorism
**Origin: stoicism · The most efficient post in the corpus**

**Skeleton**
> [Two to four words. Declarative. No verb of hedging.]

**Mechanism**
Short enough to be absorbed before the reader decides whether to keep scrolling. Reposts cost nothing to make and carry the whole idea.

**Evidence** · "Effort is attractive." — **36,203 engagements on three words.**

**For Joey** · Your Definition Flip (#2) is the same instinct at four times the length. The extreme-compression version is untested on your account.

## 14. The Unresolved Confession
**Origin: sobriety**

**Skeleton**
> [The hard thing] isn't easy. [Present tense. Still in it. No resolution offered.]

**Mechanism**
The opposite of testimony. Testimony is told from the far side; this is told from inside, and it recruits everyone currently in the middle — a much larger group than those who've come out the other end.

**For Joey** · Your archive is almost entirely resolved testimony. This is a genuine gap.

## 15. The Trait Redefinition
**Origin: discipline · Cross-niche confirmation of Structure #2**

**Skeleton**
> [Trait] is not something you either have or don't. It's something you [build/choose/practise], [one small increment at a time].

**Mechanism**
Removes the reader's excuse without shaming them — reframes a fixed trait as a repeatable action.

**Significance** · This is Joey's Definition Flip appearing independently in a completely unrelated niche. **That cross-niche repetition is the strongest evidence in the whole bank that the structure is real** rather than a quirk of one account.

---

## 16. The Open Question
**Origin: stoicism, money · Highest reply rates in the corpus**

**Skeleton**
> [One genuine question to the room. No preamble. No answer of your own.]

**Mechanism**
Replies weigh heavily in distribution, and a real question is the cheapest way to earn them. It only works when the asker isn't performing — a question with an implied correct answer reads as a quiz and gets ignored.

**For Joey** · Zero instances in 403 posts. Complete gap.

---

## 17. The Pre-empted Critic — ⚠️ use with care
**Origin: weight loss**

**Skeleton**
> "[The dismissive thing a critic would say.]" [Why you do it anyway.] [Not everything is for you.]

**Mechanism**
Manufactures an antagonist and lets the audience side with the speaker against them.

**⚠️ Flag** · This is the one structure here that sits close to Joey's reject list. It doesn't target a real person, so it isn't dunking — but it *works* by generating friction, and the line between "strong opinion" and "picking a fight with an imaginary hater" is thin. Included for completeness, not recommended.

## 18. The Optimal-Decision Release
**Origin: @growwithcolby (general self-improvement) · Swiped 17 Aug 2026 · Posted, result pending**

**Skeleton**
> [Label:] [outcome improves when you stop needing every X to be optimal]. [Three imperatives, one
> clause each, escalating toward the concrete and costly.] We waste an absurd amount of life
> [doing A] instead of [doing B]. [A good X done quickly] is worth more than [the perfect X you
> spent (specific time) on].

**Mechanism**
The label pre-frames the line as a contrarian gift rather than an opinion, so the reader accepts it
before evaluating it. The three imperatives turn an abstract claim into something doable in the
next hour. The diagnosis says **we**, not you — which lands an accusation while staying inside the
no-shaming rule. The close is a comparative with a specific time marker on the losing side, and
that specificity ("three years") is what makes it sting rather than sound wise.

**Lane fit** · Both. The secular version is decision paralysis; the faith version is
confirmation-seeking, which is live and under-addressed in prophetic culture.

**Length** · 60–75 words.

**Note on the imperatives** · Joey's rewrite escalated toward the material ("Start the project")
rather than the devotional. Naming actions the audience genuinely avoids is what makes the stack
work — praying is not one of those. See [[2026-08-17-stop-needing-confirmation]].

**Source swipe** — 12.9K likes, 1K reposts on the original.

## 19. The Cleared Suspect
**Origin: @sun.yi (general self-improvement) · Swiped 2026-08-21 · 15 drafted, none posted yet**

**Skeleton**
> [Obvious thing the reader assumes is the real failure] is not [the category]. But [the subtle,
> easy-to-miss thing] is.

**Mechanism**
Names the culprit the reader already suspects, then acquits it — which buys trust because it feels
fair rather than accusatory. The real charge lands on the second clause, which is quieter and
therefore harder to see coming. Two lines only; the whole post is the twist, so any extra words
before or after dilute it. The elided final "is" (referring back to the category named in line 1)
is what makes it read as a single thought instead of two separate claims.

**Source swipe** — "Stupidity is not a sin. / But ignorance is." — 1.8K likes, 248 reposts, 26
replies on the original.

**For Joey** · Fifteen drafted from the wiki in one pass (repentance, essence, wounds, thankfulness,
authority, Joseph's bitterness, mastery, times and seasons, glory, hearing God's voice,
consecration, dabar, appetite, rank and order, guarding the mind) — see chat 2026-08-21. Untested
on the account; the source account's 1.8K/248 suggests this shape travels. Watch reply count
specifically — the "which one is it, then" framing tends to bait a defence of the "obvious" thing
in the replies.

## Corpus health — read this before trusting the above

**The corpus is noisy.** Of 203 posts, a meaningful share is unusable:

- **Impersonation accounts.** Fake Sylvester Stallone, fake Tyler Perry, fake Eminem — engagement-farming or outright scams. Excluded.
- **Non-English clusters.** "Debt free" is dominated by a large Filipino community; "writing" surfaced Vietnamese IELTS content. Structurally interesting, but not modelable here.
- **One-word and hashtag posts.** "writers", "#sobrietyjourney" — high engagement, no structure to extract.

**The biggest caveat: photos are doing the work in the top posts.** The 40K-like weight-loss posts are before/after image posts. The copy is almost incidental. Joey cannot reproduce that with text alone — *except* that his hospital-room post is exactly this form, and it's his highest-reply post ever. Milestone + photo is a real option, just a rarer one.

**Sample sizes are small.** Threads search caps returns; 203 posts across 10 niches is roughly 20 per niche. Treat these as leads, not laws.

## Notes on reading this bank

**Rate vs reach are different problems.** The Definition Flip has the best engagement rate but rarely travels far. The Unglamorous Absolute travelled 65× further than median. High rate means the people who saw it responded; high views means it escaped the follower base. Structures 1, 6 and 9 escape. Structures 2, 4 and 5 convert.

**The archive's real lesson:** the best structures were used once or twice and abandoned. Nothing here needs inventing — it needs repeating.
