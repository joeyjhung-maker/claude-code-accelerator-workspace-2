## 1. The overdose and the transplant — the spine

The foundational event. Everything else dates from it.

**As Joey has told it:**
> "God saved my life in this hospital room. I'd been admitted for a horrendous drug overdose.
> Required a liver transplant, one of the sickest patients in the UK... It was here I first
> called to God and he answered." — 11 Aug 2026 · 310v · **16.1%**

> "11 years ago I woke up from a coma. I'd overdosed on drugs... my mum visited everyday. She
> prayed over me. Told me 'Trust in God, the best days are yet to come'. I gave my life to Jesus
> in that hospital." — 15 Nov 2025 · 121v · 9.9%

**ANCHOR DATE: January 2014** — confirmed by Joey 2026-08-17.

Store the date, never the elapsed number. "11 years ago" was true when he wrote it in Nov 2025 and
is wrong now. **Compute from Jan 2014 every single time you use it.**

As of August 2026 that is **12 years** (12 years 7 months). It ticks over to 13 in January 2027.

⚠️ **Still open — retelling varies on one detail.** ICU length is given as "1 month in ICU then
2 months recovering" (Aug 2026) and as "3 months in ICU" (Jan 2026, Nov 2025). Same three-month
total, different split. **Joey — which is right?** Everything else in this entry is settled.

**Details available across the tellings:** coma · one of the sickest patients in the UK · five
open-wound surgeries · multiple organ failure · liver transplant · couldn't lift his head off the
pillow · his mother visited every day and prayed over him · full recovery.

**Unused shape:** this is the strongest Milestone Number (#11) available and it has never been
posted in the pure form — *the number, where it started, then silence*. Every telling so far
explains. The corpus says explanation deflates it.
