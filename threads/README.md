2. **Pick the nouns** from [[testimony-bank]] or a Research Reveal dig
3. **Draft against** [[threads-voice]]
4. **Check the reject list** below before posting
5. **Log the result** into `data/threads-archive.csv` so the bank keeps learning

## Rules that bite (Threads lane)

- **No shaming.** Ever. Diagnose with "we", not "you". Delivered as news, never as a telling-off.
- **No dunking.** No manufactured antagonist, no imaginary hater to rally against. This is why
  structure #17 is flagged do-not-use.
- **Never invent a spiritual experience.** No vision, dream, word, healing, or answered prayer
  unless it is in this folder or Joey said it in session. This is the faith-lane version of the
  Dan-truth constraint, and it matters more, because a fabricated testimony is not a copy error —
  it is a credibility kill.
- **Never quote scripture from memory.** Joey supplies it, or the reference gets verified. A wrong
  citation in a prophetic post destroys the authority the post depends on.
- **No guaranteed outcomes.** No prosperity promises, no "God will definitely", no claiming a word
  over the reader's specific situation.
- **Structures travel, content doesn't.** Swiping a shape from fitness or stoicism is legitimate
  and already produced the best post on the account. Swiping words is not.
