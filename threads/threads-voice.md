## The hard limit: 500 characters per post

**Threads caps a post at 500 characters.** This is mechanical, not stylistic. The archive proves
it — max is exactly 500, **zero posts over**, and 38 sitting in the 480–500 band. Joey already
writes to the cap.

**For Joey's writing, 500 characters ≈ 91 words** (his archive runs 5.45 chars/word).

**Always count characters, never words, and always state the count.**

### One post or two — say which, every time

Every draft must declare **ONE POST** or **SPLIT**, with the character count of each part.
Never hand over copy that silently exceeds 500 — it gets truncated or rejected at post time.

- **Under 500** → one post. Default. Most ideas should fit; the median post is 246 characters.
- **Over 500** → a split, and then the cliffhanger rule below is mandatory.

### THE CLIFFHANGER RULE — for any split

*Banked 2026-08-17 on Joey's instruction.*

**When a post splits, part 1 must end mid-sentence, cut at the point of maximum incompletion.**

The reader has to click through to part 2 to find out what happened. A part 1 that ends on a
complete thought gives them permission to stop, and the rest of the thread dies unread.

Joey's own examples:

| Part 1 ends… | Part 2 opens… |
|---|---|
| "…and nothing I did worked. So I just" | "stopped." |
| "…what we built here in intimacy away from the world became" | "the foundation of my faith." |

**How to cut well:**
- Break mid-clause, ideally one word before the payoff — "So I just" / "became"
- Never end part 1 on a full stop
- Never end on a complete image the reader can walk away satisfied with
- The word you withhold should be the one carrying the meaning

Threads shows a `1/n` counter, so the reader knows there's more. The cliffhanger makes them care.

## Shape on the page

- **Median post: 246 characters / 45 words.** That's the *style* number. Quartiles 22 / 45 / 71
  words. The 103-word maximum in the archive is the 500-char ceiling, not a preference.
- **Line break between every idea.** Blank line, not a paragraph. Almost no post in the archive
  is a block of text.
- **Lists use `•`, `-`, or `1.`** — all three appear. Short items, no sub-clauses.
- **Emoji: rare and never decorative.** A 🙌🏻 on a genuine celebration, a 🔑 heading a list.
  Roughly one post in twenty.
- **Text posts almost exclusively** — 387 of 403. Only 14 images and 2 carousels.
  Worth knowing, because the strongest cross-niche shape (#11 Milestone) usually carries a photo.

## Register

British spelling — "prioritising", not "prioritizing".

But the register is transatlantic-casual, not formal British. The archive freely uses:

> "If God ain't in it, I don't want it."
> "You gotta be careful during wilderness seasons."

So: contractions yes, slang yes, but no swearing and no irony. The tone is **sincere**.
That is the single biggest separation from the Flexxable voice, which runs on dry wit.

**No hedging.** Declaratives. "The Lord will not send you until he forms you." — not
"I think maybe the Lord tends to."

## The vocabulary

Highest-frequency substantive words across all 403 posts:

**spirit** (58) · **Jesus** (56) · **heart** (52) · **prayer** (37) · **kingdom** (34) ·
**season** (33) · **spiritual** (32) · **presence** (27) · **father** (20) · **heaven** (18)

This is the insider register. Get it wrong and the post reads like someone writing *about*
Christians rather than from inside. Words like *season*, *presence*, *kingdom* and *breakthrough*
carry specific weight in this world and cannot be swapped for plain-English synonyms.

**Adjacent prophetic vocabulary** to use with care: confirmation, activation, discernment,
soaking, standing in the gap, quickening, open/closed door, wilderness season, the still small
voice. All real usage — but reaching for too many at once reads as performance.

## Pronouns for God — LOCKED

**God, He, Him, His, Himself — always capitalised.** Same for Jesus, Holy Spirit, Father, Lord.

Locked by Joey 2026-08-17. The archive is inconsistent ("His presence" and "More him." days
apart); Joey's ruling is that the lowercase instances were laziness, not style. Treat every
lowercase occurrence in the archive as an error, not as evidence.

This applies to anything drafted from here on, including quotes pulled forward from old posts —
if an old line gets reused, fix the casing.

## What performs, in his own numbers

**Reach and rate are different problems.** The archive's biggest post by views did 354,177 views
at **0.06%** engagement — distribution without connection. His best *real* post did 19,233 views
at **12.2%**. Judge drafts on rate, not reach.

Highest-rate posts (300+ views) are all one of three things:

1. **A list of what happens when X** — "What happens when the Hand of God is upon you:" 22.4%
2. **A flat declarative reframe** — "You know you've truly surrendered when you no longer care
   about the outcome." 14.5%
3. **First-person testimony, concrete** — the hospital room, 16.1%; the overdose vision, 14.6%

## Reject list

- Shaming the reader, in any dose
- Manufactured antagonists ("some of you need to hear this…")
- Guaranteed outcomes or prosperity promises
- Invented spiritual experience — see the hard rule in [[threads-README]]
- Scripture quoted from memory
- Corporate or marketing register bleeding in from the day job
- Three abstract benefits stacked in a row — the [[no-stacked-abstract-triplet]] rule applies here too
