## A — What your answer decides

*Structure: #2 Definition Flip · 217 characters · ONE POST*

> Access isn't what you ask God for.
>
> It's how you answer what He's already asking you.
>
> Requests are limited by your needs. Answers are the size of your heart — and that decides how much of His agenda you get to carry.

**Source line:** "My answers to God matter more than the requests I make to God... The size of your answer determines the size of your heart and readiness to carry God's agenda."

## D — The mechanism nobody clocks

*Structure: #6 Research Reveal · 471 characters · ONE POST*

> In Matthew 16 everyone quotes "I'll give you the keys." Almost no one clocks the mechanism.
>
> The door is on earth. The keyhole is on earth. The key is used on earth.
>
> But the key comes from heaven.
>
> Consecrate yourself down here and you provoke a response up there. Peter didn't ask for the keys — he answered a question, and heaven responded.
>
> Every ministry, business and relationship you're building is the same shape: an answer on earth, waiting on heaven to respond.

**Source line:** the "How Keys Work" mechanism + Matthew 16:13-19 scripture, both direct from the note.

## E — How keys actually work

*Structure: #9 Stacked Directive · 403 characters · ONE POST*

> This is how spiritual keys actually work:
>
> • The door is on earth
> • The altar is built by you
> • Consecration provokes a response
> • Speaking opens what silence can't
> • The key still comes from heaven
>
> Keys aren't abstract. They're tangible — a ministry, a business, a relationship you keep putting off starting.
>
> You don't inherit access. You build the door on earth, and heaven sends the key to open it.

**Source line:** "How Keys Work" bullets + "Keys Are Tangible" section, both direct from the note.
