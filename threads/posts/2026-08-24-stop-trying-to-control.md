## What Joey changed, and why it matters

My draft: *"Start trusting the One who already has it."*
His: *"Start trusting the King who already has it."*

"The One" is generic — could be anyone's god. "The King" is specific to the kingdom-vocabulary
register the account already runs on (**kingdom** is the 5th-highest word in the archive at 34
uses) and it pairs with "already has it" better — a King *holding* something reads as authority,
where "the One" just reads as vague reverence.

**Signal for [[threads-voice]]:** when naming God generically in a reframe, reach for a title
that carries authority (King, Father, Lord) over an abstract pronoun (the One, a higher power).
Single instance — watch whether this repeats before promoting it into the locked voice rules.
