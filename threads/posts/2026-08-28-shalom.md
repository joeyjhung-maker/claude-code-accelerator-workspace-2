## D — More than the English word

*Structure: #6 Research Reveal · 377 characters · ONE POST*

> Shalom means way more than "peace."
>
> In Hebrew it's completeness, welfare, health, prosperity, quietness, contentment, good relationships — and specifically peace that survives having been at war.
>
> Isaiah 26:3 ties all of it to one place: a mind stayed on Him.
>
> Guard the mind, and shalom follows. Shalom multiplies. What multiplies becomes the ground real authority stands on.

**Source line:** the Hebrew word-study definition + the "Shalom and Authority" chain, both direct from the note.

---

## E — The chain in order

*Structure: #9 Stacked Directive · 398 characters · ONE POST*

> Everyone wants the authority. Almost no one wants the chain that produces it.
>
> • Guard your mind
> • A guarded mind produces shalom
> • Shalom produces rabba — the Hebrew word for increase, multiply, fill up
> • Multiplication is what real authority gets built on
>
> Skip a step and the next one doesn't hold. You can't multiply what you haven't guarded, and you can't lead from a mind that's still at war.

**Source line:** "Guarded mind → Shalom → Rabba (multiplication) → Authority," the note's own chain, direct.
