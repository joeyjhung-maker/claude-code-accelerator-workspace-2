*Drafted 2026-08-17. Status: UNPOSTED — awaiting Joey's pick.*
*Brief: an unconventional mistake immature believers make, and what to do instead.*

**The brief's built-in problem:** "mistake immature believers make" points a finger by

## A — Naming the season ← recommended

*Structure: #2 Definition Flip into #8 Blunt Command · 52 words*

> We rush to name the season.
>
> Breakthrough year. Wilderness. Warfare.
>
> Naming it feels like discernment. Most of the time it's impatience wearing a spiritual word.
>
> You don't need the label. You need to stay.
>
> God usually tells you what it was on the way out of it, not on the way in.

**Why this is the unconventional one.** It critiques the insider vocabulary the audience uses
daily — *season*, *wilderness*, *warfare*, *discernment* all appear in Joey's own archive. Turning
that language back on itself is a thing almost nobody in the lane says. "Impatience wearing a
spiritual word" is the line that gets screenshotted.

## B — Only bringing God the big things

*Structure: #10 Confession-to-Principle · 57 words*

> I prayed hard about the big things. The move. The business. Whether to go at all.
>
> And almost nothing in between.
>
> Years in, I still struggled to know His voice — because nobody learns a voice from six
> conversations a year.
>
> Intimacy isn't built at the crossroads. It's built in the traffic.
>
> Bring Him the small thing today.

⚠️ **Needs Joey's confirmation before posting.** This makes a first-person claim about his prayer
life. The move is documented ([[testimony-bank]] #10, a year of praying about leaving the UK) and
"I still struggled to connect with God" is a real line from his archive — but the specific claim
*"I only prayed about the big things"* is mine, not his. **Confirm it's true or I reshape it.**

#10 is underused: one instance in 403 posts, 10.7%.

## C — Conviction misread as condemnation

*Structure: #4 Counter-Intuitive Method · 52 words*

> We feel conviction and hear condemnation. So we hide.
>
> But conviction always comes with an invitation attached — it names the thing and points at the
> door in the same breath.
>
> Condemnation just names it and leaves you standing there.
>
> If it drives you away from God, check the source. It isn't Him.

⚠️ **Doctrinal position, not mine to assert.** "Condemnation isn't from God" is mainstream
teaching, but Joey is the theologian here, not me. Co-sign it or cut it.

Also the most-trodden of the three — this gets preached often. Least unconventional.
