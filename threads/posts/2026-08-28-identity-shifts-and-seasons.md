## A — Heaven first

*Structure: #13 Compressed Aphorism · 44 characters · ONE POST*

> Heaven shifts first. Then the earth follows.

**Source line:** "Until heaven shifts us, we cannot shift the earth." (paraphrased, not quoted
verbatim — the note attributes this to Dale Mast directly.)

---

## B — The one that sets you free

*Structure: #2 Definition Flip · 110 characters · ONE POST*

> Insecurity binds you.
>
> Pride blinds you.
>
> Confidence is the only one of the three that actually sets you free.

**Source line:** "Insecurity is a binding force, and pride is a blinding force. Confidence, however,
is a liberating force that empowers us for our destiny."

---

## C — What the old identity can't hold

*Structure: #4 Counter-Intuitive Method · 150 characters · ONE POST*

> You don't grow into a new season by trying harder in the old one.
>
> You grow into it by letting new thoughts replace the identity that can't hold them.

**Source line:** "A new way of thinking cannot be contained in an old identity. The old identity
will eventually derail the new set of thoughts if we do not allow those thoughts to shift our
identity."

---

## D — Three signs the shift isn't finished

*Structure: #9 Stacked Directive · 435 characters · ONE POST*

> Three signs your identity hasn't caught up with your season:
>
> • You keep complaining — treating your circumstances as bigger than who God says you are
> • You define yourself by what you do, not by whose son you are
> • New levels of authority and responsibility feel uncomfortable instead of natural
>
> None of these mean you've failed. They mean the old identity is still trying to hold thoughts it was never built to carry — and it can't.

**Source line:** the "Warning signs of a failed shift" list, direct from the note.

---

## E — What Satan actually attacked first

*Structure: #6 Research Reveal · 406 characters · ONE POST*

> Satan's first move in Eden wasn't attacking the fruit. It was attacking identity — casting doubt on the Father's love, then implying we were made inferior.
>
> Same pattern shows up at every transition since. Before David took the throne, he went back to the sheep.
>
> The call to destiny doesn't cancel the ordinary. It just asks you to do the ordinary in a way that proves you already carry the extraordinary.

**Source line:** "Identity and the Garden" section + the David/Samuel "Parallel Realities" section,
both direct from the note.
