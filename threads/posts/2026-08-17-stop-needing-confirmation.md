## What Joey changed, and why it matters

My draft: *"Send the text. Give the money. Say the prayer out loud."*
His: *"Start the project. Give the money. Take the first step."*

Mine escalated toward the spiritual — the prayer was the biggest ask. **His escalates toward the
material.** "Start the project" is a business-lane imperative sitting inside a faith-lane post,
which means the post now speaks to both pillars at once instead of only the faith one.

He also cut "Say the prayer out loud", which was the weakest of the three — praying is not the
thing his audience avoids, so it broke the pattern of naming genuinely avoided actions.

**Rule this suggests:** in the imperative stack, escalate toward the concrete and the costly, not
toward the devotional. Watch whether this repeats before promoting it.
