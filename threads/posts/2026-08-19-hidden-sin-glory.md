## What Joey changed, and why it matters

My last draft used "drowns the [noun]" and offered 10 candidate nouns pulled from the essence
note's own vocabulary (calling, gift, anointing, office, mantle, bones, assignment, voice,
garment, season). Joey picked neither the verb nor a noun from that list.

**His verb: "taints."** Softer and more precise than "drowns" — sin doesn't destroy the glory,
it corrupts/discolours it. Closer to the source material's own language (essence-and-identity.md
describes hidden sin as *dishonouring*, *corrupting the presentation* — not obliterating).

**His noun: "glory."** Not on my list at all — it comes from a different note
(`glory-as-manifest-goodness.md`) about glory being tangible, measurable, and able to relocate
(Ichabod). This is a stronger pairing than anything I offered: it ties hidden sin directly to the
idea that glory can depart and go sit with someone else who's ready to steward it, which is a
sharper, more theologically loaded consequence than a generic "calling" or "gift."

**Rule this suggests:** when a word choice feels off, the fix may not be a better synonym for the
same concept — it may be pulling in a different note's vocabulary entirely. Worth watching whether
this repeats.
