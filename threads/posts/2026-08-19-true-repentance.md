## What Joey changed, and why it matters

My draft ran as a single unbroken sentence: *"Repentance is agreeing with God that what He called
sin actually is sin, then walking the other way."* Joey split it into two beats and added "True"
before the second one.

**Splitting it does the work the voice file already documents** — one idea per line, not a block —
but it's worth banking as a live example: a belief-shift post reads better as *claim, then
reframe* on separate lines than as one sentence carrying both halves.

**Adding "True"** turns the second line into an explicit answer to the first ("Feeling sorry isn't
repentance" → "**True** repentance is...") rather than a stray restatement. Small word, but it's
what makes the two lines argue with each other instead of just sitting next to each other.
